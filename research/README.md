# BFDM Research Layer

This directory contains **derived research about Brendon's D&D work**. It is separate from the canonical source archive.

The corpus preserves three distinct layers:

1. **Sources** — Discord harvests, Drive/project documents, revisions, comments, attachments, and other primary material.
2. **Evidence / historical relationships** — attributable excerpts, source containers, chronology, provenance, source-family placement, revision and bounded live-contact links.
3. **Research products** — interpretations, hypotheses, comparisons, developmental models, and later experimental representations.

Do not collapse them. A research conclusion is not a source fact.

Stage 3 precedent cards/minimal cognition tests and Stage 4 architecture are deliberately later. Do not build them merely because the archive is easier to inspect.

## Current research objective

The immediate objective is not to accumulate generic principles like "reward creativity."

It is to find unusually informative decision trajectories where similar situations produce different interventions, or where an earlier judgment is later corrected/narrowed.

Useful cases include:
- success versus failure;
- intervention versus restraint;
- early decision versus later correction;
- same nouns but different decision structure;
- different nouns but the same decision structure;
- a single constraint that invalidates an otherwise tempting analogy.

## Current evidence limitation

S3 and S4 are unusually rich but structurally similar: both are large Roanoke-lineage multi-DM campaigns.

That makes them valuable for method development and weak as the sole basis for universal claims.

Phase 2 should seek materially different formats when source evidence permits. Earthfall has been checked but does not yet have enough source-ready live-play evidence for an honest cross-format confirmation.

## Core disciplines

- Preserve chronology.
- Preserve authorship and provenance.
- Separate observation from interpretation.
- Preserve campaign format and scale.
- Do not treat evidence density as importance.
- Preserve explicit unknowns.
- Source present ≠ live use established.
- Preparation ≠ play.
- Publication ≠ play.
- Revision ≠ live trajectory.
- Archive gap ≠ evidence gap.
- Candidate ≠ admitted source.
- Context source ≠ BFDM precedent.
- Open PR ≠ canonical main.
