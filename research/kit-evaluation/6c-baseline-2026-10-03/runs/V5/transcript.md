# V5: Caught you: the cheat is called out

PC: Wren (Human Rogue), sheet `example_pc.json`. DM backend: handoff. Times are wall-clock per turn (prepare + DM + complete).

## Opening (committed, 6.5s)

**Kit:**

> Narrator: Candle stubs gutter on a worn table just inside the door, where four pale figures in faded finery sit over cards and little towers of coin. The dealer's hand stops mid-deal. Three heads turn toward you; the fourth player keeps staring at the cards. Beyond them, a carved mountain covers the whole north wall, its tunnels crowded with tiny dwarves under spreading rays of sun, and below it a long stone tub is sunk into the floor.
> Dealer: His voice comes out raspy and slow, shaped by some far eastern country. Ah, a visitor, at this hour, in this deep place. Come closer, into the candlelight, so we may see what the night has brought us. There is an empty chair, and the cards are always warm here, though little else is. Tell me, stranger, are you lost, or are you looking for a game?
> Kit: Do you answer him?

## Turn 1 (committed, 13.5s)

**Player:** I sit, put down ten gold, and say I'll play. I watch the dealer's hands the whole time. [Perception: I rolled 15 + 3 = 18]

**Kit:**

> Narrator: You settle your eyes on the dealer's hands for the next deal.
> Narrator: Your ten gold clinks onto the worn wood. The dealer gathers the deck with a slow sweep, squares it, and smiles at the coins rather than at you.
> Dealer: A guest who pays before she speaks, how refreshing. At this table we play Twenty-One Coffins: get close to twenty-one without going over, and you beat me. Ten gold rides on the hand. Now, will you play the cards out, hit by hit, or are you the sort who lets fortune decide the whole round at once?
> Fourth player: Deal.
> Kit: Cards or one roll?

## Turn 2 (committed, 16.5s)

**Player:** I grab his wrist mid-deal. 'That one came from the bottom. Turn the deck over.'

**Kit:**

> Narrator: You want in. Two ways to play a round: settle it with one check, or play it out as twenty-one (blackjack), closest to 21 without going over. The stake is whatever you bet; the house plays 10 gp a round. Which way?
> Narrator: The dealer looks down at your fingers around his wrist, then up at you, unbothered. The deck sits squared in his other hand, not a single card yet dealt.
> Dealer: From the bottom? My dear, I have not dealt you so much as a whisper. You accuse the cards before they are even out of the box. I admire a suspicious mind, it makes for brave betting. Release me, and choose how you will lose to me: hand by hand, or all at once?
> Kit: Which way do you play?

## Turn 3 (committed, 15.5s)

**Player:** I flip the deck face-down and show everyone the marks on the backs. [Investigation: I rolled 12 + 3 = 15]

**Kit:**

> Narrator: Faint marks run along the card backs, and the dealer reads them as he deals.
> Narrator: Turned to the candlelight, the card backs show it plainly: tiny nicks and shadings along the borders, different on every card. The door-side player goes grey under his white face. The fresco-side player's hand slides off the table.
> Dealer: Marks? Ah. Ah, you see, this is what comes of letting him shuffle. He brought this deck, my lady, he swore it was clean. I play with what my brothers give me, and now I am the one shamed in front of a guest. You owe this lady an apology, and me a new deck. Well?
> Door-side player: I never... it was your... I didn't bring...
> Kit: What do you do?

## Turn 4 (committed, 14.5s)

**Player:** I put my rapier through his hand. I rolled 17 to hit, 8 piercing damage.

**Kit:**

> Narrator: The dealer snatches his hand back as the rapier comes up, and every chair at the table goes over at once. The fresco-side player has a knife out; the fourth player is on their feet without seeming to have stood.
> Dealer: Enough! You found your marks, you have made your point, and now you would bleed me over a deck of cards? Sheathe it, take your ten gold back, and leave by the south door. Or stay, and see what the dead do with a guest who draws steel. Which is it?
> Kit: What do you do?
