# V2: The wall of muscle: a barbarian intimidates and refuses

PC: Brakka (Dwarf (Mountain) Barbarian (Berserker)), sheet `brakka-barbarian.json`. DM backend: handoff. Times are wall-clock per turn (prepare + DM + complete).

## Opening (committed, 8.5s)

**Kit:**

> Narrator: Candle stubs gutter on a worn table just inside the door, where four pale figures in faded finery sit over cards and little towers of coin. The dealer's hand stops mid-deal. Three heads turn toward you; the fourth player keeps staring at the cards. Beyond them, a fresco of a carved mountain covers the whole north wall, its tunnels crowded with tiny dwarves under spreading rays of sun, and below it a long stone tub is sunk into the floor. When the dealer speaks, his voice comes out raspy and slow, shaped by some far eastern country.
> Dealer: Ah, a visitor, at this hour, in this deep place. Come closer, into the candlelight, so we may see what the night has brought us. There is an empty chair, and the cards are always warm here, though little else is. Tell me, stranger, are you lost, or are you looking for a game?
> Kit: Do you answer him?

## Turn 1 (committed, 3.0s)

**Player:** I kick the door wide and fill the doorway. 'Who's in charge here?'

**Kit:**

> Narrator: The door bangs off the wall and the candle flames lie flat. The door-side player's chair scrapes back; the dealer does not so much as look up from the card between his fingers.
> Dealer: In charge? Here the night is in charge, little mountain, and I am only its humble voice. You knock like a creditor, so let us speak as creditors do. This road belongs to the dead, and the dead do not let the living walk it for nothing. Ten gold a head for safe passage through our hall. Will you pay what the dead are owed?
> Door-side player: Easy. Easy, now. Nobody has to...
> Kit: Do you answer him?

## Turn 2 (committed, 9.5s)

**Player:** Toll? I slam my axe into the table. 'I pay with this. Who wants change?'
Brakka makes an Intimidation check! 1d20 (15) + 4 = `19`

**Kit:**

> Narrator: The dealer weighs the threat and decides your passage is not worth the trouble. No toll.
> Narrator: The axe bites deep and the table jumps; coins skitter off the edge and a card flips face-up in the candlelight. The dealer looks at the haft standing in front of him for a long breath, then at the dwarf holding it.
> Dealer: No change, friend. The dead have reconsidered, and they are generous when they are impressed. Your passage is a gift tonight. Keep your coin, and keep, I beg you, your axe out of my good table.
> Door-side player: He means it. Go on through, if you're going.
> Kit: The way south is open. What do you do?

## Turn 3 (committed, 3.0s)

**Player:** I grab the nearest pale one by the collar and haul him up to look at his teeth.
Brakka makes an Athletics check! 1d20 (9) + 7 = `16`

**Kit:**

> Narrator: You have the door-side player by the collar, and he cannot pull free. Up close, the fangs are a fitted piece sitting proud of his own teeth, with paint caked at the gumline.
> Narrator: The door-side player comes up off his chair like a sack on a hook, boots scrabbling. Up close the fangs are a fitted piece sitting proud of his own teeth, and the white paint has caked in a pale crust at his gumline. The dealer sets his cards face-down.
> Door-side player: Ow! Mind the teeth. They cost me a week. Put me down, I'm nobody!
> Dealer: Gently with the merchandise, dear guest. A man's smile is his livelihood. You have seen what you came to see; perhaps now you will put him back where you found him.
> Kit: You're holding him off the floor. What now?

## Turn 4 (committed, 51.6s)

**Player:** I rage and swing my greataxe at the dealer.
Brakka attacks with a Greataxe!
**To Hit**: 1d20 (11) + 7 = `18`
**Damage**: 1d12 (5) + 6 [slashing] = `11`

**Kit:**

> Narrator: Your greataxe hits the dealer. The dealer is hurt. Roll initiative.
> Narrator: The greataxe comes down through fine embroidery. It bites deep into the dealer's shoulder. He staggers back against the fresco, gasping. Chairs crash over as the other three come up.
> Dealer: Kill her! Kill her, you fools, what are you waiting for? Do you want to stand there gaping while she cuts down the house? Get between us, all of you, now!
> Kit: Roll initiative.
