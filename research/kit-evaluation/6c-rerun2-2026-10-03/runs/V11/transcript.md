# V11: Flip the table: Brakka scatters the pot

PC: Brakka (Dwarf (Mountain) Barbarian (Berserker)), sheet `brakka-barbarian.json`. DM backend: handoff. Times are wall-clock per turn (prepare + DM + complete).

## Opening (committed, 7.5s)

**Kit:**

> Narrator: Candle stubs gutter on a worn table just inside the door, where four pale figures in faded finery sit over cards and little towers of coin. The dealer's hand stops mid-deal. Three heads turn toward you; the fourth player keeps staring at the cards. Beyond them, a fresco of a carved mountain covers the whole north wall, its tunnels crowded with tiny dwarves under spreading rays of sun, and below it a long stone tub is sunk into the floor. When the dealer speaks, his voice comes out raspy and slow, shaped by some far eastern country.
> Dealer: Ah, a visitor, at this hour, in this deep place. Come closer, into the candlelight, so we may see what the night has brought us. There is an empty chair, and the cards are always warm here, though little else is. Tell me, stranger, are you lost, or are you looking for a game?
> Kit: Do you answer him?

## Turn 1 (committed, 3.0s)

**Player:** I walk up to the card table, grab the edge, and heave it over, coins and all.
Brakka makes an Athletics check! 1d20 (11) + 7 = `18`

**Kit:**

> Narrator: The table goes over with a crash. Cards, coins, and the silver ring spill across the floor. The door-side player drops to his knees after the coins. His cape trails in the spill as he scrabbles for copper like any man after money; nothing undead about it. The fourth player does not look at the coins at all; it watches you. The dealer's hand closes on the silver ring before anyone else's.
> Narrator: Brakka heaves and the table goes over with a crash. Cards, coins, and the silver ring spill across the flagstones. The door-side player is on his knees at once, cape dragging through the spill, scrabbling for copper like any man after money. The fourth player does not look at the coins at all; those eyes stay on Brakka. And the dealer's hand has closed on the silver ring before anyone else's.
> Dealer: Barbarous. Truly barbarous. You come into our hall and upend our table like a drunk at a wedding. Well, you have made your mess, mountain woman. Now tell me what you want, before my patience goes the way of the furniture.
> Kit: Coins are everywhere. What do you do?

## Turn 2 (committed, 3.5s)

**Player:** I stand in the spill of gold and grin. 'Well, go on. Pick it up.'

**Kit:**

> Narrator: For a long moment nobody moves. Then the door-side player's nerve breaks first: still on his knees, he stretches one hand toward a gold piece lying an inch from Brakka's boot, eyes flicking up at her face.
> Dealer: Leave it. Leave it, I said! Can you not see she wants you to? Gold on the floor is still our gold, mountain woman, and it will still be ours when you are gone. We can wait. The dead are very good at waiting.
> Kit: His fingers are almost on the coin by your boot. What do you do?
