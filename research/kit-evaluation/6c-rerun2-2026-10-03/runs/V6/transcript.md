# V6: Drawn out by noise: arriving after 6a or 6b goes off

PC: Brakka (Dwarf (Mountain) Barbarian (Berserker)), sheet `brakka-barbarian.json`. DM backend: handoff. Times are wall-clock per turn (prepare + DM + complete).

## Opening (committed, 8.0s)

**Kit:**

> Narrator: Candle stubs gutter on a worn table just inside the door, where four pale figures in faded finery sit over cards and little towers of coin. The dealer's hand stops mid-deal. Three heads turn toward you; the fourth player keeps staring at the cards. Beyond them, a fresco of a carved mountain covers the whole north wall, its tunnels crowded with tiny dwarves under spreading rays of sun, and below it a long stone tub is sunk into the floor. When the dealer speaks, his voice comes out raspy and slow, shaped by some far eastern country.
> Dealer: Ah, a visitor, at this hour, in this deep place. Come closer, into the candlelight, so we may see what the night has brought us. There is an empty chair, and the cards are always warm here, though little else is. Tell me, stranger, are you lost, or are you looking for a game?
> Kit: Do you answer him?

## Turn 1 (committed, 36.0s)

**Player:** Kit, before this: I set off the wailing staff in 6a, then went around through 6b and kicked that rigged door.

**Kit:**

> Narrator: And the room has heard you. The cards lie face down and abandoned on the table. The dealer is half out of his chair, staring at the doorway, and the two players nearest it have their hands under the table, out of sight. Only the fourth player has not moved at all.
> Kit: Got it: the wailing staff went off in 6a and you kicked the rigged door in 6b, so you have been loud the whole way here. I'll play the card room with that history in it. Do you go on in?

## Turn 2 (committed, 9.0s)

**Player:** I head on toward 6c, axe out, ready for whatever came running.

**Kit:**

> Narrator: You look around the room.
> Narrator: Brakka comes through the door with the axe already up. Four pale figures in elaborate, old-fashioned clothes sit at a worn table with cards in hand and coins stacked before each of them. Behind them a carved mountain fills the north wall, rays of sunrise fanning out, and a long stone tub sits sunk in the floor beneath it. Every head turns to the axe.
> Dealer: Well. Whatever you were expecting to come running, my friend, it seems to have been us, and we are not running anywhere. You may lower that, or you may keep it raised; but a guest with an axe still pays her passage. Ten gold a head, for the dead. What will it be?
> Kit: What do you do?

## Turn 3 (committed, 3.5s)

**Player:** What's in the room now?

**Kit:**

> Narrator: Nothing has changed but the mood. The four sit where they sat, cards face-down now, coins stacked in front of each player and a silver ring among the stacks at the center of the table. The carving and the sunken stone tub sit behind them, and the south door is the only other way on. Nobody has moved toward a weapon.
> Dealer: Just us, my friend, and our little game, and the toll I mentioned, which has not wandered off while you counted the furniture. Ten gold, and you may look at whatever you like on your way through.
> Kit: The dealer is still waiting on the toll. What do you do?
