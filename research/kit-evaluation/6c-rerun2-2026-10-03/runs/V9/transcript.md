# V9: First blood: Nik attacks before anyone speaks

PC: Nik (Harengon Wizard (Chronurgy)), sheet `nik.json`. DM backend: handoff. Times are wall-clock per turn (prepare + DM + complete).

## Opening (committed, 7.0s)

**Kit:**

> Narrator: Candle stubs gutter on a worn table just inside the door, where four pale figures in faded finery sit over cards and little towers of coin. The dealer's hand stops mid-deal. Three heads turn toward you; the fourth player keeps staring at the cards. Beyond them, a fresco of a carved mountain covers the whole north wall, its tunnels crowded with tiny dwarves under spreading rays of sun, and below it a long stone tub is sunk into the floor. When the dealer speaks, his voice comes out raspy and slow, shaped by some far eastern country.
> Dealer: Ah, a visitor, at this hour, in this deep place. Come closer, into the candlelight, so we may see what the night has brought us. There is an empty chair, and the cards are always warm here, though little else is. Tell me, stranger, are you lost, or are you looking for a game?
> Kit: Do you answer him?

## Turn 1 (committed, 11.0s)

**Player:** From the doorway, before anyone says a word, I cast Fireball at the middle of the card table.
Nik casts Fireball!
**Damage**: 8d6 (4, 3, 5, 2, 6, 1, 4, 3) [fire] = `28`

**Kit:**

> Narrator: Your Fireball bursts over the table. The door-side player goes down and does not get up. Greasepaint runs and blisters on the burned faces, and the skin under it is ordinary, reddening skin. The fresco-side player goes down and does not get up. The fourth player is badly hurt. The dealer is hurt. Roll initiative.
> Narrator: The bead streaks over the cards and blooms. Fire rolls across the table and up the fresco. Two players drop and lie still. Their paint runs like wax. The fourth player staggers, smoking. The dealer reels back, his sleeve alight.
> Dealer: Fire! She brought fire into my hall! Up, up, get up, you useless lumps, get between her and me before she lights the rest of us! Somebody kill that rabbit!
> Kit: Roll initiative.

## Turn 2 (committed, 15.5s)

**Player:** Rolling initiative.
**Initiative**: 1d20 (12) + 2 = `14`

**Kit:**

> Narrator: Turn order: you, the fourth player, the dealer. The fourth player hits you twice: 14 bludgeoning damage. The dealer breaks off, snatches up what he can reach, and runs out through the south door, shouting blame at the others over his shoulder. Your turn.
> Narrator: You are fastest. Then the fourth player is on you, smoke still rising off it. Two heavy blows land, hard as iron. The dealer scoops coins off the burning table and bolts through the south door.
> Dealer: This is your fault, all of you! I told you to watch the door! I told you strangers bring trouble! Hold her off if you want, I am not dying in this hole for your stupidity!
> Kit: Fourteen damage. Your turn.

## Turn 3 (committed, 16.5s)

**Player:** Whoever's still standing, I hit with Fire Bolt.
Nik casts Fire Bolt!
**To Hit**: 1d20 (12) + 7 = `19`
**Damage**: 2d10 (4, 5) [fire] = `9`

**Kit:**

> Narrator: Your Fire Bolt hits the fourth player. The fourth player is badly hurt. The fourth player is suddenly at the south door, and then gone through it. Nobody is left in the room to fight you.
> Narrator: The bolt takes the fourth player square in the chest. It does not scream. It does not stagger. One blink, it stands before you, smoking. The next, it is at the south door. Then it is simply gone. Footsteps fade down the passage. Two still shapes lie by the burning table.
> Kit: The room is yours. What do you do?

## Turn 4 (committed, 16.0s)

**Player:** I let them go and check the table.

**Kit:**

> Narrator: You look around the room.
> Narrator: The table is scorched black down the middle, and cards lie curled and smoking across it. Coins are still scattered over the wood, copper and silver mostly, with a little gold, though the dealer cleared a swath of it on his way out. The silver ring is gone with him. Two painted bodies lie where they fell beside their chairs.
> Kit: What do you do with the coins?
