# Phase 2 — Stress pass on the ten contrast families

**Status:** attack on `contrast-families-v1.md`. Not a new set of families.  
**Base:** `main` at `3310c19c` (Drive history substrate merged).  
**Labels:** the v1 names (story-bearing uncertainty, purpose viability, and the rest) stay disposable handles. They are not a Kit ontology.

This pass looked for three kinds of evidence on each family:

- **SUPPORT** — a different case with the same boundary;
- **LIMIT** — the boundary holds only under a narrower condition;
- **BREAK** — a case where using the proposed boundary would pick the wrong decision.

Search was Brendon's Discord messages in the Roanoke S3, Empire City, and Empire City dev indexes, plus whether Earthfall, Bastion/Redoubt, and At War's End containers carry a live-decision link. Earthfall (`BCS-000054`, `BCS-000055`) and Bastion/Redoubt (`BCS-000056`–`BCS-000058`) have no `IMPLEMENTED_IN` link. Their texts are scripts and introductions. They were not used as live trajectories. At War's End remains draft revision, not live adjudication.

Every new hit below is still Roanoke-lineage multi-DM play. None of these is the materially different format the v1 file asked for. No family died in this pass. That is a search result, not a confirmation.

## CF-01 — handwave vs preserve procedure

**LIMIT, with support inside it.** `865473071165734923`, `865474003006128128`, `865474136716345365` (Empire City `#out-of-character`, 2021-07-16).

A player missed a live event and was overwhelmed. Brendon: "I'm more likely to hand wave things that get you to come play." Then: "Its the leaving that we usually need gated." Then: "I don't put obstacles out there to keep you from playing."

The v1 boundary says procedure stays when it still holds unresolved consequence. Here he waives the entry step even though a scene was available, and he keeps a different gate: leaving. Applying "preserve any procedure that could still be a scene" would have refused this handwave. That application would be wrong. The surviving condition is narrower: an entry barrier to play is expendable; a commitment/exit gate is not, unless the skipped step is itself the future-defining problem (the Golden Dawn memory case).

Same shape, smaller, in S3: `734502868723433572` offers a dock-worker scene or a handwave, and `735253731406381078` handwaves shopping when the prices are already known and time is short.

**BREAK:** not found. The memory-removal refusal still stands as the case where the shortcut would erase the arc.

## CF-02 — world support vs outcome authorship

**SUPPORT.** `748245430990536715` through `748245541535744080` (`#influences-for-s4`, 2020-08-26).

A player offers the period phrase "the Great Unwashed." Brendon: "Yeah. The Great Unwashed are followers of the Spider God Web." Then: "i hadn't planned it / but now thats canon."

He authors the setting fact immediately. He does not hand the player the decision of what the phrase means. That is the world-support side of the v1 split, not the hands-off side. It does not break the Victor "hands off the wheel" case, which was a player moral conflict rather than a new setting noun.

**BREAK:** not found.

## CF-05 — local correction vs architectural rewrite

**LIMIT.** `741750463157764126` (`#hex-m-howling-woods`, 2020-08-08), during the Ozark Howler fight.

He had just quoted the wail. Then: "im nerfing the damage from wail to just those not at 1 hp." The next line applies 5 psychic damage to those who passed.

This is a local correction, so the scale claim survives. It limits the resurrection case. There, tonight's resurrections were preserved and the rule changed afterward (`744056939096571924`). Here the ability was already declared and the damage was cut in the same resolution. "Preserve outcomes already earned" is not part of the general local-repair rule. It was a choice in the resurrection correction.

**BREAK of scale-matching:** not found. No message in this pass shows him rewriting a campaign because one ability was too strong, or nerfing one die because the ending architecture had failed.

## CF-07 — world autonomy vs adjudication eligibility

**SUPPORT inside the same format, not a format transfer.** `850796729494536193` (Empire City `#server-rules`, 2021-06-05): "No PVP without DM present/ and all parties consent."

The S3 consent gate is restated as a server rule in S4. `#level-20-pvp` is a channel where that consented mode happens. That supports the eligibility boundary. It does not show him adjudicating an unconsented attack, which is what a break would be.

`867290263960813569` ("Do you consent to the rite of ink?") is consent inside the fiction. It is not the table gate. It is not used here as support.

## CF-09 — late-campaign density vs missing structure

**LIMIT.** `741742535495647282` (`#town-square`, 2020-08-08), before the week-5 announcement.

"(player led exploration, the hunt will begin as soon as you head over, feel free to RP for as long as you want, I'm just not providing any prompts)"

He then runs the Howler encounter in `#hex-m-howling-woods`, including initiative and the wail. Withholding prompts is not the same as withholding the scene. And it happens in week 4, not only as a final-week reduction. `742121160170537050` likewise calls a Mason rose-garden quest line player-led.

The diagnostic claim can survive: he removes the kind of authorship the table already has (prompts, pacing of the approach) and still supplies the function it does not have (the creature). The v1 wording "late-campaign" is narrower than the practice.

**BREAK:** not found. Nothing here shows him filling a late campaign with more authored scenes because an outline said the week was full, against accumulated play.
