## What this tranche is

An adversarial reading of the ten Roanoke-lineage contrast families against Earthfall and Saturday D&D evidence that is materially different in format, plus a bounded Phase 2B creative-method reading of the same texts and of Bastion/Redoubt.

Phase 2A dispositions are in Part B. They attack the existing families. They do not add an eleventh family and they do not rewrite the v1 or stress files.

Phase 2B material is Part C, and the design-source cases in Part A are marked **DESIGN**. A design case is not a live adjudication. A user report inside a partial workbench recovery is not a complete transcript.

## Source limit

Earthfall workbench history is `BCS-000152`–`BCS-000162`. Saturday D&D workbench history is `BCS-000163`–`BCS-000172`. Both are partial recoveries. Bastion/Redoubt here is the introduction and character-creation text (`BCS-000057`, `BCS-000058`), not a live log. The Earthfall system texts are `BCS-000054` and `BCS-000055`.

These containers are not complete transcripts. Four kinds of text stay distinct:

1. **USER** — Brendon's recovered message, or a verbatim user fragment the recovery marks as such.
2. **ASSISTANT** — generated proposal. Not Brendon's act, acceptance, or table delivery.
3. **USER-REPORTED LIVE** — Brendon describing what players did or how a session felt, inside a workbench thread. That is evidence of his report. It is not an independent play transcript.
4. **DESIGN** — an authored system, introduction, or script. It can support a design-method claim. It cannot prove the table received it.
5. **RECOVERY NOTE** — the archive's own status paragraph. It is not a chat turn. Where a recovery says a passage is not verbatim, this file repeats that limit.

Silence in a partial transcript is not deliberate non-intervention during live play. A rejected assistant proposal is observable only where the user text rejects it.

At War's End was not read for this tranche.

Locator notation is the container id, then inclusive line numbers in that container's `source.md`, written `BCS-######:Lstart-Lend`. Every such citation below was checked against the file. The example pattern in this sentence is not a source.

### CF-01 — NOT_COMPARABLE

CF-01 asks when procedure can be skipped because its uncertainty is already spent (the consented limb) and when it must stay because it still holds a future (the Golden Dawn memory). E01–E02 are about not scripting the players' path through a fight, and then accepting a bypass into a puzzle. E02 is a preparation ban on predetermined beats. S06 later asks for conditional beats with triggers and freeze-paths. Those are both about how much of the future the prep is allowed to write. They are not about handwaving a procedure whose function has expired. Using CF-01 to bless or forbid the manticore puzzle would answer a question the family does not ask.

### CF-02 — LIMIT / NEW_DIMENSION

CF-02 splits world support from outcome authorship: investment can make the object real and still forbid the DM from authoring what the players do with it. E01 supports the second half only in a limited way. He calls the combat avoidance good and asks for a puzzle so they can make the manticore theirs. He does not take their choice away. He also does not promote an incidental institution the way Victor or Ferrytown were promoted. The Neris correction (S04) is a different limit: an assistant's useful continuation was invalid because the NPC did not know the fact the repair required. Agency of the players and epistemic validity of an NPC are both constraints on intervention. Neither one is the "incidental player-owned institution" half of CF-02.

### CF-03 — NEW_DIMENSION

CF-03, in the v1 and stress files, is creativity tested against shared-world externalities: a cool exception can still be refused when other people have to live with it. E04 and E06 and the manticore labels are creative use of affordances already in the fiction (quarters, a tagging gun, audience bait, an achievement voice). They do not show him granting an exception that spends a constraint other players depend on. S01's silence rule is a limitation turned into the puzzle, not a waived rule. That is a design candidate (H3), not a CF-03 adjudication.

### CF-04 — NEW_DIMENSION

CF-04 is whether prepared play still has a purpose after the table's circumstances change. E03 abandons Fryvern art production in that thread and keeps the encounter; the cabinets are deferred, not judged nonviable. E05 reports that a delivered Fryvern fight fizzled and asks for a transition. That is a purpose problem after delivery, and the repair is unproven. Component abandonment and encounter abandonment are different axes (H4). The tranche does not show him keeping or scrapping a prepared encounter because the live circumstance had changed its purpose, which is what would move CF-04.

### CF-05 — LIMIT / NEW_DIMENSION

CF-05 separates a local correction from an architectural rewrite. The stress pass already limited it: a live nerf need not preserve the blow just declared. E05 and S04/S07 do not test that scale claim. The fizzle is a reported experiential failure followed by a request for pressure. The Neris repair fails at a prior layer: the assistant's continuation used knowledge the NPC did not have. Both are "something went wrong, then someone tried to fix the next beat." Neither is a local rules patch, and neither is a campaign rewrite. Final repairs are unproven in both trajectories. The limit is causal layer, not a BREAK of scale-matching. No text here shows him rewriting Earthfall or Saturday because one ability was too strong.

### CF-06 — NOT_COMPARABLE

CF-06 distinguishes authority the players claim from authority they have performed. E07 designs paid access to more character options. B01 designs the starting role of city guard, with requisition instead of owned arsenal. Those are authored on-ramps. They are not a record of a player earning jurisdiction by doing the job. S02's House corrections are about not inventing over an existing map. They do not award anyone authority.

### CF-07 — NEW_DIMENSION

CF-07 is which attempted actions are eligible to be adjudicated, including consent before PvP. S04/S07 is which facts an NPC is eligible to know before that NPC may act on them. H1 names that candidate. It is not the table-consent gate, and it is not a ruling about whether a player action enters the fiction. E01's acceptance of the bypass is about a player approach he was willing to adjudicate. It does not restate or break the consent rule.

### CF-08 — LIMIT (DESIGN VS LIVE)

CF-08 is preserving real pressure while repairing the information that makes the pressure legible. E04 is an intention to telegraph the cabinets. No player reading is in the source, so it cannot support "the players understood." E05 is a report that a climax failed because the party was overpowered, plus a request to put pressure back. That request is not an observed correction of danger the players could not see. The stress file already left the deadliness/telegraph case unadvanced outside Roanoke. This tranche does not advance it into live Earthfall evidence. The limit is design and request versus observed repair.

### CF-09 — NEW_DIMENSION

CF-09 is about late-campaign density: removing authorship the table already has, and still supplying a function it lacks. The stress pass already found that player-led withholding of prompts is not only a final-week habit. S04 is a stalled investigation in a Saturday workbench: the clues are not being found, the scene is barricaded, and he asks to get back on the rails. The gap is real in his report. The assistant's first fill was still illegal because Neris did not know Tasha's name. A missing function does not license a fact the world does not contain. This is not the late-campaign authoring-density case, and it is not evidence from the end of a long Roanoke season.

### CF-10 — NOT_COMPARABLE

CF-10 is lead-DM restraint toward another human DM's canon and table. No co-DM, delegated human authority, or cross-DM canon dispute appears in the Earthfall, Saturday, or Bastion spans read here. Assistant error and user correction are not that relationship.
