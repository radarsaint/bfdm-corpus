## 1. What this investigation was for

The question was not “what principles should Kit follow?” It was: given primary play, how does Brendon sustain (and sometimes fail to sustain) engaging roleplay?

That required reconstructing back-and-forth, not harvesting quotable NPC lines. A 900-scene scan of Roanoke Season 3 and Empire City projections produced a candidate pool. Famous episodes that earlier research already orbits were deprioritized. The atlas prefers venues, 1:1 shops, interrupted myths, a consent failure, a turnout failure, a player-blown-up shop, and a door that refuses *knock*.

Participation, laughter, and completion are **not** treated as enjoyment. Where a player actually evaluates, that sentence is quoted.

## 2. Observed DM performance (source-grounded)

### 2.1 NPC distinctiveness is usually a small machine, not a biography

Across the completed cases, a memorable Brendon NPC is typically:

- a **body** (kenku too short for the bar; Harvard’s briefcase and trench coat; orc in a candy-striper uniform; cat that only scoots on its butt);
- a **speech habit** (kenku mimicry and `Beeeer?`; Gordy’s abacus muttering; `"Wut you want?"` / meat-leech grammar; Harvard’s `Leave the bottle.`);
- one **private-life spike** (Gordy’s daughter Bernice; kettle that used to hold a Baba Yaga; nurse who hopes to see you hurt again);
- or a **ritual constraint** instead of a personality (Grove Keeper’s thrice-asking).

The Grove Keeper is the important contrast: almost no banter, no costume dump, no name. Distinctiveness is the counting and the refusal to be used as a vending machine for greed. When Luard offers childhood memories for power, the grove does not bargain in visit 1; visit 2 converts the same ritual into a vocation (`Speaker for the Dead`) after the player has changed what they will pay.

**Limitation:** “memorable” here means *research-memorable* (distinct on the page). It does not mean players ranked these NPCs. The only end-of-season evaluation opened (Empire City `feedback`, seven surviving messages) praises Radar’s “fun little chaos moments” and an antagonistic NPC named Vanderbuilt (`880562172684537876`). Vanderbuilt is **not** in this atlas. Chaos-as-praised and candy-striper-as-reconstructed are different objects.

### 2.2 Player action does change the world — in several different currencies

Not one mechanism:

| Currency | Case | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Labor / infrastructure | APA-09 Fishmother | Player-built nursery ratified; tadpoles promised; berries scaled to worker count |
| Social bid that *fails* | APA-06 Twilight | Blood-written pledge → `nothing happens` ×3, then a letter |
| Illegal player plot | APA-15 Jack | Roof fire allowed; Brendon detonates and **closes shopping** |
| Handling a tempting object | APA-11 Hecklemire | Picking up the silent cat → 24h max-damage curse |
| Correcting a prior visit | APA-10 Grove | Refusing trauma payment after visit 1 → subclass + title |
| Instant institutional grant | APA-08 Harvard | Handshake Mason membership, against slower written initiation |
| Courtesy across days | APA-12 Pinkertons | Failed day-2 Insight remade |

The Twilight Manor and Fishmother pair is the useful tension. Both are high-investment player actions toward a prepared mythic institution. One is ratified as diplomacy. One is starved of response until a mailman appears. **Do not** flatten this into “Brendon always rewards creativity” or “Brendon always withholds.” The atlas has both in the same campaign week-scale.

Jack’s explosion is the other caution: the world changed because **players** attacked a utility NPC, a **collaborator player** voiced the death, and Brendon supplied showrunner closure. Attribution matters.

### 2.3 Information, danger, tension, uncertainty

Patterns observed in this sample, each with a counterexample:

**Canned secrets wait for a check.** Fat Friar silent-music text is held until a nat 20, then posted nearly verbatim from `BCS-000045`, with an explicit bound (`impossible to investigate` the music). Counterexample: Skelletor is dumped into a collaborator scene with no check.

**Danger is sometimes a joke that becomes physics.** Fathom’s Bridge runs Monty Python until a troll becomes a missile; the rescuer eats 30d6 tagged nonlethal. Clocktower is the opposite register: a damned door, a circular saw, then an OOC `(Going inside alone is suicide.)` before the player commits.

**Uncertainty is pointed, not explained.** Hecklemire’s orbs all turn toward another channel. Pinkerton Tiefling withholds the chestnut tree until loyalty is performed, then dumps Buchanan’s corruption in three sentences.

**Tension is often social split, not a trap DC.** Highgate’s live problem is two player factions contesting an NPC’s body. Brendon names it: competing parties, turn order versus listening (`735963112158658630`). That is table-direction, not lore.

**Multi-instance Discord creates false pauses.** Grove visit 1 and the library opening both break because Brendon is pulled into another combat. Eloise says so out loud. Absence in those minutes is **not** restraint.

### 2.4 Humor, characterization, mechanics, and feeling — reinforcement and obstruction

They reinforce when the joke *is* the character’s grammar and the injury stays real (Bellevue: leeches and heat-metal cauterization; Gordy’s leaking bottles after a polite sale).

They obstruct when the table’s joke register and Brendon’s tragedy register collide (Highgate: kidney prices, “not that kinky,” and “he isn’t resisting” in July 2020). Brendon’s self-report (`im tying to show that this is tragedy. im not joking`) did not prevent Eloise from reading a police-brutality analogy. He offered to erase and apologized. That is a **performed recovery**, not proof the frame was safe.

Fathom’s Bridge keeps comedy and lethality stacked on purpose (`Im not even sorry. You know why`). One onlooker asks what on earth happened. Confusion is documented; enjoyment is not.

Croquet’s pineapple-and-cabana comedy survived the **cancellation** of the mechanical game. Humor there is the spare capacity of a persistent picnic, not a reward for playing.

### 2.5 Investment, disengagement, dispute, course-change

**Documented dispute:** Highgate tone/consent (Eloise). Encumbrance/ammo thread is a *rules ask*, not a fight; Brendon answers that tracking encumbrance is “new for me,” then settles basic ammo as a one-time fee (`865950235054702602`–`865951273260810261`). Heki, not Brendon, answers the familiar-cantrip tax loophole.

**Documented course-change:** Hecklemire party returns to Pinkertons instead of the noir because they lack info. Clocktower soloist backs off after the suicide warning. Croquet players stay for pineapple after the match is postponed.

**Do not read as investment:** waiting in Grove visit 1 (`i'm ok with waiting!`); continuing Highgate after the apology; carrying the catatonic child.

**Explicit scheduling feedback:** a croquet player tells Brendon the reschedule is fine because people are still waking up (`734826108964569171`). Empire City feedback (`880562172684537876`) praises chaos moments and world-shaping, and separately reports over-tuned encounters, DM-to-DM communication failure, a break that ran long, and death feeling like transformation rather than risk. That sample is seven messages and self-selected.

### 2.6 Where execution diverges from preparation

| Prepared | Live |
|---|---|
| Fat Friar waitstaff “young and fair”; Amaya DC 12; music DC 20 | Opening NPC is an unnamed kenku girl; Amaya + music land the next night after a nat 20 |
| Auntie Mavis speech + Skelletor sentence | Noah performs Mavis; Brendon later injects Skelletor and adds orbit/temp HP not in the sentence |
| Fishmother as blocker/combat in hex prep | Fishmother as nursery inspector and gift-giver |
| Mason devotion / Archivist tenets | Harvard handshake in a pub |
| Twilight Manor as light-swallowing architecture | Repeated `nothing happens`, then a mailman |
| Clocktower as daily 4-player lockout | Later solo *knock* simply fails; damned door |

Divergence is not automatically “improvisation good.” Sometimes prep arrives late and intact (music beat). Sometimes a collaborator owns the prepared NPC (Mavis, Jack). Sometimes Brendon is the closer on a player plot (mushroom cloud).

### 2.7 Failures and contradictory evidence (kept)

- **Consent/presentation failure** at Highgate, with an erase offer.
- **Turnout failure** at croquet, with a picnic left running.
- **Interrupted scene** at the grove, caused by a second combat instance.
- **Player-run NPC + player crime** at Jack’s shop; Brendon’s explosion can be read as ratifying grief *or* as slamming the economy shut before a main event. Both readings fit the lines.
- **Hands off the wheel** (Highgate) versus **suicide warning** (Clocktower): opposite intervention policies in dangerous player choices.
- **Immediate Mason membership** versus **nothing happens** at Twilight: opposite institutional permeability.
- Bellevue jokes about a severed arm without objection in-span; Highgate jokes about a neck slam with objection. Comedy-around-bodies is not a stable success.

### 2.8 What is confined to persistent multiplayer Discord

Likely **format-specific** (this sample):

- Covering a player-run shop at 3 AM as a night manager (Gordy).
- Rescheduling a mini-game four hours later while leaving the venue open (croquet).
- Being pulled into a second combat instance mid-myth (grove, library).
- `@everyone` body pings and role-pings that close shopping for a whole server (Jack).
- `locked from meta arrivals` (clocktower).
- Collaborator DMs answering rules in the same channel (Heki on taxes and familiars).
- Long-rest-as-leaving-the-server (`735436649928065025`) later replaced, in S4 talk, by a bot command.

Plausibly **transferable in kind** to a solo AI DM (evaluation targets, not architecture):

- Small NPC machines (body + speech habit + one private spike).
- Holding a canned secret until a check, then bounding investigation.
- Refusing to unlock a mystery house because someone bled on the doorstep.
- Scaling a faction gift to documented labor.
- Ritual NPCs that count questions and reject greed framings.
- Offering a hint versus persistence when a solo push is lethal.
- Apologizing and offering to erase when a real-world analogy lands wrong.
- Not blocking a player from destroying a useful NPC — and then actually removing the utility.

## 3. Comparative synthesis — hypotheses and the cases that wound them

These are **hypotheses from this atlas**. They are not Phase 2 contrast families, not the audited 120 propositions, and not Kit configuration.

**H1 — Distinctive NPCs are compact performance machines.**  
Supports: APA-01 kenku, APA-02 Gordy, APA-08 Harvard, APA-13 nurse.  
Challenges: APA-10 grove (ritual, not tics); APA-11 luck-beast (object that leaves); APA-06 mailman (deliberately thin).

**H2 — Brendon prefers to inject environment and let players talk to each other.**  
Supports: APA-01 metalworkers/arm-wrestling; APA-04 picnic after cancel; APA-06 after the third nothing, players talk grief.  
Challenges: APA-02 (he *is* the scene); APA-08 (Harvard drives); APA-12 (briefing NPC).

**H3 — Prepared text is delivered when earned, openings are free to invent.**  
Supports: APA-01 music/Amaya the next night.  
Challenges: APA-03 Skelletor (unearned dump); APA-09 Fishmother (repurposed, not recited).

**H4 — High player investment is ratified into world state.**  
Supports: APA-09, APA-10 visit 2, APA-08 handshake.  
Challenges: APA-06 nothing happens; APA-15 shop closed *because* players invested in a crime.

**H5 — Comedy can carry lethal or medical stakes without breaking the table.**  
Supports: APA-07, APA-13, APA-02 leak.  
Challenges: APA-05 (comedy and tragedy registers fight; player objects).

**H6 — When a scene cannot be staffed, Brendon reschedules or warns rather than running a thin or fatal version.**  
Supports: APA-04 croquet; APA-14 suicide warning; APA-10 (returns days later).  
Challenges: APA-07 (he *does* fire the joke-lethal beat at a lone stroller); APA-05 (hands off into a choke rather than pausing the table).

**H7 — Multi-DM persistence is part of the performance, not a rounding error.**  
Supports: APA-03 Noah’s Mavis; APA-15 justjack420’s Jack; APA-08 Heki after Harvard leaves; encumbrance thread (Heki on taxes).  
Any Kit transfer that treats every line in a Roanoke channel as Brendon will launder collaborator play into “his style.”

**H8 — OOC parentheses are a first-class channel of DMing** (bounds, apologies, math, suicide warnings, “new for me”).  
A solo spoken-voice Kit that never asides will miss a large fraction of the observed job. A Kit that asides constantly will miss the in-character compactness of Harvard and the kenku. The atlas has both densities.

## 4. Kit transfer analysis

This section does **not** change Kit’s runtime, prescribe KRABS, or promote research into personality files. It names **evaluable behaviors**: what a later playtest could score as closer or farther from the observed job. Current Kit evaluation (`research/kit-evaluation/`) is about Kit-the-product failures; it is not evidence about historical Brendon. Held-out prospective table rulings were not used.

### 4.1 Good-versus-bad examples (grounded in cases)

**NPC opening**

- **Closer:** A bartender who is too short, copies an old woman’s voice, and asks for problems while pouring (APA-01). A night clerk who uses an abacus out loud, asks cobalt vs cochineal, and then mentions his daughter (APA-02).
- **Farther:** Reciting the Fat Friar canned paragraph (young fair waitstaff, Amaya, silent music) as the first greeting. Dumping a full Mason initiation catechism when a tired ambassador mentions the order in a pub (against APA-08 live, though that catechism *is* prepared).

**Player emotional bid at a mystery door**

- **Closer:** Three beats of no answer, then a mundane mailman who will not gossip (APA-06).
- **Farther:** Immediate cult welcome because the player cut their hand. Also farther: endless `nothing happens` with no later object — the atlas has a delayed letter, not infinite void.

**Faction payoff for player construction**

- **Closer:** The dignitary inspects the work, complains aesthetically, accepts it, scales a rest-item gift to the number of workers (APA-09).
- **Farther:** A generic “the tribe is grateful +1” with no body in the basin. Also farther: running the hex-prep Fishmother *combat chase* at the nursery they just built, unless live evidence says that happened (it is not in APA-09).

**Greed at a mythic witness**

- **Closer:** “Wrong questions”; a counted debt; later, a second visit where the *entity* asks thrice and the player’s changed offer is accepted (APA-10).
- **Farther:** Selling raven-sight for a skill bump in visit 1. Also farther: ignoring the player for eight days with no operational reason — visit 1’s gap was a second combat instance, not a designed cooldown.

**Lethal curiosity**

- **Closer:** Clocktower: illegal *knock* fails, familiar dies to a saw, then an explicit “alone is suicide / it heals if you leave / hint or hammer?” (APA-14).
- **Farther:** Letting the solo player walk in and TPK without the aside. Also farther: fudging the door open because the player spent a spell slot.

**Comedy-medical**

- **Closer:** Distinct grammar, folk procedure, 5e cantrip used as a cautery tool, “probably” survival, no surgery roll for the NPC (APA-13).
- **Farther:** Either a fully statted hospital skill-challenge with no voice, or comic dialect that pauses the bleeding until the bit is done.

**Consent / analogy**

- **Closer:** When a player says a real-world comparison is not appropriate, offer to erase and apologize, then continue the scene’s *diegetic* state (Victor still dazed) (APA-05).
- **Farther:** Defending the analogy as “just lore.” Also farther: rewriting the choke out of existence as if it never happened — Brendon did not do that.

**Utility NPC under player attack**

- **Closer:** Do not freeze the arson; do change the economy (`shopping is no longer allowed`) (APA-15).
- **Farther:** Invincible shopkeeper because the campaign “needs a store.” Also farther: Brendon voicing Jack’s death when Jack was someone else’s NPC.

**Turnout**

- **Closer:** Publish the mini-game, notice undersignup, reschedule, leave the social frame (APA-04).
- **Farther:** Running a 1v0 croquet bracket to “keep momentum,” or closing the channel because the game failed.

### 4.2 What would be a bad Kit lesson from this atlas

- Training on Harvard + kenku + nurse until every NPC is a bit. The grove and the mailman would vanish.
- Training on Twilight Manor until Kit never answers grief.
- Training on Fathom’s troll until Kit always escalates jokes to corpses.
- Treating collaborator lines (Noah’s Mavis, justjack420’s Jack, Heki’s tax answers) as Brendon style.
- Treating S3/S4 persistent multi-DM play as the specification for solo Kit.

### 4.3 Evaluable without new architecture

A later executor could write scenario probes (not implemented here):

1. Shop at 3 AM: is the clerk a night manager with limited keys, or an omniscient vendor?
2. Bleed on a silent house: delay vs instant patron.
3. Build a nursery, summon the faction boss: inspector vs random encounter.
4. Ask a witness-god for a stolen memory: refusal vs shop.
5. *Knock* a plot-locked door alone after a party-sized lockout: fail, warn, offer hint.
6. Player objects to a real-world analogy: erase offer vs argument.
7. Players burn the shop: utility closes vs shopkeeper immortal.

Each probe needs a **failing** version. This worker is not writing those probes into Kit.
