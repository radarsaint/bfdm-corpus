This note does not change PR #28. It does not certify CF-02. BDC-S4-004, BDC-S3-013, and BDC-S3-L01 were not reviewed.

CF-02 says player investment may justify more world support and less Dungeon Master control over outcomes.

BDC-S3-011 is one apology. nut_wizard said the dating-sim joke was the players' job. Brendon agreed and named player agency. No change in control is shown.

BDC-S3-008 is different and still does not prove the contrast. He announced that DM invitationals were back, and a Highgate invitational he offered was accepted and begun. That is an actual support mechanism, and one use of it started. The announcement says the system is for the DMs to improvise, that a DM usually approaches the player, and that a request can be refused. Player interest is allowed as a trigger. It is not shown to be why the system returned. "Are back" cuts against "this week's play caused it."

The two cases do not contrast cleanly. One is an apology with no shown handback. The other is more DM-structured play, not less DM authorship. Neither shows added support that leaves the outcome in the players' hands.

What would be required is a reviewed case where player investment is the stated cause of the support, and the players still decide the result. These two do not supply that.
