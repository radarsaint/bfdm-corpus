# CF-01 dependency after BDC-S3-004 and BDC-S3-005
> **Judgment status: NOT_A_STANDING_PRACTICE.** A citation, a confidence label, or a reusable-judgment sentence is derived wording. It is not a semantic verdict and it does not authorize Kit to use the claim.


This note is only about those two reviewed cases. It does not change PR #28. It is not a Kit rule.

The proposed contrast was that Brendon handwaves a procedure once its story work is finished, and keeps the procedure when unresolved story consequences remain. BDC-S3-004 did not support that generalization. BDC-S3-005 was read on its own.

## What the two cases actually share

In both, during the same week, Brendon answered an out-of-character question about handwaving. In both he left something in play: on 24 July, stopping the bleeding if they wanted; on 26 July, allegiance and another reason to leave. Both are single scenes. Neither prep citation supports the case.

## What was falsified

They do not show a rule that mechanics are skipped when their story job is exhausted and kept when it is not.

On 24 July he was willing to handwave the rest of an injury. The recoverable scene does not show that the mechanics were exhausted, and it does not recover the player's request. On 26 July he refused to handwave who can cast modify memory, called it a good arc, and then let a limited version of the spell happen in the scene. He did not keep the procedure untouched, and he did not refuse the fictional outcome.

"Good arc" is explicit in the second scene only. It is not a motive shared with the first, and it is not a principle.

## What remains

A narrower description is available and is not a replacement hypothesis: the July 24 reply offered to skip remaining injury resolution, and the July 26 reply refused one bypass and then set a limit on what memory could erase. That is a difference between two replies. It is not enough to treat the comparison as reusable Dungeon Master judgment.

No replacement rule is proposed.

## What would be required before reuse

More than these two incidents. For the first, the player's own request, if it exists outside this harvest. For the second, play that shows the trial/allegiance limit mattering because of this ruling, rather than a later wish and a player saying the trial is remembered. A third case would have to be reviewed the same way before any contrast is taught to Kit.

Status of CF-01 from these two cases: the proposed contrast is unsupported.
