The twin cities of Bastion and Redoubt are the pinnacle of life on the smallest continent in the world. Far away from the machinations of the major empires of the world, these two cities form the only real civilization that most of her inhabitants will likely ever see. The world seems largely at peace now. It has been 15 years since the great war, fought in far away lands, brought change to the world. But not all change is obvious. 

In the war, the twin cities of Bastion and Redoubt played almost no role whatsoever. Too far away from the foreign problems, they were largely content to just quietly prosper from the unseen tragedies. Since the end of the war that prosperity has grown. Refugees from a place now known as “the craterlands” 

Adventure Summary:  
The story focuses on the city guard of Redoubt, known as the Lamplighters. Named for their ceremonial evening rounds where they bring light to the city each night, the Lamplighters are the stalwart protectors of Redoubt's bustling streets. Players will begin as rookies and ascend the ranks, eventually specializing in certain types of crimes and threats. This campaign is designed to accommodate players who may need to drop in or out as time and life permits, ensuring that there can always be a rookie on the case and returning veterans ready to lend a hand.

As the story progresses, the Lamplighters will face an array of challenges that test their skills, loyalty, and resolve. From solving petty thefts and disturbances in the vibrant Trade Quarter to unraveling complex conspiracies that threaten the city's stability, each mission will bring new opportunities for growth and heroism.

Setting:  
Redoubt is a city of contrasts, where the ancient and the modern coexist in a vibrant tapestry of cultures, ideas, and ambitions. The bustling Trade Quarter hums with the activity of merchants and artisans, while the University District buzzes with the pursuit of knowledge and innovation. Yet, beneath the surface of this thriving metropolis, darker forces lurk, seeking to exploit the city's riches and influence for their own nefarious purposes.

Gameplay:

As the Lamplighters delve deeper into their roles, they will uncover a series of mysteries and challenges that threaten the stability of Bastion and Redoubt. They will grapple with ethical dilemmas, such as upholding the king's propaganda that "There are no monsters in Bastion," even as they encounter unsettling evidence to the contrary. Their investigations will reveal the troubling truth about war criminals from the Craterlands who have seamlessly blended into the city's populace, living unnoticed among ordinary citizens. Additionally, they will encounter whispers and sightings of the Goddess Bast, who is rumored to walk among them, influencing events in ways both benevolent and mysterious. The presence of this deity will add a layer of complexity to their duties, as they must discern her true intentions and the impact of her influence on the city's fate. Each revelation will challenge the Lamplighters' sense of duty and justice, pushing them to question the true cost of security and the moral boundaries they are willing to cross to protect their communities.

Character Customizations by Race  
In this world, characters are divided into three classes by race: Short-lived, Long-lived, and Native. Each class will have unique customizations and background options that provide both advantages and disadvantages, enriching the gameplay experience.

Short-lived Races  
Races: Changeling, Dragonborn, Half-Orc, Human, Genasi, Goblin, Goliath, Firbolg, Kender, Lizardfolk, Plasmoid, Simic Hybrid, Triton, Warforged.

Customization:  
Short-lived races have access to a series of body augmentations inspired by Transhumanoidism. These augmentations provide unique abilities but also come with their own drawbacks, reflecting the complex nature of technological and magical enhancements.

Examples of Body Augmentations:

Crystal Veins: Crystalline structures replace veins, allowing for energy manipulation and resistance to certain types of damage. However, they make the character more susceptible to shattering and brittle injuries.  
Ioun Lymph Nodes: Embedded ioun stones within lymph nodes grant enhanced magical abilities and rapid disease resistance, but can cause uncontrollable magical surges and mana overload.  
Umbral Heart: A heart infused with shadow magic grants the ability to phase through solid objects and enhanced stealth, but causes vulnerability to light-based attacks and spells.  
Soulshifted Skin: The skin can shift to reflect the character's emotional state and provide camouflage, but this can also betray their true feelings and intentions, making them easier to read.  
Adamantine Circle of Willis: Reinforced blood vessels in the brain provide protection against psychic damage and enhance mental fortitude, but the inflexibility can lead to headaches and difficulty with quick thinking.  
Long-lived Races  
Races: Autognome, Dwarf, Elf, Giff, Gnome, Half-Elf, Halfling, Githyanki, Githzerai, Orc, Thri-Kreen, Verdan, Yuan-Ti.

Customization:  
Long-lived races will have access to unique backgrounds that reflect the rich history and aftermath of a great war. These backgrounds grant features and bonuses tailored to the long-lived races' extensive experiences and wisdom.

Examples of Backgrounds:

Newly Minted Aristocrat:

Feature: Noble Patronage. You have recently been elevated to nobility, granting you access to resources, influence, and a network of allies. Gain advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks when interacting with other nobles and access to restricted areas within noble districts.  
Equipment: Fine clothes, signet ring, a letter of introduction from a noble patron, and 25 gold pieces.  
Bonus: Gain proficiency in History and Persuasion. You also gain an additional language of your choice.  
Drawback: Your recent rise to power may attract jealousy and political intrigue from established noble houses. You might face hidden rivals and subtle sabotage attempts.  
Diplomat’s Child:

Feature: Diplomatic Immunity. Raised in an embassy, you are familiar with political intrigue and cultural exchange. You can invoke diplomatic immunity in foreign lands, granting you protection from local laws but attracting scrutiny and potential distrust.  
Equipment: Diplomatic passport, fine clothes, a token of friendship from a foreign dignitary, and 15 gold pieces.  
Bonus: Gain proficiency in Insight and Persuasion. You also gain an additional language of your choice.  
Drawback: Growing up in a transient environment has made forming genuine relationships difficult. You may struggle with long-term commitments and trust issues.  
War Criminal Hunter:

Feature: Relentless Tracker. You are dedicated to tracking down and bringing war criminals to justice. Gain advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track humanoids and Intelligence (Investigation) checks related to uncovering hidden identities.  
Equipment: Hunting gear, a list of known war criminals, a set of manacles, and 10 gold pieces.  
Bonus: Gain proficiency in Investigation and Survival. You also gain an additional language of your choice.  
Drawback: Your secretive nature and dangerous mission make you paranoid and distrusting of others. You may have difficulty forming alliances and rely heavily on your own skills.  
Native Races  
Races: Aarakocra, Aasimar, Bugbear, Centaur, Fairy, Grung, Harengon, Hadozee, Hobgoblin, Kenku, Kobold, Loxodon, Locathah, Minotaur, Owlin, Satyr, Shifter, Tabaxi, Tiefling, Tortle.

Customization:  
Native races will have backgrounds reflecting their local upbringing and unique narrative advantages. These backgrounds grant features and bonuses equivalent to those offered to the short-lived and long-lived races but focus on local knowledge, cultural insights, and connections within the community.

Examples of Backgrounds:

Papacy Scribe:

Feature: Divine Archivist. Trained within the secretive confines of the Papacy, you have access to religious texts and ancient lore. Gain advantage on Intelligence (Religion) checks and the ability to decipher and create coded messages.  
Equipment: A holy symbol, a set of common clothes, a book of religious texts, and 15 gold pieces.  
Bonus: Gain proficiency in Religion and History. You also gain an additional language of your choice.  
Drawback: Navigating the political machinations of the church can be treacherous. You may face internal conflicts and pressure to maintain the church's secrets.  
Sympathetic Ender:

Feature: Underground Ally. Once part of "Every New Dawn," you have seen the movement's hardships and the hope it brings to its followers. Gain advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks when interacting with the Enders and access to safe houses within the city.  
Equipment: Common clothes, a token from the Enders, a set of lockpicks, and 10 gold pieces.  
Bonus: Gain proficiency in Stealth and Persuasion. You also gain an additional language of your choice.  
Drawback: Your association with the Enders may bring distrust or suspicion from authorities. You must be cautious when revealing your past to others.  
Guild Artisan:

Feature: Guild Membership. As a member of a respected guild, you have honed your craft and built a network of professional contacts. Gain advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks related to your trade and the ability to call upon guild resources for assistance.  
Equipment: Artisan’s tools (one of your choice), a letter of introduction from your guild, a set of traveler's clothes, and 10 gold pieces.  
Bonus: Gain proficiency in one type of artisan's tools and Investigation. You also gain an additional language of your choice.  
Drawback: Adhering to the guild's strict regulations and demands can limit your freedom. You may be called upon to perform tasks or face consequences for breaking guild rules.
