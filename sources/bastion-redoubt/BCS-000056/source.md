\### Session 1: The Gathering Storm

\#### Objectives

\- [<u>Introduce the players to Bastion and Redoubt.</u>](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1BF3WaMa_Mg3-2jMr8vnBY94MhRRGJwhxENKjOHBxEaE/edit?usp=sharing)

\- Establish the players' roles as new members of the Lamplighters.

\- Conduct initial patrols and handle minor disturbances.

\- Set the stage for the first major case.

\#### Key Locations

1\. \*\*Lamplighters' Headquarters:\*\* Briefing and initial introduction.

2\. \*\*The Light House:\*\* Symbolic starting point for the Lamplighters.

3\. \*\*Royal Palace:\*\* Brief visit to underscore the city's governance.

4\. \*\*Commoner Residences:\*\* Initial patrol area.

5\. \*\*Merchant Quarter:\*\* Minor disturbances and first clues.

\#### Maps Needed

\- \*\*Lamplighters' Headquarters:\*\* Detailed map for orientation and initial interactions.

\- \*\*Commoner Residences:\*\* Basic map for patrols.

\- \*\*Merchant Quarter:\*\* Basic map for encountering disturbances.

\#### NPCs

1\. \*\*Luminate Commander:\*\* Introduces the players to their duties.

2\. \*\*Aric Altovolo:\*\* Leads the initial chalk talk and provides gear recommendations.

3\. \*\*Fennic Ingegno:\*\* Briefs players on available items.

4\. \*\*Various Citizens:\*\* Minor NPCs encountered during patrols.

\#### Gear Recommendations (Aric's Chalk Talk)

\- \*\*Basic Patrol Gear:\*\* Healing potions, lanterns, basic weapons.

\- \*\*Specific Items:\*\* Depending on the nature of disturbances (e.g., rope, grappling hooks, whistles for calling backup).

\#### Detailed Session Plan

\*\*Introduction (30 minutes)\*\*

\- \*\*Setting the Scene:\*\* Describe Bastion and Redoubt, emphasizing the blend of ancient architecture and bustling streets.

\- \*\*Lamplighters' Headquarters:\*\* Players are summoned to the headquarters for their first briefing.

\- \*\*Luminate Commander's Speech:\*\*

\- \*\*Luminate Commander:\*\* "Welcome, new recruits, to the ranks of the Lamplighters. You are now the eyes and ears of Bastion and Redoubt, the shield that guards against the chaos that seeks to take root in our fair cities. Your duty is to uphold the law, protect the innocent, and uncover the truths hidden in the shadows. Today marks the beginning of your journey. Perform your duties with honor, and remember, the light you carry dispels more than just the darkness of night."

\*\*Orientation and Briefing (30 minutes)\*\*

\- \*\*Tour of the Headquarters:\*\* Players are shown around the headquarters, including the armory, barracks, and briefing room.

\- \*\*Aric Altovolo's Chalk Talk:\*\*

\- \*\*Introduction:\*\*

\- \*\*Aric Altovolo:\*\* "Listen up, rookies. I’m Aric Altovolo, your head of patrol. Today, you’ll be assigned to your first patrols. This isn't just a walk in the park. Pay attention to your surroundings, keep your wits about you, and always be ready to act. Your primary areas are the Commoner Residences and the Merchant Quarter. Keep an eye out for anything unusual."

\- \*\*Gear Distribution:\*\*

\- \*\*Aric Altovolo:\*\* "Fennic will be here shortly to distribute your gear. Basic patrol kits include healing potions, lanterns, and your standard-issue weapons. You’ll also get some tools for more specific needs—rope, grappling hooks, and whistles. Use them wisely."

\- \*\*Patrol Assignments:\*\*

\- \*\*Aric Altovolo:\*\* "Your patrols are crucial. In the Commoner Residences, we’ve had reports of petty theft and disturbances. The Merchant Quarter, as always, is bustling and ripe for trouble. Be vigilant, and report anything suspicious."

\*\*First Patrol (1 hour)\*\*

\- \*\*Commoner Residences:\*\*

\- \*\*Minor Disturbances:\*\* Players encounter minor issues such as petty theft, disputes, and suspicious activity.

\- \*\*Interaction with Citizens:\*\* Opportunities for role-playing and gathering information about the neighborhood.

\- \*\*Citizen (disgruntled):\*\* "Thefts are becoming common. You Lamplighters better do something before we start losing more than just our coin purses!"

\- \*\*Citizen (helpful):\*\* "Thank the gods you're here. I saw someone lurking around the corners last night. Seemed out of place, very suspicious."

\- \*\*Merchant Quarter:\*\*

\- \*\*Market Bustle:\*\* Describe the busy market atmosphere, vendors, and shoppers.

\- \*\*Initial Clue:\*\* Players notice a suspicious figure lurking around a merchant's stall. Investigation reveals a bloodstained letter hidden under the stall (foreshadowing the first major case).

\- \*\*Suspicious Figure:\*\* "I was just passing through! I swear I don’t know anything about that letter!"

\*\*Encounter and Resolution (45 minutes)\*\*

\- \*\*Confrontation:\*\* Players confront the suspicious figure.

\- \*\*Skill Checks:\*\* Persuasion or Intimidation to get information from the suspect.

\- \*\*Discovery:\*\* The suspect flees, dropping the bloodstained letter.

\- \*\*Return to Headquarters:\*\* Players return to the headquarters to report their findings.

\*\*Debriefing and Next Steps (15 minutes)\*\*

\- \*\*Debriefing:\*\* Players report to the Luminate Commander, presenting the bloodstained letter.

\- \*\*Luminate Commander:\*\* "A bloodstained letter in the market, you say? Troubling. This could be the start of something more sinister. We'll need to investigate further. Good work today. Rest up and be ready for more tomorrow."

\- \*\*Discussion:\*\* The Luminate Commander and Aric discuss the implications of the letter, hinting at a larger conspiracy.

\- \*\*Preparation for Next Session:\*\* The Commander tasks the players with investigating the letter further, setting the stage for the first major case in the next session.

\#### Props and Clues

1\. \*\*Bloodstained Letter:\*\* A letter with cryptic hints about an impending danger. Players can investigate the blood and the contents of the letter.

2\. \*\*Suspicious Figure:\*\* An NPC with potential ties to the upcoming murder case.

\#### XP and Rewards

\- \*\*XP:\*\* Award XP for completing the patrol, handling disturbances, and discovering the clue.

\- \*\*Rewards:\*\* Basic monetary reward for patrol duty, potential minor item from Fennic for discovering the letter.

\#### NPCs

1\. \*\*Luminate Commander:\*\* Provides initial briefing and debriefing.

4\. \*\*Merchant’s Family:\*\* Provides background information and potential leads.

5\. \*\*Suspects and Witnesses:\*\* Various individuals connected to the case.

\#### Gear Recommendations (Aric's Chalk Talk)

\- \*\*Case-Specific Gear:\*\* Medallion of Speak with Dead, Lantern of Revealing, Bag of Holding.

\- \*\*Investigation Tools:\*\* Magnifying glass, evidence bags, forensic kits.

\#### Detailed Session Plan

\*\*Briefing (15 minutes)\*\*

\- \*\*Luminate Commander's Briefing:\*\*

\- \*\*Luminate Commander:\*\* "Our merchant, Lord Farrow, was found dead in his study late last night. This is no ordinary murder; there's more at play here. Your task is to investigate the scene, gather evidence, and find the truth behind this heinous act. Keep your eyes sharp and your wits sharper."

\*\*Aric’s Chalk Talk (15 minutes)\*\*

\- \*\*Introduction and Gear Distribution:\*\*

\- \*\*Aric Altovolo:\*\* "Alright, listen up. This is no routine patrol; this is a murder investigation. Fennic has prepared some specialized gear for you. Take these Medallions of Speak with Dead—you might need to ask Lord Farrow a few questions. Lanterns of Revealing will help you see anything hidden at the scene. And take a Bag of Holding for collecting evidence."

\- \*\*Gear Explanation:\*\* Aric explains how to use each item and when it might be most effective.

\*\*Investigation at Merchant’s Home (45 minutes)\*\*

\- \*\*Crime Scene Examination:\*\*

\- \*\*Physical Clue:\*\* Torn Fabric

\- \*\*Description:\*\* A piece of fine, expensive fabric found clenched in the victim's hand.

\- \*\*Discovery:\*\* Investigation check to identify the fabric and match it to a suspect's clothing.

\- \*\*Skill/Spell:\*\* Perception to notice the fabric, Investigation to match it to a suspect.

\- \*\*Dialogue Example:\*\* "This fabric... it’s from a very expensive garment. We need to find out who in the area could afford such luxury."

\- \*\*Magical Clue:\*\* Amulet of Speak with Dead

\- \*\*Description:\*\* Amulet found on the victim, allowing players to ask the victim questions.

\- \*\*Discovery:\*\* Arcana check to identify the amulet’s properties.

\- \*\*Skill/Spell:\*\* Speak with Dead to gather information about the attacker.

\- \*\*Dialogue Example (using Speak with Dead):\*\* "Lord Farrow, who did this to you? What did they want?"

\- \*\*Social Clue:\*\* Merchant’s Family Testimony

\- \*\*Description:\*\* Family members provide background information and possible leads.

\- \*\*Discovery:\*\* Persuasion or Insight check to get detailed and useful information.

\- \*\*Dialogue Example:\*\* "Please, tell us everything you remember about that night. Did Lord Farrow have any enemies? Anyone who might want him dead?"

\*\*Following Leads (30 minutes)\*\*

\- \*\*Nearby Streets:\*\*

\- \*\*Investigation and Interrogation:\*\* Players follow the leads and inquire with local residents about suspicious activity.

\- \*\*Dialogue Example:\*\* "We’re looking for anyone who might have seen something unusual last night. Did you notice anyone out of place or behaving oddly?"

\*\*Confrontation at Suspect’s Hideout (45 minutes)\*\*

\- \*\*Discovery:\*\* Players track the suspect to a hideout.

\- \*\*Detailed Map:\*\* Create a map of the hideout with various rooms and hiding spots.

\- \*\*Confrontation:\*\* Combat or negotiation depending on player choices.

\- \*\*Dialogue Example:\*\* "This is the end of the line. We know you were at Lord Farrow's last night. Surrender now and explain yourself, or face the consequences."

\*\*Debriefing and Next Steps (15 minutes)\*\*

\- \*\*Return to Headquarters:\*\* Players return to report their findings.

\- \*\*Luminate Commander:\*\* "Good work, team. You've uncovered important leads, but this is just the beginning. Keep digging and we’ll get to the bottom of this."

\- \*\*Discussion:\*\* The team discusses their next steps, including any new clues found at the hideout.

\- \*\*Preparation for Next Session:\*\* Set the stage for further investigation and connections to the larger conspiracy.

\#### Props and Clues

1\. \*\*Torn Fabric:\*\* A piece of expensive fabric that can be matched to a suspect.

2\. \*\*Amulet of Speak with Dead:\*\* Allows communication with the victim.

3\. \*\*Witness Testimony:\*\* Information from the merchant’s family and local residents.

\#### XP and Rewards

\- \*\*XP:\*\* Award XP for gathering evidence, interrogating suspects, and confronting the suspect.

\- \*\*Rewards:\*\* Additional items from Fennic for successfully using the investigation tools.
