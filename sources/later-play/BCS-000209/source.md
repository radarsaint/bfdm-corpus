WORLD  
![][image1]

The setting of this world is on the moon (satellite) of a much larger planet (the moon-dwellers call it Zyphoxia), one mostly shrouded in fog and storms which is home to the Shattered Isles among other continents within the magical storms that ravage the land.  The bluish swirling mass can be seen from the sky of the moon during both the day and the night.

The small moon is called Duna, and it's mostly desert, the sun beating down onto the satellite causing nights to be chilly and days to be brutally hot.  Water isn't scarce on the moon, but the borders between the tropical regions and the desert tend to be extremely dense with rainforest that hug the coastline, being home to immensely large insectoid beasts that lumber through the canopies seeking food.  Floating islands are also present, floating by pure magic and magnetism, and are only recently visited due to the inventions of the Skyrails.

Civilization is far and few in-between, making what little cities there are booming and magically adept metropolises, using highly advanced magitech to combat the heat and harsh climate.  Almost all of the cities are by coastlines, as travel through the waterways is much safer than traveling through the unforgiving climates of the desert.  However, not all settlements are cities.  Small despot villages and settlements dot the world across the land, finding refuge in the unforgiving deserts and badlands, as tribes settle within the vicious jungles to live side by side with nature.  The villages are often made by formerly nomadic troupes that have banded together to survive, often having to deal with the rather lawless and desperate lands, being no stranger to the beasts that live within the sand and stone as well as the opportunistic bandits that make themselves home just about everywhere.

Duna is broken up into a few factions, all holding shaky alliances with two of the factions currently neck-in-neck in full blown war over land disputes.  These are the main nations.

**Aisha** is known as the nation of gold, with it being tucked within the sparkling white cliff sides of the Orange Coast.  It is indisputably the nation with the most old money, having an extremely lucrative mining industry that supplies the rest of Duna.  It is run by a questionable democracy that plays more like an oligarchy by the books, mostly being controlled by the oldest and wealthiest families who make all of the decisions behind the scenes.  Aisha has been in dispute for the past century (a little less) or so, over the labor disputes of the poor working classes and the children who are required to work in the mines as well.  Many rebellion factions have sprouted up in the past years to fight against this injustice, but while they've made a few waves, the oligarchy remains stalwart in their business decisions.  The middle and affluent classes live quite luxuriously, and many never even see the poor in their day to day lives, leading some to live their entire lives not even knowing the poor conditions of their fellow Aishans.  Nations keep a steady alliance with Aisha as it supplies the majority of their metal and minerals.  Aisha, while it dons powerful military (mostly marine), doesn't wish to wage war, even if it means to help other nations in pursuit of keeping their own image clean.

**Telum** is known as the Beating Heart, and is the technology nation of the world.  Surprisingly, it's the only nation that has its capital not hugging the coastline, instead residing by a few large river mouths that lead into a massive lake.  It's located within the Angel's Garden, a huge rainforest of towering, flowering trees-- most of which weathered down to their barebone stems by industrialization and the buildings built into their sides.  Telum is best known for its recent invention of the Skyrails, magitech trains that fly on rails forged of powerful magic to avoid the entirety of the dangerous satellite of Duna, reaching their destinations much faster than by sea or land, and being able to travel to the many magical floating islands that float ever so elegantly in the sky.  Telum is run by a true democracy, and the president, a rabbit folk by the name of Garreth Foxe, pursues the pursuit of knowledge over all else.  Telum also has a very large magical college, which draws the attention of many across all of Duna.  However, technology requires money, and with Telum lacking natural resources it's of utmost importance to utilize the Skyrails to offer transport to the rest of Duna.  Some rumors have even come up that Garreth has been doing some shady dealings in secret, selling his technologies to other nations to assist in the warfront.  

**Dunmerum** is known as the nation of Eternity, located in the northernmost part of the moon besides the Hoarfrost Cairns.  It's located on the icy mountains beside the frozen sea, and is a monarchy led by an eternally sleeping white dragon, frozen within a prison of ice so that she can live forever.  Remmellica is her name, and she speaks to those she chooses within the recesses of her mind to guide her nation.  Dunmerum is harsh and stalwart with a powerful military and an industry made on mining the oil from the millions of fossils lost within the frozen stone.  Dunmerum besides the unclaimed desert wilderness is the only source of oil, and they hold a very strong monopoly on its price.  Dunmerum, due to its lack of land resources and difficulty producing their own food, is currently in a very long war with the nation of Moonbridge, which they have long since won but are working to enslave or genocide the entire country by Remmellica's order.  She has denounced Moonbridge as being degenerate due to many of their leaders and people being infected with demonic influence, but it's since been watered down by millennia. 

**Moonbridge** is the nation of Shimmering Stars, and is also referred to as the nation of magic.  It's located on a beautiful tropical coastline, but much of the nation has been torn asunder by Dunmerum's war effort.  A long time ago, Moonbridge was led by demonic tyrants, but those demons have long since been slain by Remmellica.  However, due to the demons, much of the nation has sorcery rooted in demonic nature, or are tieflings.  It's said that Moonbridge was the first nation made, and much of that is present in the wonderful ruins both within and above the sea level.  However, much of this splendid architecture and history has been eliminated by Dunmerum armies.  Moonbridge is also a monarchy, however their king in recent developments was assassinated, presumably by a Dunmerum assailant.  With the nation in shambles, they have been reaching out to other nations in desperation to assist them in the warfront.

Many of the nations including the seedlings to new nations have decided to go to the floating isles, excited to claim this new great frontier for their own.  Much of the desert is still unexplored, as it's very harsh.

[Here is the information about the deities\!](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19kANNe4Kwc8cTWfO3UpifNkN8sLCvVBIr-yyOtb76ZE/edit?usp=drivesdk)

- Magick has been fading for quite some time now, with much of the desert growing due to the moon being drained of its natural properties.  Why this is, is unknown to the greater public, but many speculate that it's due to the death of many of the most powerful magickal beings such as the dragons, as well as the infrequent invasion of demonkind.  
    
- Under the surface of the moon is a series of very dangerous caverns and ecosystems regarded as the Crystalline Depths.  Much like the insides of a geode, the entire topography of this region is crystalline.  Many don't dare venture within this part of Duna due to horrific beasts that make their homes there, as well as dangerous sentient species such as the Drow (spider-worshiping elves) and the Bombyxians (cannibalistic moth men.) Most dangerous of them all are the Lifebringers-- which are said to be the last remaining dragons besides Remellica herself, wreathed in auras of demise and shimmering crystal.  
    
- Remellica single-handedly wiped out all of the dragons due to the demons beginning to influence her kindred.  Many of these demons and dragons which the Greatwyrm has promised to purge from Duna are still alive, indestructible and unkillable but trapped within her magicked eternal ice.  The only dragons she has not been able to eliminate have been the Lifebringers.  
    
- Moonbridge is called as such because it's said to be where the hero Blackrose arrived from Zyphoxia to rejuvenate the dying moon with his magicks about eight-hundred years ago.  There, he traveled to found the magickal college of Wetherstarr located above Telum, but technically unassociated with the city.  Wetherstarr is where people are trained for magickal skills, and Archmagi are honed to protect Duna and preserve its magicks.  Although Blackrose died a long time ago, his descendants-- the Shimura family control Wetherstarr to this day.  Archmages are a special force of magickal soldiers unassociated with any world alliance.  They exist for the sole purpose of eliminating dangerous threats such as terrorists, criminals, magickal beasts, and natural disasters.    
    
- The most notorious criminal being hunted by the Archmagi is a man by the name of The Wandering Flame.  He often attacks Skyrails, trading groups, adventurers, and even threatens towns and holds.  He's only known as a violent, winged bandit with a crown of thorn-like antlers and flames sprouting from his mouth.  He has a massive bounty of one thousand platinum pieces, and is seen as a threat to the balance of Duna.  Nothing is known about his past or his origins, but he does not attack innocents or civilians.  
    
- Moonbridge is home to the Opalescence Stage, the only Bard’s College within Duna.  Due to the war between Dunmerum and Moonbridge, it has been shut down, currently being used as a shelter for refugees displaced by the ongoing war.  
    
- Said to be deep underground or in remote places, there are massive black gates of promethium (Godsteel) scattered across Duna, protecting magickal phenomenons called Arcane Wells-- huge pockets of raw magickal energy that can be harnessed as portals between worlds, planes and dimensions.  Long ago it was said Blackrose created these gates to keep horrors from crawling out of the wells.  However, after centuries, it's said that a few of these gates have been breached, being the cause of demons invading the moon.

CHARACTER CREATION

The party will be esteemed members of a Telum company called the Stellaris Corp, specialized delivery men and women who conduct one of the Skyrails.  Their mission is to take a careful path through the sky islands and Duna to travel across the world to Moonbridge for a secret delivery.  It's a dangerous task, with many monsters and men alike wishing to disrupt the delivery, but the brave Stellaris Corp won't let anything get in their way\!

Howl Argentes

Age: 46  
Gender: Male (He/him)  
Species: Half Owlin, Half-Halfling  
Height: 5’4” (Medium)  
Class: Artificer (Artillerist)  
Alignment: Lawful Neutral  
Role: Engineer/ Head Mechanic

The longest standing crewmate aboard the Starlight Rail is a middle-aged half-owlin, half-halfling man by the name of Howl Argentes.  Despite being in his mid-forty years of age, due to his faer roots, he barely looks a day over his mid twenties.  Howl is the ship’s engineer, and one of the original inventors who created the Skyrail systems.  He works directly under president Garreth, and is quite proud of both his prowess and knowledge.  But despite being erudite, Howl is extremely poor with social interaction, often being crippled by his social anxiety and lack of communication skills.  He's a nervous man, preferring the company of himself and his work-- enjoying toiling away with machines and mechanisms than entertaining a lengthy conversation that strays away from the topic of his work.  Howl is frustrated quickly by people who he deems dumber than him, and is quick to shut down stupidity.  

Howl begins the story as a level 6 Artillerist Artificer.  His alignment is Lawful Neutral, and he specializes in healing and long-ranged combat with firearms and weaponized turrets and gattlings.  Howl prefers to avoid combat and leaving the Skyrail, but will assist the party if his presence is needed.

Howl is a short, somewhat stout and pudgy man with short, vestigial wings he also uses as arms and hands.  His skin is brown, and his feathers are ashen and brown.  His messy hair is grey, often worn with a pair of gold-tinged protective goggles which often cause reddish markings around his hazel green eyes since he wears them so often.  He wears worn navy blue work overalls and a rough-hewn olive green turtleneck underneath.  His legs are very feathery and his feet are taloned.  Howl unfortunately cannot fly due to his clumsy anatomy.  His face is rather round, soft and boyish, matching his big round eyes and button nose.

Haru Shimura

Age: 22  
Gender: Male (He/him)  
Species: Kenku  
Height: 6’2” (Medium)  
Class: Sorcerer (Divine Soul)  
Alignment: Chaotic Neutral  
Role: Treasurer

Only just joining the Stellaris Corp and the Starlight Rail in the past three months, Haru is a spoiled young man hailing from a powerful family of mages and pedigree within Telum.  He's bitter and sarcastic, often with his pretty head held high to hide just how truly empty it is.  Haru only got the job through connections, not by credentials as a final chance given to him by his family to finally grow up-- after he blundered his entire time at the magickal Wetherstarr college of Telum by using his tuition to party.  Haru is good with his words, but carries a strong mean-spirited streak.  He has a short temper, and lacks the intelligence to truly back up a lot of his tantrums.  However, when he's sat down and given a task, he always makes sure he does his damnedest, and surprisingly always does a good job.  He dislikes his post on the Starlight Rail, and promises that some day, he'll be the one paying everyone else's bills.  

Haru begins the story as a level 6 Divine Soul Sorcerer.  His alignment skirts at Chaotic Neutral, but is volatile depending on the decisions that his allies make.  Haru specializes in dealing large amounts of damage to groups of enemies, and buffing his allies and assisting in much needed positioning.  He is eager to prove himself, even if that zealous nature drags those around him into trouble and danger.

Haru is a tall, elegant and beautiful man with a menagerie of black, blue and white feathers like that of a decorated magpie.  He has long black hair, fair skin, and violet eyes.  He often dresses in elaborate and exotic robes and kimono, sticking out like a sore thumb as he parades his noble heritage around like a bejeweled rooster.  Both his hands and his feet are taloned like a bird's, which he's happy to show off to prove just how pure his blood is in relation to his great grandfather-- Blackrose; a man of great fame and power supposedly *from* Zyphoxia who shared his cast gift of magic with the moon.  He rarely walks around without his shoulders back, and a beautiful walking cane in his left hand which he uses proudly as his spellcasting focus.

Kamir Uly

Age: 44  
Gender: Male (He/him)  
Species: Yuan-ti  
Height: 6’0”  
Class: Bard (Lore)  
Alignment: Lawful Good  
Role: Chef

Being with the Starlight Rail for two years now, Kamir is the talented chef of the Skyrail.  Having gotten the position from President Garreth himself, Uly’s dreams of owning his very own restaurant went up in smoke when he instead got stuck on a train essentially being forced to serve cafeteria food.  Thankfully, the hard-working man is paid a gracious sum by his boss, which he sends back to his doting wife and daughter back in Telum.  Kamir is a no nonsense man on the outside, seeming to only care about punching in and out of his shifts and getting his work done.  But those that know him know that he's deeply emotional, caring, knowledgeable and sentimental.  He loves cooking more than anything else, and his passion can often be contagious.  Before Kamir was a chef, he worked as a musician in Moonbridge, which is actually where he met his wife and married.  He doesn't really like talking about his musical career, however.  Kamir also hates anyone prying into his past, and will usually rebuff any attempts very sternly.

Kamir begins the story as a level 6 Lore College Bard.  He is very stalwartly Lawful Good, and will angrily disagree with any outwardly evil actions committed around him.  Kamir specializes in debuffing and disrupting foes in combat, using tricky illusions and curses to trick and disrupt them.  Kamir will come with the party if needed, especially if there's injustice happening right under his mustache.

Kamir is a tall man, built muscularly with deep brown skin, slitted yellow eyes, and black hair with silver specks, often greased back and kept neat under a nice brown cowboy hat.  He keeps his facial hair trimmed, and is especially proud of his mustache.  Kamir’s face is beginning to knot with age, a telltale sign of his hard life and experience.  Down his neck, sand-coloured scales speckle and sparkle under the collar of his simple flannel shirt, worn under a leather utility apron which often houses many of his tools of trade.  His trousers are thick and brown, and he wears tough leather boots.  Kamir has a long snake-like tail with raised scales like a rattlesnake, with a beaten-down rattle at the very tip.
