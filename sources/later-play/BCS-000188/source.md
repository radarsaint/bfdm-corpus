Backstory:

Elijah was part of a particular Mainlander’s militia that served under the God of Sun Raidance's name. This sector was known as ¨The Executors¨. Their purpose was to intercept and gather intel by any means possible about any advancement of the Divanol army.

During one of the battles a man was captured deemed to have intel that would sway the momentum of the fight. This man had knowledge of the new plans of attack of the Divanol's Crimson army.

This man was a tall half orc and strong half orc with fire burning in his eyes. Elijah who had been in various battles but never in the front lines himself was taken back, he knew this man had no fear of death by simply glancing at his eyes. He knew the fate that awaited this man. Executors were trained in the most horrific torture methods known, but the worst of it was the promise of freedom to captives that would never come.

Elijah had taken part in many , seen how the men cracked under immense stress. He would beg his superior  to send him back to battle as his fighting skills would be of more use. They declined.

Elijah was assigned to oversee the half-orc prisoner. After 3 days of brutal torture they managed to extract nothing from him. Elijah was frustrated…every time anyone would walk in the room the half-orc would greet them with a ¨Good Morning¨, ¨How's your day¨. Taking many of the guards by surprise. The man even asked for Elijah´s name. Hesitant Elijah gave it to him. The half-orc grinned and spoke

¨Ray. My name is Ray¨. Elijah responded sarcastically ¨Very fitting¨. Both men laughed momentarily. After 3 more days of torture conducted by other guards (under supervision of Elijah)  the two men learned more about each other´s through stares. Out of respect Elijah did not look away from the violence being inflicted on Ray.  Elijah did all he could to lessen the torture Ray would recieve without being caught by his superior, this gave them more time to exchange conversation. But the most marking conversation happened on day 7.

Elijah: Ray, be honest with me. How many men have you killed? Serving on the front lines.

Ray: You first.

Elijah: I´d say like 60?

Ray: Zero

Elijah: Don't bullshit me now…

Ray: In battle who is the man that doesn't kill but is still useful.

Elijah is cold when the realization hits.

Elijah: You were a combat medic…

Ray slightly nods.

With a shaky voice Elijah says: How many have you saved..

Ray: 200

Elijah storms out of the room. All of his executions flashing through his head like an erratic montage. In his superior´s office he points his gun at his superior..

Elijah: You made me torture a medic?

Mainlander Superior : You realized he is no ordinary man. For someone to save more than 200 men in battle is unheard of.

Elijah: He never had any intel did he.

Mainlander Superior: Correct.

Elijah: If you simply wanted me to take him out why. Why torture him. Why?

Mainlander Superior is silent.

Elijah: Answer me Alana.

Alana: Who are you fighting for again? Or perhaps are you one of the Divanol’s spy?

Elijah: Fuck you.

Mainlander Superior: Turn your back on me and all your allies are gone.

Moments before security is called on him he runs towards Ray´s cell and gives him the offer of liberation, death. Ray smiles and hands him the only item he was able to keep. A gold necklace and inside a picture of a small half-orc girl. Elijah recognized this was the daughter Ray often talked about seeing again.

In between tears Elijah points the shotgun at Ray for his final moments.

Ray: Thank you dear Friend.
