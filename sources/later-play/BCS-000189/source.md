Asari (Mass Effect)

Asari are long-lived, blue-skinned humanoids with tentacle-like head crests who carry a profound biotic connection to the galaxy's life force. They channel that energy to create barriers, meld minds, and unleash controlled bursts of force.

Asari appear among any galactic population but retain a distinctive appearance: blue or purple skin, tentacle-like scalp tendrils, and a graceful, athletic build. They live for centuries, with features that hint at their biotic heritage—faint glowing markings, luminous eyes, or a subtle biotic aura—that become more pronounced when they fully embrace their powers.

Time Bandits

Time Bandits are mischievous, treasure-obsessed adventurers who tumble through time via map portals, stealing riches from history while constantly arguing and scheming among themselves. They survive absurd dangers—from Titanic disasters to Napoleon’s palaces to the lair of Evil—through sheer stubbornness, dumb luck, and quick wits.

Time Bandits appear as a ragtag crew of short, broad-shouldered travelers in mismatched historical clothing (pirate coats, medieval tunics, odd modern scraps), with thick beards or wild hair, scarred from failed heists, and eyes that sparkle with greed and mischief.

Themyscirian (Wonderwomen)

The Themyscirians, also known as the Amazons of Themyscira, are a nation of exceptional female warriors dwelling in eternal seclusion on the mist-shrouded island of Themyscira. Created by the goddesses Aphrodite, Artemis, Athena, and Hestia from the souls of women slain by men in ancient wars, they were granted immortality, superhuman prowess, and divine gifts to live in harmony, training ceaselessly in the arts of war, philosophy, and justice.

Themyscira's people reflect humanity's breadth, with skin tones ranging from deep bronze to obsidian, often marked by a divine sheen. Many bear flowing white or silver hair as a sign of goddess-touched favor, and their eyes gleam crimson red. Clad in ornate Greek-inspired armor, bracers, and tiaras, they move with graceful lethality, their forms athletic and statuesque.

## Gary Clones

Gary Clones are identical survivors repeating the same name, the same voice, and the same presence, fractured across multiple bodies. They echo what they hear, respond with familiar tones, and mirror behavior with eerie precision, as if each one is trying to remember how to be a person by copying the last thing that worked.

They appear as near-identical humans with subtle differences from wear, injury, or time—same face, same build, same expression that never quite settles. Their speech is made of repeated sounds, borrowed tones, and fragmented phrases, delivered with unsettling timing and misplaced emotion. In a group, their voices overlap and repeat, creating the sense of a single identity struggling to exist in multiple places at once.

## Wookiees

Wookiees are towering, fur-covered beings from a forested world of immense trees and vertical cities, known for their strength, loyalty, and deep sense of honor. They form lifelong bonds, stand fiercely by their allies, and carry a quiet dignity beneath their imposing presence.

They appear as tall, broad-shouldered humanoids covered in thick fur ranging from dark brown to black or lighter tawny shades. Their faces are expressive beneath heavy brows, with deep-set eyes and powerful jaws, and their voices emerge as layered growls and roars understood more through tone than language. They move with surprising grace for their size, equally at home climbing through towering canopies or standing their ground in a fight.

## Mutants (TMNT)

Mutants are former animals transformed by unstable mutagen into upright, thinking beings with exaggerated physical traits and unpredictable behavioral shifts. Some retain discipline and identity, others become crude reflections of instinct, shaped by environment, influence, and whatever they were exposed to during transformation.

They appear as humanoid hybrids of their original animal forms—turtles with armored shells and powerful limbs, rats with sharp features and twitch reflexes, warthogs and rhinos with massive builds and blunt force presence. Their bodies are exaggerated, functional, and often asymmetrical from mutation, with gear, clothing, or scavenged equipment layered over forms never meant to be human.
