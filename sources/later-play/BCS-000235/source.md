# **Jigsaw:**

**After the war, Jigsaw is stuck with a big question... What now? No battles to win, no dirty coats to smash, no queen to yell at. Everyone else seems to have plans, but for Jigsaw, all that's left after the war is time. So much time. At first they fill it with wild inventions. Malathor’s workshop is quickly filled with over engineered contraptions and machines of whimsy. But Jigsaw finds that without a cause, inventing just doesn’t hold the same merit as it used to. They then decide to focus their energy on the project Roy proposed to them. Jigsaw enjoys gutting the airships and repurposing them with all of the tools now at their disposal. It is good work, and for a good cause. But after a few weeks, the Hudson starts to clear and Jigsaw is thanked profusely for their service to the city and it’s denizens. Just like that, another project is already over.** 

**To Jigsaw, it seems that there are only two things that bring them joy. Rax and Mike. At first after Jigsaw realizes this, Mike becomes very spoiled, and Rax is confused as to where all these toys came from. But eventually, after a talk with Rax, Jigsaw slows down a bit. They do their best to take things slow, but are still filled with unused chaotic energy.** 

**Until one day, Granny suggests that they put more energy into raising Mike. Until now, Jigsaw acted as more of a babysitter than a parent. Perhaps a guardian in charge of keeping him safe. But in Granny’s old age, she asks Jigsaw if they would be willing to put their full effort into raising him. Taken aback, Jigsaw cautiously accepts, with a request that Granny take the time to help them learn how to raise a child. With Rax’s help, Micheal begins to grow into a chaotic and fearless young boy, who even at a young age has a passion for snakes and his tool set.**

**Rax holds Jigsaw in his arms, sobbing, willing them to come back.**
