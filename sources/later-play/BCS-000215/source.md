  
Our Humble Greetings.  
Know that before us, for the health of Arcania it’s living veins, that which you know as  ley (which is as lifeblood unto us, to our ancestors and to our descendants) has been given freely unto you.

We, the Four Wardens. The first of our kind. Keepers of the barrier. Enemy of the dead of the deep welcome you once again to our land.

Firstly though the governance of this land has fallen to us for thousands of years, it is clear that you mean to now govern yourselves. Therefore we proclaim that your rights as you see fit to be confirmed and the rights of your heirs in perpetuity shall be of your own making. That we wish that all of Arcania heed this proclamation that this right shall remain undiminished and it’s liberties unimpaired.

Your borders therefore shall be any place you make for yourselves as an occupied residence (occupied by a citizen of your nation). The wild of Arcania shall remain under our governance. We retain the right to revoke passage through our lands. 

Should the wild folk find common cause for trade these shall be made open and free and there shall be no taxation, tariff or embargo upon the wilds. 

Should the wild folk, the dryad and naiads, the thinking beasts of the land find grievance   
Against the Arcanian Empire, they may be granted a binding should a Warden or an Arbiter find just cause. A binding shall prevent a person or persons from leaving the city to which they are bound.

Should an Arcanian find grievance against the creatures of the land, air or water their spirits may be given over to you to be at the mercy of your justice, if the claims be verified by a Warden or an Arbiter. 

The Wardens, so long as they are excluded from representation amongst the governing bodies, shall not be bound to an expectation of aid in warfare except in the form of relief. Any other request may be given, but each warden shall have the right to refuse answer.

Let it also be so with the Arcanian Empire in return.

The role of arbiter shall be established to uphold the edict of the Warden and shall be granted passage and the rite of justice upon the people and things under the protection of the Wardens.

Similarly, the Arcanian Empire is welcome to issue Sheriffs to have governance upon their people in lands that are not theirs within the New World. 

Land which is not inhabited by your citizens shall not belong to your empire. Arrangements may be granted between our nations for the use of common natural resources. 

Marriage between the citizens of our nations occurs, it shall be at once and without trouble. There shall be no governance dictating who and when any (of age) union shall take place. The married couple may choose either nation to belong to, but not both, nor may they remain each individual a citizen of a separate country than their partner(s).

No partner of a deceased person shall be compelled to remarry, so long as they wish to remain without a partner. If they are no longer a citizen of their country of origin, they may renounce their current citizenship in favor of their citizen of origin. It shall be so immediately and without trouble. 

Land may not be traded as a payment of debt. Nor shall it be requested.

The region called Cascadia shall enjoy all its ancient liberties and free customs, both by land and by water. We also will and grant that all other cities, boroughs, towns, and ports under our protection shall enjoy all their liberties and free customs.

There shall be no court to settle matters of a litigious nature between nations. An arbiter and a sheriff shall confer with the leader of the Arcanian Empire and a Sage of the Wilds. Together they shall find the truth of the matter, and deal with it honestly, without malice, or greed. Should any grievance upon this council be found to be true, one of the four shall be executed. It shall be determined by casting lots.

For a trivial offence, a sentient being shall be fined only in proportion to the degree of his offence, and for a serious offence correspondingly, but not so heavily as to deprive him of his livelihood. Likewise any who depend upon an asset to maintain their livelihood shall not have it be taken. These fines shall be imposed on the assessment on oath of either an Arbiter or a Sheriff. A standard of fees that may be changed by agreement of both countries shall be used, and there shall be no barter in this dealing, only coin.

Should a death of a citizen of either nation occur in a territory not their own, they shall be returned promptly and without cost to the nation’s border.  

This agreement has been marked by the wardens in their own ways as an oath should it be ratified by the country we refer to as The Arcanian Empire.
