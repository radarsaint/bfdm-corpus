Hiram champions forethought and planning, and the magic of his Freemasons equate to having empathy for the struggles of those around you. The Golden Dawn represents a reactionary world. Coming to Arcania
Coming to Arcania
The setting is in the first half of the 17th century. Nearly 100 years since Christopher Columbus stole the secrets to accessing the New World from a group of Masonic Vikings known as the Roseline Temple. Along with the other members of Roseline Temple, the First Arcanian Cryptid to set foot on the shores of The Old World was a Sasquatch named Bigfoot. He, along with the Roseline Temple leaders Hiram Abiff, the half Orc Pym “of the Rosecutter”, and many more of their ranks worked with the Wardens of the New World (of the Continent Arcania) Columbia, Cascadia, Fontaine And Oro to build a barrier between the two locations to prevent the Gods of the Old World from dominating the New World.
Despite the fact that roughly one third of the Gods are aligned to the ideas of good and benevolence, two thirds are either indifferent, or have dominating designs for the lesser beings beneath them. The dangers outweighed the possible benefits of opening their shores to the Cleric, Paladins and Warlocks of the Old World.
Colonialism in the Worlds bothThe New world (Everything in the Western Hemisphere of real world geography) is the home of Arcane magic. While Arcane magic is used around the world, it is pristine and unfettered by the will of the Gods of the Old World. Leylines carry Arcane magic from its source in Arcania to all other locations in the world. And this causes a subtle pull on all spell casters to journey to its source. The search for the point of origin for the nexus of all arcane magical power is called the search for the NorthWest Passage, and is the obsession of many Wizards, Sorcerers and other types of Arcane Casters.

	O ld and New.	
Your reasons for traveling to the New World are yours and yours alone. Understanding your “coming to America” story starts with an understanding of the people who actually came here. It represents change. Change that can be either carefully thought out with good intentions and considerations for those around you, or change that can be brutal, violent and scar your heart. In this fantasy remimaging of early American History, you are what you bring with you from the Old World. Or in the case of a Native Race, you are left with the choice of how to deal with those who have come. This is not intended to follow history. It is intended to give you the chance to imagine being at the beginning of a new civilization. The conflicts you have in the game, and the resolutions you make are likely to inform the shape of the games to come.

The Order of Kairo:
After the mission from Spain failed to produce a long term foot hold in Arcania, the Spanish crown simply denied that it ever happened. The loved ones of those who disappeared in the mysterious disappearance banded together to seek answers on their own. Kairo, a member of the British Museum has found a way to search for their families while still getting the government to pay for it.
The Origins of the Order of Kairo are shrouded in mystery. It is often believed to be the remnants of an intrepid group of adventurers who were among the first to try to colonize the island of Roanoke. Within the “Legend of Kairo” details are few and often seem incomplete at best, however it is regarded as the apocryphal account of the colonists that witnessed the death and supposed rebirth of one, Kairo the Kenku.

The end of Season 2 left the fate of the Tatankan people in grave peril. This passive culture was turned to stone with but a single Tatankan left as guardian of the pen.

The Halfwudgie
If the Uru were our Skeksis, then the halfwudgies would be our podlings. Our final race, and the only new race to this year’s campaign, the halfwudgies are medium sized humanoids covered in thick brown fur and long quills that can be used as natural weapons.
