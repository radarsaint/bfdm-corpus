     **Arknamundis**, a world beset by fiends and monstrosities, where even the dead stand against the living. Yet the mortal races thrive, sailing the seas in their endless pursuit of wealth, power, and knowledge. The dragons and titans that once ruled them have gone, either sleeping, hiding, or dead. The gods are distant, unknowable except to those with strong faith, and their influence in the world is limited.  
    
   Each of the continents and island chains on this map offers a unique setting with its own opportunities for adventure. **Mythragar** and **Kaldrstronde** have seen many heroes and villains come and go over the years, but most of the other locations have yet to be explored by Player Characters.

     In a "Duet" campaign style (one player and one DM), this world is your sandbox. Make a character following the guidelines below; after the character is finished following these steps, we can work together to come up with their backstory and determine where they start, what they are doing, etc.  
The players will have a lot of agency when it comes to what kind of adventures to take on. Want to raid dungeon after dungeon? Fight in the front lines of the war against the goblins, demons, or undead? Or, at the other end of the spectrum, be a diplomat rubbing elbows with royalty, a happy-go-lucky bard, or a studious wizard. 

     Whatever mix of action, exploration, and role-play you want, whatever themes and subjects you'd like to explore, Arknamundis probably has a place for it. Having established that, please follow the character creation steps that follow\! There's always someone that shows up with abilities generated wrong, or using some home-brew that wasn't approved. Don't be that guy.

     \**Character Record Sheets:* It's up to you to choose your sheet. As long as I'm able to see it, whatever your preference is. 

**Character Creation Step by Step Guide**

* Ability scores: Use *standard array* or *point buy* per the PHB.

* Races: \**Darkvision*: PC races do not have darkvision. Replace the darkvision feature with one of the following: a skill or tool proficiency, 2 languages, or a \+2 bonus to all *perception* skill checks.   
  Choose from the following: **Human** (variant OK), **Dwarf** (hill, mountain), **Elf** (Wood, high, sea), **Gnome** (rock, forest), **Halfling** (lightfoot, stout), **Half-elf**, **Half-orc**, **Tiefling**, **Aasimar**, **Dragonborn**, **Aarakocra**, **Shifter**, **Genasi**, **Warforged**, **Goliath**, **Lizardfolk**, **Tabaxi**, **Gith**.  
    
* Choose from the 13 core classes (including artificer). Get the starting equipment provided (minor swaps of items with DM approval). You will start at level 1 (with maximum HP for your Hit Die), but will quickly advance to 3rd level and beyond, so consider your subclass options. If you need to choose a deity or patron, consult the DM.

* Choose a background from the PHB, the variant versions such as knight and merchant included. Gain the starting proficiencies, special feature and equipment. Also choose 2 personality traits, 1 ideal, 1 bond, and 1 flaw. These traits may be modified or paraphrased from the lists in the PHB. Don't write a lengthy background at this stage, just do these steps to give us a starting point.

* Choose your alignment. Any alignment is allowed. 

* If you haven't already, name your character and fill in their physical description.

* Any starting gold you have may be used to purchase more starting equipment from the PHB.

* Finish filling in any final details (spells, saving throws, etc.).

* You're done\! Now you and the DM can collaborate to create a more detailed background including allies, family, enemies, organiizations, homeland, etc. Thanks for being a player\!
