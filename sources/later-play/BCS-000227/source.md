The Vigil of Siege Perilous
Before there was England there was the throne. Before the
first of the angler tribe departed the main land of the Old
World, there was the throne. At the dawning of the dragons,
the green and the red, the white and the black laid claim to all
of the land. A single gold dragon looked on the isles and wept
for their future. And so Merlin the Golden Dragon, and 33rd
degree mason, came to Wales. He set the capstone in Cardiff,
of what would one day become Camelot. And Upon that place
Merlin returned to make a throne for his King. Not the throne
he would sit on, but a masonic machine of perfecting justice.
The measuring tool upon which good is divided from bad.
The Siege Perilous. Those who prefer no uncertain terms in
life, rigidity, habit and discipline are likely to belong to a local
chapter of the the Siege Perilous.
A Cult of Vigilantism
You believe that the world is clearly and absolutely divided by
Good and Evil. And the dividing line between them exacts a
toll upon those who live their lives as anything less than
good.The vigil of Siege Perilous seeks to safeguard Arthur’s
kingdom in his absence by deed and song. The deeds done
are recorded in a heraldic tome called the Y brenin unwaith
ac yn y dyfodol (The Once and Future King). Any deed one
wishes to record may be inscribed in the tome for a fee of 5
silver pieces. This has made the leadership of the Vigil both
rich, and sure of the rightness of their deeds (however correct
or misguided they may be). Talk to your DM. Work out where
you fit in this rigid religious structure. Are you a shrewd and
clever leader ready to lay claim to a mayoral race? or perhaps
you are a hedge knight, roaming the king's roads in search of
evil to punish. In most small towns a chapter of the Vigil
chooses to self police their home and help protect their
region against invasion. Were you an upstanding citizen of
some hamlet assigned to town defense only to find that after
a day's trip to the market, the home you returned to had been
sacked by monsters leaving you to seek a new start in the
world?
The fate of the New World relies on you
Decide the moral cause you will champion. Chastity,
Temperance, Charity, Diligence, Patience, Kindness, or
Humility. And a vice you wish to eliminate from the world:
Lust, Gluttony, Greed, Sloth, Wrath, Envy, or Pride. Design a
character with the two attributes in mind. Perhaps you are of
the Charitable Vigil of the Siege Perilous’ wrath. Your
character would then be pleased by charitable things, and
quick to stand between a raging barbarian and his intended
foe. Or perhaps your cause is Temperance and the sin you
wish to cleanse the world of is lust. You may be a member of
the Sober Vigil of the Siege Perilous’ harlot. Discuss with
your DM what your chosen options do for your lifestyle and
RP interactions.
Feature: Arbiter
Most rural leadership rely on Vigils to keep the peace. In
times of minor conflict most peasants will trust a Vigil
member to settle their agreements fairly and without
bias.The traditional payment for such an arbitration is 5 gold,
as decreed by a clever bard’s song many years ago.The
symbol of the vigil is the manacles. And a member is never
without them in case a threat presents itself.

d8 Traits

d6 Ideals
1 The Siege. Finding someone worthy of the seat is all
that really matters.(Chaotic)
2 Order. The law is meant to ensure Arthur’s kingdom
while he is away.(Lawful)
3 Peace. The ultimate objective of the violence of Siege
Perilous is to remove violence from society.(Good)
4 Recognition. No deed should be kept from the siege,
Good or Evil. All must be recorded. My deeds are sure
to be rewarded.(Neutral)
5 Punishment A public display of consequences is the
model for how to deal with those who cross us.(Evil)
6 Legislation. The laws that guide the country must be
cared for by those devoted to our cause.(Any)
d6 Bonds
1 I failed to give justice to a criminal once. Their
subsequent costly betrayal has boosted my thirst for
justice.
2 I wish to write the laws, not just enforce them.
3 Despite the rigours code of the Siege, I believe mercy
is Arthur’s will.
4 I successfully convicted a murder who later escaped
my judgment. I still seek them.
5 I long to sit in Arthur’s chair once I have proved my
worth.
6 I’ve modeled my career after a highly respected Arbiter
who convicted my mother for crimes against our King.
d6 Flaws
1 I’m incapable of recognizing deception.
2 I seem like a harsh judge to others, but I judge myself
most harshly of all.
3 I have broken the law and am aware that I am eluding
justice.
4 I was traumatized by being forced to commit a crime
as a child.
5 I’m unable to distinguish between the letter and the
spirit of the law.
6 I wish I had joined the siege publicly, but I fear they’d
never accept me.
