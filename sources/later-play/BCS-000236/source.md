# 🗼**The Mortal Trials**

## **📜Welcome to The Mortal Trials:**

## **The Rules Of The Tower📜**

**Welcome, brave adventurer, to the Tower of Gods.**  
Before you can enter the trials and face the legendary horrors within, there are a few things you need to know.  
Fear not, for these rules will guide you to glory, treasure, and the ever-hopeful light of victory.  
Let’s break it down into what really matters for your journey.  
Ready?  
Then, let’s get started.

1. ## **🧑‍🎤Character Creation: Your Path Begins**

**Random Character on D\&D Beyond:**  
The Gods have chosen you to enter the Tower, but **you** get to decide who you are.  
A party playing in a run of The Mortal Trials is composed of strictly 4 characters, starting from Level 2, and making sure to add only Starting Equipment items from your Origin and Class, even when joining an on-going Run.  
Feel free to use **D\&D Beyond** to make a random character for your adventure, or take control of your fate with a custom creation by using any method of character creation you prefer, be it via other apps or just a character sheet pdf. That also means that you can choose any method of choosing your initial Statistics: Standard Arrays, Point Buy, or rolling dices.  
If you do decide to roll your Statistics, remember that you have 1 general free reroll to your disposal, and 1 free reroll if the average of the total score that you get from the dice is below 70, which is total average.  
The choice is yours.  
As for which materials you can choose, any of the official content, from 2024 or older, is to your disposal when creating a character. Feel free to also mix and match newer features with older ones. Just remember that in **The Mortal Trials**, only the 2024 core rules apply.

2. ## **⚔️The Trials of the Tower: Survive or Perish**

**No Flanking, No Wish:**  
The Gods have taken the flanking rule out of their divine handbook, so no sneaky advantages here.  
You can get advantage in so many other ways already.  
And as for the **Wish spell**? It does not exist in The Mortal Trials, as the only ones that can grant mortals their wishes, are the Gods themselves.  
**Pick something cool** for your level 9 spell, something that shows you’re worthy of facing the Tower’s dark horrors.  
An additional rule is applied to the **Stable** condition specifically for **The Mortal Trials**. Now, the description of the **Stable** condition reads:  
A creature is Stable if it has 0 Hit Points. The creature is still required to make Death Saving Throws, but don’t suffer the detriments if it fails the Death Saving Throw. Instead, if the result of the Death Saving Throw is 15 or higher, at the end of the character’s turn, they regain 1 Hit Point.  
Once a party in any Run reaches **Level 20**, no new players may join that Run. From that point on, only the current adventurers may see it through to the end. This keeps the final Trials consistent and preserves the weight of the journey.

3. ## **💰The Rewards of Your Victory**

**Experience & Gold:**  
For every encounter, **your strength will be tested**.  
If you succeed, **experience points** and **gold** will be your reward.  
As you progress, the gold you collect will grow \-- just like your character\!

**Rations:🍗**  
The party of ascendants have a total of **3 Rations** at the beginning of any Run.  
When you’ve fought hard and your body demands rest, you’ll be gifted with **Rations**.  
Depending on your fortune, you could receive:

* **None**  
* **1 Ration**  
* **2 Rations** (if you’re lucky)

Rations are your gateway to **rest** \- the party can spend ½ if they want to take a **Short Rest**, or **1** entire ration if they want to take a **Long Rest**.  
And remember, a well-rested adventurer is a deadly one.

**Oscar’s Blessing, Bearer Of The Final Sentence:**  
The first time per Run the party finds themselves having 0 Rations, they instead immediately gain 1 Ration. This is following Oscar, Bearer Of The Final Sentence wish upon reaching the stars and meeting the Gods above the Tower during the Aquila Run.

4. ## **💎Gifts of the Gods: Magical Items**

**The Divine Treasures:**  
As you progress through the Tower, the Gods will bestow upon you their **Gifts** \-- **magical items** designed to aid you on your journey. These are **predetermined** by divine will, ensuring a seamless experience where your focus remains on the adventure ahead.

* **First 4 Encounters:** Expect **Common** and **Uncommon** items, tailored to help you survive the early trials.  
* **After that:** As the difficulty increases, so do the **rarities** of the Gifts, with **Rare** items becoming more *common* as you ascend.  
* Finally, for those wou prove worthy, **Legendary** items await.

The Gods’ **Gifts** will provide the edge you need to face the Tower’s darkest horrors.

### **✨Wondrous Items: Gifts Beyond Reason✨**

The *Tower* is alive with *strange* magic—and among the treasures granted by the Gods are **Wondrous Items** from the 2024 core rules, woven seamlessly into your journey. Most of the **Wondrous Items** have also been *adapted* or have been *powered up* to grant useful features within **The Mortal Trials**.

After each **Encounter**, there is a **30% chance** that one of the **Magical Items** in the loot is replaced by a **Wondrous Item** of the same *rarity*. On average, this results in **10** to **11 Wondrous Items** appearing in each full **run** of **The Mortal Trials**.

In addition, during **Shop Encounters**, there is a **35% chance** that a special fifth *free* category will appear alongside the four chosen by players. This fifth section contains **3 Wondrous Items**, selected at random from the appropriate rarities.

**Wondrous Items** are *powerful* and *rare*, and the Gods demand a steeper price for their gifts. In **Shops**, **Wondrous Items** cost **twice as much** Gold as a standard **Magical Item** of the same rarity. However, adventurers may **trade in Magical Items** to reduce the price. The value deducted is based on the rarity of the traded item:

* **Uncommon: 400 GP**  
* **Rare: 4,000 GP**  
* **Very Rare: 40,000 GP**  
* **Legendary: 400,000 GP**

🧠 *Knowing Your Treasure*: When a character first receives a **Wondrous Item**, its magical properties are **unknown**—and cannot be used until understood.

* **Common Wondrous Items** are automatically understood and can be used immediately.  
* For all others, a character must succeed on an **Intelligence (Arcana)** check to identify the item:  
  * **DC 17: Uncommon**  
  * **DC 20: Rare**  
  * **DC 22: Very Rare**  
  * **DC 25: Legendary**

Each party may attempt to identify **1 Wondrous Item per Rest** (*Short or Long*). During **Combat**, a character may use a **Magic Action** to attempt to learn the properties of a **Wondrous Item** by making the **Arcana** check. If successful, they may immediately activate or attune to the Item as part of that same Action, if applicable.

Once a **Wondrous Item** has been identified, its wielder may share its properties:

* During a **Short** or **Long Rest**, a character may teach **all** party members the Item’s effects.  
* In **Combat**, a character who knows the Item’s effects may use a **Bonus Action** to teach **one** ally how to use it.

*Use this power wisely—knowledge is the key to survival.*

5. ## **🛍️The Shops: Merchants of Fate**

**A Place of Wonders:**  
At the end of every 4th encounter, you’ll find a **shop** \-- stocked with goods for your journey.  
Here, you’ll find useful items to survive and thrive, chosen by the gods themselves, with **no rolling necessary**. All shop inventories are **predetermined**, making sure you can focus on what matters: your strategy and your team.  
So, when entering the shop, each player take their turn to choose **1** of the **8** categories of Items that can be found, so that the shop will contain **3** Items of the chosen category.  
Repeat this for each player, and you’ll have your special shop.  
Players can only be Magical Items that are randomly chosen, but they can choose variations of the Items.  
Meanwhile, Common Items are always available in every single shop.  
After a Player buys a Magical Item within the shop, that same Item will no longer be available, even during the second time Players visit the same shop.

6. ## **👑Boss Fights: The Ultimate Trial**

**⚔️Face the Boss:**  
**After every 8 Encounters**, your party will be tested in a Boss Fight — a brutal milestone of endurance and teamwork. These are no ordinary encounters. Boss Fights are shaped by **High difficulty standards**, crafted to challenge even the most tactical minds.

* One **abnormally high-CR creature** assumes the role of the Boss — a force far more powerful than anything typically faced at your level.  
* The Boss is supported by **additional enemies** to intensify the encounter.  
* The Boss always enters combat with its **maximum possible Hit Points**.  
* The Boss always has Advantage to its **Initiative Roll**.

These rules make **Boss Fights** feel imposing and theatrical — the kind of challenge that forges legends or ends stories.

**🧪 Unique Rewards**  
Survive a **Boss Fight**, and each character is rewarded with powerful magic items worthy of their resolve:

* 🏆 **1st Boss**: One **Very Rare** item per player.  
* 🏆 **2nd Boss**: **Two Very Rare** items per player.  
* 🏆 **3rd Boss**: One **Legendary** item per player.  
  These are critical power spikes, earned through hardship and preparation.  
  **🏔️ The Final Boss**  
  A full run of **The Mortal Trials** culminates in the **final Boss Fight** — the ultimate test of strategy, resilience, and mastery. Here, level 20 characters stand fully equipped with powerful magic and Wondrous Items, ready to ascend.  
  The final encounter is balanced at the peak of mortal challenge:  
* 1 creature of **CR 20 to 24**  
* 1 creature of **CR 15**  
* 2 creatures of **CR 10**

This is the **pinnacle of strategic elevation**, the closing chapter before standing in judgment before the Twelve Gods.

*You have climbed the Tower.*  
*You have braved the impossible.*  
*One last step remains. Ascend.*

7. ## **🌌End of the Run: The Final Trial**

**A New Beginning \-- Demigod Status Awaits:**  
If you are victorious and survive the trials, you will ascend to the title of **Demin-God**.  
The Gods, impressed by your strength, will grant you one **Wish** of your choosing.  
**But beware**, mortals may have their desires, but they are not Gods.  
You may ask for anything, but remember: there are limits.  
No demands against the Gods themselves will be tolerated\!  
The Gods are generous, but they also know **your** limits.

* **Your wish** will be granted, but it may be altered slightly, depending on its scope. The Gods enjoy a good challenge, after all.

You will become a **Demin-God**, forever remembered for your victory \-- until the next trial calls.

8. ## **💀Death in the Tower**

Death is not the end \- but it always leaves its mark.  
If your character dies during a combat encounter, the Gods take notice.  
Once the encounter ends, your soul is pulled back into your body through divine force or fate’s cruel will.  
You return to life immediately after the combat ends \- **but you must roll on the Resurrection Consequence Table** to determine what price you’ve paid to walk again.  
This ensures players stay in the game, but death still holds weight.  
After having rolled on the table, players will still gain the Experience Points, the Gold and the possible Ration for that Encounter, and they will receive Divine Gifts as normally, minus the amount of characters that died during the Encounter. So if 2 characters die in an Encounter, the party will receive only 2 of the Magical Items, and so on. Then, they will continue their Run onto the next Encounter.  
*“The Tower does not let go so easily. You live… but not unchanged.”*

### **Resurrection Consequence Table (d12)**

**1 \- Weakened Vessel:** Max HP reduced by 50% until end of the cycle.  
**2 \- Exhausted Soul:** Gain 2 Levels of Exhaustion.  
**3 \- Scar Of The Divine:** Disadvantage on all Saving Throws vs magic until you take 2 Long Rests.  
**4 \- Fractured Focus:** Disadvantage on all Constitution checks for the rest of the Run.  
**5 \- Disrupted Channels:** Lose one class feature or spell for the next 2 encounters.  
**6 \- Cursed Echo:** Roleplay Compulsion. Obey it: \+1 to Attack Rolls. Disobey: Disadvantage on all d20 rolls.  
**7 \- The Hunger Returns:** Must consume 1 Ration at the start of each encounter or 1 Level of Exhaustion.  
**8 \- Dead Man’s Lag:** \-2 to Initiative and can’t take Reactions during next 2 Combat Encounters.  
**9 \- Shattered Timing:** On start of each turn next 2 Combat Encounters, roll 1d6. On 1: You cannot take an Action this turn.  
**10 \- Soul Marked:** Enemies deal \+2d4 damage to you until you take a Long Rests.  
**11 \- Divine Silence:** Cannot be the target of allied healing spells/features during next 2 Combat Encounters.  
**12 \- Touched By Death:** \-20 Max HP, but+1d6 Necrotic damage on all weapon attacks for 2 Combat Encounters.

This rule does not apply if a character died during a **Boss Fight Encounter**.  
If a party is completely defeated during a Boss Encounter, the Run is not immediately lost. The Gods allow one **Reclaim Attempt**, where a new team of challengers may face the same Boss. The fallen players may spectate but cannot participate. If the new team succeeds, the Run continues, and the fallen players may create new characters to continue the Run, starting with basic equipment but the same level; if they also fall, the **Run is closed permanently**, and a new Run begins in its place.

## **🙏Gods’ Blessings**

Before each run begins, the gaze of one of the Twelve will fall upon the adventurers, and their silent favor will shape what is to come.  
The Gods do not walk among mortals, but their will is felt in subtle sings \-- a shift in the winds, a whisper in the flame, the shimmer of a coin caught in twilight.  
At the dawn of every journey, a single deity is chosen at random, by rolling 1d12, to bless the run.  
No two ongoing runs may share the same patron, for the Gods do not quarrel over mortal threads \-- each story belongs to one alone.  
Each Blessing carries the echo of the divine it stems from: some may offer a surge of power in the moment it is needed most, while others bend the laws of the world throughout the run, subtly but irrevocably shifting fate.  
These are not gifts.  
They are threads in the tapestry \-- and the Gods always watch how the thread is woven.  
Before the first encounter of a run, an omen or sign may appear \-- a brief vision, an altered sky, a changed voice in the wind \-- to mark the presence of the divine.  
From that moment on, the journey is shaped by the hand unseen.  
Whenever a Blessing instructs players to roll using X, treat X as the number of the Cycle you're currently in.

### **👁️1 \- “Eye Of Twilight” Aquila:**

Aquila, the God of the sky, celestial bodies, and the cycle of day and night, was also the God that once had the largest realm. They reside above the heavens, encompassing and watching over the entire world. In most legends, Aquila’s pride does not obscure their kindness. They gaze upon humanity, warming the earth and bringing down life-giving rain. However, they can also transform into the most fearsome calamity, harshly punishing any who dare to overcome the sky. Legends depict Aquila as a colossal sky-bird with a hundred eyes. When their eyes open, light fills the world, and when their eyelids fall, darkness envelops the land. Aquila is also the God of creation and invention.  
**Blessing**: At any given moment, once per run, you immediately know the position of all enemies on the battlefield, and until the start of the next round, you gain the benefits of the Haste spell, you have Advantage with all attacks and enemies have Disadvantage with any Saving Throw caused by you.

### **📖2 \- “Bough Of Rift” Cerces:**

Cerces is the master of wisdom, intellect, and vegetation. They wield authority over ecologies, knowledge, and rationality, and people refer to them as the hermit in search of wisdom. Nature is Cerces’ courtyard and also their library. They allow people to study and rest there, and convey their knowledge to the ignorant tirelessly and generously. Cerces graces the world in the form of a grand and holy tree. Where they take root, vegetation spouts to become massive forest.  
**Blessing**: For this run, at the beginning of combat, gain Xd4 Temporary Hit Points. At any given moment, once per run, if you would fail a Saving Throw, you can choose to succeed instead. The next time you fall to 0 HP, regain this Blessing.

### **🪨3 \- “Pillar Of Stone” Georios:**

Georios is the God of earth. The skies and the seas were also born of their dormant breath. Mythology believes that every mountain range was once a physical manifestation of Georios. They are the largest, gentlest, and most benevolent God that protects the mortal world. Georios is also the God of agriculture and history.  
**Blessing**: At any given moment, once per run, until the start of your next turn, you gain \+4AC and if you are hit by an attack, you reduce incoming damage by 20\.

### **🌀4 \- “Gate Of Infinity” Janus:**

Janus is the God of passages and roads. They know the existence and ends of all paths. Janus’ power can open the doors to all broken mortal worlds, create non-existent paths, and point to unseen directions. Janus is the God most revered by humanity. They created roads and connected cities. The passages that Janus rules over are not just doors humans traverse through, but also the path that connects destiny. They reveal fate through prophecies and point the way forward for humanity. Janus made countless roads, as well as countless choices. They guide humans to the correct path, so mankind will avoid the destiny of confusion. Very few are aware that the God of doors is also the God of isolation and imprisonment.  
**Blessing**: At any given moment, once per run, you can change the position of each creature, including characters, on the battlefield however you want, inside the battlefield. After the changes, until the end of the current round, you have double your speed and enemies cannot move.

### **🗿5 \- “Throne Of Worlds” Kephale:**

Kephale is the omniscient, omnipotent God of original creation that holds up the skies. Kephale was born from the combination of lightning bolts from the skies striking the spine of the earth. They were then given the duty of the creation of life. The first humans were created in Kephale’s own image and given life. Kephale loved these tiny beings so deeply that their majestic figure would always remain in the mortal world to accompany them.  
**Blessing**: At any given moment, you can either make an attack you just missed hit instead or make an attack that would hit you miss instead. Once you use this Blessing, you regain it after scoring a Critical Hit. Each time you regain this Blessing, the number of Critical Hits required to restore it increases by 1\.

### **💖6 \- “Chrysalis Of Gold” Mnestia:**

Mnestia is the God that symbolizes beauty and love. Their appearance is often illustrated as a beautiful and elusive butterfly with golden patterns carved into their wings. They pursue beauty in the world, weave it into the golden thread, spin the romances of humankind and grant everyone love and affection.  
**Blessing**: At any given moment, once per run, gain 30 Temporary Hit Points. If you take any damage while having these Temporary Hit Points, the creature that dealt damage is Charmed by you. At the end of the current round after using this Blessing, remove all remaining Temporary Hit Points you have and heal Hit Points by the same amount.

### **🩸7 \- “Lance Of Fury” Nikador:**

Nikador is the God of strife and competition. He is portrayed as a noble warrior that wears pure white sacred armor. Legend has it that the Lance of Fury that they wield can raze even the most powerful and prosperous city to the ground. Their bloodthirsty temperament has also made them a God of blood and sacrifice. Warriors gather under them and yearn to follow Nikador into war and achieve an honorable death.  
**Blessing**: At any given moment, once per combat, you can add Xd4 to any attack roll or damage roll. At any given moment, once per run, if you are Bloodied, you gain the following benefits for the rest of the combat or until you are no longer Bloodied: you are resistant to Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage, you add Xd6 to attack rolls and damage rolls, and after inflicting damage to a creature, heal for half of the damage inflicted.

### **🌌8 \- “Veil Of Evernight” Oronyx:**

Oronyx is the God of time and night. Before the first dawn of the universe, the world was caged in the purest darkness. When the borders and day and night were separated, the young Onoyx jolted awake from the newborn concept of time, heading toward the outer reaches of the universe alone. There, they materialized as the Evernight Veil and henceforth remained afar from humanity, quietly spending their time managing the fabric of time.  
**Blessing**: For this run, each character starts with the Lucky feat. If they already have the Lucky feat, they gain 1 use more for it.

### **🍷9 \- “Chalice Of Plenty” Phagousa:**

Phagousa is the God that controls oceans and storms, as well as the master of honey brew and banquets. They are capable of freely moving bodies of water across any place as they please. According to mythology, they are a capricious and pleasure-obsessed God that sees the oceans as their place of alcoholic revelry. Phagousa also bears the duty of washing clean the filth of the world. Baths that have received the benediction of the God are able to not only clean the body of impurities, but can also cleanse the wounds of the soul. They are an irreplaceable joy in the lives of all people. At the same time, Phagousa is also the God of banquets and drunkenness. They passed on the art of brewing to humanity and taught them the first songs and dances with the ocean breeze and waves.  
**Blessing**: For this run, each Healing Potion recovers the maximum amount of Hit Points they can give. The first time a character drinks a Healing Potion, there is a 20% chance to gain 1 level of Exhaustion. Each subsequent time a character drinks a Healing Potion without gaining the level of Exhaustion or before taking a Long Rest, the chance doubles. After taking a Long Rest, the chance resets to its original 20% chance.

### **⚖️10 \- “Scale Of Justice” Talanton:**

Talanton is the God of law and justice. They rarely appear in the mortal world in physical form and there are almost no records of them in legend. People often say that they are the rules of destiny, the law of all things, and the divine retribution for violation of such rules and laws. Talanton is seen as a manifestation of justice. They uphold the taboos and boundaries of the world and lay down logic and laws for all things. They created all currencies to ensure that all transactions are of equivalent exchange. They maintain the balance of the world. Any action that would damage these laws will be subject to retribution. Just as a scale that never tilts, all things must be free of excess. If one wishes to obtain, they must first lose.  
**Blessing**: At any given moment, once per run, for the rest of the current combat, you can gain 6 points to any three of your statistics, but lose 6 points from the other three.

### **💀11 \- “Hand Of Shadow” Thanatos:**

Thanatos is the God of death and annihilation, and no one knows their true form \- for the dead cannot relay legends to the living. It is said that their touch causes flowers to wither, soil to crack, metal to corrode, and flesh to turn to ashes \- everything shrouded in thick, dark mist and disintegrating into meaningless fragments.  
**Blessing**: Once per run, when you would die, return to consciousness standing with half maximum HP. At the end of the current combat, if you have not recovered any amount of Hit Points from any source other than this Blessing, you die.

### **🪙12 \- “Coin Of Whimsy” Zagreus:**

Zagreus is the God of lies and fortune. The true Zagreus is often depicted as a two-faced God who is both smiling and crying. Its two parts are much like the coexistence of lies and truth in the world. One cannot exist without the other. Zagreus has no interest in fulfilling their divine duties. They love to cause divisions in the paths of the Gods and play tricks to sow chaos and disorder. They do not have their own altars or temples, either. This is because even Zagreus’ disciples would not worship such a God. Thus, they become the God of the poor and unremarkable. Legend states that they often appear in barren desert areas to provide people with unpredictable opportunities and change. Before setting out on a long journey, people would often curse the name of Zagreus to reduce the chances of them encountering thieves or ill fortune. They would also offer up prayers in times of suffering, in the hopes of everything suddenly turning around. This is precisely the reason that they are tolerated. Nothing is perfect, and no truth remains eternal. Change and carelessness can be fatal, but they can also be a savior in a critical moment.  
**Blessing**: At any given moment, you can consume one magical item to gain Xd6 x 200 gold pieces. If you consume one legendary magical item, also get a random card from the Deck of Wonder that activates immediately.

# **🔱Decrees Of The Wardens**

Within the echoing halls of the Tower, there are voices not of Gods but of mortals whose *devotion* and *insight* have earned them a place of rare distinction. These are the **Wardens of the Tower**, challengers of fate who also help shape it. Through their support of **The Mortal Trials** via the official **Patreon** at **Tier 3**, these players leave lasting marks on the structure of the **Trials** themselves.  
The **Decrees of the Wardens** are their contributions. Each one introduces unique *mechanics*, *monsters*, *artifacts*, or *character* options, forged through *creativity* and *commitment*. These additions become part of the **Tower’s** ever-expanding legacy.

* *Mechanics* and *character* options presented here are available to all players participating in **The Mortal Trials**.  
* *Monsters* and *magic items* are added to the shared roster, becoming part of the general encounter and reward pool used throughout the **Trials**.

Each entry in this section represents a spark of mortal influence within the divine crucible. The **Tower** listens, and it remembers.

1. ## **The Jebrani Jump**

   *Affectionately named after the Satyr Paladin Jebrani, a character made by @grimd3l1ght, who first pulled off this maneuver.*  
     
   As an Action, you can attempt to leap onto a creature that is at least one size larger than you, but not larger than Huge. Choose a creature within your reach. Make a contested check: your choice of Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) vs. the creature’s choice of Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics).  
   On a success, you mount and begin riding the creature. While mounted in this way, you remain in that position until you are dislodged or choose to dismount as a free Action. At the start of both your turn and the creature’s turn, repeat the contested check. If you fail, you are thrown off and land prone in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the creature (creature’s choice). You do not take fall damage from this fall.  
   While riding the creature, you can use a bonus action to force it to move up to half its Speed in a direction of your choice. If this movement causes the creature to collide with a big enough solid object such as a wall, it takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 5 feet moved in that direction. The creature then falls prone, and you are dismounted, landing in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of it.  
   

2. ## **The Dreamling**

*Beholders reshape reality not with tools or spells, but with dreams. One such creature, a maddened aberration haunted by isolation, dreamt of a loyal servant: small, clever, draconic, and utterly obedient. That dream took root deep in the Underdark... and woke up.*

*What emerged was no ordinary spawn. The Dreamling bears a kobold’s frame twisted by psionic birth, with a cluster of eight blinking eyes and scales that shimmer with latent magic. It whispers in forgotten tongues and twitches with inherited paranoia. Though it acts servile, it hungers for control.*

> *“It dreamed of obedience. What it got was something far worse: a mind that dreams back.”*

A CR 6 aberration with powers both draconic and disorienting, the Dreamling is not to be underestimated. It sees what others cannot. It moves where others will not. It may already be watching.

This monster was created by @rpgaddict (Septre Bleu) as part of the Decrees of the Wardens. You can view the full stat block [here](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5678632-the-dreamling), or face it unprepared when it emerges in a Run.

3. ## **The Necronomicon**

Legendary Wondrous Item, Artifact  
Requires attunement by a spellcaster.  
This blasphemous tome, bound in skin, inked with ash, and whispering in the dark tongue of dead devils, is feared across all planes. 4. ## **Flickerdrakes**

Long before dragons ruled the skies, a cabal of fae tricksters sought to craft a perfect guardian for their glimmering hoards of moonstone and starlight. They wove together three creatures:

* **The dragon**, for its fire-born might and dominion over the elements.

* **The cat**, for its grace, keen senses, and unsettling otherworldly awareness.

* **The ferret/weasel**, for its cunning, agility, and relentless hunting instinct.

The result was the Flickerdrake—small by draconic standards but swift and unnervingly intelligent. Its name comes from the way its body seems to *flicker* in the shadows, vanishing from sight and reappearing an instant later. Some say this is an innate form of minor planar shifting, a remnant of the fae magics that birthed it.

Flickerdrakes were never meant to multiply; each was handcrafted, bound to a fae court or archfey as a sentinel or thief. But over the centuries, a few slipped their bonds, bred in the wild, and became tricksters and hoarders in their own right—known to steal shiny trinkets, rare herbs, or even bits of soulstuff from sleeping travelers.

They thrive where ley lines cross, feeding off residual magic, and their presence often marks a place where reality is thin. To see a Flickerdrake is considered either a blessing or a curse: they may guard a path to hidden treasure—or lure you to your doom.

5. ## **Mjork Burner**

*“They are not flame given shape, but hunger given fire.”*

On the planet **Kharoth**, the Mjork Burners are feared signs of imbalance — living wildfires born where the bones of the world groan. They manifest in volcanic ravines, battlefield pyres, and deserts where heat warps the horizon. Scholars debate whether they are true elementals or if they are byproducts of the Tower’s divine bleed, where uncontained magic fuses elemental flame with lingering mortal malice.

A **Mjork Burner** is a towering, half-humanoid silhouette of molten stone wrapped in turbulent fire. Its form is unstable — flames lick and gutter constantly from the cracks in its stony hide, shedding sparks and smoke as it moves. When it runs, fire spills from its limbs, igniting the ground behind it in a trail of molten embers. Its “face” is little more than a cavernous maw of flame, forever open in a soundless roar.

Burners are drawn to **smoke and conflict**, swarming toward areas where mortals burn wood, flesh, or structures. Entire war hosts on Kharoth have been undone not by their enemy’s blades but by the sudden appearance of a Mjork Burner amidst their burning siege engines. Their strange affinity with smoke allows them to stalk prey unseen in the haze of their own destruction.

Though not intelligent in the mortal sense, they are not mindless. They burn with a chaotic neutrality, destroying obstacles but ignoring what does not threaten them. Some fire-worshiping cults believe they are **failed children of Aquila**, the god of the eternal flame, while others whisper that the Burners are the **Tower’s punishment** for mortals who overreach with fire.

7. ## **Agnes’ Amulet**

*Created by @dimilili*  
**Rare Wondrous Item.**  
A warm pulse beats within the gem, faintly synchronized with its bearer’s heart. Scars dull, aches fade, and vitality returns while it is worn. Beneath that comfort, power gathers endlessly, pressing outward, waiting for a moment of release.  
You can use the Amulet as a **Spellcasting Focus**. You gain a \+**1 bonus** to **Spell Attack Rolls** and **Spell Save DCs**. While you are attuned to the Amulet, you know the **Witch Bolt** spell, and can cast it without using a spell slot at level 1 once per Long Rest. You can also cast this spell using maximum a level 1 spell slot. If you are not a spellcaster, **Constitution** is your spellcasting ability for this spell.  
**Untamed Magic.** The amulet accumulates unstable energy called **Strain**. You gain 1 **Strain** whenever:

* You expend a spell slot, or  
* You deal Lightning or Force damage with an attack or ability (once per turn).

**Strain** does not reset during Rests. When **Strain** reaches 10, roll on the **Wild Magic Surge** table, then reset **Strain** to 0\.  
**Arcane Release.** As a Bonus Action, you may spend **Strain** to release stored magic. Choose one effect:  
**Lightning Surge (2+ Strain)**. Your next attack or damaging spell this turn deals an extra 1d8 Lightning damage per 2 Strain spent.  
**Stolen Momentum (3 Strain).** Gain Advantage on one Attack Roll, Ability Check, or Saving Throw.  
**Arcane Restoration (5 Strain)**. Regain a 1st level spell slot, or gain 10 Temporary Hit Points if you have no spell slots.  
**Life Force Channeling.** When you cast a spell but have no spell slot, you may take 1d8 Force damage per spell level (cannot be reduced) to cast it anyway. Doing so immediately adds 2 **Strain**.  
**Sorcery Recovery.** Roll 1d6. Gain a number of **Sorcery Points** and **Strain** equal to the result.  
**Overflow Risk.** If you spend **Strain** while you are **Bloodied**, roll 1d6. If the result is 1-2, roll on the **Wild Magic Surge** table. If the result is 3-6, nothing happens.
