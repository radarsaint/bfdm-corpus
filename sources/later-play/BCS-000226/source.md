Circle of the Storm
" What if I told you I had the power to end this war, right here,
right now? I can create storm clouds in a clear sky and call
upon enough rain to drown a city. I can call upon the winds
and run a tornado through an army. I can create a hailstorm
that will shatter brick and stone. I can create a blizzard that will
freeze a man in minutes. The question isn't can I win this war,
the question is should I, knowing the cost? "
Alfie, the Archdruid of the Circle of Storms
Druids within the Circle of the Storm follow the Elements.
While other druids tie themselves to the land, animals, the
night sky, or even fey courts, you have decided to call upon
one of the most powerful aspects of nature, the weather itself.
Just as rain can nourish the land or destroy it in a flood, you
defend nature using the elemental forces it creates. At first,
you tap into those elemental forces, but over time, and with
growth, you become one with them.

Your mystical connection to the elements grants you access
to certain spells. At 2nd, 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th level you gain
access to circle spells related to the elemental forces of
nature on the following table.
Once you gain access to a circle spell, you always have it
prepared, and it doesn’t count against the number of spells
you can prepare each day. If you gain access to a spell that
doesn’t appear on the druid spell list, the spell is nonetheless
a druid spell for you.

Storm Enhancement
At 2nd level, you can start tapping into the power of the
tempest. When casting a single target spell that has a
damage type other than Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing,
you may augment that damage type to Cold or Thunder.
When augmenting a spell to Thunder damage, all creatures
damaged by the spell are deafened until the end of their next
turn and must make a Constitution saving throw. If they fail,
they are pushed 5 feet in a direction of the caster's choosing.
The spell also makes a loud peel of thunder that can be heard
clearly up to 300 feet away.
When augmenting to Cold damage, all creatures damaged
by the spell lose half of each of their move speeds until the
end of their next turn and must make a Dexterity saving
throw. If they fail, they are knocked prone due to slipping on
the ice.
You can use this feature a number of times equal to your
Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all
expended uses of it when you finish a long rest.
Eye of the Storm
Also at 2nd level, you may choose to be unaffected by any
effects due to weather and can pass through difficult terrain
made by weather conditions without slowing.
Lightning Rider
At 6th level, you can take on the form of lightning and teleport
across the battlefield. As an action, you may pick a spot you
can see within 60 feet of you as your destination. Each
creature in the line excluding you must make a Dexterity
saving throw against your Druid spell save DC, taking 3d6
lightning damage on a failed save, and half as much damage
on a successful one. If you are in storm like conditions, such
as rain or snow, add 3d6 thunder damage to the damage
done by this ability as a loud peel of thunder follows behind
you. This thunder damage also applies to any non-creature
objects or terrain within 5 feet of the line.
You may use this feature a number of times equal to your
proficiency modifier. All uses are regained after long rest.
Cloud Cover
At 10th level, you gain the ability to summon fog all around
you. You may use your action to casue fog to billow out of you.
The fog will rise to 50 feet, and spread 150 feet away from
you, including around corners. This fog will persist for 1
minute, even in strong winds. You can decide how thick the
fog is, granting all creatures within, no cover, half cover,
three-quarters cover, or full cover. Regardless of your choice,
you have blindsight to any creature within the fog.
Additionally, when you use your Lightning Rider feature, you
may make your destination any spot within the fog and gain
the Thunder damage bonus.
You may use this feature once and regain the use of it after
a long rest.


One with the Tempest
By 14th level, you have learned to take on the form of living
storms. You can now expend two uses of Wild Shape at the
same time to transform into a Living Storm (stat block
below). While in this form, you may speak any language you
know and you may cast any of the spells granted to you by
your Circle Spells feature, without needing any of their
verbal, somatic, or material components.
