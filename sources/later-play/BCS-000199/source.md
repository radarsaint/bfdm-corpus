### **Gazer**

**Alias:** *“Tom”*

### **Crimes:**

* **Unlawful Surveillance of Private Residences**  
   — Regularly hovered outside bedroom windows, bathhouses, and restricted locations, observing occupants without consent.

* **Magical Harassment**  
   — Employed its confusion and fear rays to intimidate individuals when confronted, often to escape being driven off.

* **Violation of Abjurative Wards**  
   — Bypassed magical protections, such as *Alarm*, *Glyph of Warding*, and *Arcane Lock*, to enter or observe protected areas.

* **Trespassing in Secure Locations**  
   — Illegally entered residential compounds, guild halls, and sanctuaries, often through vents, chimneys, or sewer access points.

* **Psychic Intrusion Without Consent**  
   — Used its limited telepathy to invade the thoughts of civilians, particularly during vulnerable moments, causing psychological distress.

* **Resisting Detainment by Lawful Authorities**  
   — Fled from lawful banishment and attempted capture on multiple occasions, sometimes employing eye rays to disable pursuers.

* **Repeat Offender Under Magical Restrictions**  
   — Previously marked with divination tags and minor bindings, but found ways to evade detection and return to prohibited zones.

   **— Utilized forbidden soul-binding magic not only to enslave but to utterly destroy the souls of victims, preventing resurrection, reincarnation, or afterlife passage.**

   **— Betrayed a multilateral peace accord by selling arcane secrets and military intelligence to extraplanar warlords, directly leading to the deaths of thousands.**

### **Sentence:**

**— Upon execution, the soul of Vardon was permanently obliterated by order of the High Tribunal. His body was reanimated as a mindless Skeleton, not as mercy or utility, but as a continuation of his sentence—to toil, serve, and suffer in undeath until the universe itself ends.**
