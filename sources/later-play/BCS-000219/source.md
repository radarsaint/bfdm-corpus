  * While world governments cannot get involved without provoking conflict with the empire, powerful noble houses and common folk alike are encouraged to burn, drown, crush, and hang witches and then take their land to consecrate it once the witch is dead 

## **How did it start?**

* The war was preceded by about 20 years of tension between the holy nation of Drunia and the orcish nation of Glidira, as Glidira had offered sanctuary to the very pagans and heretics the Cyranic Church was attempting to persecute through the crusades


* The First Holy War started with The Order of Yggdrasil leading the people of the city in a communal ritual in the center of Ozzugh (the orcish city that surrounds and protects Yggdrasil the World Tree). They were attempting to empower and summon the Great Spirits from the elemental planes

* The Drunian Knights Templar attacked the ritual and the civilians, as they were all taking part in the practice of witchcraft, & The Ozzugh Massacre ensues

* After this first massacre, the entirety of Glidira became a target for pillaging and raids by crusaders of the Cyranic Church (The church of Phecyra, Goddess of Magic)  
    
* At the start, the conflict was essentially Drunia & Atruna vs. Glidira

## **How did it end?**

* Arch Paradigm Devamir calls a Holy Tyberic Synod,a gathering of all individuals of religious influence and all the leaders of the 10 mortal nations, where The Drunian Theocracy proposes the idea of a Grand Tyborian Concord to end all current wars and prevent them in the future  
    
* Many years later, a vote from the second Holy Tyberic Synod crowned Arch Paradigm of the Cyranic Churc, Ilmania Devamir as the first Concordat Empress  
    
* With the creation of the concord and the new laws governing warfare between nations, the war was brought to an abrupt end and a precarious peace loomed over the 10 nations  
    
* By it’s end, the First Holy war was nearly a global conflict  
    
* Once Empress Ilmania Devmira ascended to the throne, Pontifex Garian Macer of Drunia became the Arch Paradigm of the Cyranic church

## **Why were they fighting?**

* Shortly after the creation of the gods, Phecyra (The Goddess of Magic) put a permanent ban on all use of wild magic, in an attempt to regulate how much magic any given person could use. She also created the weave, a network of pure magic that connects all living things. People that wished to do magic would have to do so by pulling magic through the weave, which could be more easily regulated by Phecyra.  
    
* The magic of the Elder Spirits was renamed “Wild Magic” and casters that filtered their magic through the Elder Spirits were labeled “witches”, “pagans”, and “heretics”  
    
* Many of these Witches sought to bring back the ancient elemental Elder Spirits that were destroyed and reshaped in the creation of the Gods  
    
* Shortly after the Orcish nation of Glidira offered sanctuary and aid to those that wish to practice the old ways, the Arcane Church of Phecyra pressured Drunia’s government to take military action against the heretics.   
    
* When the Council of The Golden Dawn crowned King Kairon and Queen Arietta Valteya as Yn’Daria’s first monarchs in nearly a century, one of Kairon’s first moves as king was to send aid to their orcish neighbors to the Southwest  
  * He even traveled to the Domari Plains in Glidira to fight on the battlefield alongside his men

# **What is the Tyberic Concord?**

* ## **The Agreement**

  * Established about 60 years ago  
      
  * After nearly 40 years of devastating war, the head of the Arcane Church of Drunia called for the gathering of a “Holy Tyberic Synod”, a council where every high-ranking member of every religion’s clergy was to be gathered  
      
  * Garian Macer, Cyranic Pontifex of Drunia at the time, proposed the creation of a “Grand Tyborian Concord”. It would be an agreement between the nations that would “act as the greatest instrument for peace that the world has ever seen”  
      
  * This concord would have it so that no one nation could rise up and take military action against another without all other mortal nations being bound by oath to enforce the laws of the concord to aid in ending the conflict


* ## **The Empire**

  * 8 years after the first gathering of the Holy Tyberic Synod, Arch Paradigm Ilmania Devamir was voted in as the First Concordat Empress  
    * The title of Arch Paradigm was passed down to the Drunian Pontifex, Garian Macer   
  * In accordance with the laws of the concord, every year the ten mortal nations each send 20% of the nation's wealth in taxes to the Imperial Capital (Drunia) and a portion of their nations soldiers to serve in The Imperial Sentinel Legion  
  * Each of the 10 nations is protected and patrolled by Imperial Sentinels   
    * Though some are more protected than others  
        
  * The law of the concord states that every 10 years the Concordat Emperor or Empress is to choose the leader of one of the other mortal nations to move to The Haven City of Nalgura in Drunia and inherit the Imperial Throne  
  * **High Priestess Kanara Jadebrooke** of West Ecria was chosen 5 years ago by Emperor Garian Macer to be the seventh Concordat Emperess
