New feature:

Courage:  Once per turn, when you make an attack against an enemy that is within 5 feet of you, and no allies are within 5 feet of you or your target, you deal +1d6 damage.

Quills:

At level 1

Quills:

Your quills are particularly effective weapons. You can, as a bonus action, pull a long quill off your person and use it as a weapon. It counts as a javelin that only you are proficient in, and lasts until you take a long rest, at which time it is damaged and cannot be used anymore. At level 5, this quill counts as a magic weapon for the purposes of overcoming damage resistance. You may pull a quill out like this four times and you regain all uses after a long rest.

At level 4:

Quill Barrage:

You shake off your loose quills and shoot them at your enemies. When you do so each target in a 15 ft cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. The DC for this saving throw equals 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus. A creature takes 1d8 piercing damage on a failed save as well as suffering a -1 on all saving throws until the end of your next turn, and half as much damage on a successful one (and no penalty to their saving throws).The damage increases to 2d8 at 6th level, 3d8 at 11th level, and 4d8 at 16th level. After you use Quill Barrage, you can’t use it again until you complete a short or long rest.
