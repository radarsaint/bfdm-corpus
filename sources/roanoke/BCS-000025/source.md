**<u>\#Look out hill: Lodestone Dungeon crawl.</u>**

**Post to players first:**

Altered play mechanics:(Discovering this mechanic requires a survival check DC 12 or the identify spell cast on a player)

**Exploration will stay in initiative order.**

**Unyielding grease**: (Within the demiplane of grease). Grease is magically inert and cannot be affected by any spell that does not have a damage type. Washing it off requires one whole bar of soap (consumes the soap)and at least 2 minutes immersed in running water.

Homebrew condition:

**Fire:** Any fire that touches grease has a 5% chance to ignite and and act like a [<u>windwall</u>](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/wind-wall) made of smoke.Unattended smoke will cause the party to begin to suffocate in real time based on the rules for drowning. (PHB pg 183).

**Slathered.** *<u>When you miss on an attack</u>:,* you drop your weapon or item in your hand. This includes component pouches and arcane focuses. *<u>Start every round prone</u>*. It will cost you half movement to stand. At the end of your turn, the grease will cause you to slip and fall prone.

***<u>Reskin properties:</u>***

*Wondrous Item, very rare (Attunement upon consumption. Duration 4 hours real time. )*

This coagulated, symbiotic goo comes sealed in a jar and slowly shifts and moves, as if endlessly exploring the jar’s interior. To attune to this item, you must first drink the contents of the jar, unlocking the following properties.

***Resistant.*** While attuned to Rutgut’s purified grease, you have resistance to fire. You are immune to the Prone Condition.

***Amorphous.*** As an action, you can speak a command word and cause your body to assume the amorphous qualities of living congealed fat. For one minute, you (along with any equipment you’re wearing or carrying) can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing. Once you use this property, it may only be used once.

***Boiling Breath.*** As an action, you can exhale scalding grease in a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 36 (8d8) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Once you use this property, it can’t be used again until the next dawn. This weapon does not ignite flammable objects.

***Symbiotic Nature.*** The grease can’t be removed from you while you’re attuned to it, and you can’t voluntarily end your attunement to it. If you’re targeted by a spell that ends a curse (or other dispelling effect), your attunement to the grease ends, as it seeps out of you.

If you die while the grease is inside you, it bursts out and engulfs you, turning your corpse into a black pudding allied with the daelkyr (see the [*Monster Manual*](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/mm) for the [black pudding’s](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/black-pudding) stat block).
