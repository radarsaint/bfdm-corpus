How to read the timeline: Day number and date corresponds with breakdowns and calendar. 
Day title (Invitationals and Tutorials) transitions to clue. (Uru eating a Chinnokin. Flies off toward Roostloft.) Clue points to the next
day’s narrative. (Not always applicable, if, for example, the next day is an event in town.)
Notated like this:
Invitationals and Tutorials> Uru eating a Chinnokin. Flies off toward Roostloft.

Stretch goal. Ensure that every day in a week gives a nod somehow to the voice event.
Stretch goal. Ensure that every voice event points to the finale.
