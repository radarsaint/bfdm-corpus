**Château du Brule**

The singing of grand bells ring throughout the plaza. A mass of poor and broken flock towards articulated stone walls displaying a banner of a smiling sun on a sword pommel. Grandiose gates open inward unleashing a warmth and smell of fresh bread for the misfortuned. As you approach the interior widens throughout a courtyard comparable to the King’s where armor clad knights are seen handing out meals to families while recruits train rigorously in the background. The inside of the Cathedral shines light through stained glass windows. The exterior connects to a large keep where the Knights du Roi Soleil continue. Bypassing the meal line into the cathedral you see ornately carved wooden pews standing beside statues and masterful imagery of numerous Gods. A knight in special chainmail with a tabard of the Roi Soleil, the Seneschal, Florian Chapelle, is seen taking care of day to day operations whilst training with his rapier, or paints upon a large canvas at the top of the balcony overlooking the grounds. He never forgets a face and greets you at the first moment he notices and is always welcoming.
