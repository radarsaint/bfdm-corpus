Irritating rules for exploring the maze.

* If you cast this round you may not speak next round.  
* If you attack this round you must change weapons before you may attack next round.  
* If you quaff a potion, everyone must quaff a potion (out of combat only)  
* If a player buffs you and you do not buff them back, both players must use all of their movement on their round.  
* If you attack at range you must do so prone.  
* All movement that is not climbing or crawling must be done with the same restrictions as moving a Knight in dragonchess. Either ten feet forward and 15 feet to the side, or 15 feet forward, then 10 to the side.




A manticore named **Illio** (guide for playing [here](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/375-how-to-play-a-manticore-like-a-cunning-beast)) tricked **Bahamut the Fallen (Sapphire Dragon statistics)** into his service shortly after his nearly catastrophic time in Arcania. The deal he made was to have Bahamut to protect the manticore from harm in exchange. Every day, the manticore lists new ways in which he can envision being harmed. This has been going on for 35 years, and so the ways. 

“I found the sky king, the dragon of the highest mountain top. I found him weak and mad. He was hungry and I had food. Because illio is so clever. Illio found it. Illio was loath to share with him. And so made him trade protection for my meal, which was but a morsel to him. But trade we did. And in my debt he now is. Every day I think of something new and horrible that could hurt Illio. And I say: But skyking you promised to protect me. What if I should starve to death when you leave? And the sky god brings me a feast. And when I have finished by meal, I say But master of the mountain top, what if cruel hunters come to kill me? And so he devises a new way to protect me. A new trap or a new trinket. Like the the strip of leather on my head. Sphinx\! The call me now. Sphinx because now Illio is smarter. Is cleverer. Has the headband of intellect. You are coming for me now. And when you come your death will be so delightfully easy. Your bones will be crushed for my egg. Your skin will be put in the fire to make your gristle fill my fortress. It is filled with the bones of the strong. With dead things that I killed because I am clever now. Hehehehehahahahahahaha.”
