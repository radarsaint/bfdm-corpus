Day 13 Golden Dawn Sabotage. (GD)
Monstrum ex Machina
This area takes up a decent portion of the ship. The whole place has been trapped significantly, as well as sabotaged by the rust monster.

One of the engine room workers rushes onto the main deck. “Help! The boiler is going to blow! Someone is sabotaging the ship. We’ve been attacked. I’m not going back in there. I just saw all my friends die. You clear it out for me, and I’ll see what I can do to fix it. But I’m not going back in there. I’ll wait by the door.”

The steam causes a continual fog cloud in the whole area, and the whole area is difficult terrain.
Dead crew.
Overheating boiler will explode and cause the ship to sink
Traps have been laid to protect the rust monster
Steam pipes rigged to explode dealing fire damage and force damage. They can be deactivated, but it will cost time.
Crude spike traps around each corner.
The boiler mechanism

Before the sabotage, an agent is asked to smuggle goods onto the ship. Pass phrase:
Where would you spend the summer?
I’ve heard Ten Towns is nice in summer.

------------
As the door opens and you enter the engine room, steam billows out of the doorway. Your vision is lightly obscured (disadvantage on perception checks to see). The technician explains that the room is 90 feet long and very narrow. There is only enough room for 1 person to walk down the main hallway at a time. On either side of the main walkway, a maze of pipes and mechanical contraptions is crammed together. Small or tiny creatures can navigate the pipes though it is difficult terrain. Just inside the door, there is a 15ft wide x 10ft long area containing small repair tables, mountains of parts, and several tools for maintaining the equipment.

Beyond this small area, the main hallway is heavily obscured rendering your blind. Loud hissing can be heard from down the hall. It seems that some of the pipes have ruptured and are leaking steam. If you can find a way to stop the steam, your vision and hearing should improve some.

The hallway is made up of three sections. One burst pipe is in each section. As players step into the first hallway, they are blinded and deafened.

Each section is similar and has the following properties:
Steam Trap - One random space contains the steam trap. Upon entering the space, a DC 10 perception check is required to avoid a blast of steam dealing 2d8 fire damage DC 12 Constitution save for half.
There are two baby rust monsters in each section. If the players are wearing any metal armor, or carrying any metal weapons, the rust monsters try to eat them. If the players attack, they are blinded. If they miss the rust monster, they damage a pipe, causing a new steam trap to appear in that space, dealing damage. Until the rust monsters are hit, they will continue to try and eat the player’s metal. After they are wounded, they will attack instead. After all the pipes have been secured, the rust monsters will retreat to the next room, Vision is only lightly obscured, and the deafened condition is removed.

Discord considerations: Each player will move to an individual room, unless they have a way of getting around blinded and deafened. For example, message will relieve deafened. A druid can shift into a creature with blindsight to relieve blinded, but they will not be able to fix the pipe unless they use the tools. While in a separate room no one will be able to see what is going on, and they will not be able to see what is happening in the main room.

Environmental challenges: Steam is spraying out of 3 different pipes. Each one can be closed off as an action with a DC 10 Intelligence Check. If all three are closed, the pressure builds
