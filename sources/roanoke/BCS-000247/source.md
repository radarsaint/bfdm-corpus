## Story Arc Outline

Monastic Order for Religious Artifact Recovery

**Week one:** 

* Holy inquisition to defeat undead and supernatural predators.  
* Raiding the Werewolves den to reveal an old Hag/witches home where an ancient puzzle manuscript foreshadows future events that can be earned. Florian leads this event and will try to recruit a number of players into the order. 

**Week two**:

* Exploration force organized to delve deep to recover artifacts from one of the final battlefield sites of the gods. 5 graves lie at the bottom of the Mantapolis sanctuary.  
* Mantapolis  
  1. Melora (Mask of Melora gain \+1 to AC when worn, can cast protection from energy without using concentration. 5 charges DC 18 con (DC 15 con for followers) check daily to regain 1 charge.  
  2. Chauntea (Bracers of Chauntea wearer is always under the effect of barkskin, cast cure wounds at 2nd level 1/day)  
  3. Kord (Warhammer of Kord cast Thunderous Smite 1/day)   
  4. Lathander (+1 Shield of Lathander allows user to inflict radiant damage instead of normal damage 3/day)  
  5. Ilmater (Blood red cord wraps allows the user to receive 2 necrotic damage {ignoring resistance and immunity} to heal 1 hp of touched creatures as an action. Also allows casting of life transference with 3/4 charges remaining. Roll a d20 at beginning of day, 11-20 item receives 2 charges, 3-10 item receives no charge, 1-2 item loses all charges of life transference and cannot be replenished)

**Week three**: 

* Ancient relics and cave drawings discovered that tell of ancient battles with the gods and the creation of Croatoan. Stories of the death of Machidiel.   
* The Mad Dragon Cave:  
  * Claw of the Pure Dragon (can make all your melee attacks vorpal for 2 rounds of combat once per day. Functions as a \+1 attack and damage rolls greatsword)   
* Hag Coven:  
  * Mystra  
    * Mystra's bag of holding (1/day you may reach your hand into the divine bag of holding to pull out 1 of 4 things (roll a d4) that will turn to dust at the end of the next short rest, this item also functions as a standard bag of holding)   
      * Pearl of power   
      * Scroll of dimension door  
      * Fragile Magnifying glass of true sight (spell true seeing)   
      * Halo of stars \- 3x 2d4 force damage stars circle your head and can be expelled as a bonus action towards a target (DC 13 dex save for half)   
  * Helm  
    * Helmet of Helm (gain \+2 AC, critical hits against you become normal hits, may use ¼ of armor class's value {round down min of \+2} as the modifier for any save 1/day in place of your default modifier) {13 AC becomes \+3 bonus, 20 AC becomes \+5 bonus}   
  * Selune  
    * Selune's silver rod (cast moonbeam 1/day, adv on perception checks at night, natural attacks made with one's hands or mouth do 1d8+str/dex modifier. claws form and teeth sharpen)   
* Nueva Esperanza:  
  * Erathis  
    * Pommel of Judgment (⅗ charges, 2 charges: target must make an intelligence saving throw or come under the effects of the banishment spell, cannot be counterspelled, may also spend 1 charge to cast command as a bonus action. Roll DC 13 intelligence saving throw at dawn to regain 1d2 charges)

**Week four**: 

* Reclamation of more artifacts found through exploration  
* Wreckage of the Falmouth:  
1. Torm (A ornate right handed gauntlet with a Celestial inscription on it. Wearing this gauntlet sets the wearer's strength to 20\. If not being worn, this item can be locked akin to an immovable rod. Unarmed strikes do d6 damage with the gauntlet and give \+2 to grapple checks)   
* Reads: "Until my dying breath, I will uphold my duty"   
2. Moradin (A full set of plate mail, While wearing this armor, you gain a \+2 bonus to AC. In addition, if an effect moves you against your will along the ground, you can use your reaction to reduce the distance you are moved by up to 10 feet.) 

* The Lighthouse:  
1. Pelor (Sword of Pelor, a hilt that activates with a beam of orange light. It has the stats of a longsword but with the finesse and versatile property. Its damage is radiant and does and extra d8 to aberrations and fiends \+2 to attack and damage rolls. This item sheds 15ft of bright light and dim light for an additional 15ft)   
2. Corellon (The Sunbow has the stats of a longbow but on a 20, adds an additional 4d6 damage. It's damage is radiant. \+2 to attack and damage rolls. Arrows shot from this bow shed 10 ft of light wherever they land)

**Week five**: 

* Reclamation of Machidiel's holy crown can provide a defensive boon to the town against Croatoan (Powering Machidiel's divine aura)   
  * Machidiel's Crown Recovered from Infernal Well  
1. Machidiel (Crown of the Heavens, this item grants a passive protection aura of 15ft radius when worn, granting those within range advantage on death saving throws, and bringing the unconscious back up to 1hp on a roll of 16-20. The wearer also benefits from the death ward spell and recharges at dawn if the feature is used) 

**Knights of the Sun King ending available:** Reversing the power of Croatoan and channeling his power through 12/15 divine artifacts, resurrecting all of the divine power consumed by him and reviving numerous gods if Croatoan is killed in material plane. Removes the barrier between worlds. The power paired with a successful ritual will keep Croatoan from regenerating in the abyss. 

The souls that Cthulhu corrupted are given a 2nd chance. 

* Perhaps it blesses players to activate for 1 minute to have demi-God stats for fight with Croatoan
