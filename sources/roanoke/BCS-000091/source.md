### Brick making so long, clay runs in their blood.
Halfwudgies are an oppressed people just in the beginnings of revolt against their Urucokran taskmasters. There is no written history amongst the Halfwudgie. But, oral tradition and myth are central to their faith and identity. They are strong, and enduring, and they have no one to look to for their salvation. Save, the courage they muster to defend themselves against their winged enemies.


Halfwudgies, like their cryptid cousins the Puckwudgies, are passive until threatened. Their grooming and clothing are habitually humble. Their family bond is extremely close. Halfwudgies are notoriously untrusting of strangers, seeing them as dangerous. Although these gentle giants aren’t aggressive, they are experts in defense. When faced with possible combat, the Halfwudgie raises the quills on its body and releases them as natural weapons.


Brown, black and grey striped quills form from their skulls where hair would be on almost any other humanoid. At 12 feet, male Halfwudgies tend to be shorter and stockier with quills that are far longer and more ornate than females. Female puckwudgies are taller, sometimes as tall as 15 feet with a more humanoid and less beastial look to them.


#### Servants of the longshadow.
Any halfwudgie who has ever served the Urucokra make a habit of keeping their eyes averted, and toward the ground. This is a brilliant strategy born of necessity. To their oppressors, the halfwudgie seem cowed. But, as any prey knows, a bird who stalks from the skies always casts a long shadow. This is a twofold philosophy. It gives the appearance of appeasement, while actively seeking to take any advantage. And it represents a cunning and patient mind.








#### The hymn of the hills.
The oral tradition of the Halfwudgie is told in song most often. Every night in the typical Halfwudgie home, the families will go to the windows and doors of their homes and sing the songs their parents taught them. They sing of hope and freedom, of despair and of pain. And then begins the song of retribution. A song that fills the night with a sound that may be heard from the mountain tops of Roostloft. When the halfwudgie sing their songs together, it is said the ground itself quakes with their story. **Gain Proficiency in Performance.** Additionally your Cryptid ancestry grants you Primal Intuition. **Gain proficiency** in two of the following skills of your choice: Animal Handling, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Perception, and Survival.

### Halfwudgie names.
Male Halfwudgies take adjectives as first names. Both sexes take their clan name as their surnames. Female Halfwudgies are typically named by an elder female on their twentieth birthday from a preset list of traditional names. Previous to this they are called by the names of local plant life.
**Male names:** Screeching Siltbloom, Mammoth Mournmore, Chonkers Gravelguild, Obnoxious Mincemaker, Wise Chaffscraper or Dunce Soresalve.  


**Juvenile Female** names: Mantrap Boatbottom, Potato Creekhovel, Orchid Blunderbluff.
**Adult Female** names (*you may only choose from this list of traditional names if you were raised in a halfwudgie community*). Melody, Harmony, Chastity, Faith, Hope or Verity
### Halfwudgie traits


You have several traits in common with all other Halfwudgies.


#### Ability Score Increase
Your Wisdom score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1.


#### Age
Enslaved or former slave Halfwudgies reach adulthood at the age of 12 and live up to 30 years.
Freeborn Halfwudgies reach adulthood at the age of 19 and can live upto 75 years.


#### Alignment
The Halfwudgie is incapable of any alignment other than good. 


#### Size
Your size is medium. At level 5, your cryptid ancestry causes you to make the champions change and grow. At level 5 your physical mass becomes greater giving you a more powerful build. You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can Push, Drag or Lift.


\page




#### Speed
Your base walking speed is 30 feet.


#### Courage 
Once per turn, when you succeed on an attack roll against an enemy that is within 5 feet of you, and no allies are within 5 feet of you or your target, you deal +1d6 damage. 


#### Cryptid Defense.


As a bonus action, a Halfwudgie may produce 1d4 natural weapons called sticky quills from the quills on their back once per day.


**Quills**:
At level one, your quills are particularly effective weapons. You can, as a bonus action, pull a long quill off your person and use it as a weapon. It counts as a javelin that only you are proficient in, and lasts until you take a long rest, at which time it is damaged and cannot be used anymore. At level 5, this quill counts as a magic weapon for the purposes of overcoming damage resistance. You may pull a quill out like this four times and you regain all uses after a long rest.
A second ability becomes available at level four: Quill Barrage: You shake off your loose quills and shoot them at your enemies. When you do so each target in a 15 ft cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. The DC for this saving throw equals 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus. A creature takes 1d8 piercing damage on a failed save as well as suffering a -1 on all saving throws until the end of your next turn, and half as much damage on a successful one (and no penalty to their saving throws).The damage increases to 2d8 at 6th level, 3d8 at 11th level, and 4d8 at 16th level. After you use Quill Barrage, you can’t use it again until you complete a short or long rest.
At level 5 gain 2d4 quills per day. 




#### Languages
You can speak (but not read or write) Coastal Arcanian and Wudge.
