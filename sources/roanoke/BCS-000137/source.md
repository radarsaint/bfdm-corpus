**Scope**: Week one has some factors that players will use to determine the future of the colony.

Let's be clear. History has already dictated that Roanoke is in fact the missing colony.

My original intention is for this to go one of several ways.

Beat 1\. The colonist’s deaths: There are only two logical ways to deal with your dead.

1. Bury the bodies.

2. Burn the bodies.

Depending on which way they choose, it will begin to map their doom.

Burying the bodies is an affront to one of the Native peoples. The Urukocra (vulture Arakocra) are cannibals and omnivores and consider not eating the dead to be an affront to nature.

Burning the bodies, I am still looking for a perfect way to deal with this. I think it should be different than burial, but don’t have a perfect story for that yet.

The latrine pit. The latrine pit is going to be a source of plague on the island for the natives if the colony doesnt ever think to burn their waste.

Either way, interactions with the natives are a red herring. There is a big bad that has nothing to do with them, but we want to give the village every reason to be paranoid while maintaining reasons for thing to happen, that if inspect will not hold up to the paranoia we generate.

**The Chinnokin**

This aquatic subrace of Chinnokin lives most of its life near the islands off of the coast of Arcania. They swim they upstream into the freshwater rivers to spawn. Many illusionist and rogues or arcane tricksters can be counted amongst their number. With their fishy features and scaly skin, this amphibious race is more at home in the water than on land.

**The running of the Chinnokin**

The main exception to coastal dwelling is when a Chinnokin has reached a point in their life cycle when they are ready to mate, somewhere around 50 years old. It is said they travel far inland to the streams and pools of their birth. Somehow, large numbers of them arrive at the same time, as if driven by a subconscious urging. Once the time is right, the females will congregate together in a large group. The males will be just upstream. A great battle will ensue for the males. They will fight savagely with each other and most will die. A small group will emerge victorious and join the females in a mating ritual that lasts for less than a day. The bodies of those fallen help to feed the new hatchlings. The adults move on and leave the young to fend for themselves as they swim downstream to the sea. These young are nearly indistinguishable from fish, and do not gain sentience, or assume humanoid form for many years. Eventually, they too will return to their spawning grounds to perform the ritual again. Males who have participated in more than one mating ritual are rare and highly regarded among their people.

Our Big bad is based on **The Sibriex.**

Or The Mouth of the God Eater. This is a force of nature that feeds on magic. Divine magic, is a far more powerful food however, and Siberix appears near where divine magic can be found and seeks it out to.consume it. As it searches, it unmakes.the reality around it, and puts it back together in horrifying ways when no food can be found.

The Arcanian Sibriex is not to be considered from the Abyss. It is instead a reaction of Arcania to divine rights being given to the dead of the ship.

Here is an interview about it’s more traditional role in D\&D

The Arcanian Sibriex [https\://www\.youtube.com/watch?v=7uysRKW-CGw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7uysRKW-CGw)

My reason for choosing him, is because I was inspired by the smoke monster on the TV show lost. The sibriex has the ability to change and warp things around him. A place you have been, if the sibriex has been there before you return, might look incredibly different after being in its presence. People can change, monsters can get stronger, and things that people are sure of can be stripped away in terrifying ways.

Think long and hard about what it would like to be on an island with nothing but the people around you to rely on. Think about what sorts of things would stress you out. This is what the sibriex is to us as a writing tool.

He makes us question our sanity. Things don't just change. They don't even have to warp into something evil. Awena (the owl bear cub) for example was in the Sibriex’ presence, and it made her more clever.

The Mason/Urukocra event with Kyle

So, you would like to hear another story?

Let me tell you one of secrets and adventure.

Once, there was a dwarven man, But he was not a dwarf the way you know dwarves. Hiram was a brunelock. A breed of dwarf almost forgotten by time. They were mocked by other dwarves. They said brunelocks built sand castles because they didn’t understand stones. But, the desert dwarves did understand. They were masters from centuries long forgotten of the mysterious ways of the Masons of Liberty. To have patience to slowly hollow out a mountain. This is a thing any dwarf can do. But to have the ability to build the same city to flow through torrents of raging sand storms and not topple, to understand that it is not the stone shaped by chisel that makes a building, but the secrets of the deep wisdom that comes from stones of all sizes, even the tiniest speck of sand, these are the secrets of the Brunelocks. Of which, Hiram Abiff is the final survivor.

This leather skinned dwarven prospector has almost been forgotten in the bustle of building the fort. But tonight, he emerges from the quarry, to once again practice the magics of true Masonic power.

Tonight he dedicates the Cornerstone of the Governor of Roanoke Island’s house. Even now, he is pushing the perfectly scribed piece of stone that will forever be the first legacy of Arcania and the last legacy of the Brunelocks. A legacy that is available through esoteric teachings, riddles and mystical ceremonies to finally leave the line of this once great Dwarven house to seed into the ideology of Arcania forever.

Come visit Hiram’s ceremony hosted by DM Kyle tonight at 9pm and bare witness to the West rising for the secret knowledge of the East. The New World that will rise from what has been distilled from all that remains of the Old.

When in the Course of world events it becomes necessary for our people to come together despite the political bands which have connected them with another and to assume among them, the powers of dominion of the earth, the separate and equal station to which the Laws of Nature hold sway against the laws of God which hold none, we understand that this requires that they should declare the causes which impel them to the separate from the Laws of the Gods who hold no sway upon our liberty.

We hold these secret truths to be evident from the sweat of our brow,, that all are created equal, that they are endowed by the fact of their existence that so long as they live, the live with certain unalienable Rights, that among these are Life, Liberty and the pursuit of Happiness. To which we, in this rite understand that the are here personified by the Ladies Fontaine, Columbia and Cascadia. We invoke them now to secure these rights. The Governments of Cormyr, Elloden, Netheril, Pondugaul, and any who lay claim to our liberties from afar are instituted among the Divine, deriving their powers from the consent of absent and petty Gods to whom no question of their power is tolerated and it’s intolerance of those governed is now seen as a poison. We embrace that whenever any Form of Government becomes destructive to the truth and the liberty, it is the Right of the People to alter or to abolish it, and to institute new Government. The Prudence of the Lady Fontaine, indeed, will dictate that Governments long established should not be changed for light and transient causes; and accordingly The lady Columbia insists that all experience has shown that the peoples who now choose to claim her land are more disposed to suffer, while the evils of tyranny are sufferable. The Lady Cascadia insists that the right to abolishing the forms of government that have no knowledge of their people, but have become estranged by it is required. To fail to do so shall bring about the wrath by Sibriex and with it, a long train of abuses and usurpations, pursuing invariably the same end  under absolute Despotism, it is our right, it is our duty, to throw off such Government, and to provide new Guards for their future security. — Such shall be the right and natural end of  the patient sufferance of these Colonies; and such is now necessity to prophesy of.
