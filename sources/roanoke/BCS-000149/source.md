Gil wakes up as he always does, his eyes open, for a brief moment he hopes to see her face, before he realizes he is still dead.  Every day, a mixed feeling washes over him, as he is sad she has not joined him yet, happy that it means she is hopefully still alive.  Time passes oddly in the afterlife, and a person can’t ever be sure if it’s been a year or eons since they were last alive.  He always mantally marks how nice it is to be able to sleep in the sun, instead of isolated in the water.  Every day the same routine, missing the only person he has truly loved, thankful they aren’t there, and then marveling at the novelty of being able to sleep on land.

He gets up, and looks around Isles of the Blessed in Thalasia, somewhere in Elysium. It’s not a bad place to end up (in fact it’s a great place to end up), and evidently he earned his place by helping that town, Roanoke.  He also is blessed to have his last day faded in his memories, and his last moments removed.  A reward for living a good life, to have the trauma of death removed.  He spent almost no time in ‘The Fugue Plane’ as a friend in Roanoke cast a spell that helped him get judged quickly.  Ever since then, he has been trapped on these islands, unable to get any real news of what happened in the world of the living.

He was surprised to find that wars break out in the realms of the dead.  As he can’t leave the islands, there isn’t much he can do to help, except, some of the celestial beings have learned they can come to him and request berries.  At some point, word in the afterlife got around, due to a faceless bard, that there was one among the dead that could turn the tides of a battle with a simple fruit, and a whole lot of faith.  Some of the denizens of the good realms sought him out.  In the afterlife Sandiberries evidently are closely related to the fruit of Arcadia, and have extra potency and effect.  So Archons and Celestials will on occasion make their way to Gil to get aid in their eternal war on devils and demons.

He spends what feels like lifetimes alone, helping people, but never really connecting, his new body more elemental, his heart always hopeful that someday, he will see her again.

So on this day, he awoke as he normally does, Osiris came to him.  Without thinking, Gil creates a bag of good berries.  The god says “I am not here for those, although I will take them if you are offering.  Instead I have a question for you.  Your home realm is going to face another threat.  A friend of yours, one of my followers named Kiln, spoke highly of you, and so I have decided to make you an offer: would you like a second chance at living, to save people you no longer know?  Time has passed, and although your life has not been forgotten, you are long dead.”

Gil thought about it, but realized he has never turned down helping people, he says “Of course I will help… show me the way”

Osirus pulls out a flask “This is full of the ashes of a phoenix that died of old age, and the tears of the same one after being reborn.  Drink it all, and I will take you to where you need to be”

Gil drinks from the flask, and his vision narrows, his soul ignites on fire.  He feels like he is drowning, even though he has never drowned.
