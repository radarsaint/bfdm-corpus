**Lilia’s Ending** 

With the fallen 15 Gods restored, Lilia fully feels a part of this community. She continues her work  both as Florians second and as a servant of the Raven Queen. Working alongside Florian and Florian Chadpell, and helping lead the other knights gives her purpose for a while. She leads them on excursions, always works to build relations with the local tribes, and works in honor of the Gods. Lilia stays close to her fellow knights, because she considers them to be her family now.   
 After sometime, Lilia resigns as Coppers second, she decides he is a fine leader and will always be there to give him advice. This doesn’t keep her from visiting  him often, after all she now considers him family, too. And when she does visit, she usually has a basket of baked goods with her from Gooses bakery. She will enjoy her time with Copper, always happy to see him. Lilia will invite Cato over for tea almost every morning if he is willing. She will visit Rigatony at his castle. She will stay close to Viola, as always. When Wynne and Note have their baby, she will offer to babysit for them. She will watch Leaf and Rockman start a goblin tribe on the island. She will watch as others leave, or stay, or comeback. Always giving them a momento, a ravens feather pin, to remember her and this town.  
After 12 years, Florian will step down and Lilia will become the new Eclipse Knight. She will proudly step up as leader of the Knights here on the island. As people come and go on the island she will be here to welcome them and see them off. She will also move into the Chateau during this time, and will permanently turn her home into a sanctuary for the Crows and Ravens of the island. It will act as a place to leave offerings and mementos for the Raven Queen. In due time, she too will pass on her title to the next Eclipse Knight, but not before growing it into a place honored by many, for their work in protecting the people and Gods of this world.   
Lilia spends her time with Roanoke very happily, she watched from it’s messy beginning grow into a thriving town. She stays with the family she found in the roots of this town, and when she does finally die, it isn’t with sadness. Upon Lilia's death, her funeral rites will be carried out by Leaf. Her coffin will be pulled by Rush, the goodest boi. The goblin pair with whatever offspring would spread the tale of Lilia. While her body stays on the island, her soul will be sent to the Raven Queen. Back in the Shadowfell once again, Lilia will be reincarnated as Malkyn, a Kenku. Perhaps this time she will accept the Raven Queens boon and become a warlock for her, but her story is yet to be written and there are more memories to collect. Lilia’s story isn’t over.
