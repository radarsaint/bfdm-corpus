**Roanoke Crafting**

You only have one crafting roll per day. Use it wisely.

To search for basic supplies in the wild, Roll a d20, DC10. On a success you find one of the basic supply you were searching for. On a failure, you find nothing and wasted the roll. On a natural 20 you found it almost immediately. You gain the item and do not use up your roll. On a nat 20 gain 2 instead of one -b.

Many items are far more difficult to find and have a much higher DC. You can search for them yourself, or recruit the help of others. You can assist another character. Spend your roll to grant them advantage on their roll. (Do Rules like lucky and divination magic apply?)

You may be more proficient in gathering certain types of items. The list of jobs details what bonuses you gain when looking for certain items.

**Jobs:**

There are two main types of jobs, harvesters and crafters. Harvesters specialize in gathering resources from the wild. Crafters specialise in crafting finished items, but need resources to do so. The DC for basic harvesting and crafting is 10.

<span class="mark">Examples:</span>

<span class="mark">Aethan wants to make a wand of magic missile. The GM rules that to do so he must do the following:</span>

- <span class="mark">Gather the branch of a rare Yew tree (DC 18 rare item, bonuses from lumberjack or naturalist apply)</span>

- <span class="mark">Gather one sheet of vellum (DC 10, bonuses from leatherworker)</span>

- <span class="mark">Gather one vial of rare squid ink (DC 15, bonuses from fisher)</span>

- <span class="mark">Once all three things are ready, find someone who can cast magic missile. They must expend 4 castings of the spell into the items as you craft it.</span>

- <span class="mark">Roll one last crafting roll (DC10). On success, you craft a wand of magic missile with 1d4+1 charges. Once the charges are used up, it is just a useless stick. On a failure, all the material components are lost, and you have no wand.</span>

<span class="mark">This procedure can be done by him alone. He could spend day after day making crafting rolls, hoping to get high numbers, or he could enlist the help of friends. He knows that Mad Jack spends a lot of time in the forest. He trades Jack a potion for help finding the yew branch. Aethan spends his crafting roll that day to help Jack by giving him advantage. Jack rolls a 4+5=9 for the first roll and a 12+5=17 for the second roll. So close. Their search turns up empty, and both have used their crafting roll for the day. Not to be thwarted so easily, He seeks out the help of Polly, the naturalist. Polly thinks he knows where a plant like that might be located. Aethan promises to make Polly a potion of his choice as soon as the wand is done. Polly agrees and goes looking for the rare item. He spends his crafting roll looking in the forest 14+10=24. Polly finds the rare stick and gives it to Aethan. The next day Aethan trades for a pelt and tries his luck making vellum. Natural 20! He manages to make the vellum quickly with enough for two sheets and still has his crafting roll. He trades another potion for the fisherman’s roll 14+5=19. He now has all the ingredients he needs. He wants to take every precaution, so he spends one more potion to have Tabs help him. She spends her roll to give him advantage. He rolls a 2 and a 16. Success. He now has a wand of magic missiles.</span>
