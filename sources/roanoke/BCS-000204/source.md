**The Urucokra**  
Touch not the ground. Sing not to the wingless.Break not the canopy lest the folly of the muddied feet infect the Aerie. See not their trouble. For the ground is below the canopy. Know not their songs. For we are of the Aether.   
\~The Canticle of the Canopy

 (Arcanian Cryptid)

**The keepers of the Carion.**  
The Vulture-like humanoids of Roostloft are the most isolated of the nations of Roanoke. They are also by far the most feared and powerful. Roostloft is nearly inaccessible except through their servants the Halfwudgie. Their arrival evokes fear and wonder. Some say many Urukocra aren’t even native to the Old World or the New. Legend says that they hail from a world beyond from a cloudbound kingdom called The Aether.  
   
They are warriors, priests and poets bound by an understanding of the unending cycle of life and death.  
   
**The Face of Death.**  
Urucokra make the business of death their sacred charge regardless of who dies or where. Their presence is marked by the superstition of outsiders and fear. It is well known that the Urucokra eat the dead of the island, creatures and humanoids alike.   
   
From below, Urukocra look much like large vultures. Only when they descend to roost on a branch or walk across the ground does their humanoid appearance become apparent. Though they are unable to completely stand upright, Urukocra might reach 5 feet tall, and they have long, narrow legs that end in sharp talons.  
   
Dark blue, black and purple feathers cover their bodies. Their plumage typically displays rank within a particular tribe. Males are more darkly colored. Females have more vibrant colors, or can occasionally be pure white. Their heads complete the vulture like appearance, being completely featherless

   
   
**Urukocra hidden names**  
Urukocra have two names. The secret name given to them by their tribe, and the face name they earn when they come of age. Only a mate or some one to whom a life debt is owed may demand to know their secret names.  
   
**Urucokra face names.**  
Blazing star, Wind trace, Wing mother, Night Flyer, Reed Stalker, Face Puncher all follow conventional Urucokra naming conventions.  
**Urucokra traits**  
You have several traits in common with all other Urucokra  
   
**Ability Scores**  
Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Intelligence score increases by 1\.

**Age**  
Urukocra reaches maturity by age 13\. Urukocra don’t usually live longer than 45 years.

**Alignment**  
Most Urukocra are neutral or evil and are almost exclusively extremely lawful. 

**Size**  
Urukocra are about 5 feet tall. They have thick, hunched muscular bodies but are deceptively lightweight. Your size is medium.  
   
**Speed**  
You have a walking speed of 20 feet. Your body was not built for the ground. You walk with a hunched posture and a waddling gait.

**Glide**  
Your wingspan is much larger than the typical winged humanoid. They are large enough to slow your fall or allow you to glide. When you fall and aren't incapacitated, you can subtract up to 100 feet from the fall when calculating falling damage, and you can move up to 2 feet horizontally for every 1 foot you descend.

**Flight**  
At level 5 your ability to use your wings will finally be mastered. You have a flying speed of 60 feet. To use this speed, you can’t be wearing armor. In Armor, you may only glide with your wings.

**Talons.**  
Your talons are natural weapons, which you can use to make unarmed strikes. If you hit with them, you deal slashing damage equal to 1d4 \+ your Strength modifier, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike. At level 6 this will be considered a magical attack.

**Cryptid Carnivore Intuition**  
You have proficiency in two of the following skills of your choice: Animal Handling, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Perception, and Survival.

   
   
**The Cover of the Canopy.**  
While in the terrain types: forest, grassland, mountain and hill, ignore difficult terrain restrictions..  
   
**Languages**  
You can speak broken Common, Auran, Coastal Arcanian and are fluent to speak, write and read in the Urucokran native language whose name is a whistle too high pitched to be heard by most humanoids.

**The Jackalopes.**  
Ostar, High priestess of the Lagowarren   
(an underground village to the north east of Roanoke Island) takes daily walks in the high grass fields that the Jackalopes tend to prefer. The top of her head stands a mere 3 feet from the ground. But with her pink and grey ears rising up her height is extended by another foot. Most jackalopes wear their ears down when calm, but they can be quite animated when they are feeling strongly about something.-The Naturalist JG Fitzsimmons

(Arcanian Cryptid)

**Somewhat Unreliable.**  
These diminutive and short lived cryptids thrive in the woods and typically have very large families. While many are lost to the brutal nature of Arcania, a successful family (especially those that excel in the use of magic, or the roguish arts) can sometimes have families whose members number in the hundreds. 

With a life span that rarely passes 35 they tend to live in the moment and are not always careful planners, instead relying on their instincts and abilities instead of wisdom.

**A plague of good cheer.**  
Jackalopes have integrated with colonies much quicker than the other native races. They understand the idea of trade, and are always seeking short term advantages. They have found that playing the fool can be a way to endear them to the newcomers. But a careful eye will notice that a Jackalope always has an angle to work. It's common to find them in taverns acting as bards or busking as street performers. It is also common to find your pockets a lot lighter than you intended if you happen to spend too much time with one.

**Cute and cunning.**  
It may be that their non-threatening appearance has opened doors for the Jackalope that other races may not notice, or perhaps more easily forgive their natural avarice. But a Jackalope will only share their deepest desires or secrets with someone they trust profoundly.

**Jackalope Names:**  
Splayfoot, Thwentwick, Spurshin, Floofin, Hopburr, Sootlyn, Boopwent, Whifour, Harkness. 

**Jackalope Traits:**  
Your Jackalope has a number of traits in common with all other Jackalope.

**Ability Score increase.**   
Your Dexterity increases by two. Your Charisma increases by one

**Age.**  
Jackalope rapidly ages in comparison to other races, reaching adulthood at the age of 7 and generally lives no more than 40 years.

**Alignment.**  
Most Jackalope lean toward a neutral alignment. Although they have a propensity for avarice and self preservation, they are usually very outgoing and talkative. It can, at times be very difficult to get to know most Jackalopes, as they tend to answer most questions with a question of their own. You know?

**Size.**  
Your size is small. Jackalopes average a height of about 3 foot. Jackalopes have ears with vestigial muscles that can lay flat, or stand up and can swivel, and powerful oversized feet. With their oversized feet extended and their ears up, they can usually extend themselves by standing on their toes and have a toe-to-ear-tip height of about 5 feet. Jackalopes have advantage on perception checks having to do with hearing.

**Speed.**  
Jackalopes are amongst the quickest of creatures with a base movement speed of 30ft and your long jump is up to 20 feet and your high jump is up to 10 feet, with or without a running start.

**Prairie Dweller.**   
Living on the plains of Arcania has taught you to be ever vigilant, you gain advantage on perception checks using hearing. You also gain a burrowing speed equal to half your movement speed.

**Be Nimble. Be Quick.**  
You can move through the space of any creature that is a size larger than yours.

**Burst of Healing**  
As a bonus action, you can draw power from your warren to spend one of your Hit Dice and revitalize yourself or a creature you touch. Once you use this trait, you can’t use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Laughing at death**  
When you make a death save, you may instead spend one of your remaining hit die to heal yourself back into the fight. However, this also permanently fails one death save per instance of your character being rendered unconscious until your next long rest.

**Language**  
You speak, read and write common as well as Woodland Arcanian.

**Writing Credit:** Roanoke RPG  
**Art Credit:** Max Redfern

**The Chinnokin.**  
Most Chinnokin live out in the open ocean. But some tribes choose to live closer to the shallow waters near shore and trade with land dwellers. Seafaring vessels happen upon tribes of Chinnokin from time to time. They are usually friendly and eager to trade their treasures of the deep for the sailors goods, but some tribes have been known to be hostile to sailors.

**Running of the Chinnokin**  
Chinnokin are seldom seen on land, but occasionally their curiosity gets the better of them, and they may take up residence in coastal cities. The main exception to their ocean dwelling life is when a Chinnokin has reached a point in their life cycle when they are ready to mate, somewhere around 50 years old. 

The Salmon folk. This coastal society has recently fallen into decline. While most of their society was dedicated to preserving life, the fall of the \*\*Arcanian Barrier\*\* has become a heavy weight on their shoulders that now drives them to desperate measures to protect their way of life. 

**Chinnokin Race Details**

After the storm subsided, our ship was run aground on a sandbar. As we waited for the tide to lift us, strange creatures could be seen playing in clear water below. Some of the men befriended them, and soon we were trading trinkets and food. They called themselves Chinnokin. They were friendly enough, but they seemed to assume everything we owned was fair game for trade. Only time will tell the true nature of these strange people, but I count it a good omen that we met them when we did.   
\~Admiral Timmond  
*(Arcanian Cryptid)* 

The Chinnokin are a race of aquatic creatures who travel the oceans in small tribes or alone. 

**Sleek and Playful**  
With their fishy features and scaly skin, these creatures are more at home in water than on land. They are slightly shorter than humans on average, ranging from well under 5 feet tall to just over 6 feet. They seem to grow larger as they age and don't always stop at adulthood. They are fairly slim, weighing only 100 to 145 pounds. Males and females are about the same height and weight.

Chinnokin range in color from dark green to bright red, and many have a combination of both colors. Some have a shimmering silver or rainbow colored stripe running down their side.

**Tribal Living**

It is said they travel far inland to the streams and pools of their birth. Large numbers of them arrive at the same time, as if driven by a subconscious urging. Once the time is right, the females will congregate together in a large group. And a great battle will ensue for the males. They will fight fiercely with each other. Most will die. A small group of victors will join the females in a ritual that lasts less than a day. The bodies of those fallen help to feed the new hatchlings. The adults move on. And the young fend for themselves as they swim to the sea. These young are nearly indistinguishable from fish, and do not gain sentience, or assume humanoid form for up to three years. Eventually, they too will return to their spawning grounds to perform the ritual again. Chinnokin who have participated in more than one mating ritual are rare and highly regarded among their people.

**Chinnokin Names**  
About 5 years after reaching the sea, Chinnokin children begin transforming into their adult form. They gradually increase in intelligence and seek out others of their kind. The tribe that accepts them will give them a name. They will also take on the name of their tribe as their family name.

**Chinnokin Traits**  
You are at home in freshwater, salt water, and you even venture onto land on occasion.\*\*Gain the ability to breath both on land and underwater.\*\*  

**Ability Score Increase**  
Your Dexterity score increases by 2\. Your Constitution score increases by 1\.

**Age**  
Chinnokin live to be around 200 years old, but many die far sooner from dangers in the sea, or on their quest to mate.

**Alignment**  
Chinnokin ply the open oceans and love their freedom. They are content living a carefree life. Then understand well that each moment may be their last, and thus are often chaotically good aligned. 

**Size**  
Chinnokin range from under 5 to over 6 feet tall and have slender builds. Your size is Medium.

**Speed**  
Your base walking speed is 30 feet, and your swim speed is 30 feet.

**Darkvision**  
Accustomed to the darkness of the deep sea, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can’t discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.  
**Water Shaper**  
As a child of the sea, you can call on the elemental magic of water. You know the cantrips Dehydrate and Shape Water. At level five also gain access to the Wall of Water spell. Once you cast one of these spells with this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Dehydrate**  
*Cantrip*  
\_\_  
\- **Casting Time:** 1 action  
\- **Range:** 60ft  
\- **Components:** S,M (A few Grains of sand)  
\- **Duration:** 1 minute (requires Concentration)   
\- **School:** Necromancy   
\- **Attack/Save:** CON save  
\- **Damage/Effect:** Necrotic

You absorb the moisture within a target, causing them to take damage as they are quickly dried. The target must succeed a Constitution saving throw, or take 1d8 Necrotic damage immediately, and at the end of each of their turns. The spell ends as soon as the target succeeds on their saving throw.  
This spell's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).

**Shape Water**  
*Cantrip*  
\_\_  
\- **Casting Time:** 1 action  
\- **Range:** 30ft  
\- **Components:** S  
\- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You choose an area of water that you can see within range and that fits within a 5-foot cube. You manipulate it in one of the following ways:  
You instantaneously move or otherwise change the flow of the water as you direct, up to 5 feet in any direction. This movement doesn’t have enough force to cause damage.

You cause the water to form into simple shapes and animate at your direction. This change lasts for 1 hour.  
You change the water’s color or opacity. The water must be changed in the same way throughout. This change lasts for 1 hour.  
You freeze the water, provided that there are no creatures in it. The water unfreezes in 1 hour.  
(*If you cast this spell multiple times, you can have no more than two of its non-instantaneous effects active at a time, and you can dismiss such an effect as an action).*

Wall of Water  
*3rd-level evocation*  
\_\_  
\- Casting Time: 1 action  
\- Range: 60 feet  
\- Components: V, S, M (a drop of water)  
\- Duration: 10 minutes

You create a wall of water on the ground at a point you can see within range. You can make the wall up to 30 feet long, 10 feet high, and 1 foot thick, or you can make a ringed wall up to 20 feet in diameter, 20 feet high, and 1 foot thick. The wall vanishes when the spell ends. The wall’s space is difficult terrain.

Any ranged weapon attack that enters the wall’s space has disadvantage on the attack roll, and fire damage is halved if the fire effect passes through the wall to reach its target. Spells that deal cold damage that pass through the wall cause the area of the wall they pass through to freeze solid (at least a 5-foot-square section is frozen). Each 5-foot-square frozen section has AC 5 and 15 hit points. Reducing a frozen section to 0 hit points destroys it. When a section is destroyed, the wall’s water doesn’t fill it.

**Temperate Aquatic Habitat:**  
As long as you have no levels of exhaustion, you have resistance to cold damage, and have advantage on any saving throw involving cold damage

**Soak**  
Chinnokin prefer to sleep submerged in water. When you take a long rest in water, you regain an extra hit die. In addition, you recover two levels of exhaustion instead of one. If you take a long rest out of water, gain one level of exhaustion up to the 4th level. You can not progress further in levels of exhaustion (death) due to this feature.

**Languages**  
You can speak, read, and write Coastal Arcanian.

**The Halfwudgie**  
Rising up, Riet Barreldame stood tall.   
"For the first time, \*she noticed the way the Uru oppressors hunched\*, the way their featherless faces reflected the \*\*death\*\* they worshiped. Uncowed, unbroken, unbent, she was the first to free herself. Riet had no trainer. Reit had no master. Her fists were forged in the Urucokra’s brick baking kiln. Countless hours of watching her own body be carved into shape like a river flowing over stone culminated in a moment of true strength. That day, standing tall, she reached her hands into the kiln. Her mind became clear, and she felt her ki flow.   
So begins the tale of our champion, Riet, the monk of the hollow."   
\~Ohma Claylore, Elder of Halfwudgie Hollow.

 *(Arcanian Cryptid)*

**Brick making so long, clay runs in their blood.**  
Halfwudgies are an oppressed people just in the beginnings of revolt against their Urucokran taskmasters. There is no written history amongst the Halfwudgie. But, oral tradition and myth are central to their faith and identity. They are strong, and enduring, and they have no one to look to for their salvation. Save, the courage they muster to defend themselves against their winged enemies.

Halfwudgies, like their cryptid cousins the Puckwudgies, are passive until threatened. Their grooming and clothing are habitually humble. Their family bond is extremely close. Halfwudgies are notoriously untrusting of strangers, seeing them as dangerous. Although these gentle giants aren’t aggressive, they are experts in defense. When faced with possible combat, the Halfwudgie raises the quills on its body and releases them as natural weapons.

Brown, black and grey striped quills form from their skulls where hair would be on almost any other humanoid. At 12 feet, male Halfwudgies tend to be shorter and stockier with quills that are far longer and more ornate than females. Female puckwudgies are taller, sometimes as tall as 15 feet with a more humanoid and less beastial look to them.

**Servants of the longshadow.**  
Any halfwudgie who has ever served the Urucokra make a habit of keeping their eyes averted, and toward the ground. This is a brilliant strategy born of necessity. To their oppressors, the halfwudgie seem cowed. But, as any prey knows, a bird who stalks from the skies always casts a long shadow. This is a twofold philosophy. It gives the appearance of appeasement, while actively seeking to take any advantage. And it represents a cunning and patient mind.

**The hymn of the hills.**  
The oral tradition of the Halfwudgie is told in song most often. Every night in the typical Halfwudgie home, the families will go to the windows and doors of their homes and sing the songs their parents taught them. They sing of hope and freedom, of despair and of pain. And then begins the song of retribution. A song that fills the night with a sound that may be heard from the mountain tops of Roostloft. When the halfwudgie sing their songs together, it is said the ground itself quakes with their story. \*\*Gain Proficiency in Performance.\*\* Additionally your Cryptid ancestry grants you Primal Intuition. \*\*Gain proficiency\*\* in two of the following skills of your choice: Animal Handling, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Perception, and Survival.

**Halfwudgie names.**  
Male Halfwudgies take adjectives as first names. Both sexes take their clan name as their surnames. Female Halfwudgies are typically named by an elder female on their twentieth birthday from a preset list of traditional names. Previous to this they are called by the names of local plant life.  
Male names: Screeching Siltbloom, Mammoth Mournmore, Chonkers Gravelguild, Obnoxious Mincemaker, Wise Chaffscraper or Dunce Soresalve.  

Juvenile Female names: Mantrap Boatbottom, Potato Creekhovel, Orchid Blunderbluff.  
Adult Female names (*you may only choose from this list of traditional names if you were raised in a halfwudgie community*). Melody, Harmony, Chastity, Faith, Hope or Verity  
**Halfwudgie traits**

You have several traits in common with all other Halfwudgies.

**Ability Score Increase**  
Your Wisdom score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1\.

**Age**  
Enslaved or former slave Halfwudgies reach adulthood at the age of 12 and live up to 30 years.  
Freeborn Halfwudgies reach adulthood at the age of 19 and can live upto 75 years.

**Alignment**  
The Halfwudgie is incapable of any alignment other than good. 

**Size**  
Your size is medium. At level 5, your cryptid ancestry causes you to make the champions change and grow. At level 5 your physical mass becomes greater giving you a more powerful build. You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can Push, Drag or Lift.

**Speed**  
Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

**Courage**   
Once per turn, when you succeed on an attack roll against an enemy that is within 5 feet of you, and no allies are within 5 feet of you or your target, you deal \+1d6 damage. 

**Cryptid Defense.**

**Quill Spear:**  
At level one, your quills are particularly effective weapons. You can, as a bonus action, pull a long quill off your person and use it as a weapon. It counts as a javelin that only you are proficient in, and lasts until you take a long rest, at which time it is damaged and cannot be used anymore. At level 5, this quill counts as a magic weapon for the purposes of overcoming damage resistance. You may pull a quill out like this four times and you regain all uses after a long rest.  
A second ability becomes available at level four:   
**Quill Barrage:** You shake off your loose quills and shoot them at your enemies. When you do so each target in a 15 ft cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. The DC for this saving throw equals 8 \+ your Constitution modifier \+ your proficiency bonus. A creature takes 1d8 piercing damage on a failed save as well as suffering a \-1 on all saving throws until the end of your next turn, and half as much damage on a successful one (and no penalty to their saving throws).The damage increases to 2d8 at 6th level, 3d8 at 11th level, and 4d8 at 16th level. After you use Quill Barrage, you can’t use it again until you complete a short or long rest.  
At level 5 gain 2d4 quills per day. 

**Languages**  
You can speak (but not read or write) Coastal Arcanian and Wudge.

**The Tatankan**  
The Majesty of the Tatankan cannot be understated. Whether you meet one up close, or see the great herd from miles away, these Plainsmen are likely to leave a lasting impression. Their cities have no names that they have assigned to them. Any local designation is made by outsiders and referring to the city as any name other than “the Pen” is considered uncultured and boorish.  
*(Arcanian Cryptid)*

**Tatankan Race Details.**  
The herd is the core of life amongst the Tatankan. Rarely alone, the Tatankan people are very conscious of customs and decorum. Within your herd it is considered impolite to move faster than the slowest member, so as not to reveal who might be weak or sick or likely to be picked off by enemies. The Tatankan are often misinterpreted as being dull or dim witted because they are brought up to move slowly, to be cautious and watchful. They are ever vigilant and care deeply about whatever causes they devote themselves to. 

**The Buffalo who Buffalo at Buffalo in Buffalo.**  
It is a cultural attitude to keep quiet about both advantage and weakness. Their presence is menacing to their enemies and a relief to their allies. It is only a fool who would sit down at a card table to play a game of skill or chance against a group of Tatankans.

**A flame ignited.**   
Sorcery is considered sacred amongst the Tatankan. Those who find it within themselves to be clever or powerful spellcasters without studying books or invoking a pact with a god or demon, or drawing their power from the balance of nature, Sorcerers are considered to be shaman, regardless of race. The First Tatankan was said to once have been a sorcerer who could change his shape at will, and it is customary to make an offering of milk and wheat to any sorcerer you may encounter to honor him.

**Tatankan Traits:**  
You have several traits in common with all other Tatankan.

**Ability Score Increase**  
Your Strength score increases by 1, and your Constitution score increases by 2\.

**Age**  
While a large portion of their adolescent  life is often devoted to a nomadic lifestyle, none of the members of a Juvenile Herd are considered adults until all of the members are mature enough to graduate into adulthood together. Typically this happens between the ages of 40 and 60, with Tatankans often living until they are 220\. A mature Tatankan may leave her pen but many choose to stay with the members of their Juvenile herd. Departing from the herd is considered to be an extreme lifestyle. Departing early from a Juvenile herd is considered to be obscene.

**Alignment**  
Despite their penchant for gambling, the Tatankan believe in the law of the herd. Though it is not a written law, and can change from region to region, it is very rare to find a Tatankan who is not Lawful Good.  
   
**Size**  
Tatankan are between 6 and 8 feet tall and weigh between 300 and 400 pounds with 1 to 2 foot horns if any. Your size is Medium.

**Speed**  
Your speed is 15ft. However if 2 or more of your party members are dashing in the same direction you may take 60 feet of movement as a dash. 

**Enhanced smell**  
You can find any party member within 30 feet despite any visual restrictions and conditions that do not impair smell.

**Powerful Build**  
You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.

**Gentle Reputation**  
Due to their massive build being paired with a cultural reputation for pacifism, the reputation of all Tatankan gains your character proficiency in deception and perception. 

**Gore**  
A Tatankan can make an attack with his horns, causing 1d8 piercing damage plus his Strength modifier.  If the target is a creature of the same size or smaller, it must succeed on a (DC 12 \+ your Strength modifier) Strength saving throw or be knocked back 5 feet and knocked prone (if applicable). Additionally if any of your party members become prone, restrained, grappled or incapacitated, as a reaction you may charge up to a maximum of 60  feet in order to make a gore attack with your horns.

**Language**  
You can speak, write and read Common. You can speak Tatankan. At 8th level  you can tie Tatankan story knots and interpret them correctly.
