**New DM Function:** Group Checks

Using a Group Check, the DM calls for the whole party to attempt a skill check. If at least half succeed, the whole party succeeds. For challenge variety assume 20% increase (70-75%)for difficult and 40% increase for hard tasks (90-95%). For enacting things like quick group fights or skills like group contests.
