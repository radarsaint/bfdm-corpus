**<u>Chinnokin Story line:</u>**

**[<u>The Fishmother</u>](https://imgur.com/a/X92EAA0) (Art)**

The Fishmother is the keeper of the Natatorium. The central aspect of which is an ancient temple beneath the waves of the Atlantic. Surrounded by a forest of Kelp, It can be seen from the sky, glowing with bioluminescent coral which outlines the stone structures. The Fishmother is a heavy set female Chinnokin. She is also one of the very few original citizens of the fallen underwater city Mantopolis still alive. The Fishmother is ancient, huge, and largely sedentary.

It is the Fishmother who implants Oleander's phylactery in the body of the levialich.

Her arrival was heralded by prophecy (see below under **Nautasha**). For a year or more, the former Chinnokin Chieftain (named Tigerlily) stepped down at the request of her priests and wisemen. Shortly after the Fishmother took her place as high priestess, tigerlily went missing.

**Nautasha (art)** [<u>(Prophesy)</u>](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16Tj1IpASTLJcXRRsdVY0IZ3d5T_YQbj0Ssyw6hRUehs/edit?usp=sharing)

A Chinnokin

Nautasha was raised to be a handmaiden to the high priestess of the Natatorium. She is bold, brash, and unwilling to accept the changes that her elders have adopted. She (not unlike Empty Hill) has been exiled from her home. But, this suits her needs as she searches for the Truth behind the Fishmother.
