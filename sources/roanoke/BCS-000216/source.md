**The keepers of the Carion**  
The vulture like humanoids of Roostloft are the most isolated of the nations of Roanoke. They are also by far the most feared and powerful. Roostloft is nearly inaccessible except through their servants the Halfwudgie. Their arrival evokes fear and wonder. Some say many Urukocra aren’t even native to the Old World or the New. Legend says that they hail from a world beyond from a cloudbound kingdom called The Aether.

They are warriors, priests and poets  bound to the idea of an unending cycle of life and death.

**The face of death.**  
Urucokra make the business of death their sacred charge regardless of who dies or where. Their presence is marked by the superstition of outsiders and fear. It is well known that the Urucokra eat the dead of the island, creatures and humanoids alike. 

From below, Urukocra look much like large vultures. Only when they descend to roost on a branch or walk across the ground does their humanoid appearance become apparent. Though they are unable to completely stand upright, Urukocra might reach 5 feet tall, and they have long, narrow legs that end in sharp talons.

Dark blue, black and purple feathers cover their bodies. Their plumage typically displays rank within a particular tribe. Males are more darkly colored. Females have more vibrant colors, or can occasionally be pure white. Their heads complete the vulture like appearance, being completely featherless

**Servants of Fontaine.**  
Nowhere are the Urucokra more comfortable than in the canopy where Fontaine’s voice is most easily heard in the breezes and zephyrs. They can spend hours in the treetops gliding from branch to branch.. In battle, they prove dynamic and acrobatic fliers, able to move through plant life with remarkable speed and grace, ambushing their opponents and baiting their enemies to chase them. Once treebound, an Urukocra leaves the canopy  with reluctance. 

**Scavenger Mannerisms**  
The resemblance of Urukocra to Vultures isn’t limited to physical features. Urukocra display many of the same mannerisms as ordinary vultures, scavenging the dead to eat.  They are fastidious about their cleanliness, frequently frequently invoking a ritual cleaning called the speckless remittance. Often, if an uru notices that they have been soiled, they will not do anything else until they clean themselves with water blessed in the name of Fontaine by their shaman.  
Many Uru  punctuate their speech with odd phrases that are mistranslations of their secret language “Wind tongue”.

**Urukocra hidden names**  
Urukocra have two names. The secret name given to them by their tribe, and the “beak name” they earn when they come of age. Only a mate or some one to whom a life debt is owed may demand to know their secret names.

**Uru beak names:**  
Blazing star, Wind trace, Wing mother, Night Flyer, Reed stalker, Face Puncher all follow conventional Urucokra naming conventions.

Backgrounds that are most appropriate for Urucokra are Acolyte, Folk Hero and Sage.

**Urukocra Traits**  
As an Urukocra, you have certain traits in common with your people. Being able to fly at high speed starting at 1st level is exceptionally effective in certain circumstances and exceedingly dangerous in others. 

**Ability Score Increase**  
Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Intelligence score increases by 1\.

**Age**  
Urukocra reaches maturity by age 13\. Compared to humans, Urukocra don’t usually live longer than 45 years.

**Alignment**  
Most Urukocra are neutral or evil and are almost exclusively extremely lawful. 

**Size**  
Urukocra are about 5 feet tall. They have thick, hunched muscular  bodies but are deceptively lightweight. Your size is medium.

**Speed**  
Your base walking speed is 20 feet.

**Flight**  
You have a flying speed of 60 feet. To use this speed, you can’t be wearing armor. In Armor, you may only glide with your wings.

**Glide**  
Your wingspan is much larger than the typical winged humanoid. They are large enough to slow your fall or allow you to glide. When you fall and aren't incapacitated, you can subtract up to 100 feet from the fall when calculating falling damage, and you can move up to 2 feet horizontally for every 1 foot you descend.

**Talons**  
Your talons are natural weapons, which you can use to make unarmed strikes. If you hit with them, you deal slashing damage equal to 1d4 \+ your Strength modifier, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.

**The cover of the Canopy.**  
While in the following terrain types gain the following features: Forest,Grassland and Hill. Quarter cover counts as half. Ignore difficult terrain restrictions. Gain advantage on stealth.

**Languages**  
You can speak broken Common, Auran, Coastal Arcanian and are fluent to speak, write and read in the Urucokran native language whose name is a whistle too high pitched to be heard..
