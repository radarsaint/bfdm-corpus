#### Somewhat Unreliable: 
These diminutive and short lived cryptids thrive in the woods and typically have very large families. While many are lost to the brutal nature of Arcania, a successful family (especially those that excel in the use of magic, or the roguish arts) can sometimes have families whose members number in the hundreds. 


With a life span that rarely passes 35 they tend to live in the moment and are not always careful planners, instead relying on their instincts and abilities instead of wisdom.


#### A plague of good cheer:
Jackalopes have integrated with colonies much quicker than the other native races. They understand the idea of trade, and are always seeking short term advantages. They have found that playing the fool can be a way to endear them to the newcomers. But a careful eye will notice that a Jackalope always has an angle to work. It's common to find them in taverns acting as bards or busking as street performers. It is also common to find your pockets a lot lighter than you intended if you happen to spend too much time with one.


#### Cute and cunning.
It may be that their non-threatening appearance has opened doors for the Jackalope that other races may not notice, or perhaps more easily forgive their natural avarice. But a Jackalope will only share their deepest desires or secrets with someone they trust profoundly.


#### Jackalope Names:
Speyfoot, Thwentwick, Spurshin, Floofin, Hopburr, Sootlyn, Boopwent, Whifour, Harkness.

### Ability Score increase
Your **Dexterity** increases by **two**


Your **Charisma** increases by **one**


##### Age
Jackalope rapidly ages in comparison to other races, reaching adulthood at the age of 7 and generally lives no more than 40 years.


##### Alignment
Most Jackalope lean toward a **neutral** alignment. Although they have a propensity for avarice and self preservation, they are usually very outgoing and talkative. It can, at times, be very difficult to get to know most Jackalopes, as they tend to answer most questions with a question of their own. You know?


##### Size
Your size is **small**. Jackalopes average a height of about 3 foot. Jackalopes have ears with vestigial muscles that can lay flat, or stand up and can swivel, and powerful oversized feet. With their oversized feet extended and their ears up, they can usually extend themselves by standing on their toes and have a toe-to-ear-tip height of about 5 feet. **Jackalopes have advantage on perception checks having to do with hearing.**

##### Speed.
Jackalopes are amongst the quickest of creatures with a base movement speed of 30ft and your long jump is up to 20 feet and your high jump is up to 10 feet, with or without a running start.


##### Prairie Dweller: 
Living on the plains of Arcania has taught you to be ever vigilant, you gain advantage on perception checks using hearing. You also gain a burrowing speed equal to half your movement speed.


##### Be Nimble. Be Quick.
**You can move through the space of any creature that is a size larger than yours.**


##### Burst of Healing
**As a bonus action, you can draw power from your warren to spend one of your Hit Dice and revitalize yourself or a creature you touch. Once you use this trait, you can’t use it again until you finish a short or long rest.**

##### Laughing at death
**When you make a death save, you may instead spend one of your remaining hit die to heal yourself back into the fight. However, this also permanently fails one death save per instance of your character being rendered unconscious until your next long rest.**

You speak read and write **common** as well as **Woodland Arcanian.**
