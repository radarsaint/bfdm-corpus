(Golden Dawn Aligned NPC) [<u>Auntie Mavis</u>](https://ddb.ac/characters/26103055/vy2Ozd)

You follow your directions to a large red victorian house with white trim. There is a short cast iron fence around a small flower garden. Somehow when you approach the clouds always seem to dissipate, bathing the garden in warm summer’s light. The smells of home cooked food mixed with the scent of rose garden give a calming sensation. Auntie Mavis always greets you at the door with a smile and a plate full of cookies. The interior of the house is well kept, if a bit worn. She proudly displays a painting of King George above her mantle and is the very picture of a modern female business owner. Her door and gate are decorated with women’s suffrage posters and ribbons.

- Serf: The Father Thames

Players share sleeping quarters 4 to a room, first come first served every evening.

You follow your directions down a tall, narrow cobblestone alleyway. Above you, laundry lines hang. Water runs through the alleyway. It flows with the waste of kitchens, chamber pots and the grime that probably feeds the things that lurk below the surface of the mighty grey Thames river.

The interior of this bar/inn is far better constructed than the exterior.
