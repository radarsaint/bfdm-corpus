Day 12. Duckton (This module is a personal project of mine to convert Paranoia into D&D terms. It will never appear in any version of the game that can be purchased at any later date.)
Duckton 2: Happiness is mandatory.

Deep into the inky black of night, a small island can be seen through the portholes. As the sub surfaces, it seems to be a lighthouse. The lighthouse has a large mallard insignia on the front that acts as a knocker. When the door is knocked. A strange whining mechanical voice says: Welcome to Duckton, now under new management. Come on in and try to win some money, buddy.  HAHAHAHA. HAHAHAHA. HAHAHAHA.

The doors open, and lamps magically begin igniting dramatically from the closest
wall to the furthest. A Bathysphere in the shape of a duck egg stands open, its door another circle of metal with a mallard insignia. The Bathysphere plays a soothing

Magical tune compelling the adventurers to venture forward. When the group is secure in the sphere it begins descending back underwater. A gas begins filling the chamber. When the group awakens, they discover that their clothing has been changed

To brown jumpsuits with green collars and a duck bill hat. Unbeknownst to the group, they have become simularums of themselves. More copies will show up. The group must pass a constitution check, Or be afflicted with the happy status. Happy characters are bound to try to follow the rules of Duckton.

Rule one.
Happiness is mandatory. Anyone not happy with their experience must be killed immediately so that a new, better, happier version of you may be given the chance to experience duckton.

Rule two.
It isn’t fun to talk about the rules. And anyone caught not having fun is evidently unhappy. And as we all know: Happiness is mandatory.

Rule three.
All hygienic standards must be followed at all times including proper care of the duck uniform. Anyone not wearing a perfectly clean and undamaged duck uniform is in violation, and cannot prove that they are a duck. And as well all know: Ducks are the happiest animal on earth.

Rule four.
Duckton is a capitalist enterprise. Evil goose communists and ganderistic activities must be reported immediately. Anyone not enjoying capitalism is clearly not having fun. And as we all know: sharing is not as fun as winning.

Rule five
Friend Beholder will always tell the truth, is always fair and always good.
(Sentence redacted due to clearance level)
And as we all know, clean guests are happy guests.

People having fun are happy people. And because happiness is mandatory, they can win a special expensive prize.

3 high elves in duck costumes outfitted in primary colored clothes greet you in duckburg

Gooey shooey and kablewie greet you with painfully forced smiles. They offer each of you chocolate eggs wrapped in colorful paper.

A 20 dc perception check will reveal that they are wearing shock collars.

The 3 characters usher you toward the haunted money pit. You stand in line for a while.
As you wait a strange metallic voice begins reciting hunting regulations for ducks.

“A special youth hunting season open to hunters under 16 years of age (must be accompanied by an adult at least 18 years old who is not hunting).
Daily Bag Limit: 7 ducks, to include not more than 2 hen mallard, 1 pintail, 3 scaup, 2 canvasback, and 2 redhead; and to include not more than 1 harlequin, 2 scooter, 2 long-tailed duck, & 2 goldeneye
Possession limit (Youth Hunting Days): Same as daily bag limit.
Possession limit (Regular Season): 21 ducks, to include not more than 6 hen mallard, 3 pintail, 9 scaup, 6 canvasback, and 6 redhead; and to include not more than 1 harlequin, 6 scoter, 6 long-tailed duck, and 6 goldeneye
Season limit: 1 harlequin.”

The ride attendant is in a costume that is an anatomically correct diagram of a duck cut in half. The haunted mansion ride is told from the perspective of a group of ducks that have been prepared for dinner.  The ghosts of the ducks dance and play through the dinner as animatronic representations of humanoid figures brutally hunt, kill, skin, prepare, and eat ducks, all with manic grins and odd mechanical laughter. The ride ends with the ghosts of ducks swimming through a giant vault of gold coins.

*Any attempt to steal the coins will result in the ride being stopped and all of the group being executed.*

As they leave, a vampire duck called “Count Duckula” bids them farewell.

The Entrance to Daffyton is oddly austere. It has a tall ornate gate with thin bars and the walkway is paved in white marble with black veining.

Here in Daffyton, our Pretendaneers are building our latest attraction, the Mallardhorn, a large mountain roller coaster that tells the story of a sleazy duck named “Howard” and his bizarre attempts to fit in with human culture.

At the end of the ride, there is a long drop leading to a splash zone water feature that covers the group in (dc 16 dex save) pond scum water.

After exiting the ride, you find yourself near some bathrooms.if searched, the bathrooms have hot air devices to dry off wet clothes. If anyone has wet clothes and isn't betrayed, the families  buying pictures will all stare at the people who are not meeting duckton hygiene standards. Speaking of which: there are several families around purchasing oil paintings of their likeness on the ride. When prompted, the sales person will offer to sell the group a group oil painting costing the group 8000 gold. If accepted it takes 15 minutes real time to complete. If it is not purchased the group will be discovered not being capitalist and drop into a spinning blade trap.
