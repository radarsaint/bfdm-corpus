Roanoke S3 V2.1

**Week 3 Passdown.**

This is the Roanoke S3 Passdown.

The purpose of this document is to note anything that you introduce to the game that might affect other dms, IE critical decisions (such as difficulty with player behavior), lore or custom items.

Please note the following suggested template:

Who you are.

The time stamp and room where the event happened.

A brief account of what happened.

Save any relevant screen caps to imgur (set it to private) then hyperlink them here.

If the issue has to do with difficulties with player behavior and you are a mod, please also note which of the DM’s were notified. If you are a notified DM, please explain your actions and their resolution.
