**The Jailer’s Daughter**

**Cast:**  
Ichabod Crane.  
Abraham Van Brunt.  
Katarina Von Tassel.   
Mother of the Bride (big energy happy squalor Mrs. Weasley)

**Sets:**  
Katarina’s cottage.  
A church in hampstead.  
A graveyard at that same church.

Prior to the game, once we have a count of players, we will set a seating chart.  
Each “table” will act as its own jury and get it’s own voice chat where they can debate what to say to the bride, how they want to deliver their opinion and who will speak to her.  
Timeline of events:

1. A murder of a lycanthrope occurs. A silver dagger was used.   
   	During the trial, zone of truth is cast, but the person who cast it was paid off to lie about the result.  
2. Ichabod is the local Jailer, he held Abraham who was convicted of the murder until the day he was hung.  Previous to this they had been friends.  
     
3. While awaiting trial, Katarina (a nurse) tended to Abraham’s grevious wounds that were used as evidence against him.  
     
4. A year passes. Ichabod has fallen in love with katarina and has been promoted to a much higher station because of his revolutionary war efforts. Katarina is now far below his station and this marriage would make her life easier, but not in an over the top way where Ichabod comes off as gross. In order for this to feel like a tough choice, both suitors must seem likeable.

The players will play as friends of the bride. They receive wedding invitations.

Narrator:

“Good evening friends and welcome to hampstead. This evening we welcome the friends and family of The Cranes and The Von Tassels.”

“Now that the wedding rehearsal is over, it is time for dinner.”

“Please announce yourselves as your enter.” (Instruct group to make introductions)

Arriving you are relieved to be indoors. The summer night is warm, and mosquitos were out in droves. There is a low hum of cicadas echoing from the surrounding woods.

Inside, a trio of bards sing softly of courtly love and virtue. 

The smell of roast lamb fills the house as you come to be seated at one of several tables.   
Baked apples filled with cinnamon and caramelized sugar are cooling in the kitchen, and several children and a three legged dog are staring at them longingly.

The Father of the Bride:

“As I look at my daughter, Katarina, I can't help but reflect on all the years I was lucky enough to watch her grow and become the beautiful woman she is today. She captured my heart on the day she was born, and I become more and more in awe of her as the years pass by. She's my little girl, my princess, and my ray of sunlight, and no matter what her age, she'll always be those things to me. But today we gather in anticipation of the joining of two families, I'm filled with pride and am confident that she will follow her heart, and that it will lead her on a wonderful journey filled with the love and happiness that can only come as man and wife. Long has she cared for those in need, and nursed them back from the brink. It is my deepest wish that she would find a husband who would be as caring to her as she has been to any of her patients.”

Open the floor to wedding toasts.

Perception check to notice that a bride’s maid is missing.

Darkness is subtle cast upon the crowd.
