**Noah Carr**

Just coming back from his post grad internship with Disney in Entertainment and as a two year veteran player of the Roanoke series, Noah has joined the production team to bring his experience in DMing, website creation, and love for great storytelling to season 3. Playing from Puyallup, WA he has been DMing for about 6 years starting with 1e, moving to AD&D, 3.5e, and now the golden child called 5e. His love for Playing and taking on the role of Dungeon Master began from watching his favorite DM, Chris Perkins, on Youtube several years ago working with Acquisitions Incorporated. The passion Chris had for the game and the imagination being the only limit sparked Noah to take up that adventure as well. Some of his favorite storytelling feats have been in the Star Wars universe, LOTR, Marvel, and currently The Dragon Prince as they explore daring adventures, meaningful developments, challenging issues, and heroic deeds. Noah is most excited to share an immersive storytelling experience that delves your characters into the atmosphere of this year’s story!

**Brendon Faulkner**

The original creator of Roanoke Season One, Brendon Faulkner has decades of experience as a dungeon master. His recent accomplishments include experimenting with the form and boundaries of what Dungeons and Dragons can be, including the 24 hour format of Roanoke, a conversion of bowling into D&D and helping shape the local community in Washington State.

He first began playing AD&D in the fourth grade. From there he explored many of the major tabletop rpg systems with games like rifts, vampire the masquerade, trinity, paranoia and shadow run. Currently he works out of his home in Tacoma Washington where both he and his wife run home games of DdD 5e. Some of his influences include Neil Gaimen's American gods, Patrick Rothfuss' king killer chronicles, stories of True Crime, and others. As a DM he has been described as "Chaotic good" and is usually seen to be on the side of the player, with a preference for improv and a flair taking your actions and exaggerating their consequences.

### **Our Moderation Team:**

**Rhys Attard**

Lives in a constant state of 1 day in the future in Queensland Australia. Where he is working on radiators by day and playing D&D in the night. Sometimes reversed if needed. Rhys is coming back for his second year of Roanoke, eager to spur the story and help create amazing and memorable moments.

He uses his wild imagination he obtained from playing D&D for about 6 years, occasionally subbing in for his DM’s home game. He would work along with them to help inspire stories and craft adventures. Rhys’ origin story (as it pertains to D&D) started one late one fateful night after attending PAX. He and his five other friends gathered together in a hotel room trying to play without any books or rules. It was in that spirit of improvisation, he fell in love with it for the first time. Out of everything that could come from modding for Roanoke, Rhys is most excited for the interactions between players. He loves falling down the rabbit hole of problems that comes with the player shenanigans that can come when dozens of people are playing at once.

**Jack Warren**

It’s much easier to tell someone else’s story than it is your own. This is a problem that has been prevalent throughout Jack’s life. from being in plays and musicals throughout school to putting on a shit eating grin while working in restaurants, it was always easier for him to present someone else. This made playing D&D an obvious choice for a hobby.

His first taste of D&D was in his late adolescence with 3.5e and the Icewind Dale games. From then on he would have the opportunity to play several times into his early 20’s, but nothing lasting more than 1 or 2 games. During this time he played 3.5e, 4e, and Pathfinder. It wasn’t until the summer of his 25th year that he found a group of friends to consistently play D&D with and shortly after was first introduced to Brendon Faulkner, who prompted him to get the Tomb of Annihilation Module.

Currently he is playing in a Descent into Avernus game and running both a Lost Mines of Phandelver game and a home game. His first season in Roanoke he played Dregen Singebeard, the grumpy dwarven blacksmith and cleric of Moradin.
