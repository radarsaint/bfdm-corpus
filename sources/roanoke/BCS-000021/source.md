**Frequently Asked Questions:**

**Q: When does the discord server go live?**

Game Play dates are Saturday July 18th 2020 - August 21st.

**Q: What do I need to play?**

An internet capable device with access to a web browser, [<u>Discord</u>](https://discordapp.com/), and it is strongly recommended that you have a [<u>D&D beyond account</u>](https://www.dndbeyond.com/). Don’t worry. Roanoke is 100% free and all of these things are easily available.

**Q: What is Roanoke?**

Practically speaking, Roanoke is a 5 week long persistent Role Play Environment that seeks to combine the improvisational storytelling ability of tabletop role playing games with the functionality and user interface of video games. This is an rp heavy town drama told in 5 acts.

Emotionally speaking, Roanoke is a 5 week long deep dive into the daily lives of your characters. The game is centered around staying as a group to build a town, grow in your professions, develop genuine story arcs and with moments both profound and hilarious. And of course you will have to overcome horrific encounters and delve into the mysteries of the secret societies to which the townsfolk belong.

**Q:How do I play?**

The discord server is laid out with rooms that correspond with locations in the game. Talented and handsome Dungeon masters will act in various locations and times depending on what the story needs. This game has a few other differences as well that we should discuss. This game moves in real time. The servers are live 24/7. And with 6+ people manning the helm you are in good hands as far as having content available to play. The last three weeks of the game are set on an island colony where you will be subjected to the rigors of daily life (our newly revamped crafting with class system) as well as certain mysterious circumstances.

In weeks 3-5 each player will be given their own chat channel to represent their home or business. They will be able to post art to describe it, and players may even choose to share their living space with another person(s).

A large portion of the day, the village has minor encounters and many players playing their roles within their own narratives. We have seen weddings (partially for the 2 week ac buff from the Ceremony spell XD), player deaths, deep and lasting friendships both in the games and in real life.

Physically, what you do to play is download Discord, if you don’t already have it. And then, once the server invitations get posted on the Facebook Group, you will be brought to the server and be assigned the role of player.

After that, you will find main player hubs and private locations (like lodgings or secret society meeting places). Every day there will be content available here, as well as space to call your own in the lodgings section.

Additionally, at various times, you will be called by one of the dungeon masters either to a public RP or to an invitational.  
  
Invitationals are where our dungeon master’s improvisational skills get to shine. A DM will call for an invitation when a player has a lore related question that they would like to pursue, or a player has managed to inspire a DM who wishes to pursue an impromptu adventure. Typically, invitationals are offered to one player who may then invite up to three friends for some smaller scale adventures that veer off the beaten path.

And finally, there are voice events. Most of the time, our dungeon masters are on voice chat somewhere on the server. But on Friday nights we come together for a voice event. Combat in large groups can be slow, but thankfully we have many tools to help us out, including a website that allows a dm to update a battle map real time (the system requires that players refresh the page, but it works well for our purposes.

**Q: What are the rules of Character creation.**

All of the published 5th edition materials from Wizards of the Coast (D&D) are permitted. <u>No unearthed arcana</u>. Only official homebrew is permitted, nothing written outside the game is allowed. Create level 2 characters, with the starting gear option. Starting gold will be discussed at a later date.

Characters should be connected to the story as a colonist currently living in London, planning on heading to the new world. Evil is an allowed alignment, however we are here to make engaging characters trying to attain their goals with similar methods (ie: coming to the new world, exploring, making a life for yourself). We aren’t here to watch murder hobos run through the forests and towns killing everything that doesn’t freely give it treasure.

We greatly encourage people to use the provided D&D beyond links in order to build and track their character. Not only is it helpful for our DM’s and mods to be able to look at your character, but our Discord bot Avrea is integrated with their website. It allows you to access the correct modifiers on your rolls without having to leave discord chat, and allows us to use it’s initiative tracker, which becomes necessary in events with massive amounts of players.

**Q:** (Ok, not really a question, but you need to know this anyways.) **How to press pause on a live game with 50 players.**

The use of an emoji as a pause function.  
The way we play allows player characters to develop in amazing ways, but for that development to happen, players must be heard! One of the biggest complaints about Discord is that there are times when it seems like participants are talking over one another. Despite intent, there are times when people can find themselves stepping on others toes. While it is possible to see when others are typing, the more verbose players, and those with the best typing skills can sometimes seem as though they are upstaging others.In the Cascadia Gamers Alliance, we understand that everyone has the same basic drive to be heard, and we feel strongly that players being heard in 5e ought to be treated as something very rarely cut short. And never cut short without purpose.

We solved for this by initiating and enforcing a simple technique we call “the pause emoji.” In Roanoke, the players use the ::raised_hand:: ✋🏿 emoji. Whenever a player has a significant amount of exposition (or is naturally a slower typist) they would simply insert ::raised_hand:: ✋ emoji, letting the other players and DM know they are working on what to say. The time taken to express a character's thoughts and actions is worth the wait. Within Discord that emoji line of text can then be edited by the player to what he/she actually wants to say in that space. Additionally, the power to go back and edit your text removes the need to add lines to the chat that are simple spelling and grammar errors.

As an etiquette, <u>it is a good idea to respect a player using the pause function, but it is not mandatory</u>, leaving room for excitable characters and people enacting a flurry of emotions. However, the DM using the freeze function (displayed by a different emoji to be revealed) should be treated as sacred. Especially if it's for time keeping or continuity reasons.

**Q: How does time work in a “real time” game.**  
Most of the dev team are located on the west coast. So the server is based around Pacific Standard Time. If it is 6 am Pacific time. Then it is 6 am in the Roanoke RPG. If a long rest takes 8 hours, then when you log off for the day, that's when your long rest occurs, where a short rest could be just an hour you pass in a tavern in game.

**Q:What happens if I want to take back something I did?**

Unfortunately with a game this big, there has to be a hard and fast about retcon. Once you do it in game, you can’t take it back. \*DM intervention will happen only if absolutely necessary to the integrity of the game.
