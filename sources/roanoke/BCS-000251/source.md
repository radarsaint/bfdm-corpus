Roanoke Season 3 Public Lore Dump

The year is 1632\. King Charles Stuart is King of England. The country is two years past the end of a violent civil war. Our London town is a wet and dreary place. But the grime covers a town full of constitution and charisma. The will to survive is marked by politeness and meryment despite the many horrors of the age. Sickness, monsters and advancements in both arcane and mundane industry equally plague the whole of the British islands.

The mystery of roanoke is now a mystery that spans generations. The original colony disappeared. The only clue to this mystery was the name "Croatoan" carved into a peculiar tree on the island's south western shore. The second colony's disappearance was equally mysterious, but with more clues. While none of the original members of the colony ever returned to England, strange tales of the men and women living amongst the mainlanders come with records of strange mutilations. According to local legends, the entire colony had a secret so great that they chose to cut their tongues out before abandoning the colony and the island, leaving it in the care of several native races.

Some 15 years after the colony collapsed, the final ship to attempt a voyage home was driven off course to the land of the huldufolk. The only witness to be able to tell the story of the voyage did not physically attend it. Rather, he was an obscure and reclusive illusionist who had sent his image and his familiar to be present when his agoraphobia prevented him from leaving his home. The tale he tells is largely considered to be the raving of a mad man about the hunger of a mad god, and a pair of laughing and dancing holy men.

This remains the last attempt at crossing. The force that drove the ship north (a seemingly endless supply of gargantuan ice breathing koi fish) guards the surface of the water tenaciously. 

London is also the home of several famously mysterious secret societies. Firstly, the Knights of the Roi du Soleil, are left over from the court of King Arthur. In a era when England and France warred so frequently that the two cultures became both symbiotic and politically poisonous to one another, the french kept alive the arthurian tradition in the tale of Le Morte de Arthur. The truth of these tales and the quest for the holy relics of the Gods lives on in these brave adventures, archeologists, and keepers of the high chivalry of days gone by.

The Order of Kairo has its roots in the second voyage of Roanoke. This stately gentleman's society is the height of British patriotism and the aristocracy's obsession with colonialism, adventure, big game hunting, and gentleman's sport or games of chance. This society might be regarded more as a private society rather than a secret, as they prefer a prestigious reputation and it is considered to be the height of the British sense of innovation and adventure to participate in their group.

The remaining two groups are considerably more ancient in origin. 

Once upon a time, there was a great city of a now all but dead race of dwarves deep in the Sahara desert. The brunelocks (or "brown haired") dwarves were nomads and masters of working sand into magical glass. They created beautiful cities and formidable citadels deep in the sand seas of north Africa. They were master builders, and the isolation provided to them by the desert allowed them to grow, unrivaled or harassed by their neighbors. Legend tells that the secrets of tenth level spells were within the grasp of their masters. And as that power became commonplace, a schism erupted within the society. The Brunelocks believed that the power to create a nation free of evil, and in theory, a peaceful and good world was within the grasp of those who built the city. The master masons believed that there was no difference between the magical and the mundane. Mundane things were just as powerful because despite the fact that it was harder to see the way in which their power affected the world, it was far more powerful. The intentions of the builders had a cumulative effect over both time and materials used. It is unclear why the society began to war. But it is clear that the sand masons became the free masons when their rivals destroyed their city, proclaiming that it was built in the wrong place and in the wrong way. A few words of their prophesy still remains to be discovered by astute scholars. "When Golden Dawn replaces red sunset above the oceans of atlantis, the dark waters shall recede, and never again will Hunger go unsatiated."

It is by way of these legends that members of both the Freemasons and the Golden Dawn are first inducted into their respective orders.

King Charles's secret keeper Sir Oliver Cromwell is rumored to be a high ranking member of the Golden Dawn. 

The Freemasons have a sanctuary for all faiths called the Roseline temple. Their leader is a mysterious 6 foot tall oddly shaped person known only as "the Archivist." Rumored to be from nepal, this is by far the most publicly accessible leader of a secret society.

A new Gnerman (Gnomish Germany) technology has provided a way to once again cross the oceans to the west. Arcania is once again open to exploration. The site of Roanoke is both a taboo and term that invites a challenge to national pride. Retaking the first colony is a symbolic challenge to a kingdom that wishes to once again become an empire. And so the Gnerman built, British funded submarine Tir Na Nog was built. Now she just needs her crew. Tickets are expensive, though some may have their fees waived by the secret societies who are all determined to have a hand in the adventure.
