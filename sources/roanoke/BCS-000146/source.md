Elevator Guantlet..

Chief Ohma tells you to “go, but go quickly, The Uru will not respect you if you cannot make it to the top, this is the right of passage given to their children on their first ascension. Fear not, if you fall and can still stand, I have a tool that can send things to the platform, I can get you back in the fight.”

Before you stand the way to roostloft, a giant freight style open air elevating platform that raises giant chains up an exoskeleton of scaffolding. And it is beginning to rise.
