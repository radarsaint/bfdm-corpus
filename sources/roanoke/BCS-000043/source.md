Every year my group runs an unusual horror game set in a fantasy retelling of early America called Roanoke. I am currently looking for DMs outside of my timezone (Pacific US & Canada) to help with players from all over the world for the next season (season 3). What roanoke is:

Rules as written: roanoke is a 5 week long persistent Role Play Environment based on d&d 5e that seeks to combine the improvisational storytelling ability of tabletop role playing games with the functionality and user interface of video games. This is an rp heavy town drama told in 5 acts. Additionally, there is an in depth job and crafting system.

Rules as intended: roanoke is a 5 week long deep dive into the daily lives of your characters. The game is centered around staying as a group to build a town, grow in your professions overcome horrific encounters and delve into the mysteries of the secret societies to which the townsfolk belong.

Our player base has currently consisted of a diverse range from Pacific West Coast of the United States all the way to New Zealand and Australia. We would love to increase the diversity of our moderating DMs to provide unique storytelling encounters to this growing population of players

Crafting:

Anybody can trade CP for resources: (we populate NPCs to accommodate requests)

24 CP a day. (1/hr/day) (must be outlined per week or day)

**Trading CP**

- Players can trade a max of 4 CP a day with other players, by assisting them with a task and spending their own CP to access the other player’s professional expertise.

- In addition players can contribute to Public works to the tune of 8 CP a day.

**Resting (**mandatory unless classes/feats override need**)**

- No rest for the wicked = 0 CP (+1 Point of Exhaustion)

<!-- -->

- Short Rest = 1 CP (Regain 1 Hit Dice of HP)

- Long Rest = 8 CP (Regain All Lost Hitpoints/Spellslots)

**Progression**

- Forage: 4 CP (1 Unit, Wood/Herbs, Stone/Metal, Food/Hide)

- Construct: 4 CP (1 Project, Frame, Walls, Roof, Doors, Windows, Furniture)

- Adventure: 4 CP (1 Encounter, Exploration, Group Quest, Invitational, ETC)

- Craft: 4 CP (1 Item, Weapon, Armor, Potion, Scroll, Meal, ETC)

- Research: 4 CP (1 Invention, or 4 Research Points towards Technologies)

**Daily Bonus Dump (Max 2/Day)**

- Socializing = 2 CP (+1 Cha Bonus or Adv on Cha Checks, Limit 1/day)

- Exercise/Workout = 2 CP (+1 Str Bonus or Adv on Str Checks, Limit 1/day)

- Reading = 2 CP (+1 Int Bonus or Adv on Int Checks, Limit 1/day)

- Meditation = 2 CP (+1 Wis Bonus or Adv on Wis Checks, Limit 1/day)

- Parkour = 2 CP (+1 Dex Bonus or Adv on Dex Checks, Limit 1)

- Yoga = 2 CP (+1 Con Bonus or Adv on Con Checks, Limit 1/day)
