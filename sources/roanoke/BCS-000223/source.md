Roanoke Constitutional Laws: 
 
 The Individual Sovereignty and Liberties are a foundational 
right to every colonist in the settlement of Roanoke 
 
 Cannibalism of citizens or allied parties is Forbidden  
 
 Necromancy on people is Forbidden  without consent from the 
person or authorization from native tribes and the colony 
 As this arcane school is founded in negative energy that spreads 
adverse effects in Arcania, extra safety measures are required 
before casting is permitted 
 
 Borders are open to all, but weapon or spell usage laws for 
Non-Citizens shall be posted and enforced 
 All physical weapons are to be clearly displayed on person 
 Usage of weapons or attack spellcasting in town is forbidden with 
the sole exception of self defense  
 
 The open Borders policy reflects Roanoke’s views towards 
creating lasting allies of native inhabitants and other parties 
 Roanoke shall aim to only fight defensively and make attempts 
towards a peaceful encounter with other races, organizations, 
governments, or others first 
 
 A new Mayor shall be selected via majority vote at the end of 
a four year term and may be re-elected if the vote sees fit 
 Mayoral powers may not be used to influence voters other than 
with promises made on the candidate’s campaign platform 
 
 Amendments to these laws may be made at any time provided 
there is a colony majority vote of seventy five per cent within 
three days of calling for a vote 

Justice System Organization 
 
In a new world the ability to carry out swift and 
fair justice is of paramount importance. When a crime 
or grievance is committed or alleged, an independent party 
commissioned by the mayor will set out to investigate and gather 
all the relevant evidence for the trial. 
 If the mayor is the alleged, then the investigating party will be 
commissioned by the town guard or other neutral equivalent. 
 
Every Defendant shall be treated as innocent until 
proven guilty. Once all evidence is gathered there will be a 
court hearing for all parties involved. Ruling council will be made 
up of the Mayor and two rotating neutral citizens that will pass 
sentencing. 
 Neutral citizens will rotate out in the form and function of a jury 
case by case. 
 
Defendants will be given a chance to plead their 
case and representatives or victims may be given a chance to 
plead their case under submission to the Zone of Truth spell. 
Prosecution may rebuttal and provide their case backed with 
evidence. The Mayor and council will come to a collective 
decision for sentencing while keeping our credo in the forefront of 
their mind. 
 Sentencing must be reasonable, fair, and must not be 
abnormally cruel or disproportionate to the crime 
committed. 
 Sentencing may include but is not limited to penalty of death, 
exile, public service, forced labor, or other just consequence that 
serve to uphold Roanoke’s vision and security. 

We the People, 
Under the Crown of Cormyr’s Guidance, begin a new 
colony of Roanoke to act as an extension of King 
George’s reign. With the promise of security and resources 
from the homeland, the people of Roanoke pledge to 
uphold a five per cent tax due to the crown. 
 
 
The Colony of Roanoke holds a great 
diversity of beliefs, but we come together through 
community and a persevering will to construct a new 
home. Through this we choose to stick to our founding 
principle: “Liberty Above All.” We promise to 
uphold this so that all colonists of Roanoke are treated 
with respect to their beliefs and individual sovereignty. All 
who seek a new beginning or religious freedom shall 
have that Liberty.
