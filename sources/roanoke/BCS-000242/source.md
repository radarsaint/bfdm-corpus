So this will walk you through what you need to know to create a character for S4.  This guide is in addition to what is in the 5e Player’s Handbook.

Before starting, remember, you, the player, are creating the story of your character, and how they are the protagonist of their own story.  You are telling your story, but so is everyone else.  The DM’s are here to weave all the stories together.  Especially in such a large game, no one DM will know everything going on, every character, every backstory.  So understand, we will have to err on the side of safety, and DMs might interpret rules and features differently.  So when building your character, keep this in mind and know that just because one DM says you might be able to pull off something that isn’t clearly printed, another might not.  Build a whole character and not just a gimmick that depends on interpretation to function.  Likewise, We encourage reskinning features, characters, and items.  As long as it functions the same, we don’t mind if your goodberries are wafers, stars, or jelly babies.  So if you want a katana, just take your longsword and call it a katana.  You wanna play a goatman instead of a minotaur?  Go for it.  As long as you are not creating new lore without DM permission, have fun.

For a year now, DMs have been working on lore, worldbuilding, new races and new classes/subclasses.  Before creating a character it is strongly recommended you go to our World Anvil page and read everything there.  To get there head to \<[https\://www\.worldanvil.com/w/tales-from-aracania-roanokerpg](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/tales-from-aracania-roanokerpg)\> and click on ‘PLAYER HANDBOOK’ under ‘WORLD CODEX’

Now that you have an idea of the world you will be playing in, start thinking about what you might want to play.  If you have questions, head to the ‘Tales from Arcania’ discord, and ask.  That discord server is now our group meeting place for between games, and at times gives out some hints or spoilers.

Currently, all WotC options are available except we do not allow the ‘Custom Lineage’ race from Tasha’s.  In addition, from Van Richten’s Guide to Ravenloft, you can only pick Dhampir, Hexblood, or Reborn if going with one of the Ancestral Legacy race.

So the question becomes: where do you build a character?  The answer is generally D\&DBeyond.  About a week before game starts, we will send links to campaigns in DDB (we call them stables) where you can build your characters.  Nearly all the homebrew should be available via those campaigns, and all of the WotC content is unlocked.  DDB links up really well with Avrae (the bot all players must use to roll dice in game) so much of the character is tracked there.  The exception: DDB does not have the tools available to homebrew full classes, only subclasses can be homebrewed.  So if you are picking a full class (like Arbiter) you'll have to build elsewhere which some players and DMs can help you find online tools or offline sheets to aid you.

**So now you’re ready to build your character:**

As for Ability Scores, we suggest everyone, especially first time players, use Point Buy or Standard Array.  This year we have allowed people to roll for stats.  If you wish to roll, head into the discord, and find the \#roll-4-it channel.  @admin before rolling, and use the command \!randchar.  This is your one and only chance to roll stats.  To prevent people GNCing (stands for Generate New Character) for good ability scores, this roll is the only roll you get.  Making a new character does NOT let you roll again.  Even if you do roll, you can still choose to point buy.

Speaking of new characters: Don’t make backup characters.  I know it’s tempting to worry about what happens if you die, but you never know what things you might want to play once you are in the game, and, in our experience, having backup characters causes people to play more frivolously with their current one.  Being out of the game for an hour or two after death won’t change much in the long term, but playing like your death doesn’t matter will affect everyone’s enjoyment.

You will get to start at level 3, and multiclassing is allowed, as long as your stats are appropriate for multiclassing.  This means every character has the potential for starting S4 with their classes subclass.

As for races, we have a preference towards the core and homebrewed races, as those would be common in this world.  But all races (but the one mentioned above) are allowed, and all their subraces.

This year, background is very important.  You will get no gear nor gold from your class, and therefore only enter the game with the gear and gold granted by your background.  You may determine how this coinage is broken down (copper, silver, gold, plat) before you enter game.  This means using the custom background option, you enter the game with absolutely nothing.  You may use WotC backgrounds, or the homebrew we created.

NOTE: This means no spell books, no familiars, no armor, etc.  The redcoats took nearly everything you have.  Blame them.

Alignment: This year no character can be an evil alignment, and even X/Neutral alignments must be good leaning.

Make sure to know what your character looks like, and have an idea of how they fit into the world at hand.

A note on age: your character must be a minimum of adult age appropriate for the race you have chosen.  There will be no children PCs this year.

**So you have your character created, now what?**

Wait.  The S4 server link will be sent out on day one of the game.  We will have a video tour made by one of our mods (Mod Goblin) on the server so you can know what to expect.  Once on the server, there is a channel you will have to post the following things before getting the player rank and being able to hop in character: Link to the character sheet (DDB share link, PDF, phone camera pic that is clear enough to read, just a way we can see that you have a character), Character name, an Avatar photo that is 40x40 pixels to be used in any combats that might need it, and a physical description of your character (couple paragraphs preferred at most, so DMs can quick glance look you up and see what you look like).  You are welcome to post a short backstory and full sized art in addition to the previous items listed here.  As it would be best for you to post everything together, but it might take multiple posts, it is suggested to get everything ready before posting, and use copy \- paste to put it all in discord.

Last thing of note, change your nickname in discord.  There are two suggested layouts: “Character name (character pronouns)” or “Character name (character pronouns) \- your nickname (your pronouns)”

Do not use brackets \[ \] in your nickname please as it can cause problems with one of the server’s bots.

Let's say I am playing a female named Min, and example of recommended nicknames for me would be:

Min (S/H)  
or  
Min (S/H) \- DM Heki (H/H)
