The Sorrow of the Statues Escape room challenge.

@ everyone At the Tatankan pen, a burst of magic makes a massive red flare in the air, visible from anywhere on the island.
Solving the clue will give access to a private escape room called “the sorrow of the statues.”

Oleander’s Phylactery has been hidden in a demiplane accessed from within the Pen (now an unholy place tainted by evil). The curse that governs this place is as enigmatic as the Jackalich Druid himself.

The room removes all of the players' items. Dungeoneers packs will be provided.

The room is played out through 3 main puzzles, a fight and several mini puzzles that link a single narrative. This ends with the Pen being an accessible town.

Puzzle A:

Description: Nine pressure plates lay in a 3x3 grid on the floor. There are nine weighted stones. The weights are marked 1-9. You must rearrange the weights on the pressure plates in such a way that each row, column and diagonal add up to the same number.

Puzzle B:

https://cracking-the-cryptic.web.app/sudoku/d8pD7tGQJB

Rules: Normal Sudoku rules apply. Each colored region is a magic square (using digits 1-9 each row, column and diagonal sums to the same number).

After solving the puzzle, to prove you have solved it, take the central 9x9 square, and give the difference of the top row and bottom row shown in gray. In the example, it would be 333 - 222 = 111 (one hundred eleven). This is the key to progress.
