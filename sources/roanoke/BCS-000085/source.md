The Order of Saint George the Dragon Slayer. (PDF)
You have spent your days in service of the Order of St. George. The Order is descended from the group of adventurers who famously slayed the Ancient Green Dragon of Wales, Bwytawr ffwl (Fool Eater). Bwytawr ffwl was a tyrant who ruled Wales through deceit and guile. Saint George turned to the scholars of the land and brought them together to find a way to overcome their oppressor. Famously, they were the first to try to use the Identify spell on him (a spell with the range “touch”). Together they infiltrated his lair and  cast ritually for hours while the dragon slept atop his horde pile.


Choose one of the Gods of the Round table and work with your DM to detail the nature of your service to the order. Are you a field researcher, or a cloistered scholar? Were you raised amongst the order? Or are you seeking sanctuary within it? Do you have rank within the organization, or are you but a humble monk? Perhaps you came from a village where you were the highest authority, only to find that upon coming to London your reputation isn’t nearly as distinguished as you had thought.

The Lazurian Oath


“And verily, the Lord of our order said: "Health is the greatest gain." He also said: "He who would minister to me should minister unto the sick."
I will use my mind to restore the health of all creatures with sympathy, compassion and heedfulness.
I will use my hands to be able to prepare medicines well.
I will use my eyes to know what medicine is suitable and what is not. I will not give the unsuitable, only the suitable.
I will use my heart to minister to the sick with an attitude of love, not out of desire for gain.
I will use my legs to remain with the sick wherever they are.
I will use my lungs to instruct the novice and bring cheer to the ill.
And when all fails, and death arrives, it is I who will thank him for the peace given unto the wretched.”


The Lazaurian Oath
Your pledge to the Oath of Lazarus is to the Oath and no other. And though many speak for the Oath takers, there is no one who governs you and no code but your own ethics. Those who have taken the Oath refer to themselves as the Afflicted as a mark of humility. You know that identifying with your patient is intended to serve the easing of their pain. You may have taken the oath early, perhaps you were too immature to understand the fullness of the implications of a lifetime of service. That you would take no family but the refugee, the meek and the ill fated. 


But with this life comes a level of access not granted to the rest of the world. For upon the battlefields of war and politics there is no shield greater than the healer who bares the traditional cilice upon their leg.This ancient form of self atonement steadys the hand of the surgeon and merchant alike. For though one of the afflicted may hold any station in life, it is considered gravest taboo to refuse to help any who tug at its chain. 


When any oath is made before one of the Gods of the Round Table, it has been decreed by Sir Cai that any who are caught be punished. Different chapters employ different methods, but discredited, denied treatment, tar and feathered, put on the rack, or even death may be invoked. 


Choose a deity in whose name you will take the oath and work with your DM to work out your path amongst the afflicted. Do you wear the Cilice proudly, or have you grown tired of it as it bites its metal teeth into your flesh day after day? Did the order have a hand in raising you, or did you abandon a life of ease to lessen the guilt of a dark burden? Do you run into the fields of war to give succor despite the many dangers, or do you seek out those who hide their pain? While the origins of the Afflicted remain shrouded by the passing of generations, they have served in every conflict, every tragedy, every life in all of the Old World.
Due to the Oath, you are expected to cure the Ill. This transcends morals, prejudice, the patient or target’s disposition toward who is helping them or the methods you use. It has been decreed by the King that it is an evil act to intentionally kill an Afflicted.It is equally heinous to impersonate one. Their elevated and protected status only protects them while they are physically in the act of healing, or the path two or from those in distress.


It is not unreasonable to assume that a guard could look the other way for an offending Afflicted particularly if that guard had ever been previously injured.  A barkeep may waive some fees associated with their tavern for an Afflicted in return for dentistry or lancing boils..


Provided their reputation is unsullied and publicly the Oath is held, they may use any medical facility a populace has available (usually a Church, University, or Large Hall) as temporary residence for themselves, their patients, wards and companions (in that order) if there is room for them. Fellow Afflicted are typically civil to one another. And in the few moments they get to stumble across one another, the stories told over a glass of ale to one another are some of the best you will ever hear.


It falls to you, then, to carry the tradition on to the new world.

The Vigil of Siege Perilous 
Before there was England there was the throne. Before the first of the angler tribe departed the main land of the Old World, there was the throne. At the dawning of the dragons, the green and the red, the white and the black laid claim to all of the land. A single gold dragon looked on the isles and wept for their future. And so Merlin the Golden Dragon, and 33rd degree mason, came to Wales. He set the capstone in Cardiff, of what would one day become Camelot. And Upon that place Merlin returned to make a throne for his King. Not the throne he would sit on, but a masonic machine of perfecting justice. The measuring tool upon which good is divided from bad. The Siege Perilous.
Those who prefer no uncertain terms in life, rigidity, habit and discipline are likely to belong to a local chapter of the Vigil of Siege Perilous.


You believe that the world is light and dark. And the dividing line between them exacts a toll upon those who live their lives in the dark.The vigil of Siege Perilous seeks to safeguard Arthur’s kingdom in his absence by deed and song. The deeds done are recorded in a heraldic tome called the Y brenin unwaith ac yn y dyfodol (The Once and Future King). Any deed once wishes to record may be inscribed in the tome of 5 silver. This has made the leadership of the Vigil both rich, and sure of the rightness of their deeds however misguided. Talk to your DM. Work out where you fit in this rigid religious structure. Are you a shrewd and clever leader ready to lay claim to a mayoral race? In most small towns a chapter of the Vigil chooses to self police their home and help protect against invasion. Were you a villager who helped on the local fire brigade and volunteered to help run town defense only to find that  after a trip to the market, the home you returned to had been sacked by monsters? 
 
The fate of the New World relies on you.


Decide the moral cause you will champion. Chastity, Temperance, Charity, Diligence, Patience, Kindness, or Humility. And a vice you wish to eliminate from the world: Lust, Gluttony, Greed, Sloth, Wrath, Envy, or Pride. Design a character with the two attributes in mind. Perhaps you are of the Charitable Vigil of the Siege Perilous’ wrath. Your character would then be pleased by charitable things, and quick to stand between a raging barbarian and his intended foe. Or perhaps your cause is Temperance and the sin you wish to cleanse the world of is lust. You may be a member of the Sober Vigil of the Siege Perilous’ harlot. Discus with your DM what your chosen options do for your lifestyle and rp interactions.


Feature: Arbiter.
Most rural leadership rely on Vigils to keep the peace. In times of minor conflict most peasants will trust a Vigil member to settle their agreements fairly and without bias.The traditional payment for such an arbitration is 5 gold, as decreed by a clever bard’s song many years ago.The symbol of the vigil is the manacles. And a member is never without them in case a threat presents itself.


Skill proficiencies: Intimidation, Insight.
Tool proficiencies:Calligraphy set. A Viol.

Groundlings of the Globe.


To Lord Chamberlain:


June 29th 1613 was the day the greatest badric college in all of England was revealed to be an private spy and smuggler organization run by the nefarious and heroic Christopher Marlow. 
Marlow’s theater was under protection from the crown, as it was one of the queen’s favorite forms of entertainment. Marlow, is now well known to be a master spy and assassin foiled an attempt on the queen by sabotaging the forgien assassin's plan by setting off the explosions as she planted them throughout the building. What remains of the building is charred timber and cratered earth.
Sincerest apologies for your poodle Persimmon.
From: Lord Chamberlain’s Men theater company, former custodians of the crater in the ground which was once your most auspicious creation. The ember of its smouldering ruins will live in our hearts the way the first rays of sun live in the hearts of the young and in-love.


Perhaps you were there that day. Or perhaps you only say that you were. Perhaps you saw as the stagecoach dashed through the city. As Marlow ran across the horses’ backs. How he knocked the reins loose and used them to tie the drivers head to the railing. Or perhaps you saw the kitten he caught as it lept from a burning building, and expertly landed into his waiting hat. 


Either way,as the legend of Marlow grows, so too grow the deeds of the playwrights and minstrels.




The Groundlings of the Globe.
You are one of the few, one of the dedicated suffering artists who is as likely to be paid in stew and ale for his art as he is to play for precious gems and sacks of gold from royalty. The Globe may be burnt to the ground, but the Groundlings of the Globe still live on in every audience member, in every rhyming couplet of iambic pentameter, is every brush stroke and every line penned. To be a groundling is to accept that you are the master of craft that must constantly justify its own existence. 


No one says to a carpenter “Show me a new way to build a house.” But a musician must constantly learn or write new songs. And a playwright cannot ask his actors to act out the same play everyday for all time. And so, they must continue to invent new worlds and characters to explore. To be a Groundling, you can be anyone one. But to be a true groundling, you cannot be anyone else. Forsake your trade, forsake your inheritance, there is only the spotlight and the knowledge that to chase it is more likely to lead you to ruin than fame. But for the groundlings who stay true through the lean times, there is a bond that is unshakable. The is a consistency of good character and a shared brotherhood between players and artists that cannot be compared. Artists who call themselves groundlings take care of one another, they share when they have nothing to give. And so long as it goes back into raising up the community to be better, they give even more when they have plenty. 


Feature:
Scribbles for nibbles.
A serf may live as well as a merchant if they secure patronage from an aristocrat. In exchange for a work of art, a play, or some other sort of performance, a Groundling may seek small favors from their patron. Additional help maybe bartered if the Aristocrat wishes help in matters of arranging a marriage or a smear campaign to help influence politics. 


You are headed to the New World. Are you following your patron? Do you want experience to draw from to adapt the story into a book or play? Are you a member of a troop of players (Talk to your DM to see if there are any other Groundlings looking to be part of a shared back story).

The Highgate Hunting Club
Highgate Cemetery is known as one the Magnificent Seven Cemeteries of London. It is from these majestic hallowed grounds that The Highgate Club takes its name.This well funded group of monster hunters is known through their tales as told in the Strand Magazine, a publication well known for its serial adventures and mysteries. Full of eccentric and wealthy enthusiasts, crackpot theories, doomsday preppers and big game hunters, there is no challenge too dangerous for the Highgate Club.


As a card carrying member of the Highgate club you are likely to adorn any place you live with massive taxidermied monsters and glorious trophies of your hunt, or the hunters that you admire most. Your interactions with the club may be as well (possibly over) armed groups that are each complete with their own themes and dramatic trappings (such as ornate armor or a particular focus on a specific kind of Old World cryptid like vampires or werewolves). 


Feature: 
Feature: Ear to the Ground
You are in contact with other eccentric members of the club. These people might be associated with the criminal underworld, the rough-and-tumble folk of the streets, or the vain glorious members of high society. This connection comes in the form of a contact in any city you visit, a person who can provide you with advantage on checks related to finding specific types of Monsters (non humanoid Beasts, Monsters, Aberrations and Cryptids) 


Your bond might involve other hunters or the organizations or individuals that employ you or whom you employee. Your ideal could be associated with your determination to always catch your quarry or your desire to maintain your reputation for being a great monster hunter.
