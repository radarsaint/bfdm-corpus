ACT 1: Flight from the Purge  
 (Approx. Chapters 1–8, though you can extend as needed)

• **Act 1 Goals**  
 – Introduce Alan, Kaliope, and Cait in the aftermath of war.  
 – Reveal each character’s early conflicts (Alan & Kaliope are performers fleeing catastrophe; Cait has helped destroy the city and is wrestling with her role).  
 – Depict the cataclysmic fall of Serraphine—Cait’s part in unleashing the floating mountains, the Water Wheel’s shattering, and the birth of the rat-hivemind Lost from the dying essence of Lustre.  
 – Show their harrowing flight across a devastated landscape under new occupation forces. Begin foreshadowing the bond between Alan and Kaliope, complicated by her history with the Devil Pox (and the cruel slur “Poxy Devil”).

1. **Cataclysm & the Birth of Lost**

   * Cait pilots the city’s final weapon: She works the controls that send floating mountains crashing down on Serraphine. At the last moment before impact, she glimpses her reflection—her wide, unblinking eyes convey both awe and horror.  
   * Far below, the Primordial Water Wheel groans to a stop, its centuries-old machinery failing in the quake. Dust chokes the undercity as the Wheel’s power bleeds away. In a forgotten chamber, hundreds of rats converge around a fading spark—Lustre’s essence—coalescing into a single, newly born intelligence: **Lost**.  
   * Lost experiences time oddly, seeing fragments of the city’s past and Cait’s vantage from Lustre’s memories.  
2. **Alan & Kaliope’s Desperate Escape**

   * In the ruined streets, Alan tries to mend cracked arches with minor transmutation, but the ongoing destruction thwarts him. Meanwhile, Kaliope uses quick illusions or conjurations to keep them one step ahead of collapsing towers.  
   * They spot Cait wandering alone through the ashes, still clutching part of the broken console. She looks unharmed but overwhelmed by guilt. Despite their shock at her porcelain form, they pull her along to avoid the occupation forces now sweeping for survivors.  
   * **Small Romance Spark**: Alan and Kaliope share a moment of closeness after fleeing a collapsing structure. Hearts pounding, they’re too out of breath to speak, but each realizes how deeply they rely on the other’s presence.  
3. **Pursued by Occupation**

   * Enemy soldiers (or mercenaries) prowl the rubble, collecting valuables or eliminating witnesses. Alan, Kaliope, and Cait hide among debris. Kaliope bristles when someone hurls “Poxy Devil\!” at her from a distance, hinting at her painful history with the disease and the stigma that remains. Alan’s hand on her shoulder silently comforts her.  
   * Lost—still adjusting to its new hive-mind—helps them evade patrols, knocking debris onto guards or swarming them with rats. They escape the city’s epicenter and see, from the outskirts, the broken skyline of Serraphine.  
   * **Act 1 End**: They press on into the wild, battered but alive. Cait is haunted by her part in the massacre, Alan and Kaliope lean on each other for the first time in years, and Lost grapples with its new identity as a watchful protector. Kaliope’s “Poxy Devil” shame is only hinted at but sets the stage for deeper conflict.

ACT 2: The Jewel Box  
 (Chapters \~9–30s, leading to the darkest point)

• **Act 2 Goals**  
 – Show the group settling in occupied Severen and struggling under oppressive rule.  
 – Develop Alan & Kaliope’s romance: from lingering tensions to real emotional intimacy. Bring in her past “Poxy Devil” stigma more vividly.  
 – Build Cait’s journey from guilt-ridden creation to someone who tries to do good.  
 – Highlight growing citywide unrest: mercenaries under Lady Caine, occupant official Kehris’s clampdown, and rebellious elements.

1. **Arrival & Severen’s Occupied Tension**

   * They reach Severen, joining lines of ragged refugees who must bribe or beg for entry. Soldiers turn a blind eye to bribery, scanning for potential threats. Kaliope flinches at hearing the slur “Poxy Devil” again from a soldier, dredging up memories of her near-fatal illness. Alan squeezes her hand, a wordless show of support.  
   * They discover an abandoned dance hall (later dubbed “The Jewel Box”) they might revive as a place to earn coin and give other refugees some respite. Molly Locket, who once ran it, is desperate enough to let them try.  
2. **Establish the Subplots**

   * **Alan’s Tinker Hustle**: He quietly fixes people’s broken items around the city, using small transmutations in exchange for meager pay—risking occupant attention if he’s caught.  
   * **Kaliope’s Conjurations & Her Past**: She conjures ephemeral illusions or objects for performances, also forging documents for refugees. Doing so exhausts her—just like the pox once did. Locals whisper about her “Devil Pox.”  
   * **Cait’s Wonder & Danger**: Helping in the dance hall, she can move massive debris and scare off bullies with inhuman strength. Many see her as a mechanical marvel; some fear she’s a hidden weapon.  
   * **Lost’s Quiet Watch**: Through small rat bodies spread across the city, Lost witnesses clandestine deals, bribes, and small pockets of rebellion. Occupation official Kehris enforces martial law; Lady Caine and Thaddeus King appear as looming threats.  
3. **The Jewel Box Thrives (Mid Act 2\)**

   * **First Opening Night**: They stage a modest performance with illusions, comedic sketches, or music. Occupant soldiers attend—some bored, some suspicious. The tension boils over when a rowdy onlooker tries to start a fight, and Kaliope’s conjured illusions pin him just long enough for Alan to talk the crowd down.  
   * **Romance**: Alan and Kaliope share quiet evenings practicing illusions, discussing how to draw bigger crowds. When she’s drained from conjuration, he steadies her, an unspoken closeness forming. Kaliope fights her shame about pox scars—“I survived, but I’m still just a Poxy Devil in their eyes.” Alan insists she’s much more.  
   * Meanwhile, occupant crackdowns intensify. A neighbor or friend disappears at night, fueling rumors of forced labor or brutal interrogations. Cait, in her naive desire to help, nearly reveals her power publicly.  
4. **Rising Threats**

   * **Growing Occupation Pressure**: Kehris imposes harsh curfews and punishes minor infractions. Open suspicion falls on the Jewel Box as a possible rallying point for dissent.  
   * **King & Caine Maneuvers**: They collaborate with occupant forces or a local figure named Vane who’s pushing for advanced mechanical augments. People vanish for “volunteer experiments.”  
   * **Kaliope’s Emotional Turmoil**: She faces relentless scorn for her pox-scarred past—people mutter that her conjurations are “unnatural” too. After an especially vile confrontation, she flees. Alan follows, comforting her under a lamplit canal bridge. She breaks down, saying she’s not just sick—she feels monstrous. He quietly says she’s the bravest person he’s ever known.  
   * **Cait’s Identity Crisis**: She tries intervening in the city’s injustices, but each time she reveals her abnormal strength, occupant eyes grow more watchful. Lost warns her that drawing too much attention will threaten them all.  
5. **Toward the Darkest Point (Late Act 2\)**

   * **Clampdown & Betrayal**: Soldiers or mercenaries raid the Jewel Box mid-performance, perhaps after discovering forged documents or suspecting rebellion. The hall partially burns. Alan’s small-scale transmutations can’t hold off advanced occupant weapons. Kaliope’s illusions crumble under stress.  
   * **Shattering of Hope**: They’re forced to scatter. Alan and Kaliope are separated briefly in the chaos. She’s pinned down, jeered at as “Poxy Devil.” Cait tries to protect them but is overwhelmed by a specialized mechanical harness that neutralizes her motions. Lost can only partially help, scattering occupant lines with rat swarms, but it’s not enough.  
   * **Act 2 End**: The Jewel Box is in ruins, the group is split up, and occupant or mercenary forces capture Cait as a “living weapon.” Alan and Kaliope retreat in shock, their fragile sense of safety shattered.

ACT 3: The Fall of Severen

• **Act 3 Goals**  
 – Rescue Cait (or free her from forced augmentation).  
 – Confront King & Caine, occupant official Kehris, and any local collaborator (like Vane) fueling oppression.  
 – Fully resolve Alan & Kaliope’s romance and her acceptance of the “Devil Pox” stigma.  
 – Show Lost’s final role in either liberating or reshaping the city.

1. **Resurgence & Allies**

   * In a cramped rebel safehouse, Alan awakens battered. Kaliope is also there, bruised but defiant. She tries to hide the worst of her pox scars from him. He gently insists her scars don’t repel him; if anything, they show her resilience.  
   * They learn Cait was taken for “research,” rumored to be forced into lethal upgrades or pressed to pilot new war machines.  
   * Lost, having scattered many of its rat-bodies around Severen, shares vital info on occupant vulnerabilities and black-market contraband.  
2. **High-Stakes Confrontations**

   * **Rescuing Cait**: She’s under lock and key at a facility run by King & Caine or an occupant lab. Alan and Kaliope fight through armed patrols. Kaliope uses illusions to create decoy squads, Alan transmutes locked doors to let them pass. They find Cait half-bound in mechanical restraints, eyes clouded by guilt but still determined not to be a weapon of mass destruction again.  
   * **Lost’s Citywide Sabotage**: Through hundreds of rats, Lost sabotages occupant towers, fouls supply lines, and confuses communications, allowing the rescue to unfold.  
3. **Final Showdown**

   * King & Caine, occupant official Kehris, and possibly Vane’s augmented fighters converge, each with their own agenda. The city teeters on open revolt.  
   * **Alan & Kaliope’s Heroic Stand**: Kaliope summons illusions on a far larger scale—phantom armies marching through the streets. Alan transmutes sections of canal walls to steer the real fight. She collapses from overexertion. He holds her, confessing that her scars never mattered—what matters is that they face life together. She reciprocates with raw emotion, letting “Poxy Devil” become a reclaimed strength.  
   * **Cait’s Resolution**: She rejects the occupant harness or any forced augmentation, using her porcelain strength and unstoppable will to stand for Severen’s freedom. Possibly duels a monstrous augmented soldier.  
   * **Lost’s Orchestrated Chaos**: Rat swarms cause critical failures across the city’s war machines or occupant lines. Soldiers flee or surrender in the confusion.  
4. **Climax & Resolution**

   * The occupant regime and mercenary alliance collapses under the combined forces of the rebellion, occupant defectors, and the group’s efforts.  
   * **Aftermath**: Severen is battered but free. The Jewel Box can be rebuilt. Alan and Kaliope, exhausted, share a final quiet embrace or even a dance on the stage’s wreckage, signifying hope. Cait, having chosen her own path, might stay to help rebuild or roam onward to discover more of the world. Lost remains watchful, existing across the city as its silent guardian—or perhaps traveling with Cait.

By the end of Act 3, each main character finds a resolution that matches their growth:

* **Alan & Kaliope**: Affirm their love despite her “Poxy Devil” stigma, forging a future of renewed art and honest acceptance.  
* **Cait**: Fully claims her autonomy, no longer just a creation or weapon but someone who decides her own fate.  
* **Lost**: Continues to safeguard life in the shadows, neither fully mortal nor divine, a presence that has learned the value of individual souls amidst war and destruction.

This plan integrates your main arcs (the flight from Serraphine, rebuilding in occupied Severen, and the climactic struggle for the city) while weaving in the deeper emotional threads for Alan, Kaliope, Cait, and Lost. It builds tension from personal stakes (Kaliope’s pox, Cait’s guilt, Alan’s desire to protect them both) into the wider conflict of mercenaries, occupying forces, and rebellion, culminating in a final showdown that decides Severen’s fate.
