## In the beginning, before gods walked the earth and mortals drew breath, there were only two primordial forces and a single cosmic artifact:

## **The World’s Cornerstone**, a perfect tesseract of prismatic obsidian, suspended in the Void. Façets of this four-dimensional cube bear the “Words of Creation”—ancient glyphs that define every law of nature, every spark of magic, every pulse of life.

## **The Mason**, a demi-cosmic artisan known as the First Carver. With a blade of starlight, he inscribes each glyph upon the Cornerstone’s faces, giving shape to reality itself. After each inscription is complete, he descends into the world—wandering among his creations to marvel at their beauty and complexity. Yet the moment he steps away, his work begins to fade.

## **The Hunger**, an unseen wind of entropy that pursues The Mason. It cannot be seen or named, but its approach is felt in the subtle decay of the Cornerstone’s glyphs: a crack here, a blurred stroke there. As The Hunger sweeps over each face, the Words erode, fracturing the very foundation of existence. When a god’s divine glyph vanishes, that deity is sundered from the world; when the last letter on the final face dissolves, all reality collapses back into the Void.

## From this cycle of creation and erasure emerged mortal Tinkers—devotees chosen by The Mason to serve as custodians of his Words. Using sacred inks or arcane chisels, they perform Renewal Passages in vaulted sanctums that echo the Cornerstone’s geometry. Each precise stroke they redraw buys precious time, delaying The Hunger’s advance. But every re-inscription costs a portion of the Tinker’s own life force; their lines are written in sacrifice.

## Yet for all his skill, The Mason has never found a way to still the wind. Driven by the knowledge that the death of gods and the ruin of empires spring from The Hunger’s erosive breath, he searches for a Final Glyph—one that might bind The Hunger and end the cycle forever. When the last face stands empty, The Mason returns to the Cornerstone once more, carving a new set of Words. Sometimes he restores what was; other times he changes the design entirely, hoping each fresh edition will break the endless pursuit.

## In this way, every era—every pantheon, every empire, every age of magic—rises from his carving, only to be swept away when the wind comes for the Words. Thus begins the story of gods born and gods killed, of empires forging Engines to seize divine remnant, and of mortal heroes caught between the Maker’s pen and Entropy’s breath—ever striving, ever rewriting, in search of an end to endings.

## **The Four Primal Wellsprings**

### **2.1. Arcane: Elemental Order & Ley Lines**

- **Core Nature:  
  ** Arcane is the very pattern of reality—the force that holds fire aloft, water flowing, earth standing firm, and air swirling. It is “order in motion,” manifesting as elemental harmony.

- **Manifestation in the World:  
  **

  - **Ley Lines:** Invisible rivers of Arcane that run beneath mountains, across plains, and through ancient ruins. Wherever they surface, elemental forces tingle in the air: stones hum, flames burn brighter, winds shift direction.

  - **Elemental Phenomena:** Sudden flickers of lightning, unexplained tremors, or spontaneous crystallization of rock might be Arcane siphoning itself to rebalance.

  - **Arcane Wellsprings:** Congealed pools of pure elemental resonance—perhaps a grotto where molten lava and tidewater meet, or a high‐peak spring that glows with prismatic mist.

- **Thematic Associations:  
  **

  - **Order & Balance:** Archmages and elemental binders see Arcane as a system you can learn and manipulate.

  - **Craft & Structure:** Many early Engines (like the Alembic of Echoes) are built atop Arcane Wellsprings to harness transmutation.

  - **Neutrality:** Arcane itself isn’t “good” or “evil”—it simply is the scaffolding upon which creation stands.

- **In-Story Hooks:  
  **

  - Scholars chart “Arcane currents” like star navigators.

  - Ruined towers often bear collapsed “Arcane seals”—once they were meant to regulate elemental floods.

  - A hero might awaken to Arcane by instinctively freezing molten iron or calling a bolt of lightning.

### **2.2. Divine: Gods, Souls, & Spiritual Law**

- **Core Nature:  
  ** Divine is the breath of the immaterial—the spark that animates souls, kindles faith, and weaves the tapestry of belief. Where Arcane is “how things work,” Divine is “why they matter” to creator spirits.

- **Manifestation in the World:  
  **

  - **Shrines & Temples:** Physical foci where Divine pulses coil around idols or holy relics. At such sites, prayers and sacrifices can cause a tangible swell of power.

  - **Soul Echoes:** When a person dies, their soul briefly flickers in the Divine current—giving rise to ancestral memory, echoes of departed ancestors, or lingering specters.

  - **Wellspring of Faith:** A hidden pool where the distinction between mortal devotion and godly grace blurs—touching its waters can grant temporary divine sight or heal wounds “in the name of a deity.”

- **Thematic Associations:  
  **

  - **Law & Covenant:** Divine power answers oaths and vows. Breaking a solemn oath near a Divine Wellspring can cause the ground to tremble or curses to ripple outward.

  - **Faith & Belief:** The mere act of communal worship “charges” the Divine Wellspring. Entire cities might live off that current.

  - **Mortality & Immortality:** Gods themselves are born, empowered, and killed within the ebb and flow of Divine—when their Cornerstone glyphs vanish, even deities pass away.

- **In-Story Hooks:  
  **

  - A priestess might awaken to Divine by inadvertently calling down a healing light that mirrors her people’s faith.

  - Divine artifact: a broken chalice said to contain the last breath of a slain god—anyone who drinks begins to “speak with the dead.”

  - Temples might align themselves along “Soul‐Streams,” narrow strands of Divine that thread between ley lines.

### **2.3. Eldritch: Mind Over Matter & Chaos**

- **Core Nature:  
  ** Eldritch is the aspect of pure thought unbound by physical law—the wild, unpredictable spark that embodies extremities: creation or destruction born of sheer will.

- **Manifestation in the World:  
  **

  - **Psychic Tempests:** At sector points where Eldritch surges, reality itself can warp—mirages shimmer, gravity wavers, time seems to skip.

  - **Focal Nodes of Madness:** Ruins of ancient towers where thought experiments went too far. Those who cross these nodes risk sudden psychotic visions or “mind‐borne monsters.”

  - **Wellspring of Extremes:** A cratered pit where, if one listens closely, they perceive the chatter of a thousand fractured realities—tapping it can grant uncanny insight or drive one insane.

- **Thematic Associations:  
  **

  - **Chaos & Unpredictability:** Eldritch is neither benevolent nor malevolent—it’s the raw churn of possibility.

  - **Mind Over Matter:** Psionicists, sorcerers who shatter elemental bounds, or people who “think objects into existence” draw on Eldritch.

  - **Forbidden Knowledge:** Many cultures treat Eldritch Wellsprings as taboo—those who pursue it risk awakening the Hunger faster, because pure thought can unravel the Cornerstone’s glyphs.

- **In-Story Hooks:  
  **

  - A character might awaken to Eldritch by solving a paradox—only to find the landscape around them fracturing into impossible shapes.

  - Eldritch cults might gather to “guide reality’s next mutation”—tempting players with glimpses of unbounded power at the cost of their sanity.

  - A crumbling archive of “Impossible Tomes” sits atop an Eldritch node—anyone who studies them can rewrite small pieces of reality but risks being overwhelmed by the Hunger.

### **2.4. Valor: Body, Will, & Mastery Over Self**

- **Core Nature:  
  ** Valor is the raw spark of volition embodied in flesh—the force that binds a warrior’s blade to their arm, that steels resolve in the face of death, that forges discipline out of chaos.

- **Manifestation in the World:  
  **

  - **Trial Grounds:** Sacred arenas or remote monasteries where warriors test their mettle. At these sites, Valor coalesces into an almost‐tangible aura—protecting the disciplined, empowering the staunch.

  - **Ember of Resolve:** A crystal or blade that glows when its wielder’s courage falters or triumphs.

  - **Wellspring of the Iron Will:** An underground vault where echoes of ancient warriors’ vows still burn—any who stand in its center can resist fear, disease, or enchantment at a distance.

- **Thematic Associations:  
  **

  - **Discipline & Training:** Valor is earned through sweat, pain, and endurance. It respects no shortcuts.

  - **Physical Mastery:** Gladiators, knights, monks—those who refine body and spirit until neither can be broken.

  - **Resilience & Sacrifice:** When Valor is corrupted, it becomes blind fanaticism—an unstoppable force that tramples nuance or runs headlong into oblivion.

- **In-Story Hooks:  
  **

  - A champion might awaken to Valor by refusing to yield to a rampaging elemental—drawing upon a Wellspring of Iron Will to stand fast.

  - Valor Wellsprings often hide in deepest fortresses, guarded by ascetics who hone their bodies into living weapons.

  - A “Fallen Valor” site: a field where all who sought to prove themselves perished—their wills trapped in the Well, begging for release.

Below is a structured codification of your Corporea and Essentia Foci—organized around the four Primal Wellsprings (Divine, Eldritch Shadow, Arcane, and Valor). Use this as a reference for how each Focus (Guts, Stride, Atlas, Maul, Icarus, Virtuoso, Resonance, Logic, Rhetoric, Nobility, Heartflame, Philosophy) combines with a Wellspring to form specific “class” archetypes.

## **I. The Two Types of Focus**

### **1. Corporea Focus**

- **Definition:** Extensions of the physical body. Corpsorea Foci draw upon anatomy—shoulders, legs, hands, gut, wings—to channel raw, tactile power.

- **Function:** Govern everything from combat and movement to feats of endurance or dexterity.

- **List of Corporea Foci:  
  **

  1.  **Guts** (Instinct & Primal Awareness)

  2.  **Stride** (Agility & Confident Mobility)

  3.  **Atlas** (Endurance & Bearing Burdens)

  4.  **Maul** (Direct Force & Unflinching Action)

  5.  **Icarus** (Wings, Aspiration & Resilience)

  6.  **Virtuoso** (Hands as Tools of Finesse & Versatility)

### **2. Essentia Focus**

- **Definition:** Extensions of personality, intellect, and emotion. Essentia Foci tap into intangible qualities—logic, speech, moral conviction—to shape abilities.

- **Function:** Govern mental acuity, social influence, and emotional resonance rather than brute force.

- **List of Essentia Foci:  
  **

  1.  **Resonance** (Voice & Emotional Attunement)

  2.  **Logic** (Analytical Mind & Reasoned Solutions)

  3.  **Rhetoric** (Persuasive Framing & Narrative Control)

  4.  **Nobility** (Conferred Authority & Collective Will)

  5.  **Heartflame** (Emotional Intensity & Transformational Passion)

  6.  **Philosophy** (Deep Insight & Ethical Inquiry)

## **II. Corporea Foci: Refined Descriptions**

### **1. Guts**

- **Anatomy:** Instinctual center (gut)

- **Core Concept:** Trusting primal intuition—“gut feelings” that guide you before thought catches up.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Combat:** Dodge or counterattack not by sight, but by an almost subconscious sense of danger—reacting to threats a heartbeat before they arrive.

  - **Social:** Speaking hard truths with unshakable confidence because something “feels” right, even without clear evidence.

- **Tension:** Listeners may call your actions reckless, but they stem from an uncanny, preconscious awareness.

- **Strengths:** Heightened reaction time, emotional radar in social situations, decisive instincts when no time to deliberate.

### **2. Stride**

- **Anatomy:** Legs & feet

- **Core Concept:** Physical agility fused with unwavering self-belief—each step driven by purpose.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Combat/Movement:** Uncanny evasiveness; leaping into advantageous positions; acrobatic maneuvers that baffle foes.

  - **Non-Combat:** Entering a room or stage with a confident gait that commands attention, project authority simply by walking.

- **Tension:** Confidence must be genuine—overconfidence can backfire if abilities are overestimated.

- **Strengths:** Superior mobility, spatial awareness, inner certainty that bleeds into everything you do.

### **3. Atlas**

- **Anatomy:** Shoulders & back

- **Core Concept:** Shouldering burdens—both physical and emotional—as if your very strength is meant to sustain others.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Combat:** Stepping forward to tank blows, protect allies, or anchor a defensive line.

  - **Leadership/Support:** Serving as the unwavering confidant in crises, the one who “carries” a team’s morale when hope fades.

- **Tension:** The impulse to protect can lead to overextension; you may absorb too much and risk collapse if you never share the load.

- **Strengths:** Exceptional endurance, empathy-driven resilience, magnetic ability to steady a group.

### **4. Maul**

- **Anatomy:** Hands, fists, or weapons in hand

- **Core Concept:** Direct, decisive action—solving problems by going straight through obstacles. Hands become the embodiment of your devotion to principle.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Combat:** Powerful, precise strikes that break enemy lines or shatter restraints.

  - **Everyday:** Tackling challenges head-on, whether laboring to build or physically confronting threats to protect what you value.

- **Tension:** While directness is effective, nuance and strategy can be overlooked; blunt force can sometimes create unnecessary collateral.

- **Strengths:** Relentless practicality, unwavering follow-through, inspires trust by “doing” rather than merely “speaking.”

### **5. Icarus**

- **Anatomy:** Wings or analogous means of flight

- **Core Concept:** The cycle of aspiration and recovery—soaring high, then learning from every fall. Wings symbolize both ambition and the wisdom of resilience.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Physical/Aerial:** True flight—navigating treacherous skies, performing daring aerial feats, quick exits or pursuits from above.

  - **Emotional:** Embracing risk and learning from setbacks; falls become lessons that fuel even greater determination.

- **Tension:** Overreach can result in a literal or metaphorical crash; humility is required to use this power safely.

- **Strengths:** Exceptional mobility in three dimensions, emotional adaptability, inner growth through each rise and fall.

### **6. Virtuoso**

- **Anatomy:** Hands & fingers—but not for brawn; for finesse, dexterity, and fine motor skill.

- **Core Concept:** Mastery of varied crafts and skills—hands that shape both art and arcane, tools and spells.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Artisanry/Spellcraft:** Intricate lockpicking, precision lock removal, or weaving arcane gestures to cast delicate spells.

  - **Performance/Creation:** Inspiring an audience with music, calligraphy, sculpture, or any art that requires supreme manual control.

- **Tension:** Multiplicity of skills can stretch focus—true mastery in many fields demands discipline and time.

- **Strengths:** Adaptability, problem-solving via manual finesse, ability to turn small gestures into profound effects.

## **III. Essentia Foci: Refined Descriptions**

### **1. Resonance**

- **Core Concept:** Voice as a transformative instrument—words that literally reshape the world by attuning to emotional and spiritual currents.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Healing/Communication:** Speaking soft words that heal living creatures or coax open locked doors simply by resonating with their “essence.”

  - **Emotional Insight:** Tuning into the undercurrent of a crowd’s feelings and guiding them toward harmony or action.

- **Tension:** Overuse can unbalance natural currents, causing unintended physical or emotional side-effects (plants overgrow, objects warp).

- **Strengths:** Empathic influence, environmental manipulation via speech, forging bonds or alliances through shared emotional resonance.

### **2. Logic**

- **Core Concept:** Mind as a crucible of order—deftly dissecting complexity to find solutions, seeing patterns where others see chaos.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Problem-Solving:** Escaping mazes blindfolded through pure deduction; unraveling conspiracies by connecting disparate clues.

  - **Strategy:** Outthinking opponents in debate or on the battlefield—anticipating moves, planning contingencies.

- **Tension:** Pure reason can become dogmatic if divorced from empathy; logic alone cannot resolve every dilemma.

- **Strengths:** Analytical clarity, rapid decision-making under pressure, ability to refine complex systems.

### **3. Rhetoric**

- **Core Concept:** Words as tools of framing—shaping perception by carefully choosing what to highlight, persuade, or provoke.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Debate/Politics:** Swaying entire assemblies by reframing facts into compelling narratives; turning conflict into consensus by spin.

  - **Social Maneuvering:** Defusing tension or inciting revolt by matching the precise emotional chord in listeners.

- **Tension:** Can verge on manipulation; trust is contingent on the speaker’s integrity rather than mere skill.

- **Strengths:** Social influence, mass persuasion, molding public opinion or morale.

### **4. Nobility**

- **Core Concept:** Authority granted by collective will or divine sanction—your presence carries the weight of others’ faith in you.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Leadership:** Simply entering a council chamber commands respect; your decrees carry moral or even quasi-divine weight.

  - **Diplomacy:** Rallying followers or forging alliances by embodying the ideals they hold sacred.

- **Tension:** Authority can be revoked if you fail to live up to expectations; it’s a partnership between you and those who granted your title.

- **Strengths:** Natural command of respect, ability to unify disparate factions, moral gravitas.

### **5. Heartflame**

- **Core Concept:** Raw emotional intensity as a crucible—feelings that burn so brightly they transform the world around you.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Inspiration:** A stirring speech or passionate embrace that kindles hope in the desperate or ignites a movement.

  - **Transformation:** Emotional outbursts that tangibly alter surroundings—plants bloom or fires flare up in direct response to your inner state.

- **Tension:** Intensity can overwhelm—unchecked, it may consume both you and those around you.

- **Strengths:** Catalyzing change through empathy and zeal, forging deep bonds, healing wounds by shared catharsis.

### **6. Philosophy**

- **Core Concept:** Wisdom as a living lens—seeing beneath surface phenomena to grasp deeper truths, and applying that insight to enact positive change.

- **Manifestations:  
  **

  - **Insight:** Turning any situation into a lesson by asking the right questions; helping others see the “why” behind their choices.

  - **Guidance:** Serving as counselor, diplomat, or mediator by applying ethical discernment to complex moral dilemmas.

- **Tension:** Overanalysis can lead to paralysis; wisdom speaks best when balanced with action.

- **Strengths:** Deep counsel, ability to unify conflicting perspectives, guiding communities through ethical quandaries.

## **V. How to Use This Framework**

1.  **Choose a Wellspring:** Decide whether a character’s core alignment is with Divine, Eldritch Shadow, Arcane, or Valor.

2.  **Pick a Focus (Corporea or Essentia):** Select which physical or intangible center drives their power—Guts, Stride, Atlas, Maul, Icarus, Virtuoso, Resonance, Logic, Rhetoric, Nobility, Heartflame, or Philosophy.

3.  **Combine to Form a Class Name:** Match Wellspring + Focus to get one of the archetypes above (e.g., “Divine Atlas ⇒ Sanctified Protector,” “Arcane Virtuoso ⇒ Maestro,” “Eldritch Shadow Maul ⇒ Psycho,” “Valor Resonance ⇒ Valor Dictator”).

4.  **Elaborate Role & Abilities:** Use the “Role Brief” as a springboard when writing or designing:

    - **Signature Abilities:** Brainstorm how that combination would manifest—e.g., a “Sanctified Protector” might create divine wards using sheer emotional empathy (Atlas + Divine), while a “Maestro” might play a lute that literally reshapes elemental flow (Virtuoso + Arcane).

    - **Backlash & Growth:** Consider what happens when they overextend their Focus (e.g., an Eldritch “Stalker” might slip into madness if they push too far, or a Valor “Skyward Champion” could crash and damage their wings if pride blinds them).

5.  **Anchor in Worldbuilding:  
    **

    - **Ley Lines & Wellsprings:** Assign a specific location for each Wellspring (e.g., “Temple of the Last Light” for Divine, “Shattered Observatory” for Eldritch Shadow, “Elemental Spire” for Arcane, “Iron Bastion” for Valor). Characters tied to that Wellspring naturally gravitate there to recharge, train, or confront challenges.

    - **Tinker & Engine Connections:** Decide how each class might interact with Engines or Tinker rites—e.g., a “Divine Virtuoso (Harmonious Artisan)” might be called on to “re-ink” a fading Word by singing the ancient hymns during a Renewal Passage, whereas an “Arcane Arbiter” might patrol ley line junctions to suppress rogue elemental surges.

## **VI. Example: Building a Character**

1.  **Step 1 – Wellspring Choice:  
    ** Suppose you want a character whose core power is “survival instinct guided by faith.” You choose the **Divine Wellspring**.
