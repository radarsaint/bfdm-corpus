Creating a setting book for your D\&D world, Arcania, covering North America during the 1700-1800s involves several key steps. Here's a structured approach to help you organize and compile the information effectively for both players and dungeon masters:

1\. Introduction and Overview  
Setting Premise: Describe the core concept of Arcania, highlighting the blend of historical and fantastical elements.  
Timeline: Provide a chronological overview of major historical and magical events shaping the continent.  
2\. Geography and Regions  
Detailed Maps: Include maps showing the entire continent, with notable locations, terrain features, and borders of different regions.  
Regional Descriptions: For each region (like Cascadia, Brownbear Republic, etc.), detail its geography, culture, politics, and role in the broader world.  
3\. Races and Cultures  
Playable Races: Describe the unique races of Arcania, their traits, customs, and relationship with the land and each other.  
Cultural Insights: Offer insights into the various human and non-human cultures, focusing on their traditions, beliefs, and daily life.  
4\. Magic and Technology  
Arcane Practices: Explain the nature of magic in Arcania, including how it differs from the Old World and its integration with society.  
Technological Innovations: Detail the technological level of the era and any unique inventions or machinery that exist in the world.  
5\. Politics and Power  
Government Systems: Outline the political structures of major powers, like the Brown Bear Republic's double blind meritocracy.  
Influential Figures: Describe key figures in Arcania’s politics, including heroes, villains, and leaders.  
6\. Economy and Trade  
Economic Overview: Discuss the economic systems, major industries, and trade routes.  
Currency and Commerce: Define the types of currency used and how trade is conducted between different regions.  
7\. Religion and Beliefs  
Pantheon and Worship: Describe the religious beliefs, gods, and their influence on society.  
Mystical Locations: Highlight places of power and spiritual significance.  
8\. Adventure and Exploration  
Notable Locations: Provide details of dungeons, ruins, and wild areas ripe for exploration.  
Plot Hooks and Quests: Offer adventure ideas, conflicts, and quests that DMs can use to engage players.  
9\. Bestiary  
Creatures and Adversaries: Include a bestiary of creatures unique to Arcania, along with their stats and lore.  
Allies and NPCs: Provide information on potential allies and non-player characters that can aid or oppose the players.  
10\. Appendices  
Rules and Mechanics: Detail any new game mechanics, class archetypes, or magical items specific to Arcania.  
Glossary: A list of terms and names used in the world for quick reference.  
Index and References: Provide an easy way to find information within the book.  
11\. Artwork and Visuals  
Illustrations: Incorporate artwork that brings the world to life, including character races, landscapes, and important scenes.  
Charts and Tables: Use visual aids to simplify complex information, like political alliances, racial traits, or magic systems.  
12\. Player's Guide  
Character Creation: Guidance on creating characters native to Arcania, including backgrounds and class options.  
Survival Tips: Tips for players on navigating the challenges of the setting, from wilderness survival to political intrigue.  
13\. Dungeon Master's Guide  
Campaign Building: Advice on creating campaigns in Arcania, including balancing historical accuracy with fantasy elements.  
Secrets and Behind-the-Scenes: Include hidden lore and secrets that only the DM knows, to create depth and mystery in campaigns.  
By structuring your setting book in this manner, you'll provide a comprehensive resource that can be used to run a wide range of adventures in the Arcania setting, catering to both new and experienced players and dungeon masters.
