**Sentinel Stuffy**  
Wondrous item (requires attunement)

A plush toy that more often than not finds the new owner, before the owner finds it. This doll attunes itself to you upon being picked up. While attuned to it and on your person, you gain a \+1 to your AC and all saving throws. The stuffy acts as an Unseen Servant with the following changes, it has an AC of 13, 10 hit points, and a Strength of 10.The Sentinel Stuffy also has minimal sentience with an Intelligence of 4(-3), Wisdom of 10(+0), and a Charisma of 16(+3) It cannot speak, but understands all languages spoken by its owner. If you are separated from the Sentinel Stuffy for over 24hrs you will lose attunement, during this time the Stuffy will do all it can to get back to you.If you were to die due to overwhelming damage or from failing death saves, the Sentinel Stuffy takes your place when dying, becoming inanimate, and ending your attunement.

**Odd Heart**  
Wondrous item (requires attunement)

This item is one that has always been with you, your own heart, and its potential only unlocked by an experimental surgery. If you manage to survive the surgery you will awaken to your heart being encapsulated in a containment chamber of your own choosing and connected by a tubing or a cord to your chest. After the surgery you will find yourself permanently under the effect of Sanctuary(DC15) until you are unattuned. Whenever you roll a hit die you always take maximum healing. Once attuned to the Odd Heart you cannot unattune to it unless you die or undergo a more dangerous reversal surgery.

*Symbiotic Nature.*  
The heart can't be removed from you while you're attuned to it, and you can't voluntarily end your attunement to it. If you're targeted by a spell that ends a curse or enter an anti-magic zone, your attunement to the heart ends, and you immediately fall unconscious and start making death saves.

**Phlogiston Pidgin**  
Wondrous item (requires attunement) 

This prosthetic tongue was made with magi who have had their tongues forcibly removed. Taking a lump of molten phlogiston and molding it carefully, a person could replace their lost tongue. 

The extra dimensional energies inherent in the Phlogiston imbue the attuned with the languages of all the planes. While attuned you can cast *Tongues* on yourself a number of times equal to half your proficiency bonus rounded down and resets on a long rest. You can also cast *Speak with Animals, Plants, and Dead* at will. 

You also gain proficiency in one of the following abilities: Deception, Intimidation, or Persuasion. If you are already proficient in the chosen ability you gain expertise instead. 

**Owl Feather Arrows**  
Ammunition

When a rogue uses these arrows or bolts they get an extra 1d6 of Sneak Attack damage.

**Staff of the Lost Land**  
Artifact, staff (requires attunement from a spellcaster)

Made of the intertwined roots of three ancient trees, each imbuing the staff with a separate gift. From the Hanging Tree, the dread and disease of the Uru that loitered it’s branches. From the Spellmart, the wonderment of creation and spirit of it’s proprietor, Iona. And from the Baeshra tree, the very spirit of the lizardfolk it was named after. You gain \+3 to saving throws, save DC, and spell attack bonus.

*The Hanging Tree:* The greying decrepit root’s energy can be harnessed to cast Negative Energy Flood at a creature. If the creature is undead, roll the 5d12 damage if the damage would kill it, it crumbles to dust instead of giving it temporary hit points. You can use this once per day and it resets at the witching hour. 

Once per week you can thrust the diseased spirit of the Uru upon a creature to truly Harm (DC17) them.

*The Spellmart:* The soft white root radiates with a calming and warm presence. you can press the staff to the ground and cast Circle of Power. When cast this way, the staff holds the concentration of the spell and all friendly creatures within the circle gain temporary hit points equal to their proficiency bonus. You can use this once per day and it resets at dawn.

Once per week, at your bequest, can extend and solidify it’s warm presence into a Globe of Invulnerability. 

*The Baeshra Tree:* The hearty roots of the Baeshra tree are brimming with wild and ancient powers. By your will these powers can surge into you and you become a Guardian of Nature. When cast this way the staff holds the concentration the Guardian forms get the following changes:  
Primal Beast: Your appearance becomes more lizard like and the bonus damage die becomes a d8.  
Great Tree: The temporary hit points refresh each turn, as long as the spell is active.  
You can use this once per day and it resets at dusk.

Once per week you can wield the power of the Baeshra Tree to Reincarnate a deceased creature, however they only reincarnate as a Lizardfolk.

The staff can be split apart into 3 separate staves: The Hanging Staff, The Spellmart Staff, and the Baeshra Staff. When split the staves lose their ability to use their weekly ability and only give a \+1 bonus to saving throws, save DC, and spell attack bonus.

**Forlorn Wheel Fiddle**  
*Bardic instrument (requires attunement by a bard)*  
rare  
After losing their lover, a young bard left for a life at sea. They jumped ship to ship across the ocean, never staying with one crew for too long. For, while they played beautifully, their songs and shanties were always sorrowful. A sailor who crewed with them described their songs “...like they were tryin’ ta speak ta tha dead…” Stories of the sorrowful sailor sonneteer died down for many years until one day this wheel fiddle was recognized in a market as the one that traveled alongside them. Dark cherry wood makes up the body and the wheel and neck are both carved from grey spruce.

When you play this instrument all of your songs come out sounding very sorrowful and forlorn no matter how ‘happy’ or ‘upbeat’ the song is. Anyone with your bardic inspiration that adds the die to an attack can change all bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage to necrotic damage.  Once per day you can use this instrument to cast *Speak with Dead* on a corpse, however their response comes in the form of a haunting shanty that echoes from the wheel fiddle. No matter the state of decay of the body you will get a response. Once per week you can cast *Legend Lore*, when cast this way all information is relayed as a shanty.

***Curse:*** If anyone calls it a hurdy gurdy, you feel compelled to correct them.

**Pseye-ball**  
Wondrous Item (requires attunement by someone with Psionic Energy Dice)  
Very rare

An ocular prosthetic designed for the Psionic inclined. Made from a glowing pink stone found in the deep of the jungles, this can be used to replace a lost eye. Sought for its ability to strengthen Psionic users. While attuned to the eye you can push your ability further while slightly taxing your mind. When you use a feature that uses Psionic Energy dice you may enhance them in the following ways: you roll 2 dice instead of 1 for any calculation made or if a save is involved the target makes it at disadvantage. Straining your mind has a cost and you take half of the number rolled in psychic damage. If no Psionic dice are rolled, roll one and take psychic damage equal to the number rolled.

You also gain another use for your Psionic Energy Dice.

***Psight***: You can spend a Psionic Energy Dice to gain truesight 30ft for 1 minute. Enhancing this feature increases the range to 60ft.

Unattuning to the Psi-ball leaves you blind in the eye it replaced. You remain blinded until you reattune to it and when you do roll your Psionic Energy dice and take damage equal to the number rolled.
