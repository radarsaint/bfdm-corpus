## ***The Primordials*** **and the Birth of Majik**

In the beginning, there were four Primordials—cosmic beings of impossible power and boundless intent. These were the shapers of all existence:

* **The Primordial of *Life***

* **The Primordial of *Death***

* **The Primordial of Might**

* **The Primordial of *Knowledge***

United briefly, they formed the first breath of reality—creating galaxies, stars, and the bedrock of magic itself. But unity bred conflict. Their ideologies clashed, and in their dispute, ***the Great Expanse*** was formed.

## ***The Great Expanse***

The Great Expanse is not merely a void between stars, but a **literal stretch of bound time**—a frozen battlefield suspended between moments, where the four Primordials clashed in divine war. Across the galaxy, fragments of this celestial freeze remain, anchoring entire worlds in ancient stillness. In each frozen shard of the Great Expanse, a Primordial can be found locked in their final moment of wrath or defiance.

To disturb these timeless echoes is to risk all of existence. Should any ***Primordial*** be awakened—or worse, destroyed—the fragile weave of reality could unravel. Entire galaxies may implode, revert, or collapse into chaotic time-loops.

These scattered Expanse Fractures are now hunted, worshipped, and feared by the lesser gods and mortals alike. Some believe they hold the keys to ultimate power. Others believe they are the seals keeping reality intact.

From this fracturing of divine unity, each Primordial forged their own domains, beings, and philosophies. In doing so, **Majik** was born—woven from their essence and handed down through their divine descendants: the **Lesser Gods**.

Every strand of Majik traces back to these four, but over time, it diverged into two dominant expressions on the world of **Gaudium**:

* ***Veytus***, the inherited breath of the world, drawn from the Primordials of ***Life*** and ***Might.***

* ***Novus***, the sharpened edge of intellect and ambition, descended from the Primordials of ***Knowledge*** and ***Death***.

## **VEYTUS \- The Old Magic**

**Philosophy:**  
 "You are not separate from the world. You *are* the world."

**Description:**  
 Veytus is the primal Majik of Gaudium—the breath of the earth, the whisper of beasts, the memory of stars. It is not cast, but remembered. Not summoned, but awakened. Veytus flows through root and bone, instinct and blood. It predates civilization and thrives in places forgotten by time. All who draw upon Veytus must do so with reverence or risk being unmade by its ancient will.

**Source:**  
 Natural leylines, living environments, dreams of ancient spirits, ancestral memory, and the raw emotion of living beings.

**Classes Aligned:**

* **Barbarian** – Rage born of storm and mountain, and purest might

* **Druid** – Keepers of the old forms. Protectors of Organic Life

* **Ranger** – Hunters bonded with beast and land.

* **Fighter** – Warriors whose skill flows from instinct and tradition.

* **Sorcerer** – Bloodlines blessed or cursed by primal forces.

* **Monk** – Seekers of internal balance through the rhythms of the world  
    
* **Cleric** \- Neutral/Depends on Build

**Aesthetic:**

* Colors: **Blue**, **Green**, **Purple**, shifting like a twilight sky.

* Highest Power: ***Luminous white***—a pure, soul-deep energy.

* Visual: Bioluminescent patterns, glowing runes etched into bark or bone, flora that reacts to spellcasting, flowing auras that echo animal movement or natural decay.

**Religion:**

* Ancient nature spirits, beast-totem worship, dream gods, sunless pantheons.

* The *Silent Wound*—a mystical scar on the world worshiped as the source of all Old Majik.

* **Power Risk:**  
* Overuse causes users to slowly lose their identity to the wild or ancestral echoes.

* Terrain may mutate around them—growing wild, aggressive, or sentient.

**Region Presence:**

* Wildlands, untouched forests, ruins, nomadic cultures, druidic enclaves, old mountain clans.

**Technology View:**

* Shunned as an insult to the natural order.

**Cultural Perception:**

* Revered in tribal communities, feared by cities, mythologized by Novus scholars.

---

## **NOVUS \- The New Magic**

**Philosophy:**  
 "Power is not inherited. It is built. Refined. Perfected."

**Description:**  
 Novus is the rising force of innovation—Majik distilled through study, doctrine, and human ambition. It is the chosen flame of the lesser gods, the fuel for pistons and pulses, and the key to unlocking the very structure of reality. Novus Majik is not drawn from the world, but imposed upon it. A sharp contrast to Veytus, Novus seeks control, evolution, and transcendence.

**Source:**  
 Arcane formulas, divine contracts, synthetic rituals, experimentation, technological enhancements, and direct communion with the lesser gods of Gaudium.

**Classes Aligned:**

* **Artificer** – Engineers of spell-tech and arcano-mechanics.

* **Wizard** – Scholars of arcane law.

* **Paladin** – Holy warriors empowered by gods.

* **Warlock** – Pactbound manipulators infused by otherworldly patrons.

* **Bard** – Enchanters of the modern age; couriers of cultural revolution.

* **Blood Hunter** – Experimental warriors pushing the boundary of mortality.

* **Rogue** – Thieves, holder’s of secrets, gadgeteers, or arcana-enhanced killers.

**Aesthetic:**

* Colors: **Red**, **Orange**, **Yellow**, and **Pink**—all pulsing like a furnace.

* Highest Power: **Pure Void Black**—the perfected entropy of Novus energy.

* Visual: Cracking, glowing sigils; mechanical limbs; synthetic eyes; hover-reactive ink; guns or gear that hiss and vent Majikal residue.

**Religion:**

* Temples to lesser gods of innovation, ambition, and domination.

* Cults that worship *The Coil*, *The Mirrorborn*, or *The Clock of God*.

**Power Risk:**

* Novus corrupts through obsession—knowledge that *wants* to be misused.

* High-level exposure can cause mutation, breakdown, or existential dissociation.

**Region Presence:** Cities, research academies, underground labs, warfronts, forge-chapels.

**Technology View:** Integral. Magic and machine are one.  
**Cultural Perception:**

* Glorified in cities, distrusted in frontier regions, seen as heretical by Veytus worshippers.  
---

**Final Note:**  
 The two currents of Majik—Veytus and Novus—do not cancel one another, but instead pull Gaudium toward opposing destinies. One is to *return to what was*. The other is to *become what has never been*. The Amber-Mound stands at the fulcrum.   
**\*\*\***Classes are not Hard set to their Aligned world of Majik, Things can change depending on Subclass and Flavor.
