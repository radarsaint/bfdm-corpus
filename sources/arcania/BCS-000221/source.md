The Great Expanse 


The Great Expanse - A moment from the past that exists suspended shards in eternity..
The Great Expanse is not merely a void between stars, but a literal stretch of bound time—a frozen battlefield suspended between moments, where the four Primordials clashed in divine war. Across the galaxy, fragments of this celestial freeze remain, anchoring entire worlds in ancient stillness. In each frozen shard of the Great Expanse, a Primordial can be found locked in their final moment of wrath or defiance.
To disturb these timeless echoes is to risk all of existence. Should any Primordial be awakened—or worse, destroyed—the fragile weave of reality could unravel. Entire galaxies may implode, revert, or collapse into chaotic time-loops.
These scattered Expanse Fractures are now hunted, worshipped, and feared by the lesser gods and mortals alike. Some believe they hold the keys to ultimate power. Others believe they are the seals keeping reality intact.
From this fracturing of divine unity, each Primordial forged their own domains, beings, and philosophies. In doing so, Majik was born—woven from their essence and handed down through their divine descendants: the Lesser Gods.

Fate is a two-sided patron formed from the union agreement of Primordials Death and Life. 
It embodies the inevitability of endings and the preciousness of beginnings. Its essence threads through moments of choice and consequence, recording every life’s turning points in a living ledger that subtly nudges mortals toward paths that avert or cause catastrophe to enforce balance.

Symbolic imagery: A ledger bound in living amber, its pages etched with runes that shimmer like breath on glass; a quill that writes with ink made from starlight and ash. 
The Strings that tie the fate of mere mortals
The Ever continuation of time, the ticking of the

Domains of influence
Endpoints and consequences: Fate subtly guides outcomes when mortal choices threaten catastrophe or collapse of communal harmony.
Memory of decisions: The patron keeps a living record of smoothed or broken promises, rewarding restraint and mercy that preserves life & roads to death
Relic governance: Fate judges when some relic’s use will heal or harm the many, often tipping the balance toward the less destructive option.

Bu’Qwuall is the memory-god (Lesser god) who preserves the Planet’s histories, including the names and deeds of former rulers, legendary heroes, and infamous tyrants. Making it able for collecting, curating, and weaponize memories to guide governance, quell dissent, and justify expansion—arguing that whoever remembers most truly holds the power to shape reality.
Patron and tower: Bu’Qwuall is worshipped as the patron of memory and truth-gathering. Their primary seat is the Archive Tower, a colossal, faceted spire that records histories in real time. The tower’s interior is a shifting labyrinth of memory-rooms, each chamber containing living records: carved runes that glow when a memory is vivid, shelves that rearrange themselves to reveal related histories, and portals that transport viewers into past eras.
Beliefs and purpose:
Remember to rule: memory anchors legitimacy; leaders are expected to consult and cite past rulers’ deeds and misdeeds when making decisions.
Record to protect, protect to expand: every memory is safeguarded against corruption, with ritual wards; memories are used to justify expansion into new territories by projecting the wisdom or destiny of past leaders onto current campaigns.

Goddess of Peace, Renewal, and Music
Harmony is the benevolent counterpart to Blight: a goddess-queen who shepherds renewal, serenity, and the gentle passage of souls, now with a sovereign domain over music as a sacred conduit of harmony. Music is not merely ornament for her; it is a force that heals, clarifies truth, pacifies conflict, and guides the living and dead along the pathways of renewal. She is an emanation of Primordial Life, a princess-turned-dream-queen who channels the Mother’s warmth into both the living world and the paradise beyond.

Realm: The Eternal Hymn 
Environments: A radiant archipelago of sanctuaries above glassy seas, where melodies literally shape the air—gentle choirs drift as visible auras, and harmonies bend light, guiding souls to the Positive Afterlife.
Musical landscape: Every temple or grove contains a living instrument or resonant stone that responds to devotion with tuneful resonance. The realm’s acoustics shift with mood and season, turning disputes into duets and conflicts into crescendos of resolution.
Connection to Primordial Life: Harmony remains a direct emanation of Life’s nurturing aspect, a dynastic steward whose musical power embodies the Mother’s compassion and the perpetual renewal of being.
