Opinion
Sugar and Tea Would Do Nicely
Might get my head chopped off for saying this, but I really like what those brits brought to our shore. Err- not the killings and stealing and terror and all. But the tea. Sweet, Baby Jesus, those tea. They’re a delight. All my coins’a spent buying them little bags - some sugar too. Have them every morning when I wake up. 
Now I’m gonna let y’all in on a lil’ secret: It’s how I keep alive on this here city. I guarantee: you will not die whenever you drink this tea. On any given day. Face a Redcoat for all I care. You could crush ‘em like little mice - or bugs. Seven hells, a combination of the two if you even want. Death isn’t an option when you got tea in your system. It’s just not possible.
Especially if had  with them little sugar boxes, ya’know? It gives a little bite; and a lotta crunch too. They let me have a kick in my step, gives a little more to look forward to in the day. Another secret, just between me and ya readin’ folks: If ya really want a kick in your step? Put some of them spirits in it. It’s gonna cost ya more, usually need twice the teabags in just one cup. But ya need it so the booze don’t just flood your stomach whole. And o’course you still want that taste of tea to appear, ya’know? It’s the star up in this concoction, it gives that oomph of flavor to kick your ass into gear. 
I love them tea so much. So, so much. So much that I started calling other things I like “my cup of tea”. If I’m being honest, I even heard other people sayin’ it too. Who knows, it might eventually catch on. Now that’d be a pretty thing to leave behind. “My cup of tea. My cup’a tea.” Oh, just think. People would  be so grateful I introduced them to tea, they’d gimme some for free! My, my, my. That’s certainly my cup of tea.
