Opinion
A Writer's Confession ~ Ode to the People of Empire City
-by L.S.L


I, the writer, am dead, yet living, killed by a Redcoat in a distant town.
Having no memories, I believed myself to be unique in this trait, keeping it hidden out of fear for what life I have, fear that if I were to be discovered I would surely lose my second chance.
I came to Empire City to find a cure for my condition, to find out why I still exist, but instead I found a purpose.


Just like me - the people here have had everything taken from them by these fiends, these tyrants who claim their way is 'justice', those who rule with an iron fist and destroy things of worth. And yet, we keep fighting. Even from beyond the grave.
The determination of those who wish to resist the Redcoats is so strong that the City allows them to return from death. I personally witnessed three heroes return - including the good 'thief' mentioned previously in 'Slothful Redcoats Assault Innocents' (July 13th), and one who accepted me, helped me find myself - as well as assisted in the revival of a fourth, and met one whose determination is such that he has returned no less than thirty-nine times. A twisted revenant, barely recognisable as alive, disfigured even more so than myself - but alive, and fighting, with hopes and dreams in his heart, and rebellion against injustice on his lips.
At the time of writing, Eldon - the good citizen also mentioned previously, who was cruelly executed by the Redcoats to enforce their 'justice' - has not returned, but I have hope. Even if not, he will be forever seen as a symbol of freedom - a patriot who went out in a blaze of fire, and like the story of the Phoenix, will serve as a guiding flame in our hearts as we fight the cruelty and injustice of those with cloaks of blood.


I - Lady Sabrine Lovlatar - may be dead. I may be a disfigured husk of who - or what - I once was. But I will fight nonetheless.
For the people of Empire City, and the war against injustice, we march together. We shed the chains of fear, of iniquity, that the Redcoats use to bind us. And we will emerge victorious.

Rapscallion Rebel Scum Seen Rousting Rooftops
We already know that the rebels operate in the deepest bowels of the City. It appears that the Rebels are now attacking the colonies from the sky as well. Several eye witness reports have claimed that a flying hooligan has been terrorizing the local rooftops.
This hooligan has been causing a terrible ruckus and interrupting several British officers in their conjugal exploits that are the very core tenant that sustains the Empire in perpetuity forever and ever. God save the King. Amen.
Wherefore are we now to see these "martyrs of freedom. What is next? Terrorizing us in our evening baths? I, for one, will not stand for it. I refuse to. I am an Englishman and dammit I will not be looking over my shoulder waiting for the hawkish rebels to swoop down upon mine. Wrenching asunder what foothold the King exerts here. This is of course our imperative to snuff this fire out. Once and for all!
