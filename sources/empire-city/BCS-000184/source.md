Barber
Working with hair is not your only ability as a barber. You have spent your years of cutting hair being asked to do odd jobs around the body of many humanoids. Jobs include pulling teeth, sewing arms back on, or chopping off gangrenous limbs. The unwanted expectations of others have made you competent in the bloody work of healing.Skill Proficiencies: Medicine, Deception.Tool Proficiencies: Herbalism kit, Poisoner's kit.Languages: Common and Costal ArcanianEquipment: A warm compress filled with leeches (use the rules for a healer’s kit.)A straight razor and a bar of soap. A leather apron.
Feature: The barber’s assurance.A barber should never be without their implements. The nature of your job requires sharp objects. As such acquiring them is far easier for you than other people. You are granted a +2 to persuasion checks when asking for a small knife or scissors. After all, it's just for a haircut. right?Bootlegger:Everyone needs something and bootleggers know how to get things past the redcoats and the minutemen. While others rely on stealth and guile, you have built hiding places and dead drops.
Skill Proficiency: Deception, Survival
Tool Proficiency: Vehicle (Land), Brewing
Equipment: One permanent Counterfeit Curfew Pass for Hampstead (DC10), a set of common clothing, a mason jar of 190 proof alcohol, and a leather pouch containing 5 gold
Languages: Common and Costal Arcanian
Feature: Give em the slip
You always keep an eye out for an exit if the situation goes south, and know how to create an opportunity to slip away.  When in the streets and needing to give someone the slip, you can make an opposed roll, your survival vs their perception.  If you succeed, you get away with the items on your person.

Skill Proficiency: Stealth, Survival
Tool Proficiency: Navigator’s Tools
Languages: Coastal Arcanian
Equipment: a bayonet, an explorer’s pack, a shovel, a hunting trap, and a bag containing 10 gold pieces
Feature: Guerilla Tactics
Ambushes are how you fight 10 to 1 odds, and you have no scruples laying in wait, just to attack unsuspecting enemies.  When you roll initiative, and the enemies have not detected you, you may add +5 to the initiative roll.  Likewise if you use a net to restrain an enemy, the Strength check DC becomes 15.
