Empire City Crafting:
Season 4


How to Craft:
Step 1. Look up the cost of the item in the Sane Magical Prices document.
https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B8XAiXpOfz9cMWt1RTBicmpmUDg/view?resourcekey=0-ceHUken0_UhQ3Apa6g4SJA
Step 2. Expend crafting essence with a value equal to the price listed. If your essence quality matches the item quality, its value is doubled when used in crafting. Contact a DM to learn what qualities an item has. (for example, a “Flametongue” weapon has the “Fiery” quality, and “headband of intellect” has the “Theurgic” quality)
Step 3. Post the items you spent and the items you gained in the #essence-crafting discord channel. When your post has been approved by a DM, you may use the crafted item.


Crafting Essence:
Each essence has a rarity and one quality associated with it as shown below. Take special note of the Chaotic and Theurgic qualities.


1. Acidic. Associated with acid damage, evocation spells that deal acid damage, conjuring acid, acid resistance, rusting, and corrosion.
2. Chaotic (Wild Magic). Associated with wild magic, randomness, unpredictability
3. Cold. Associated with cold damage, evocation spells that deal cold damage, conjuring ice, and cold resistance.
4. Dimensional (Planar, Extraplanar). Associated with teleportation, portals, conjuration magic, summoning, bigger-on-the-inside spaces, and pocket dimensions.
5. Fiendish. Associated with fiends, the lower planes, evil effects, and the warlock fiend pact.
6. Fiery. Associated with fire damage, evocation spells that deal fire damage, conjuring fire, fire resistance, the element of fire, and fire elemental creatures.
7. Forceful. Associated with force damage, evocation spells that deal force damage, force resistance, force fields, many abjuration spells that ward or hedge, pushing or shoving, and the Strength ability.
8. Inert (Antimagic). Associated with magic resistance, antimagic, magic deflection, the element of earth, earth elemental creatures, and giants. Also associated with resilience and immovability.
9. Lightning. Associated with lightning damage, evocation spells that deal lightning damage, and lightning resistance.
10. Mercurial. Associated with transmutation magic, shapeshifting, fluidity, the element of water, and the Dexterity ability.
11. Necrotic. Associated with necromancy magic, necrotic damage, necrotic resistance, and animating or summoning undead creatures.
12. Phantasmal (Fey, Glamered). Associated with illusion magic, senses, awareness, perception, music, the fey, the Feywild, the Wisdom ability, and the warlock archfey pact.
13. Poisonous. Associated with poison damage, evocation spells that deal poison damage, poison resistance, conjuring poison, inflicting the poison condition, or protecting from and neutralizing poison.
14. Psychic. Associated with psychic damage, enchantment magic, spells that deal psychic damage, mind reading, mind shielding, and the Charisma ability.
15. Radiant. Associated with radiant damage, evocation spells that deal radiant damage, light, celestials and upper-planar creatures, the gods, divine blessings, and good.
16. Theurgic (Metamagic). Associated with the rules of magic, spell slots, magical storage, divination, knowledge, and the Intelligence ability. Associated with construct creatures and permanent animated objects. 
17. Thundering. Associated with thunder damage, evocation spells that deal thunder damage, thunder resistance, and loud noises or explosive sound.
18. Vital (Quickened, Living, Life-Giving). Associated with healing, regeneration, purification, treatment of disease, longevity, and the Constitution ability.
19. Void. Darkness, destruction, madness, nonexistence, aberrant creatures, and the warlock Great Old One pact.
20. Zephyrous (Windy, Airy). Associated with wind, elemental air, air elemental creatures, flight, breathing, and objects that can float or hover under their own power.

Discovering the essence quality:
- When you receive an essence, you know its rarity and value. Each essence also has a quality. Roll a d20 on the Crafting essences table above to discover the essences quality. 
- You may make one Arcana check DC 15. On a success, roll another d20 to discover its second hidden quality. The identify spell counts as a successful Arcana check for this purpose. 
- If you are given an essence with an unknown rarity, roll on the following table to discover it:


1-39 - Common
40-69 - Uncommon
70-89 - Rare
90-99 - Very Rare
100 - Legendary!


Harvesting:
Savvy adventurers with the appropriate skill can sometimes find additional crafting ingredients out in the world. Once per day, you can make an investigation check to try and gain a magic essence. After making this check, you can’t try again until the next day. 


DC 10 - You find one Common crafting essence.
DC 20 - You find one Uncommon crafting essence.
DC 30 - You find one Rare crafting essence.


If you make this check while on a DM led adventure, roll with advantage. For example, after slaying a monster, you can seach its corpse for a crafting essence. Or if you are traveling through a swamp, you can search around the area for treasure. 


Converting:
You can try to convert one quality of essence into another. You need two essences. You must first have an essence of the desired quality. This is the catalyst. The catalyst essence is safe during this process. Place the “catalyst” essence and the “reaction” essence that you want to change near each other. Make an Arcana check DC 10. On a success, the reaction essence changes to the catalyst essence’s quality. On a failure, it changes to a random quality. Roll a d20 to discover its new quality. On a critical fail, the reaction essence is destroyed. Converted essences can only have one quality, and lose any other qualities they had before. Roll these checks in #advanced-crafting.


Crafting and Upgrading Airships:
Airships are inherently magical vehicles. All air ships have the “Zephyrous” quality, and can be crafted. Airship upgrades may have other qualities. Ask the DM.
