Issue 1                                                        July 18th, 1776                                        Price: 2 copper
________________




It’s been 8 long years since the Boston Massacre. When we arrived on these shores as refugees from the wars of the Old World we found a haven. We were protected for a time by those gracious enough to lend their strength and friendship to allow us to settle along their eastern coast. Some of those who came were naturalists and inventors. Some were scholars and leaders. Together with the ingenuity of both the Old World and the New, there was a new renaissance of arcane invention. The methods of both worlds combined in surprising ways that changed our lives forever. Of all of the Old World, only England secured the rights of trade peacefully. Where the French sent troops to the north, and the Spanish sent troops to the south The English signed a treaty of mutual defence. We became dependent on their money. And they became dependent on the inventions and marvels of the New World. We traded away our governance for promises that England would protect us from the wars of the Old World.
Some said the wars were our fault. Gods that were dead awoke on our shores. They awoke and returned to the Old World with wrath on their minds. A barrier went up, to keep their strife from our homes. But the hundred years war still rages. And though the fighting hadn’t reached our shores, the Old World both needs and resents us. We have had the time and the resources to change the way life is lived. But where we saw scholars, they saw soldiers. We backed England to defend us. Now, they own us. Little by little their offers of help and protection meant accepting their leadership. And the Continental States became the Colonies of England. We gave it all up to keep out the French and the Spanish. But where saw a shield the English saw the means to shackle us.
Slowly they have built up their navies and left them on our shores and in our skies. Using our methods and inventions and their money, their troubles that we thought we were escaping never left our shores. Daily we are reminded of the threats they say they are facing on our behalf. They don’t ask for things anymore. They quarter their troops in our homes. 
They tax us for our “protection” and we don’t get a say in what happens. And then some of us had had enough, our protests were met with murder. How exactly, I ask you, are they protecting us with violence against us?
When unrest fomented into war, Master Mason Washington took the lead of a real defence. For two years his fight has spanned all thirteen of These United States. Now their influence converges on the capitol. That melting pot of races and refugees, Empire City is where it will culminate. The Red Coats walk our streets, live in our homes, run our factories, and continue aided and abetted by their loyalists. Washington himself has overtaken Governors’ island and built a fortress to defend the hudson. But I fear that a military campaign cannot defend a city if that city has already crumbled from within.
It will be up to those that live there to change the tides of war. If they cannot overcome their difference and rise against the red tide of manipulation and propaganda, the seat of our little empire will fall, and with it, I fear, the colonies and then the rest of the continent. We must congress together as one nation with one voice and vision. 
May Columbia’s guiding light wash away the blindness of our people.
-Poor Richard Saunders
