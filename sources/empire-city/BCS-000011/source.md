STOCK MARKET 
101


If you are reading this article, it means you are interested in making money. The perfect place to do that is the stock market, where you can make your money grow. This article will give you the basics, so keep on reading my capitalist friends.
        The stock market is, well, a market. However, instead of buying physical goods, you buy company shares, what we in the biz call stocks. Once you buy stocks you now have the opportunity to get that gold. As a company performs well it’s value will go up, along with its stocks. The stocks you bought for 100 gold yesterday might now be worth 150 or maybe even 200! Once your stock values go up, the thing to do is to sell. The difference in the prices is your profit! While easy profit is fun, sometimes a stock will keep on rising, so patience is key. Do note my wording; “as a company performs well.” Not all stock prices go up. Certain companies might suffer catastrophic losses thus lowering their value. When their values go down instead of profiting you lose money when you sell your stocks. 
This is not random however. You can judge whether a company will perform well or not by keeping up to date with the latest happenings. If the papers report customers suddenly flocking to buy airships or a new trend in robes start popping up, companies that cater to those markets will enjoy an increase in value. Do note that the opposite can happen as well. You also play an active role in this! The more a company’s stock is bought, two things happen. First, the company receives additional funds that allow them to develop, increasing their value. Second and more importantly, like in physical markets, the more a stock is bought the more it’s desirability and value increases. The opposite happens when selling. So keeping an eye on recent news and what other investors are up to will pay in the long run.
I have taught you all I can. If you want to learn more about the market, f*** around and find out! Happy investing capitalists!

Market Trends


Stocks have seen a frightening drop over the last few days. Save for a few openly Redcoat-supporting companies, there are losses all across the board. Investors are panicked, but others are sensing an opportunity. 
The continental currency CON is one of the hardest hit. An emergency meeting was scheduled yesterday between Robert Morris and other financial titans. If the meeting was successful, then investors from around Arcania will be pouring in much needed gold for the mustering of an army. If the meeting went poorly, it may spell the end for the upstart currency, and possibly the dream of freedom.
