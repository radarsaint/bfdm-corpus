Are we really outgunned?


I've lived in Hampstead for longer than I can remember. I've bled to die here to build this city and it's outrageous what this rebellion has been up to. Bombing a jail? How many fellow patriots would that hurt? A shootout in Hampstead was bound to lose, the quartering act makes it a slaughter, everybody knew that right? If we want any ground against these bloody coats, we need to be alert, and be prepared. There are all sorts of things we can scrape together out of this city and damn it we have hardly touched the resources we have. We don't have to get super fancy, we just have to take things smart. Learn where they're strong, learn where they're open. They aren't sneaky, they often get obvious about what they do. You don't need sneaking around to tell when they're up to most of the time. And what about the Noir? Nobody showed up when they were attacked, Ferrytown was left high and dry. If a fight seems too much then take a step back, look for the other guy’s next punch, and get him before he gets to throw it. We need to make a plan before we make these kind of moves against them. We have our friends and we have other people in the city, we lost the lamplighters and god only knows what damage that is going to bring. We are forgetting what we know. We have history in the making and we get caught with our pants down. Use the essences for more than just Knick knacks and drinks. That’s the one thing people will buy from round here. Just be careful, stick together, take it slow. Rome wasn’t built in a day and wars aren’t won in just on battle. Keep your eyes peeled and watch your surroundings. And for fuck sake don’t meddle with those bombs, someone will get hurt.
