Kimmelian (Garden Gnome)

First constructed by an ailing Gnerman wizard to take care of his hedge maze, these constructs have taken on a spark of life all of their own. Born of fire, clay and glaze, this is the only branch of the Gnomish race whose offspring have their physical appearance hand sculpted by their parents. Kimmelians tend to gravitate toward any place in Empire City that still has greenery. The largest collection of which make their home in central park.

Studying them is intensely frustrating. The image of the Gnomish gardener as a popular collectible means that it can be relatively difficult to spot a genuine specimen amongst the hundreds of thousands of  ornamental statuettes that have become so popular. It is my belief that the charm which allows them to create more of themselves also applies to those around them, and it is a matter of survival that they influence others to be drawn to placing the things everywhere to provide them cover. It’s maddening.

— Mabel Richard, Cryptid Xenobiologist.

The city that never sleeps is full of nightlife. Kimmelians, being naturally nocturnal, are popular entertainers and are intrinsic to the entertainment of the residents of Empire City. They are generally good natured, but can become gruff over time. Their internal state of mind can often be reflected in their physical condition. A broadway star might look just as pristine as the day they strode forth from their family’s kiln. Whereas a surly bouncer at a nightclub may have cracks in their patina and a permanent frown.

Vibrant Colors.  
A gnome’s energy and enthusiasm for living shines through every inch of his or her small body. Gnomes average slightly over 3 feet tall and weigh 40 to 45 pounds. Their tan or brown faces are usually adorned with broad smiles (beneath their prodigious noses), and their bright eyes shine with excitement. Their fair hair has a tendency to stick out in every direction, as if expressing the gnome’s insatiable interest in everything around.

A gnome’s personality is writ large in his or her appearance, which requires special tools to alter. A male gnome’s beard, in contrast to his wild hair, is kept carefully trimmed but often styled into curious forks or neat points. A gnome’s clothing, though usually made in modest earth tones, is elaborately decorated with embroidery, embossing, or gleaming jewels.

Delighted Dedication  
As far as gnomes are concerned, being alive is a wonderful thing, and they squeeze every ounce of enjoyment out of their three to five centuries of life. Humans might wonder about getting bored over the course of such a long life, and elves take plenty of time to savor the beauties of the world in their long years, but gnomes seem to worry that even with all that time, they can’t get in enough of the things they want to do and see.

Gnomes speak as if they can’t get the thoughts out of their heads fast enough. Even as they offer ideas and opinions on a range of subjects, they still manage to listen carefully to others, adding the appropriate exclamations of surprise and appreciation along the way.

Though gnomes love jokes of all kinds, particularly puns and pranks, they’re just as dedicated to the more serious tasks they undertake. Many gnomes are skilled engineers, alchemists, tinkers, and inventors. They’re willing to make mistakes and laugh at themselves in the process of perfecting what they do, taking bold (sometimes foolhardy) risks and dreaming large.

Seeing the World  
Curious and impulsive, gnomes might take up adventuring as a way to see the world or for the love of exploring. As lovers of gems and other fine items, some gnomes take to adventuring as a quick, if dangerous, path to wealth. Regardless of what spurs them to adventure, gnomes who adopt this way of life in Empire City as much enjoyment out of it as they do out of any other activity they undertake, sometimes to the great annoyance of their adventuring companions.

Speed 25ft

Age. Gnomes mature at the same rate as humans do, and most are expected to settle down into an adult life by around age 40\. They can live 350 to almost 500 years.  
   
Size. Your size is Small. Gnomes are between 3 and 4 feet tall and average 40 pounds.  
   
Augmented Tremorsense  
Despite their animated features, A Kimmelian construct does not use their facial features in the typical fashion. Kimmelian uses an augmented tremorsense to detect and pinpoint the origin of vibrations within an 80ft radius sphere and are effectively blind beyond that point. 

Gnome Cunning. You have advantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws against magic.  
   
Natural Mystic: You are born with innate magical abilities. These include knowing the Druidcraft Cantrip and Dancing Lights cantrip  
   
Limited Hide in Plain Sight: While the sun is up, you can use your natural camouflage to try to hide by standing still. By hiding in this way you will not be able to be distinguished from an ornament.   
You gain a \+10 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks as long as you remain there without moving or taking Actions. Once you move or take an action or a Reaction, you must camouflage yourself again to gain this benefit.

Languages. Common, Gnomish (Kimmelian Dialect)
