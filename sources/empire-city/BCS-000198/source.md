# **The Isle of Fae aka Illo'fae (modern day Coney island)** 

Illo’fae is a large fairground at the South of burrough. Entirely fairground/amusement park, including park areas. This is the perfect place to spend a sunny day off enjoying various games, shows, and delicacies. It is also home to a luxury hotel structured entirely of the surrounding trees.   
![][image1]

### **Boompaleis- Luxury Tree hotel**

* This hotel is known for its opulence, unpredictability, and secrecy. Boasting stunning views of the ocean and grown entirely of huge trees intertwining, it is no wonder this hotel has become a sought after vacation spot. Hiring only the best entertainers with the most fabulous costumes, and the most over the top talents Boompaleis provides an experience you won't soon forget. What happens in Boompaleis stays in Boompaleis.   
* Las vegas meets great gatsby meets literally every elf tree village ever depicted  
* Serves as an evening getaway/watering hole for residents of Risehall  
* The stock market bros be here a lot, as well as the elite of Arcadia occasionally  
* Rooms are modified liemans tiny huts. Patrons can change the opacity temp from inside the room.   
* Check out is strictly enforced as the hut will disappear (with featherfall automatically cast) upon checkout time  
* Prepay for an extension on your room/spell  
* Furniture costs extra  
* George Washington hates it here and refers to it as a sin palace   
* 1300 g per night, walk out with effects of heroes feast, and unable to talk about specifics of your stay.

## **Main show**

* **Main NPC**: Ferrah  
  * The ringmaster and overseer of Illo’fae is an Eladrin named Ferrah. Ferrah’s skin seems to shimmer the colors of the rainbow. She is always looking for entertainment and fun. She is larger than life and generally over the top for the material plane, and she likes it that way. She has a violet Faerie Dragon named Vlinder with her at all times. Vlinder can polymorph once a day into any CR2 or lower creature. Go to forms: Saber-Toothed Tiger, Quetzalcoatlus (flying dino), a rhino, and a giant constrictor snake. He usually chooses to stay shades of purple but can be convinced to take on each beast’s natural appearance. Vlinder, like Ferrah, loves a good prank and is always looking for moments of mischief.  
* **Start**\- A rainbow colored Eladrin in glitter and costume takes center stage. A small purple dragon sits atop her shoulder. A spotlight follows them. “Greetings faedies, gentlethems, and to all our visitors native to this realm\!” Her voice carries throughout the tent with ease, captivating the audience. “Whether this is your first time on Illo’fae, or not, you’re in for the show of your lives\! We ask you to sit back and allow yourselves to be taken on a magical journey with us\!” And with that all manor of beast and performer flood the stage with her, giving the crowd a glimpse of what is to come. Suddenly this stage darkens and empties. A different voice begins to sing.   
* **Act 1-** A spotlight appears at the top of the tent. Above your heads are two lythe, muscular elfish folk in costume. One is adorned in yellows and golds, the other in dark blues and silvers. Without warning they both leap off of their pedestals from the top of the tent and soar through the air, at the last moment catching bars strung from the ceiling. They swing over your heads at incredible speeds, contorting their bodies around various colored silks with ease. The singer, who remains unseen, sings a song in a language you don’t recognize. Although you don’t know what the lyrics are, you feel the meaning. She takes you through a journey of sadness and remorse, war and turmoil, justice and harmony. The two figures continue in tandem with the music until the story reaches its finish. All goes silent until an applause from the crowd erupts.   
* **Act 2**\- Various performers come out onto the stage all at once. A blonde man with windblown hair juggles an impossible amount of items much higher than you’d think possible. A girl with short red hair lights a sword on fire and begins sliding it down her throat. A gnome in suspenders trots around on stilts, surprisingly agile. Multiple colorful haired, pale skinned humanoids come out. They fall over each other and the other performers causing an uproar of laughter from the crowd. A band plays somewhere off stage and each instrument seems to highlight a different performer.   
* **Act 3** Ferrah Returns to the stage atop a purple saber-toothed tiger,. The other performers exit the stage with the accompanying music. “How is everybody doing\!?” She asks to the crowd, voice seemingly magically enhanced   
* “The feywild is known for many things, including the creatures who inhabit it, next you will see all manor of Faebeast…”
