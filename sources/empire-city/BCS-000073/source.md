Airship Qualification Module


This module can be used at any time during the Empire City Role Playing Event in 2021.


Contents
1. Airship basics
2. Training targets
3. Random encounters


1. Airship Basics:
Cadets start out at the Continental Air Force training grounds, one of the few protected areas safe from Redcoat harassment. 
Sgt Manley will run the players through a quick overview of ship systems, weather hazards, and answer any questions. The DM should refer to the Airship Rules document with any questions. 


Sgt Manley is a short bald Firbolg with a scottish accent. He is very knowledgeable and wants to see his students succeed. He is a Level 10 fighter, though he won’t intervene unless absolutely necessary, even if the craft is in danger. He has let his students drive their airship into the ground on more than one occasion in the past. He calls it a good learning experience. He has a wand of feather fall at the ready in case anyone tumbles overboard. His customary farewell saying is “Fly Safe!”


2. Run the player through a training exercise with Sgt Manley. Each player chooses one role to practice (Pilot, Gunner, Mechanic, Captain). The standard exercise is to take to the skies, and shoot down 6 target practice balloons at the combat range. The training ship’s Artillery is defective, and each time it fires, it becomes disabled. That will give the mechanics something useful to do right away. Feel free to invent your own training exercises. 


3. A complication inevitably arises at some point during the exercise. Common random encounters include:
- Air Elemental attacks. https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/air-elemental
- Called to immediately investigate air pirates nearby who have been harassing Colonial shipments - Chase another airship into the high altitude zone where wind threatens to batter the training ship. The pirate ship does fire back, but tries to escape. Deal with high altitude challenges.
- Distress call. A battle between two airships is raging nearby. Your side is doing badly. Friendly Airship is down to 10 health. Enemy airship is at full health. 
- Ambush. There is thick fog in the zone. An enemy airship grapples and tries to board your ship. Hand to hand combat ensues. 


Note that any airships captured during these training missions will be commandeered by the Continental Air Force. Players don’t get to keep them. Other loot should be modest. The real reward is earning your wings!


Rewards: 
“Trained” Status in your role for the mission - Add your proficiency bonus to actions for that role.
If you are already trained in the role you completed, gain “Ace” status (add twice proficiency bonus to actions)
