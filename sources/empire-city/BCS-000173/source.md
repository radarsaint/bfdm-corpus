# Ferrytown 

## Borough Description/Introduction

As the ferry departs from the harbor and the city fades behind you, a small old woman clambers up from the worker's area and grins to the passengers. She appears to be the old lady who collected your tickets, but that woman definitely had brown hair and you could have sworn she waved you off from the shore. You shrug it off. Probably just your eyes playing tricks on you, or maybe you misremembered.

"Now dearies, before we arrive at Ferrytown, there are some things ye best know." Her voice is old and creaky, and she has an accent both distinctive and unfamiliar. "I'm sure some of ye have heard the stories about strange creatures and mysterious happenings, but I can excitedly confirm that they are in fact, all true\!" She pauses for a minute, observing the shock on your faces before breaking out into raucous laughter. 

"I'm joking, of course\! Would hardly be able to have visitors if Ferrytown was everything the stories said we are." You notice that she seems to add in an extra y to the name this time. "Course, it ain't perfectly safe. No place is, these days. But ye stay in the main areas and don't wander into the wilder areas of our little district and ye should do just fine." The ferry begins to approach its destination, slowing down. 

"One last thing, dearies." She says with a grin. "In Ferrytown, if someone asks you 'can I have your name?', the polite response is 'no, but you can call me...'" Each person riding the ferry sees her wink at specifically them, before she ducks back to the underbelly from whence she came. 

When you finally step off into the town, you can see her already on shore, boarding passengers for the return trip. Her hair is blonde, rather than grey or brown, and she appears to be in the midst of a conversation that has been going on for quite some time. You shake off the strange feeling and wander off into the metropolis.

Ferrytown, also known as Feytown or Feyrytown to the locals, is the most natural burrough due to its series of 'bleeds', areas where the feywild bleeds over into the material plane, creating pockets of vibrant colors, intoxicating smells, beautiful flora and fauna, and denizens not typically found on this plane. These areas are mostly home to the more fantastical residents of Ferrytown, though peoples of all types live across the burrough. Many of these areas are seen as parks, and fulfill that function for many of the residents as well as visitors. The most notorious use of these bleeds, however, is their application in smuggling. The grassy bay bleed being a patchwork of the material plane and the feywild means that it is difficult to track and follow shipments in and out, especially when the smugglers are much more connected to the area than those pursuing them.

There are no bridges to or from ferrytown, as all transport not by land is done by ferry. As the burrough is largely known for its transport by water, both of people and goods, the burrough has three separate ports, not counting the grassy bay bleed. All sorts of goods pass in, out, and through Ferrytown, though there is one exception: iron. Creatures from the feywild, particularly fey, find iron unpleasant to the point of it hurting when they make contact with it. As such, ferrytown does not trade in iron, and its use is frowned upon in the burrough. The buildings, tools, weapons, and armor in ferrytown are not constructed from iron or iron-based metals, and are instead largely built from wood and glass. The wood comes from various trees throughout, though the largest and best-known tree is Cael’fii, or the gentle mother.

### Shifter Transformation:

If a player sleeps in Ferrytown parks for a cumulative 7 days, their race permanently becomes shifter unless they are fey. When replacing your race with shifter, you lose all racial features from your original race, apart from any flying, swimming, or climbing speed and all but one of any proficiencies granted by your race. To clarify, a player will lose one racial proficiency of their choice (if their race had no proficiencies this has no effect) and all non-proficiency, non-flying, swimming, or climbing speed features from their original race. They will replace their original ability score improvements with those from their shifter subrace, and gain all appropriate shifter features apart from languages and proficiencies. Which shifter subrace a player is will be up to DMD/mariam depending on their animal, though most will be given a choice between two options. For example, someone who becomes a bear shifter might be a longtooth or a beasthide shifter.
