Pigeon Lord v2

Pigeon Lord.
Wreathed in their feathered friends, these hardy and insightful wanderers are connected to Empire City’s hivemind network of Pigeons. Robed in patchwork leather coats and earth tone scarves, their mystical connection to the pigeons is as innate as breathing. High upon their roof top roosts, they are the eyes of the city. 
Quick Build
You can make a Pigeon Lord quickly by following these suggestions. First, Constitution should be your highest ability score, followed by Intelligence. 
Class Features
As a Pigeon Lord, you gain the following class features.
Hit Dice: 1d6 per pigeon lord level
Hit Points at 1st Level: 6 +your Constitution modifier
Hit points at Higher Levels: 1d6 (or 4) +your Constitution modifier per pigeon lord level after 1st
Proficiencies
Armor: Light, Medium
Weapons: Simple weapons, guns, longswords, shortswords
Tools: Cobblers tools
Saving Throws: Constitution, Intelligence 
Skills:  Take Animal Handling, Then Choose Two from Insight, Perception, Sleight of hand, Stealth, and Survival
Spellcasting
A Pigeon Lord should never be without bread. These highly mystical creatures require bread to use their spell slots for you in combat. The amount of bread that it takes to cast spells is equivalent to spells that you are requesting them to cast by gently cooing. Feeding pigeons bread brings them to your position and will cause them to cast with the same requirements as a normal spell caster. When you run out of bread (which magically regenerates after a long rest) you can no longer request that they cast for you. Your bread is contained in a pouch and counts as their spell casting focus. It is assumed that you are also tossing them the appropriate material components. Pigeons do not know cantrips.
Spell Slots
The Pigeon Lord table shows you how many spell slots the pigeons have to cast their spells of 1st level and higher. To cast one of these pigeon lord spells, your pigeon must expend a slot of the spell’s level or higher. They regain all expended spell slots when you finish a long rest.

Equipment
You start with the following equipment:
Any two simple weapons
A simple handgun and 20 bullets
Your choice of studded leather armor or scale mail
Thieves’ tools and a dungeoneer’s packAnd a scarf that can be used as a 10 foot rope.
In addition to the equipment granted by your background.
Ability Score Increase
When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.
Coo Tounge
Through years of isolation and living with your pigeons you have learned their hidden language. An outlandish unimaginable combination of coo’s and chirps that allow you to ask them for the spells and abilities they grant you. Only other pigeon lords or pigeons can understand you while in conversation.
Fresh Batch
When you reach level 7 you may regenerate up to half of your bread pool with a short rest. 
Tethered flight  
If you could get enough pigeons to carry you by strings held under the dorsal feather, could you fly? Yes. Beginning at level 11, you may summon a flock of pigeons to move you or any creature that you can see. If the creature is unwilling, they must make a con save equal to your spell save dc. The target gains a flying speed of 10 feet per round for one minute. An unwilling creature may repeat the save at the end of their turn. When the feature ends, the target falls if it is still aloft, unless it can stop the fall. You may use this feature a number of times per day equal to your constitution modifier with a minimum of one.
Flock of burdenPigeons are not known for walking. However, some are known for rolling. Beginning at level 11 you may take 10 minutes to summon a flock of rolling pigeons to move your things for you. The duration of the flock will be 1 hour.This feature creates a circular, horizontal plane of made of pigeon feathers, heads, backs and feet, 3 feet in diameter and 6 inches thick, that rolls along the ground in an unoccupied space of your choice that you can see within range. The flock remains for the Duration, and can hold up to 500 pounds. If more weight is placed on it, the pigeons die, and everything on the disk falls to the ground.
The flock is immobile while you are within 20 feet of it. If you move more than 20 feet away from it, the flock rolls along and  follows you so that it remains within 20 feet of you. It can move across uneven terrain, up or down Stairs, slopes and the like, but it can't cross an elevation change of 10 feet or more. For example, the flock can't move across a 10-foot-deep pit, nor could it leave such a pit if it was created at the bottom.
If you move more than 100 feet from the flock (typically because it can't move around an obstacle to follow you), they will get bored and leave causing the feature to end.You may use this feature a number of times per day equal to your constitution modifier with a minimum of one.
Sub Classes: The Flock of Names
Me and a Pigeon named Steve.
Starting at 3rd level, you become owned by a pigeon named Steve. The pigeon assumes a bond with your left shoulder. Steve has the game statistics of a canary, and it always thinks of your commands as its own idea which you can give directly through your innate connection with it while it is within 100 feet of you. Steve has a fly speed of 30.
While Steve is perched on your shoulder, you gain +1 to ac and a bonus to your passive Charisma (Perception) score and to Wisdom (Animal handling) checks. The bonus equals your Charisma modifier. While perched on your shoulder, the Steve can’t be targeted by any attack or other harmful effect; only you can cast spells on it; it can’t take damage; and it is incapacitated. 
Steve’s can see through your eyes and hear what you hear while it is within 100 feet of you. As a courtesy, he will allow you to see and hear what he sees and hears too. In combat, Steve always goes after your turn. Again, it assumes that your thoughts are it’s own idea and will act accordingly. If it is slain by a creature, you gain advantage on all attack rolls against the killer for the next 24 hours. Long live Steve.
Steve doesn’t require sleep. Steve does require cuddles while in private. While it is within 100 feet of you, it can awaken you from sleep. If it senses danger, roll a d20. On an 8 or higher to alert you to danger as a bonus action. On a 7 or lower it will awaken you to alert you to of Steve’s need for more bread.
Steve explodes when it dies, if you die, or if the two of you are separated for more than 15 minutes. If Steve dies, another identical pigeon named Steve will assume Steve’s old position as your owner and land on your shoulder after a long rest.
Me and a Pigeon named Marv.
Starting at 6th level you may expend 2 uses of your bread as a bonus action to summon Steve’s scrawnier cousin Marv. Marv coo’s are far more soothing than his mangled appearance would leave a spectator to assume. Marv’s coos grant itself and each creature of it’s choice within 10 feet of it a number of temporary hit points equal to 1d8 + your Constitution modifier (minimum of +1). The number you rolled is the number of pigeons who land on the target receiving hit points. They will take the amount of damage represented. Marv will continue to sing the song of his people for 10 minutes. Marv has a fly speed of 40.
 
Your time spent cooing has caused you to develop a masterful command of the song of Marv’s people, enhancing the healing and damage you create through him. Whenever you spend bread to cast a spell you may spend an additional usage of bread to gain two bonus dice to your roll of the spell’s damage. That roll must restore hit points or be a damage roll. The damage type dealt by the spell becomes your choice of thunder(flap flap flap), psychic (pigeons be crazy), necrotic (pigeons be gross), or poison (pigeons be dirty) damage.
Me and Bad Bertha
Starting at tenth level, in pigeon society, you have become as iconic as one of the many statues of Empire City. By expending 3 uses of your bread as a bonus action, Bad Bertha can be tempted to claim your head as her roost. When Bertha claims your head as her roost, she will summon a mighty flock to her aid. Her flock can target any creature you can see with a range of 80 feet. As an action you can request in coo tongue that her flock hinder your mutual foe. To request that she hinder more foes, more uses of bread (one more use per subsequent foe) may be given on subsequent turns. Each target must succeed on a Wisdom (your spell save DC plus con mod) saving throw or be charmed by you until the spell ends, or until you or your companions do anything harmful to it which will scatter the flock. While charmed in this way, a creature can do nothing but use its movement to approach you in a safe manner. While an affected creature is within 5 feet of you, it cannot move, but simply stares at Bad Bertha in wonder. At the end of each of its turns, an affected target can make a Wisdom saving throw. If it succeeds, this effect ends for that target. While under this effect, the creature’s move speed is reduced by 10.
Gunther’s Wall of pigeons.
At level 14 you may create a non magical wall of blood thirsty pigeons which congregates at a point you choose within range. The wall is 5 feet thick and ten feet high, and extends up to 80 feet in length.
If the wall cuts through a creature's space when it appears, the creature is pushed to one side of the wall (your choice). If a creature would be surrounded on all sides by the wall (or the wall and another solid surface), that creature can make a Dexterity saving throw. On a success, it can use its reaction to move up to its speed so that it is no longer enclosed as they rapidly descend.
The wall can have any shape you desire, though it can't occupy the same space as a creature or object. The wall appears in any orientation you choose: horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. It can be free floating, or it can rest on a solid surface.
The wall is an object made of pigeons that can be damaged and thus breached. Each 5 foot section has AC 15 and 100 hit points. Reducing a 5 foot section of the pigeons to 0 hit points destroys it. 
The wall blocks line of sight, but creatures and objects can attempt to pass through it. A creature entering the wall treats it as difficult terrain, and must pass a con save or take 3d10 magical piercing damage and be blinded.
Sub Class: The Flock of the Lame:
By 3rd level, the hivemind of cranium pigeons has taken pity on you. Knowing that you cannot do everything for yourself, not only will they help you cast, but they will help you fight too. Beginning at level 3, by expending one use of your bread, a flock of pigeons (one for each level of pigeon lord, each with 4 hp and an ac of 14) will carry a shield for you. The shield flutters in your space to protect you, granting you a +2 bonus to your ac. The pigeons are willing to hold the shield for you for one minute before they get too exhausted and return the shield to wherever they found it (most pigeons are actually very polite). If the pigeons are killed however, the shield drops to the ground. This feature may be used a number of times equal to your con modifier with a minimum of one.By 6th level, the hive mind of cranium pigeons has taken further pity on you. Your frail humanoid form shouldn’t have to swing a sword or aim a crossbow. The pigeons (who have every weapon proficiency) will be called by expending one use of your bread. A flock of pigeons (one for each level of pigeon lord, each with 4 hp) will carry and attack using a one handed weapon for you. The flock flutters within a 15 foot cube of you and follows you when you move. This attack is considered an extra attack (allowing you to cast and attack on a single turn). 
By 10th level, the hive mind of cranium pigeons is thoroughly invested in your success, because if it stopped now, what would have been the point of all those pigeons that got killed for you before? The pigeons (who still have every weapon proficiency) are assisted by a massive mutant pigeon (HP 30 AC 14 all saves are  +0). It will carry and attack using a two handed weapon for you. The mutant pigeon flutters within a 15 foot cube of you and follows you when you move. This attack is considered an extra attack (allowing you to cast and attack on a single turn). 
By 14th level the hivemind of cranium pigeons is unwilling to let you fail. An extraordinarily beautiful pigeon (HP 20 AC16, all saves are +2)  (which follows the rules of a familiar and is resummonable upon any rest) will also be able to attune one extra item for you.
