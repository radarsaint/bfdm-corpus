Recipe: 
Baked City Pigeon
-One freshly killed pigeon
-One onion
-One sprig of thyme
-Salt & Pepper
-Cedar plank
-Olive Oil


Dry brine pigeon in salt and pepper. Place onion and olive oil on cedar plank. Place thyme inside pigeon. Bake for 2 hours. Discard pigeon. Eat plank. Enjoy!
