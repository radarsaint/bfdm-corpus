Hampstead Players Guide.

Hampstead Players Guide

As each part of Empire city has its own unique flavor, each also has its own mechanics.  Some mechanics you will encounter as you go, there are some you need to track yourself, and be aware of just to be able to operate in the zone.

So without further ado, here are some mechanics all players should be aware of.

Hampstead Damnations

“The Alchemists were mad.  Those things should have never been experimented on.  Now the whole town is cursed to hell because of their perverse experiments against the natural order.  Don’t come to Hampstead, don’t breathe it’s air, Don’t go into the fog, and don’t let the bedbugs bite.”

Hampstead’s Damnation is the slow mutation of your mind, body, and some believe your very soul.  As you get exposed to the woes of Hampstead, you will receive layers of corruption that bind to your body, and force it to go through changes. The only way to remove levels of this damnation unless specified by the source of the damnation are:

A long rest outside of Hampstead will remove one level of long term damnation.

A long rest in Ferrytown will remove one additional level of long term damnation.

Being subjected to Remove Curse or Lesser Restoration will remove one level of long term damnation per casting (will not affect conditional damnation)

Greater Restoration or Wish can fully cleanse all levels of damnation from your body and soul.

These effects are not cumulative, so when you move up on this list, you lose the previous effect, and gain the new one.  Make sure to note each conditional level you are afflicted by, so that you know when you can remove it.

Special Note: Immunity to disease does not protect you from Hampstead Damnations.

1

Unease - Something doesn’t feel quite right.   You have disadvantage on all saves vs fear.  If you have immunity to fear effects, you now make the saving throws with advantage instead.

2

Paranoia - You have a feeling of being watched or followed by unknown agents.  You suffer a -1 to perception, investigation, and insight.

3

Dread - That feeling that has been crawling up your spine, in the corner of your eye, it’s now in your head.  Try as you might, you are not feeling like yourself, and others can tell.  You have disadvantage on all Charisma skill rolls, except Intimidation.

4

Sweet Relief - The feeling of unease, paranoia, and dread has left you.  Everything is fine, right?

5

Crawling Skin - Is there something crawling under your skin?  You can feel it, it feels like thousands of mites digging.  It itches, but no amount of scratching will alleviate the itch.  You suffer -1 to any Dexterity check, Dexterity saving throw, or Concentration check, as you want to scratch the itch all the time.

6

Cracking Bones - Your bones hurt.  It feels like every bone in the body is being flexed, pushed nearly to the breaking point, but not snapping.  Disadvantage to all Constitution Saving Throws

7

Soul Aflame - You have discovered something most people never do, that a soul can burn.  You feel as if the very essence that is you is being burned up, but never allowed to burn out.  It’s not pain in a traditional sense, but it is so much worse than any pain you’ve ever felt.  It doesn’t make you want to scream, but it overwhelms your senses.  Disadvantage to Perception, Investigation, and Insight checks (Disadvantage to these also causes a -5 to the passive scores)

8

Maddness in Pain - Your body is pain.  But you drink in the pain, rather than being put off by it.  You know the solution.  Seek out more of Hampstead’s Damnation.  Breath the fog, sleep in Hampstead overnight.  DC 15 Wisdom save when you get to this level (you may opt to fail this saving throw if you wish).  If you fail, you feel compelled to stay in Hampstead, and if you are unwillingly taken out, you cannot lose levels through rest until you come back.  In addition, all temporary levels stay with you until you are no longer at this level.

9

Euphoria - The pain is gone, you feel better than ever.  You gain +1 to all skill checks, but -1 to all saving throws.

10

Damned - You no longer feel the oppression of this list, and cannot move up or down on it again.  Contact a DM for what happens next.

Ways of getting Hampstead Damnation levels -

While in Hampstead, you suffer one level of conditional damnation, that is removed when you are outside the burrough.

If you long rest outdoors in Hampstead, you gain one level of long term damnation instead of losing a level.

If you breathe the fog you gain one level of long term damnation, but won’t gain additional levels from the fog for 12 hours.

Being brought back to life with spells or items (instead of with Science) gives a number of Levels of Damnation equal to spell level.

There are other sources for damnation that you will discover as you interact with the people and places of Hampstead.

As mentioned in this list, once you hit level 10 of Hampstead Damnation, contact a DM, and they will talk to you about what happens next.  Note, this may take out out of play for a little while, but will give you new roleplay and mechanic opportunities.

Hampstead Fog

Periodically, fog will roll in and vanish in Hampstead.  When this fog is around, not only does it give each player in any outdoor area of Hampstead a long term level of Hampstead Damnation (read above), but it limits all vision to 20 feet.  Anything beyond 20 feet has full cover and advantage on stealth rolls to hide.  Conversely, sound carries particularly well through the fog, causing disadvantage on stealth checks to move quietly.  The only outdoor part of Hampstead that is fog free is the Airship Port, but the path into town will be covered in fog.

Coming Back From the Dead

In the land of magic and science, death doesn't have to be the end, but can be a new beginning.  Players have two methods of coming back.  If they come back via magic, they gain a number of levels of Hampstead damnation based on the spell level.

If you don’t wish to come back via magic, there will be an NPC that can rebuild you, with some negotiation.  They may have to use extra parts from other cadavers and as such, this may cause a change of race.

Regardless of where you died in Empire City, or how much of a body you have left, you can seek new life in Hampstead.

Quartering Act

Hampstead is the home of the quartering act.  As such, rentable space is limited and costs extra.  In addition, all goods and services are so bare, they cost an additional 25% more due to scarcity.  Also All silver is to be handed over to the soldiers.  Anyone found with silver on them in Hampstead will have it confiscated and fined (10 gp in addition to the silver items lost).  If the fine isn’t paid, the individual is subject to being jailed.  Lastly anyone out after curfew, who is discovered by the local Law enforcement without a pass, is subject to a fine (100 gp) or being arrested.  Receiving a pass is difficult, but a single night’s counterfeit pass costs 10 gold, and is a DC 17 investigation for a guard to tell that it is counterfeit.
