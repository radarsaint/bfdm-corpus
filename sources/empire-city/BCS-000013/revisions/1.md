Lights on 
in Kingsbridge


It's been a lot of strange things happening in Kingsbridge ever since the Red coats attacked. We have reports of strange white room, we've had a new lamp lighter who keeps the lights on, and it looks like the umbral cup is under new management (the previous tenant violet stepped down without so much as a word). This might be a new turned leaf of the Kingsbridge area, maybe great good could come of this. We haven't heard much from the pinkertons but we know that they have been working harder as of late. Not much is known to the public right now. Strangely With these new positive changes, there are also rumors of a creature flying in the dark. I say that it might be some kind of large moth seen around the umbral cup. I saw a pretty white one inside, seemed a bit out of place. I'm there helping out my buddy bo harkness figure out some of the details of it all, previous owner left the place a mess and the building is upset(maybe she couldn't take a moth infestation). We're trying to get it nice and comfy, but we are trying to figure out these strange sights of bodies. we come back to the rooms and don't see the same thing, almost as if some crook is messing with us in some sick game. Melody Turner Coming to Town!
Melody Turner, of course, needs no introduction. We have just learned that the legendary Aviatrix is on her way to Empire City. The British Admiral and her famed ship, the Misty Blade, should be arriving at King George III Port within the week. Her presence will surely bolster Redcoat forces, but all the same it’s hard not to be in awe of the incredible accomplishments she has done throughout her illustrious career. Personally responsible for shooting down no less than 50 confirmed enemies, she went on to write the handbook for advanced air combat. I wish you all the best of luck because the skies are about to get much more dangerous.
