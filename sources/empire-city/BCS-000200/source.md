6 Shots \- A drinking game where you get 6 magical shot glasses set in front of you.  Everyone chips in an amount of gold, and whoever successfully drinks the most gets the pool.

Each shot glass corresponds to a stat:  
Shot of Weakness  
Shot of Stumbling  
Shot of Illness  
Shot of the Dunce  
Shot of Foolishness  
Shot of Repulsion

Each shot you take, if you pass the save, gives you a \+1 to that stat for 6 hours, if you fail, gives you a \-1 to that stat for 6 hours.  Each round the NPC will announce how potent the next shot is by rolling a d12+6 and pulling down a bottle associated with that potency.  Then the players will decide which shot glass to drink from.  The NPC will fill the shot glass, and the player rolls a saving throw tied to the stat in question.  If they pass, they glow golden for a moment, if the fail they glow a dull silver.  The shot glass vanishes back to the bar, and the next round starts.  Once all six shots are done, whoever succeeded the most gets the gold.  If there is a tie, the winners can split the pool, or agree to double down and do 6 more shots, but this time the save DC is 2d12+6.  Each time there is a tie, if they want to continue, the DC will go up by 1d12.

If someone wants to quit before they have drunk the 6 shots, they can walk away from the table, but will take a \-1 to each stat they have not drank the shot of yet.
