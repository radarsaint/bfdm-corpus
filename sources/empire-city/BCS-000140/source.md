Ferrytown Main Event Schedule.

Ferrytown Main Event Schedule

Week 1:

Day 1: Carnival

Saturday, July 10th.

You’ve just arrived in the Empire City, and what way to better kick off your visit than a raucous night of fun and whimsy on Coney Island? Players can compete in various tests of their abilities and win fun prizes! Several of these prizes will not be reasonably obtainable with the player’s current abilities, so the carnival will remain open on select nights in the future for players to win some of the more difficult items. Main NPC is Ferrah.

Day 6: Something in the Water

Thursday, July 15th

A shipment of valuable materials has gone missing, and now the water in a few areas has stopped behaving as it should. Prosser is anxious to get their shipments back up and running, and in order to do that you’ll have to calm the waters. An underwater adventure with special rules for combat, where players will be able to get a proper ‘in’ with the ferrytown council. Main NPC is Prosser.

Week 2:	The Tax

Day 11: Fading Light

Tuesday, July 20th

Ferrytown’s connection to the Feywild is weakening as magic becomes limited, and for those who call the wild areas of Ferrytown home, this could have disastrous consequences. Players will have to work together in multiple parks to help keep them intact as some of their parts held together by magic begin to degrade. A lower focus on combat, with much of this adventure requiring inventive solutions to numerous problems. Players will find it difficult to solve all of the issues, though unless they fail spectacularly there will be no persistent consequences. Main NPC is Sutri.

Week 3:	The Redcoats are coming.

Day 16: Chaos Reigns

Sunday, July 25th

In order to prevent the British from limiting magic again, you had to make some quick and drastic changes to the leylines around Empire City themselves. And in doing so, the bleed from the Feywild has become increasingly chaotic and erratic as the weave lashes out at the world around it. Players will have to hold off a mass of creatures for a set number of rounds before the flood can be stopped and the creatures calmed. Combat heavy, horde combat. Main NPC is Demetrius.

Day 21: Lockdown (Third Act Finale.)

Friday, July 30th

In order to better establish their hold on the Empire City, the British are looking next to Ferrytown. As a smuggler haven and the source of any remaining rebellion ports, they are seeking to lock it down entirely. Beginning over the night, there will be checkpoints set up between Ferrytown and the other districts. No players will be stopped or thoroughly questioned, though they may hear mention of a full lockdown later. At the beginning of the event, the main Ferrytown channels will be locked, and the players will be forced to continue any RP in channels for this event until the event is over. Main NPC is Trapero.

Players outside of Ferrytown at 6 will be in one group, while those inside at 6 will be in another. Those inside will be tasked with causing general chaos: disrupting redcoat communications, sabotage, and overwhelming checkpoints. Those outside will be in charge of protecting a shipment that needs to enter Ferrytown to help the revolution. They will have to convince several redcoats, though a fight may break out. Should the players succeed, the British will be forced to withdraw and preserve their troops.

Intermission.

Week 4:

Day 26: The battle of Brooklyn

Wednesday, August 11th

The British are here now, and this time they aren’t pulling any punches. A line has been drawn in Ferrytown. It is your job to defend that line. Players must engage in a large-scale military battle. Unlike the day 16 event, this fight will be more strategic and will hopefully involve some implementation of terrain mechanics and an evolving battlefield map. Very heavy combat with tactics. Main NPC is Pirrame.

Week 5:

Day 31: Morale Boost

Monday, August 16th

There’s no point in fighting a war if you can’t ensure peace after your victory. For a long time there was a division between industry and magic, fey and humanoid, wild and city. Tensions have been running high in the rebellion, and if you can help to mend those rifts, you may be able to lift some spirits. Players will be assigned to various groups of combined humanoid and fey troops, and go on small missions. Some combat, though should be on the easier side of 6pm events. Hope is that by bonding in combat, can help to seal rifts between groups. Players will have to both fight and prevent NPCs from going at each other’s throats. Main NPC is Uncle Paddy.
