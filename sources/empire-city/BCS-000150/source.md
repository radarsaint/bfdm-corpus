Monster Chess.

Monster Chess a 3 player mini game for Empire City.

Roll a d20 for initiative.

The first player chooses Red, Green or neutral.

Place your pieces within your claimed territory, repeat for the other two players.

Red and green get 4 pieces. Neutral gets 5 if they own 5.

Light squares count as ground. Dark squares count as water.

It costs 30 movement to move from one square to the next.

Players may dash as an action.

Players may only use one piece on their turn.

Combat plays out as with normal dnd using the stats of their owned Monster Chess pieces.

Monster Chess Pieces may be found in various places throughout Empire city. One starter set per person may be purchased for 50 gold.
