Hat Thief Fights, Apprehended 
By Heroic T. Rex
-by (L.) S. L. the Ex-Dead Frog


In early hours of the morning, a shocking encounter: witnessed by the writer, her familiar (K), and friends.
A pigeon. But not any ordinary pigeon, at least by City standards. Just sitting, bathing, harassing bystanders for food - none of the oddities which plague our pigeons.
The first oddity was caused by the Redcoats' vile 'taxes', stealing silver from anyone in the vicinity of the City - including the pigeon, which first seemed unfazed, despite silver flying out from who knows where under those feathers. But the loss of its hoard apparently drove it to madness: it attempted - and somehow succeeded - asserting dominance over K's pigeon form - horned, four-winged, and infinitely more menacing. Dominance intact, it took K's spot on the writer's hat.
Now, the writer would have had no issue with this, however! In a bout of insanity, the pigeon tried stealing said hat! Readers, take note - this hat could easily encompass the bird, but yet it succeeded! While only able to fly with it four feet above the ground, still a remarkable feat of might - before K roosted on the hat to weigh it down. The writer's friend R, able to briefly understand animals, decided to negotiate. The pigeon made its ultimatum, 'Hat and berries.'; R offered berries and a replacement hat, using his superior (to a pigeon) strength to prevent the hat's theft.
As witnesses were trying to safely eject it, a saviour arrived - Grimlock, resident T. Rex and objectively high quality bean. The bird had, in a quote from K, 'Overconfidence so high it overflowed to zero and filled up again.' - according to them, a common Infernal proverb. Nevertheless, the pigeon charged Grimlock, pulling along the hat, K, and even somehow R! Fortunately, it could not hold on for too long, flying upwards and leaving its unwilling passengers to fall; R took minor fall damage, but was alright.
The pigeon, in the meantime, had decided - unwisely - to fight Grimlock. It managed one ineffectual peck before the T. Rex let out a powerful breath, sending it sky-high like a reverse meteor. The clear victor, Grimlock the hero received the replacement hat and berries.


Readers - do your Civic Duty and appreciate heroes like Grimlock today! She likes scritches behind the ears.
