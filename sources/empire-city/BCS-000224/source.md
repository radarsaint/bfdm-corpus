The Highgate Hunting Club
Monsterslayers and Safari Hunters
Highgate Cemetery is known as one the Magnificent Seven
Cemeteries of London. It is from these majestic hallowed
grounds that The Highgate Club takes its name.This well
funded group of monster hunters is known through their
tales as told in the Strand Magazine, a publication well
known for its serial adventures and mysteries. It is chock full
of eccentric and wealthy enthusiasts, crackpot theories,
doomsday preppers and big game hunters, there is no
challenge too dangerous for the Highgate Club.
A Card Carrying Member
As a member of the club, you are likely to adorn any place you
live with massive taxidermied monsters and glorious trophies
of your hunt, or the hunters that you admire most. Your
interactions with the club may be as well (possibly over)
armed groups that are each complete with their own themes
and dramatic trappings (such as ornate armor or a particular
focus on a specific kind of Old World cryptid like vampires or
werewolves).
Feature:Ballroom Brawlers
You are in contact with other eccentric members of the
club. These people might be associated with the criminal
underworld, the rough-and-tumble folk of the streets, or the
vain glorious members of high society. This connection
comes in the form of a contact in any city you visit, a person
who can provide you with advantage on checks related to
finding specific types of Monsters (non humanoid Beasts,
Monsters, Aberrations and Cryptids)

Suggested Characteristics
Your bond might involve other hunters or the organizations or
individuals that employ you or whom you employee. Your
ideal could be associated with your determination to always
catch your quarry or your desire to maintain your reputation
for being a great monster hunter.
d6 Traits
1 I always have a plan for what to do when things go
wrong. My wallet.
2 I am like a leaf on the wind. Always calm and able to
change with my circumstances.
3 The first thing I do in a new place is note the locations
of every possible threat or weakness.
4 Friendships and camaraderie are more important to me
than a successful hunt.
5 A true hunter embodies patience.I can outwait my
prey.
6 Having part of a plan is better than no plan. Just, never
tell me the odds.
7 Challenging my competency will always result in me
needing to prove myself.
8 Despite the many advantages of my lifestyle, I have a
very short fuse.
d6 Ideals
1 Honor. I take responsibility for the lives that I end and
the repercussions of my actions.. (Lawful)
2 Freedom. Barstools are meant to be broken, and the
best hunts are spontaneous. (Chaotic)
3 Charity. I am bringing people into the order so that
they may have a place in the world.
4 Greed. I hunt monsters for profit. (Evil)(Neutral)
5 People. I’m loyal to my friends, everyone else can go
cry in a bucket. (Neutral)
6 Redemption. I hunt to make up for my own monstrous
ways. (Good)
d6 Bonds
1 My obsession with monster hunting has lost me my
inheritance. I owe to a generous benefactor.
2 My trophies represent memories of good triumphing
over evil.
3 A rival got the kill shot while I was still aiming. I must
be a better huntsman.
4 There will never be a greater story or song than the
ones that will one day describe my triumphs.
5 I’m a serf who hunts to impress a Noble, be it for gain
or love..
6 While the other hunters take risks, I take the time to
protect my group members and ensure their success.
d6 Flaw
1 I’m trigger happy, kill it now. Kill it with fire and sticks.
2 I am unable to fund my own activities, so I’m
constantly trying to invent things with makeshift
materials and roughshod craftsmanship.
3 I’m likely to ignore a plan that I didn’t come up with.
4 I never miss a chance to boast or exaggerate.
5 I’ve never actually killed anything. I just like taxidermy.
6 I pester the others about the morality of their choices
while making no qualms about the murders I commit
with the other club members.
