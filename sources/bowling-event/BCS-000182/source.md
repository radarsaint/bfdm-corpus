Bowling Event:

Roll for Damage Bowling Event for the Cascadia Gamers Alliance -Written by Brendon Faulkner
Adventure 1: The Big Blackcloak.
Introduction (spoken like an old cowboy):
“A way out west there was a fella,  fella I want to tell you about, fella by the name of Halaster Blackcloak. At least, that was the handle his lovin' parents gave him, but he never had much use for it himself. This Blackcloak, he called himself the dandy. Now, dandy, that's a name no one would self-apply where I come from. But then, there was a lot about the dandy that didn't make a whole lot of sense to me. And a lot about where he lived, like- wise. But then again, maybe that's why I found the place s'durned innarestin'...They call Waterdeep the City of Splendors. I didn't find it to be that exactly, but I'll allow as there are some nice folks there. 'Course, I can't say I seen Neverwitner, and I never been to Netheril, and I ain't never seen no queen in her damn undies as the fella says. But I'll tell you what, after seeing Waterdeep and thisahere story I'm about to unfold--wal, I guess I seen somethin' ever' bit as stupefyin' as ya'd see in any a those other places, and in Common too, so I can die with a smile on my face without feelin' like the good Bahaumut gypped me.Now this story I'm about to unfold took place back in the early day--just about the time of our conflict with T’mat and the ‘Ragon Culty types. I only mention it 'cause some- times there's a man--I won't say a hee-ro, 'cause what's a hee-ro?--but sometimes there's a man.And I'm talkin' about the dandy here-- sometimes there's a man who, wal, he's the man for his time'n place, he fits right in there--and that's the dandy, in Waterdeep....and even if he's a crazy man, and the dandy was certainly that--quite possibly the Craziest in Waterdeep..which would place him high in the runnin' for Craziest worldwide--but sometimes there's a man. . . sometimes there's a man.Wal, I lost m'train of thought here. But--aw hell, I done innerduced him Enough. 
Now This Crazy old mage, he had a penchant for rolling at the lanes, and I’m fixin to think that you do too. Come on down to roll a few with us. In character (if you know what I mean) and see if we can’t stop the whole world from going crazy too. 
Mechanics:
Strikes: A strike is critical damage. Deal 20 damage to your enemy. (If you roll a Strike you can use a “Bonus Action”- don’t roll,  to use a Health Potion, if you have one, on yourself, healing 15HP)
Spares: +5 Gold pieces
Any cosplay done grants you plus one pin to any ball thrown that isn’t a gutter ball.
Every pin you knock down is damage done to the enemy for that encounterEvery pin left standing is damage done to you.Encounters last through a certain number of frames.
If a monster is defeated prior to frame completion, all pins knocked down at that time become loot and count as 1 GP.
If a monster is not defeated prior to frame completion, it is assumed the PC’s run away in fear and humiliation. 
All characters start with a Health Potion and may be used on a fallen comrade using a Action (First Roll of a Frame) and the character will receive 2x the number of pins downed on your roll as HP.
Each Character Ability can be used once per encounter.
Intended for 5 groups of adventurers. 5 players in each group.

Dwarf Battlerager Barbarian
HP 50
Damage: Slashing (Poleaxe)
Known as Kuldjargh (literally “axe idiot”) in Dwarvish, battleragers are dwarf followers of the gods of war and take the Path of the Battlerager. They specialize in wearing bulky, spiked armor and throwing themselves into combat, striking with their body itself and giving themselves over to the fury of battle.
Class Feature: Rage! In order to enact your rage feature, make your angriest face at your team before you bowl! 
As a battle rager, imagine that it is not the ball which strikes pins, NAY! It is you! Wrapped in spiked armor being hurled forth into the chaos of battle. Any damage you take is also damage your enemy takes. 
Class Feature: Gutter ball rageOn any gutter ball you roll, you go into a fit of blind rage. Roll a D6 and both you and the enemy take that much damage.

Encounter Mechanics: 
Beginning of the game PC’s will roll for initiative 
No AC’s, pins down are dmg to Baddy, pins up are dmg to PC. All damage for your turn is totalled after you have either thrown a strike, or attempted a spare. 
Some enemy abilities happen when an even (or odd, you decide) of pins are left up.
No abilities can affect a PC’s ability to roll their second ball. 
Actions and bonus actions: Your first roll is your attack action. You may only take a bonus action to do something other than attack (such as pick up a dropped weapon or use a health potion if you have one) if you do not need to attempt a spare.
When/if a monster is defeated prior to frame completion, all pins at that time become loot. Team with the highest sum of gold at the end of 10 frames wins.
Players begin with a hp potion that may be used to revive a fallen comrade. It will take your action and the player will receive 2X the number of your roll as hp.
Skill are useable once per encounter.
Encounter 1: It really tied the room together.
Frame 1
Resistances: None
Hit Points 33
Your room at the inn is plain. Rundown, even. It’s meager furnishings have become very familiar to you and your party. To the point that it's even begun to feel like home. Typically the room costs 20 gold a week. It’s steeper than in the country, but here in Waterdeep, that amount is just a pittance compared to what most homes run. Fortunately, you and your party have become friendly to the lonely failed bard who is incharge of the inn. Even though you are late on the rent, he is willing to let it slide so long as you are willing to attend his show (a painful attempt at interpretive dance).
He meets you at your door just before you enter, and reminds you that the rent is due.
You force yourselves to smile and make polite small talk. 
As you say your goodbyes, you turn and enter your room. Something is different. There's a faint smell of something acrid.
As you enter your room, roll initiative.
The carpet, scratch that, your favorite carpet, the one that really tied the room together, jumps up and attempts to strangle the person with the highest initiative.
Smother. Melee Weapon Attack:This special attack happens when an odd number of pins are left up after the first ball (leaving an odd number of pins up after the second ball does nothing). The first person to leave an odd number of pins up becomes grappled. The carpet can only grapple one PC at a time. Damage Transfer. While it is grappling a PC, the carpet takes only half the damage dealt to it, and the PC grappled by the carpet takes the other half.
To break the grapple, the PC currently grappled must leave up an even number of pins on their second ball or pick up the spare. OR if the carpet loses more than 10 hp it will drop it’s grapple. If a PC breaks free or it has taken enough dmg to drop a currently grappled PC, the carpet will attempt to grab the next person to bowl.
Gain 10 gold per player standing.  
Encounter 2 Say what you want about the Kobolds, at least they have an ethos.Frame 2
Resistances: Magical does half damage
Waking up after a fitful night's rest you wander out into the town. Light on coin, and looking for work, you peruse the listings of work posted near the tavern. Clearing out a cellar of rats, a lovelorn woman asking for ingredients for a love potion, and all of your standard fair. Not looking for anything too back breaking before lunch, you find a poster of an adorable Bunny who seems to have escaped her gilded cage. The Bunny belonged to an elderly man in a wheelchair who seems to think that it may have been kidnapped. The quest details include a cave of Kobold Nihilists.
If you have any gold at this point you may spend some of it on healing potions, but be warned! Your gold is going to be what counts in the end. Healing potions are 10 gp and heal for 15 HP. 
Your previous encounters initiative seems to have gotten you through the last encounter alright, so go ahead and continue bowling in the same order. 
You enter the cave. The rogue finds some poorly constructed traps and makes short work of them. 
The cave system has a small stream in it. Eight of the Kobolds seem to be eating together. Another is sleeping in a raft that seems to be circling their den like a lazy river. They are unaware as you begin to wantonly assault their humble den. 
HP 45 (9 kobolds. 5 points per Kobold)
Kobolds: Pack tactics. 
All of the damage that the kobolds will do will be determined by your rolls as before. However. It will have a plus one modifier for every kobold still standing. For instance. If the first person rolls a 6 the pins generate 4 damage to you plus 1 per kobold standing at 39 points 8 kobolds remain making the damage to your character (4+8) 12 points. 
Gain 12 gold per player still standing.
Encounter 3: You are out of your Element, Donny!
HP 50
Frame 3
Resistances: Piercing, Slashing, Blunt and Fire all do half damage.
Weakness: Ice does double damage.
After the raid on the Kobold Lair (even if there are some still standing), you discover the Bunny had kidnapped herself! Not wanting to return someone into imprisonment, you set her free in the wood.
As you make camp that night, you reminisce about your lives and bond under the stars. Late at night, so late that it is nearly morning, you hear in the distance several voices speaking. Your party gathers together just before day break to investigate. It appears that you have come across some sort of small outdoor shrine. Two giant elemental creatures are speaking to one another. As they speak, they appear to be dining on offerings left for them at the shrine. As they eat they begin to shine a bit as the coals of their skin begin to ignite.
The heavier set one, the one called “Wall of fire,” begins speaking to someone unseen while the skinnier one with the sad but friendly face has his back turned, apparently engaging in some sort of game. 
“This is about aggression, Dandy” Wall of fire says, gaining a sort of fierceness to his voice. “And this aggression will not go unchecked!”
“What are you talking about Walter” The sad faced friendly one says.“Forget it, Donny, you aren’t in your element” Wall of fire dismisses him.
“Well yeah, it was my favorite animated rug, and those guys at the inn just killed it.” The disembodied voice of the Dandy rings out over the woods.“And it really tied the room together, did it not?” Wall of fire replied.“Hey, I’m an Elemental, you are too” Donny attempts to jump back into the conversation just in time for Wall of fire to notice you.
All of your HP is healed from the previous encounter after your short rest. 
The Elementals have no other special abilities besides their resistances.
Gain 15 gold per player left standing.
Encounter 4: The siege of the Tree Horn Treant
Hp 90
Building Hp 60
Frames 4-5
Resistance: Blunt
Weaknesses: Fire, Slashing
After your encounter with the elementals, and a long walk back toward town, you come across a small cabin in the woods. The choice to proceed into the dark seems like a bad one. After all, in the dark you are likely to be eaten by a Grue.
The Cabin is creepy at first, but once you get the fire going, it's still creepy, but with a warm fire to distract you. Also there are pleasant food smells that help you forget about the seemingly endless taxidermy collection.
The wind begins to blow and howl. And as it does, tree branches rake against the window and cause you to jump. No big deal. Completely normal. Until it isn’t.
The tree rears up and slams into the cabin, breaking a large hole in the wall. It’s trunk turns and twists until a large face is revealed in its bark. 
The building seems to quake before you. The woods are dark and full of things that are scary. This building is all that is standing between you and leaving yourself out on the ground like some sort of hobo party. All damage done by the Treant is done to the building first. 
He takes two attacks per round (The pins you leave standing x2). Defeat the Treant before the building falls lest you sleep in the mud tonight, covered only in the shame of defeat.
Gold: 20 per adventurer standing. +100 party gold if the building is still standing.
Encounter 5
The Valkyrie Maude
Frames 6-7
HP 110
Resistances: none
On your way back to town, the next morning something can be seen in the sky circling you. A woman on a flying horse gives an unusual sounding cackle. The Valkyrie Maude has spotted you. She is a recluse and an enigmatic artist. She comes from money and thinks that it could be very practical to make friends with a band of adventures like you, even if you are just a bunch of hapless pedestrians. However, her friends think that you are beneath her. Also, she rides a flying nightmare steed that hungers for human flesh, and although she herself bares you no ill will, that horse of hers is going to try to eat you now. 
Negative Energy aura. Whenever you attempt to attack the stead, its Aura will cause you to take an extra 5 damage.
The steed’s breath billows with smoke, it's beginning to obscure your vision. You must knock down at leasts 2 pins to do any damage to the nightmare. Under a 2 will cause you to lose track of your enemy for this frame.
When the frames are over, Maude will give you 25 gold each for every person left standing. If you slay her nightmare steed however, she will let you sell its body back in town for an additional 250 gold.
Encounter 6
Halastar Blackcloak as The Dandy.
Frames 8-10
HP 150
After returning to the inn and resting, you awaken to a knock at your door. Apparently this room used to belong to The Dandy. It's been his home all along, and he’d like it back now. 
“Also, you can’t just like, barge in here, man.”Apparently he has had a long night, and he is just kind of fed up with running into all of the people you just done battle with. 
As you begin to try to reason with him, one of you pipes up saying “Excuse me, Mr. Blackcloak.”
“Hey, one more thing.” He replies. “I’m not Mr. Blackcloak, your Mr. Blackcloak, I’m the Dandy, so that's what you call me. That or, uh, His Dandyness, or uh, Dander, or El Danderino, if you're not into the whole brevity thing.”
His meek form swirls and changes into a wild haired wizard in a bathrobe and jelly slippers. He holds aloft an orb of fierce thundering power. A churning storm cloud forms around the sphere and a storm begins to brew. The Dandy casts STORM OF VENGEANCE. For the next 3 frames, the spell will slowly grow stronger.
Frame one: Acid Rain.
Take 3 damage per frame.
Frame two: Hail.
For this frame only, take double damage.
Frame three: Frozen gusts of wind. 
If the Dandy hits you for more than 5 points of direct damage (not including the acid rain) You are a frozen adventuresicle.
Gold 25 per adventurer left standing. 
Bonus 500 gold if you defeat the Dandy. 
Upon completion, if you defeat the Dandy, congratulations, his fierce visage recedes and he becomes the docile and fun loving Dandy we all know and love. Congratulations, even if you failed on the other encounters, you have beaten the game. If there are other teams competing alongside you, tally up your gold and compare it. The team with the highest amount of gold as a group is the winning group. 
If all of your adventures are dead or frozen, you will be resurrected or thawed in a day, but the scoundrels and ruffians of the town will have sacked half of your belongings.
