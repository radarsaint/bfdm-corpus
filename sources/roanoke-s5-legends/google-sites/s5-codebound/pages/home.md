Oath of the Codebound

Paladins who follow the

Oath of the Codebound

embody the principles of the cowboy code, upholding honor, fairness, and truth in all their actions. They are steadfast guardians of justice and protectors of the innocent, guided by a strong sense of duty and respect for their surroundings. These paladins adhere to a strict code of conduct, never wavering from their principles.

Oath Spells

You gain oath spells at the paladin levels listed below. These spells are always prepared, and they don't count against the number of spells you can prepare.

3rd level:

Sanctuary, Shield of Faith

5th level:

Calm Emotions, Zone of Truth

9th level:

Aura of Vitality, Aura of Purity

13th level:

Banishment, Compulsion

17th level:

Circle of Power, Geas

Channel Divinity:

Codebound's Vow

Starting at 3rd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to invoke the power of the Codebound's Vow. As an action, you present your holy symbol and make a solemn vow to abide by the tenets of the cowboy code. For the next minute, you gain the following benefits:

Your attacks deal an additional 1d8 radiant damage against any creature that has attacked a non-hostile creature or broken a promise or trust within the last minute.

You have advantage on saving throws against effects that would cause you to break your vow or compromise your code.

You emit a calming aura, granting advantage on Charisma checks and saving throws to creatures within 10 feet of you.

Aura of Honesty and Honor

At 7th level, your unwavering commitment to truth blossoms into a radiant aura that envelops you and those within 10 feet of you. This divine essence grants you and your allies an innate advantage when it comes to navigating the treacherous realm of deception. Within this aura, you and your trusted companions find it easier to discern falsehoods, your instincts honed to recognize the subtlest hints of dishonesty; you have advantage on Insight checks to detect falsehoods. Furthermore, the strength of your conviction shields you from magical influence that seeks to twist your words. No enchantment or compulsion can force you to speak anything other than the unvarnished truth, as your resolute spirit triumphs over any attempt to bend your will. In the face of deceit and manipulation, your steadfastness remains unyielding, and the purity of your words shines forth as a beacon of absolute truth. Magic is unable to force you to lie.

Additionally:

your unwavering adherence to the principles of the cowboy code grants you the ability to swiftly respond to threats and protect those under your watch. When a friendly creature within 10 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to interpose yourself, becoming the target of the attack instead. This selfless act of heroism demonstrates your dedication to safeguarding others, allowing you to shield them from harm with your unyielding resolve. Additionally, if you have used your Divine Smite feature during this turn, you can add your Charisma modifier to your AC against the triggering attack, further exemplifying your mastery of both defensive techniques and righteous retribution. This feature can be used a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1), and all expended uses are replenished when you finish a long rest. Through your self-sacrifice and unwavering commitment to the code, you embody the spirit of a true protector, always willing to step in and defend those who cannot defend themselves.

Purity of Intent

At 15th level, your dedication to fairness and honorable combat enhances your strikes. Whenever you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you may expend a spell slot as with your Divine Smite feature, but choose to forgo dealing extra damage to instead impose disadvantage on the target's next attack roll against you until the end of their next turn.

Codebound's Redemption

At 20th level, you become the embodiment of the cowboy code, a paragon of honor and righteousness. As an action, you can unleash the full power of your oath. For 1 minute, you gain the following benefits:

You have resistance to all damage.

Your attacks deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage.

Whenever you hit a creature with a melee attack, it must succeed on a Charisma saving throw against your spell save DC or be frightened for 1 minute.

Friendly creatures within 30 feet of you have advantage on all saving throws.

Once you use this feature, you must finish a long rest before you can use it again.

Tenets of the Codebound

The Oath of the Codebound paladins uphold the following tenets:

1. Never Shoot First: You must never initiate violence or harm against others unless it is in defense of yourself or those who cannot defend themselves. Your weapon is a last resort, to be used only when all peaceful options have been exhausted.

2. Protect the Weak: You must never use your strength or abilities to harm those who are smaller or weaker than you. Instead, use your power to shield and defend them from harm, ensuring their safety and well-being.

3. Fairness and Honor: You must always fight with fairness, adhering to the principles of honor and justice. Never take advantage of your opponents' weaknesses or exploit their vulnerabilities. Engage in combat with integrity, giving your foes a fair chance.

4. Keep Your Word: Your word is your bond. Once you make a promise or give your word, you must always honor it. Your trustworthiness and reliability are vital to maintaining the integrity of your code.

5. Speak the Truth: You must always speak the truth, regardless of the consequences. Deception and falsehoods have no place in your words or actions. Your honesty and transparency inspire trust in others.

6. Show Compassion: Show kindness and gentleness towards children, the elderly, and animals. Protect and care for the vulnerable, providing them with comfort, support, and assistance. Your empathy and compassion are a testament to your character.
