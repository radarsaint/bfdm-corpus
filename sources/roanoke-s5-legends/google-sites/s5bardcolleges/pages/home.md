Bard: College of the Trail

In the world of wandering bards, names hold immense power. These roving minstrels possess a natural talent for granting names, and their spoken words carry a mysterious enchantment. Through their unique ability to bestow names upon others, they weave a tapestry of folk magic that resonates with the essence of their nomadic existence, creating a connection between language, identity, and the supernatural.

Beguiling Diversion

Starting at 3rd level, your mesmerizing performance allows you to not only charm the hearts of your audience but also to manipulate their focus. When you play a musical instrument, narrate an enthralling tale, or engage in a traditional Old West game (such as poker, faro, checkers, horseshoes, or Nine Man Morris) for at least one minute, you can use one usage of your Bardic Inspiration to unleash the power of the "Beguiling Diversion."

As part of this charm, humanoids affected by your Trailblazing Performance have disadvantage on Perception checks to notice anything else in their surroundings while they remain charmed by you. However, if a charmed creature does manage to make a Perception check, you can use your reaction to initiate the Beguiling Diversion. By doing so, you interrupt their focus and force them to reroll the check with disadvantage. This interruption can be used once per round, and it must be declared before the DM announces the result of the original roll.

This captivating ability allows you to redirect the attention of those affected by your charm, making it harder for them to detect threats or activities beyond your performance. By unleashing the Beguiling Diversion, you reinforce the notion that they should keep their eyes on you, enhancing your control over their awareness.

The charm effect ends immediately if your performance concludes, if any harmful actions are taken against the charmed creature, or after 10 minutes have elapsed, whichever comes first. Once the charm ends, the creature's attitude returns to its normal state, influenced by their true feelings and the events that transpired during the enchanting performance.

After using this feature, you must take a short or long rest before you can unleash the Beguiling Diversion again. This brief respite allows you to rejuvenate your captivating abilities, ready to inspire and enchant once more.

Note:

The Beguiling Diversion feature provides the bard with a reactive ability to maintain focus on the bard, even if the charmed creatures make Perception checks. The limitation of one reaction per round ensures the feature is balanced and not overly disruptive to gameplay.

Grant a name to one of your instruments.

A named instrument may be used as a spellcasting focus. When using your named instrument as a focus, you may use bardic inspiration after the DM declares a roll to be a success or failure to influence either a spell attack you make or a spell save your target makes. While performing with your named instrument, any performance check of 9 or lower is treated as a 10.

Level 6:

Harmonious Ensemble

You have honed your musical abilities to blend seamlessly with other musicians and bards. Whenever you perform alongside another creature who is proficient with a musical instrument, you both gain the following benefits:

Advantage on performance checks made while performing together.

If you both spend a bardic inspiration die during the same performance, the duration of the effects is extended by 1 minute.

Level 6:

An adventurer of any other name.

As a bonus action, you can spend one use of bardic inspiration to grant a nickname to a humanoid creature or an object within 60 feet of you. The nickname bestowed tends to stick around, becoming associated with the target. When you use a bardic inspiration in this way, your pool of inspiration points diminishes by 1 for 24 hours. Choose one of the following options:

Yclepes of Glory:

Nicknamed weapons or spellcasting foci gain the following ability: Once during the next minute, the damage type of attacks made with the weapon or through the focus is changed to your choice of psychic, force, necrotic, or radiant. The weapon or focus counts as a magical weapon for overcoming resistances. Additionally, attacks made with this weapon or through this focus gain an extra 1d4 damage of the chosen damage type on a hit. This increases to 1d6 at 11th level and 1d8 at 17th level.

Yclepes of Fortune:

Nicknamed spellcasting focuses or component pouches gain the following ability: Spells cast using the named focus or component pouch may have their spell save DC increased. The inspired creature may choose one save type: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. For the next minute, one spell cast using the named spell focus that requires a saving throw of the chosen type may have its DC increased by +1. This bonus increases to +3 at 11th level and +5 at 17th level.

Yclepes of Fame:

Nicknamed humanoid creatures gain expertise in a pair of skills of their choice for 24 hours. This does not apply to tool, vehicle, or instrument proficiencies or gaming sets.

Yclepes of Resilience:

As a bonus action, spend one use of bardic inspiration to grant a nickname to a humanoid creature within 60 feet. The creature gains the following benefits for 24 hours:

They have advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed.

When they roll hit points during a short rest or level up, they can reroll any 1s and take the new result.

Yclepes of Prosperity:

As a bonus action, spend one use of bardic inspiration to grant a nickname to a humanoid creature within 60 feet. The creature gains the following benefits for 24 hours:

Whenever they roll hit dice during a short rest to regain hit points, they roll an additional hit die and add it to the total.

They have advantage on ability checks made to find or obtain valuable items, such as treasure, gems, or rare materials.

Level 14: The Myth. The Legend.

As an action, you can cast the legend lore spell without expending a spell slot. You can use this feature twice per long rest.

Additionally, as a bonus action, you may spend one use of your bardic inspiration. Choose one creature you can see within 60 feet, other than yourself. That creature is granted legendary status for one minute. While inspired in this way, whenever the creature fails a melee, ranged, or spell attack roll, it can choose to succeed instead. This inspiration lasts for one minute or until used.
