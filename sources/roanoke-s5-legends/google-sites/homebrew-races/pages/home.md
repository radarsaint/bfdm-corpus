Jackalope!

Somewhat Unreliable:

These diminutive and short lived cryptids thrive in the woods and typically have very large families. While many are lost to the brutal nature of Arcania, a successful family (especially those that excel in the use of magic, or the roguish arts) can sometimes have families whose members number in the hundreds. With a life span that rarely passes 35 they tend to live in the moment and are not always careful planners, instead relying on their instincts and abilities instead of wisdom.

A plague of good cheer:

Jackalopes have integrated with colonies much quicker than the other native races. They understand the idea of trade, and are always seeking short term advantages. They have found that playing the fool can be a way to endear them to the newcomers. But a careful eye will notice that a Jackalope always has an angle to work. It’s common to find them in taverns acting as bards or busking as street performers. It is also common to find your pockets a lot lighter than you intended if you happen to spend too much time with one.

Cute and cunning:

It may be that their non-threatening appearance has opened doors for the Jackalope that other races may not notice, or perhaps more easily forgive their natural avarice. But a Jackalope will only share their deepest desires or secrets with someone they trust profoundly.

Jackalope rapidly ages in comparison to other races, reaching adulthood at the age of 7, marriageable at 20 but generally lives no more than 40 years.

Most Jackalope lean toward a neutral alignment. Although they have a propensity for avarice and self preservation, they are usually very outgoing and talkative. It can, at times, be very difficult to get to know most Jackalopes, as they tend to answer most questions with a question of their own. You know?

Jackalopes have advantage on perception checks having to do with hearing.

Jackalopes have a base movement speed of 30ft and a burrow speed of 15 feet. If burrowing incurves an attack of opportunity from another creature, that attack will be made at advantage until your movement is complete. Once burrowed in this way you will then gain a +2 to ac from cover until you emerge.

Living on the plains of Arcania has taught you to be ever vigilant, you gain advantage on perception checks using hearing. You also gain a burrowing speed equal to half your movement speed.

Tatankan

The Tatankan, a buffalo-headed minotaur-like race, hails from the vast plains of Minnesota, a land steeped in natural beauty and rich history. These noble beings embody the spirit of guardianship and unwavering loyalty, with a unique attribute that sets them apart from others. The Tatankan are known for their commitment to protecting the weakest among them. As a cultural tradition, they refuse to move faster than the slowest member of their community. This self-imposed restriction ensures that no one is left behind or left vulnerable to dangers that lie ahead. Physically, the Tatankan possess the sturdy, muscular build of their buffalo ancestors, with the head of a majestic buffalo atop their humanoid bodies. Their features are adorned with intricate tribal markings, symbolizing their deep connection to nature and ancestral heritage. The Tatankan possess exceptional strength and endurance, attributes gained from their buffalo lineage. They are also highly perceptive, able to sense the subtlest changes in their surroundings, making them excellent protectors and guardians. Their horns, both formidable weapons and symbols of strength, are often adorned with intricately carved totems representing their individual accomplishments or personal beliefs. Due to their deep reverence for nature and the land, the Tatankan have a natural affinity for druidic magic. Many of them dedicate themselves to the study of nature-based spells, using their innate connection to the earth and the spirits of the land to heal and protect their companions.

As a society, the Tatankan value communal living and the well-being of the whole above personal gain. They have established close-knit communities within the vast plains, constructing sturdy, earthy dwellings that blend seamlessly with the natural landscape. Each community is led by a council of elders, wise individuals who make decisions through careful deliberation and consensus. The Tatankan's unwavering dedication to protecting the weakest and their harmonious existence with the land make them a formidable and respected race within the realm of Arcania. Their stoic presence, fierce loyalty, and unique cultural traditions provide ample opportunities for engaging role-playing and storytelling experiences.

CHINNOKIN RACE DETAILS

SLEEK AND PLAYFUL:

With their fishy features and scaly skin, these creatures are more at home in water than on land They are slightly shorter than humans on average, ranging from well under 5 feet tall to just over 6 feet. They seem to grow larger as they age and don't always stop at adulthood They are fairly slim, weighing only 100 to 145 pounds. Males and females are about the same height and weight. Chinnokin range in color from dark green to bright red, and many have a combination of both colors. Some have a shimmering silver or rainbow colored stripe running down their side.

TRIBAL LIVING:

Most Chinnokin live out in the open ocean. But some tribes choose to live closer to the shallow waters near shore and trade with land dwellers. Seafaring vessels happen upon tribes of Chinnokin from time to time. They are usually friendly and eager to trade their treasures of the deep for the sailors goods, but some tribes have been known to be hostile to sailors.

RUNNING OF THE CHINNOKIN:

Chinnokin are seldom seen on land, but occasionally their curiosity gets the better of them, and they may take up residence in coastal cities. The main exception to their ocean dwelling life is when a Chinnokin has reached a point in their life cycle when they are ready to mate, somewhere around 50 years old It is said they travel far inland to the streams and pools of their birth. Large numbers of them arrive at the same time, as if driven by a subconscious urging. Once the time is right, the females will congregate together in a large group. And a great battle will ensue for the males. They will fight fiercely with each other. Most will die. A small group of victors will join the females in a ritual that lasts less than a day. The bodies of those fallen help to feed the new hatchlings. The adults move on. And the young fend for themselves as they swim to the sea. These young are nearly indistinguishable from fish, and do not gain sentience, or assume humanoid form for up to three years.

CHINNOKIN NAMES:

About 5 years after reaching the sea, Chinnokin children begin transforming into their adult form. They gradually increase in intelligence and seek out others of their kind The tribe that accepts them will give them a name. They will also take on the name of their tribe as their family name.

You absorb the moisture within a target, causing them to take damage as they are quickly dried. The target must succeed a Constitution saving throw, or take 1 d8 Necrotic damage immediately, and at the end of each of their turns. The spell ends as soon as the target succeeds on their saving throw.

Seraphs

Seraphs, celestial beings of ethereal grace and wisdom, possess a form of communication that transcends mundane speech. Seraphs have developed a pattern of speech that reflects their profound nature. Instead of relying on vocal inflections or facial expressions, they employ directed telepathy to convey their thoughts and emotions. When engaging in telepathic communication, Seraphs impart their messages with an inherent tone. These inherent tones serve as an additional layer of meaning, enhancing the depth and intention behind their thoughts. For example, when a Seraph wishes to express gratitude, their telepathic message carries a warm and gentle undertone, enveloping the recipient's consciousness with a sense of appreciation and thankfulness. The inherent tone of their message conveys the depths of their gratitude without the need for spoken words. Conversely, when a Seraph intends to convey concern or deliver a warning, their thoughts bear a subtle tone of caution. This tone is laced with an underlying sense of urgency, alerting the recipient to the gravity of the situation at hand.

The directed telepathy employed by Seraphs fosters an intimate and profound connection. Through this method, they establish deep bonds and shared experiences, as the full weight of their thoughts and emotions is directly conveyed to the recipient's mind. However, it is important to note that this form of communication is limited to individuals who possess the ability to receive and interpret telepathic messages. Seraphs bestow this gift upon those they consider worthy or trustworthy, ensuring that their intentions and wisdom are shared with care. In this manner, the Seraphs' unique speech pattern, reminiscent of the distinctive mannerisms of a certain race, utilizes inherent tones within their telepathic messages. Through this profound form of communication, they establish connections, convey emotions, and share their celestial wisdom with those fortunate enough to receive their telepathic transmissions.

You regain the use of this feature after finishing a long rest.

Renowned for their incredible strength, they have even developed a peculiar technique for catching salmon.
