Jackalope!

Somewhat Unreliable: These diminutive and short lived cryptids thrive in the woods and typically have very large families. While many are lost to the brutal nature of Arcania, a successful family (especially those that excel in the use of magic, or the roguish arts) can sometimes have families whose members number in the hundreds. With a life span that rarely passes 35 they tend to live in the moment and are not always careful planners, instead relying on their instincts and abilities instead of wisdom.

A plague of good cheer: Jackalopes have integrated with colonies much quicker than the other native races. They understand the idea of trade, and are always seeking short term advantages. They have found that playing the fool can be a way to endear them to the newcomers. But a careful eye will notice that a Jackalope always has an angle to work. It’s common to find them in taverns acting as bards or busking as street performers. It is also common to find your pockets a lot lighter than you intended if you happen to spend too much time with one.

Cute and cunning: It may be that their non-threatening appearance has opened doors for the Jackalope that other races may not notice, or perhaps more easily forgive their natural avarice. But a Jackalope will only share their deepest desires or secrets with someone they trust profoundly.

Jackalope Traits: Your Jackalope has a number of traits in common with all other Jackalope.

Ability Score increase: Your Dexterity increases by two and your Charisma increases by one.

Your creature type is:  Arcanian Cryptid.
Age: Jackalope rapidly ages in comparison to other races, reaching adulthood at the age of 7, marriageable at 20 but generally lives no more than 40 years.

Alignment: Most Jackalope lean toward a neutral alignment. Although they have a propensity for avarice and self preservation, they are usually very outgoing and talkative. It can, at times, be very difficult to get to know most Jackalopes, as they tend to answer most questions with a question of their own. You know?

Size: Your size is small. Jackalopes average a height of about 3 foot. Jackalopes have ears with vestigial muscles that can lay flat, or stand up and can swivel, and powerful oversized feet. With their oversized feet extended and their ears up, they can usually extend themselves by standing on their toes and have a toe-to-ear-tip height of about 5 feet. Jackalopes have advantage on perception checks having to do with hearing.

Speed: Jackalopes have a base movement speed of 30ft and a burrow speed of 15 feet. If burrowing incurves an attack of opportunity from another creature, that attack will be made at advantage until your movement is complete. Once burrowed in this way you will then gain a +2 to ac from cover until you emerge.

Prairie Dweller: Living on the plains of Arcania has taught you to be ever vigilant, you gain advantage on perception checks using hearing. You also gain a burrowing speed equal to half your movement speed.

Be Nimble. Be Quick. You can move through the space of any creature that is a size larger than yours.

Burst of Healing: As a bonus action, you can draw power from your warren to spend one of your Hit Dice and revitalize yourself or a creature you touch. Once you use this trait, you can’t use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

Laughing at death: When you make a death save, you may instead spend one of your remaining hit die to heal yourself back into the fight. However, this also permanently fails one death save per instance of your character being rendered unconscious until your next long rest.

Language:You speak read and write Common as well as Woodland Arcanian and Arcanian Sign Language.


Tatankan 

The Tatankan, a buffalo-headed minotaur-like race, hails from the vast plains of Minnesota, a land steeped in natural beauty and rich history. These noble beings embody the spirit of guardianship and unwavering loyalty, with a unique attribute that sets them apart from others. The Tatankan are known for their commitment to protecting the weakest among them. As a cultural tradition, they refuse to move faster than the slowest member of their community. This self-imposed restriction ensures that no one is left behind or left vulnerable to dangers that lie ahead. Physically, the Tatankan possess the sturdy, muscular build of their buffalo ancestors, with the head of a majestic buffalo atop their humanoid bodies. Their features are adorned with intricate tribal markings, symbolizing their deep connection to nature and ancestral heritage. The Tatankan possess exceptional strength and endurance, attributes gained from their buffalo lineage. They are also highly perceptive, able to sense the subtlest changes in their surroundings, making them excellent protectors and guardians. Their horns, both formidable weapons and symbols of strength, are often adorned with intricately carved totems representing their individual accomplishments or personal beliefs. Due to their deep reverence for nature and the land, the Tatankan have a natural affinity for druidic magic. Many of them dedicate themselves to the study of nature-based spells, using their innate connection to the earth and the spirits of the land to heal and protect their companions. 

As a society, the Tatankan value communal living and the well-being of the whole above personal gain. They have established close-knit communities within the vast plains, constructing sturdy, earthy dwellings that blend seamlessly with the natural landscape. Each community is led by a council of elders, wise individuals who make decisions through careful deliberation and consensus. The Tatankan's unwavering dedication to protecting the weakest and their harmonious existence with the land make them a formidable and respected race within the realm of Arcania. Their stoic presence, fierce loyalty, and unique cultural traditions provide ample opportunities for engaging role-playing and storytelling experiences.

Ability Score Increase: Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1.

Your creature type is: Arcanian Cryptid.

Age: Tatankan have lifespans similar to humans, reaching maturity around the age of 18 and typically living up to 80 years.

Alignment: The Tatankan's strong sense of duty and protectiveness often leads them towards lawful alignments, valuing order and the well-being of their community.

Size: Tatankan are large and robust, standing between 7 and 8 feet tall. Your size is Medium.

Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

Stalwart Protector: Due to your commitment to protecting the weakest among you, you refuse to move faster than the slowest creature in your group. As a result, your walking speed cannot exceed the speed of the slowest creature within 5 feet of you.

Charge of the Buffalo: When you move at least 20 feet in a straight line towards a target before making a melee attack, you can use your bonus action to make a horn attack. On a hit, the target takes additional bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier.

Nature's Resilience: You have proficiency in the Survival skill and advantage on saving throws against diseases and effects that would cause exhaustion.

Languages: You can speak, read, and write Common, Superior lakeland and Tatankan Knot language.

Subrace: Choose one of the following Tatankan subraces:

Plainsguard: The guardians of the open plains, Plainsguard Tatankan are known for their unyielding endurance and vigilance.

Ability Score Increase: Your Constitution score increases by 1.

Longstrider: You can cast the Longstrider spell once, regaining the ability to do so when you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

Spiritspeaker: Tatankan with a strong connection to the spirits of nature, they are skilled in the ways of druidic magic.

Ability Score Increase: Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

Druidic Affinity: You have proficiency in the Nature skill, and you can speak, read, and write Druidic.


CHINNOKIN RACE DETAILS

SLEEK AND PLAYFUL: With their fishy features and scaly skin, these creatures are more at home in water than on land They are slightly shorter than humans on average, ranging from well under 5 feet tall to just over 6 feet. They seem to grow larger as they age and don't always stop at adulthood They are fairly slim, weighing only 100 to 145 pounds. Males and females are about the same height and weight. Chinnokin range in color from dark green to bright red, and many have a combination of both colors. Some have a shimmering silver or rainbow colored stripe running down their side.

TRIBAL LIVING: Most Chinnokin live out in the open ocean. But some tribes choose to live closer to the shallow waters near shore and trade with land dwellers. Seafaring vessels happen upon tribes of Chinnokin from time to time. They are usually friendly and eager to trade their treasures of the deep for the sailors goods, but some tribes have been known to be hostile to sailors.

RUNNING OF THE CHINNOKIN: Chinnokin are seldom seen on land, but occasionally their curiosity gets the better of them, and they may take up residence in coastal cities. The main exception to their ocean dwelling life is when a Chinnokin has reached a point in their life cycle when they are ready to mate, somewhere around 50 years old It is said they travel far inland to the streams and pools of their birth. Large numbers of them arrive at the same time, as if driven by a subconscious urging. Once the time is right, the females will congregate together in a large group. And a great battle will ensue for the males. They will fight fiercely with each other. Most will die. A small group of victors will join the females in a ritual that lasts less than a day. The bodies of those fallen help to feed the new hatchlings. The adults move on. And the young fend for themselves as they swim to the sea. These young are nearly indistinguishable from fish, and do not gain sentience, or assume humanoid form for up to three years.

CHINNOKIN NAMES: About 5 years after reaching the sea, Chinnokin children begin transforming into their adult form. They gradually increase in intelligence and seek out others of their kind The tribe that accepts them will give them a name. They will also take on the name of their tribe as their family name.

CHINNOKIN TRAITS: You are at home in freshwater, salt water, and you even venture onto land on occasion. Gain the ability to breath both on land and underwater.

ABILITY SCORE INCREASE: Your Dexterity score increases by 2. Your Constitution score increases by 1.

 

AGE: Chinnokin live to be around 200 years old, but many die far sooner from dangers in the sea, or on their quest to mate.

Your creature type is:  Arcanian Cryptid.

ALIGNMENT: Chinnokin ply the open oceans and love their freedom. They are content living a carefree life. Then understand well that each moment may be their last, and thus are often chaotically good aligned

SIZE: Chinnokin range from under 5 to over 6 feet tall and have slender builds. Your size is Medium.

SPEED: Your base walking speed is 30 feet, and your swim speed is 30 feet.

DARKVISION: Accustomed to the darkness of the deep sea, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

TEMPERATE AQUATIC HABITAT: As long as you have no levels of exhaustion, you have resistance to cold damage, and have advantage on any saving throw involving cold damage Temperate Aquatic Habitat: As long as you have no levels of exhaustion, you have resistance to cold damage, and have advantage on any saving throw involving cold damage

SOAK: Chinnokin prefer to sleep submerged in water. When you take a long rest in water, you regain an extra hit die. In addition, you recover two levels of exhaustion instead of one. If you take a long rest out of water, gain one level of exhaustion up to the 4th level You can not progress further in levels of exhaustion (death) due to this feature.

LANGUAGES: You can speak, read, and write Coastal Arcanian.

WATER SHAPER: As a child of the sea, you can call on the elemental magic of water. You know the cantrips Dehydrate and Shape Water. At level five you also gain access to the Wall of Water spell Once you cast one of these spells with this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

DEHYDRATE Cantrip

• Casting Time: 1 action

• Range: 60ft

• Components: S,M (A few Grains of sand)

• Duration: 1 minute (requires Concentration)

• School: Necromancy

• Attack/Save: CON save

• Damage/Effect: Necrotic

You absorb the moisture within a target, causing them to take damage as they are quickly dried. The target must succeed a Constitution saving throw, or take 1 d8 Necrotic damage immediately, and at the end of each of their turns. The spell ends as soon as the target succeeds on their saving throw.

This spell's damage increases by 1 d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).

SHAPE WATER Cantrip

• Casting Time: 1 action

• Range: 3 Oft

• Components: S

• Duration: Instantaneous

You choose an area of water that you can see within range and that fits within a 5-foot cube. You manipulate it in one of the following ways: You instantaneously move or otherwise change the flow of the water as you direct, up to 5 feet in any direction. This movement doesn't have enough force to cause damage. You cause the water to form into simple shapes and animate at your direction. This change lasts for 1 hour. You change the water's color or opacity. The water must be changed in the same way throughout. This change lasts for 1 hour. You freeze the water, provided that there are no creatures in it. The water unfreezes in 1 hour. If you cast this spell multiple times, you can have no more than two of its non-instantaneous effects active at a time, and you can dismiss such an effect as an action.

WALL OF WATER 3rd-level evocation 

• Casting Time: l action

• Range: 60 feet

• Components: V, S, M (a drop of water)

• Duration: l0 minutes

You create a wall of water on the ground at a point you can see within range. You can make the wall up to 30 feet long, l 0 feet high, and l foot thick, or you can make a ringed wall up to 20 feet in diameter, 20 feet high, and l foot thick. The wall vanishes when the spell ends. The wall's space is difficult terrain.

Any ranged weapon attack that enters the wall's space has disadvantage on the attack roll, and fire damage is halved if the fire effect passes through the wall to reach its target. Spells that deal cold damage that pass through the wall cause the area of the wall they pass through to freeze solid (at least a 5-foot-square section is frozen). Each 5-foot-square frozen section has AC 5 and l 5 hit points. Reducing a frozen section to 0 hit points destroys it. When a section is destroyed, the wall's water doesn't fill it.

Seraphs
Seraphs, celestial beings of ethereal grace and wisdom, possess a form of communication that transcends mundane speech. Seraphs have developed a pattern of speech that reflects their profound nature. Instead of relying on vocal inflections or facial expressions, they employ directed telepathy to convey their thoughts and emotions. When engaging in telepathic communication, Seraphs impart their messages with an inherent tone. These inherent tones serve as an additional layer of meaning, enhancing the depth and intention behind their thoughts. For example, when a Seraph wishes to express gratitude, their telepathic message carries a warm and gentle undertone, enveloping the recipient's consciousness with a sense of appreciation and thankfulness. The inherent tone of their message conveys the depths of their gratitude without the need for spoken words. Conversely, when a Seraph intends to convey concern or deliver a warning, their thoughts bear a subtle tone of caution. This tone is laced with an underlying sense of urgency, alerting the recipient to the gravity of the situation at hand.

The directed telepathy employed by Seraphs fosters an intimate and profound connection. Through this method, they establish deep bonds and shared experiences, as the full weight of their thoughts and emotions is directly conveyed to the recipient's mind. However, it is important to note that this form of communication is limited to individuals who possess the ability to receive and interpret telepathic messages. Seraphs bestow this gift upon those they consider worthy or trustworthy, ensuring that their intentions and wisdom are shared with care. In this manner, the Seraphs' unique speech pattern, reminiscent of the distinctive mannerisms of a certain race, utilizes inherent tones within their telepathic messages. Through this profound form of communication, they establish connections, convey emotions, and share their celestial wisdom with those fortunate enough to receive their telepathic transmissions.

Ability Score Increase: Your Wisdom score increases by 2, and your Charisma score increases by 1.

Your creature type is: Divine Outworlder

Size: Your size is medium.
Astral Manifestation: Seraphs have the ability to manifest in the material realm, although it requires effort to maintain their presence. While in the material realm, you have a flying speed of 30 feet. However, you must make a Constitution saving throw at the end of each turn you remain hovering or stationary in the air. The DC for this saving throw starts at 10 and increases by 5 for each consecutive turn you remain stationary. On a failed save, you descend and can no longer hover until the start of your next turn.

Angelic Grace: You cannot be grappled.

Languages: You can speak only by directed telepathy. This will be understood by any creature that can understand language. You understand common. 

Inherent Radiance: Once per long rest, as an action, you can emit a burst of radiant energy from your celestial form. Each creature within 10 feet of you must make a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC, taking radiant damage equal to your level on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. You regain the use of this feature after finishing a long rest.

Astral Connection: Your connection to the astral realm allows you to cast the spell Misty Step once, regaining the ability to do so after finishing a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

The Seraphs, divine beings from the city of Legend, embody the grace and radiance of biblical angels. Their ability to manifest in the material realm through the thinning membrane between the astral and material realms grants them the power of flight, although their stationary presence requires a constitution check to maintain. Their celestial heritage grants resistance to radiant damage, while their angelic grace protects them from charm and fear. With their inherent radiance, they can unleash bursts of radiant energy, and their astral connection allows for quick teleportation through Misty Step.

Goblin Subrace: Gritgiblin (Otterbobs)


Gritgiblins, also known as Otterbobs, are a unique subrace of goblins native to the coastal city of Tacoma, Washington. These sea-faring goblins share a striking resemblance to otters, with sleek fur, webbed digits, and a natural affinity for the water. They are known for their nimbleness, adaptability, and their playful nature.

Ability Score Increase: Your Dexterity score increases by 2 and your Constitution score increases by 1.

Aquatic Adaptation: Gritgiblins , with their otter-like features, have special abilities suited for an aquatic lifestyle. You gain the following abilities:

Size: Your size is medium.
Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
Swimming Speed: You have a swim speed of 30 feet, allowing you to move effortlessly through water.

Amphibious: You can breathe air and water, enabling you to survive both on land and underwater.

Natural Swimmer: You have proficiency in the Athletics skill related to swimming.

Seafaring Expertise: Gritgiblins  possess specialized skills and knowledge related to maritime activities. You gain the following benefits:


Proficiency in Water Vehicles: You gain proficiency with water vehicles (such as boats and ships) and navigator's tools.

Keen Senses: You have advantage on Perception checks made in aquatic environments.

Gritty Resilience: The harsh coastal environment has toughened your body and mind. You gain the following benefits:


Natural Armor: Your fur is matted with hydrophobic oil and provides natural armor, granting you an AC of 13 + Dexterity modifier when you are not wearing armor.

Stand Tall: When you are subjected to an effect that would knock you prone, you can use your reaction to attempt to resist it. Make a Dexterity saving throw with a DC equal to the attacker's spell save DC or the opponent's attack roll. On a success, you remain standing and do not fall prone.

Playful Nature: Gritgiblins  have a playful demeanor and enjoy engaging in games and activities. You gain proficiency in one musical instrument of your choice.

Locatha Subrace: Mississippi Mudskipper .

They form close-knit family units known as "Brine Clans," comprising several generations that live and work together. Mudskipper settlements often consist of sturdy huts built on stilts above the waterline, providing safe spaces for them to rest and gather.

Tabaxi Subrace: Appalachian Bobcat

The Appalachian Bobcat is a unique subrace of Tabaxi that hails from the rugged and untamed mountainous regions. These feline humanoids embody the spirit of the wilderness, possessing characteristics and traits influenced by the rugged terrain and mysterious forests of the Appalachian mountains.

Ability Score Increase: Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1.

Size: Your size is medium.
Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

Your creature type is:  Arcanian Cryptid.

Stealthy Prowess: You have proficiency in the Stealth skill. Additionally, when you take the Hide action as a bonus action on your turn, you can attempt to hide even when you are only lightly obscured.

Darkvision: Thanks to your keen feline senses, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

Mountaineer's Expertise: You have proficiency in the Athletics and Survival skills. Additionally, you have advantage on checks made to climb difficult or treacherous terrain.

Keen Senses: You have proficiency in the Perception skill.

Natural Weapons: You are proficient with your unarmed strikes, which deal 1d4 slashing damage instead of the usual bludgeoning damage.

Languages: You can speak, read, and write Common and Appalachian.

Culture: Appalachian Bobcats are known for their affinity with nature and their resourcefulness in surviving the rugged wilderness. Centaur Subrace: Palomino Centaur

The Palomino Centaurs are a unique subrace of centaurs that hail from the majestic Colorado Rockies, a land teeming with untamed beauty and wild magic. Born within a roving pod of Tarasques, they embody both the indomitable spirit of the mountains and the untamed power of these legendary creatures.

Ability Score Increase: Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Charisma score increases by 1.

Size: Your size is medium.
Speed: Your base walking speed is 40 feet.

Your creature type is:  Arcanian Cryptid.

Roving Power: Due to your connection with the roaming pod of Tarasques, your hit point maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 every time you gain a level.

Wild Charge: As a bonus action, you can use your incredible speed to carry another willing creature on your back. The creature must be one size smaller than you or smaller. While carrying a creature, your movement speed is reduced to 30 feet, but the creature gains the benefits of being mounted, including advantage on melee attacks against targets smaller than you. You can use this feature for a number of hours equal to half your proficiency bonus (rounded down), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

Unbridled Heart: You have immunity to paralysis.

Languages: You can speak, read, and write Common and Rocky Mountain Arcanian.

Appearance: Palomino Centaurs possess the upper body of a human and the lower body of a majestic horse. Their coats shimmer with shades of golden hues, evoking the beauty and strength of palomino horses. Their flowing manes and tails, often blonde or light brown, cascade in the wind as they gallop across the rugged terrain. With a strong and sturdy build, Palomino Centaurs exude an aura of power and resilience.

Culture: Palomino Centaurs are deeply connected to the Colorado Rockies and the roving pod of Tarasques. They have a strong sense of community, working together to protect their homeland and maintain harmony with the untamed magic of the mountains. They are known for their courage, tenacity, and unwavering loyalty to their kin.

Palomino Centaurs are formidable allies and companions, carrying the spirit of the Tarasques within them. Their ability to bear another creature on their back and act as a mount reflects their innate strength and willingness to protect those who travel alongside them. With their resilience, wild nature, and ties to the powerful Tarasques, Palomino Centaurs embody the untamed spirit of the Colorado Rockies.

Goliath Subrace: Klondike Goliath

The Klondike Goliaths are a unique subrace of Goliaths who have adapted to the harsh and unforgiving environment of the Alaskan Klondike. Born amidst frozen tundra, towering mountains, and treacherous icy terrains, they possess the endurance and resilience required to thrive in such extreme conditions. Renowned for their incredible strength, they have even developed a peculiar technique for catching salmon.

Ability Score Increase: Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1.

Size: Your size is medium.
Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

Alaskan Adaptation: You have resistance to cold damage.

Frost Step: You are acclimated to the icy landscapes of the Klondike. You ignore difficult terrain caused by ice or snow and have advantage on Dexterity saving throws made to avoid slipping or falling on ice.

Mountain's Resilience: You have proficiency in the Athletics skill. Additionally, when determining your carrying capacity and your ability to push, drag, or lift objects, you are considered one size larger.

Salmon Puncher: The Klondike Goliaths possess a unique ability to catch salmon by punching them out of waterfalls with their bare fists. You gain proficiency in the Unarmed Strike attack, and your unarmed strikes deal 1d4 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage. Additionally, when attempting to catch salmon using this method, you have advantage on any checks or saving throws related to the task.

Klondike Heritage: You gain proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Survival, Nature, or Stealth.

Languages: You can speak, read, and write Borealian (Canadian) and Giant.

Appearance: Klondike Goliaths have a powerful and imposing build, standing taller and broader than most humanoids. Their skin is weathered and often exhibits a pale blue or grayish hue, reflecting the icy environments they call home. Their hair and eyes range in shades of white, silver, or icy blue, further emphasizing their connection to the frozen wilderness.

Culture: Klondike Goliaths are a proud and self-sufficient people who value strength, resilience, and self-reliance. They have adapted to survive in the frigid Alaskan Klondike, mastering the art of traversing icy terrains and withstanding freezing temperatures. Apart from their exceptional survival skills, they are known for their unique ability to catch salmon with nothing more than their fists, a testament to their extraordinary strength and precision. Klondike Goliaths are revered for their salmon-punching prowess, often participating in friendly competitions to see who can catch the most salmon in a single bound. This tradition not only demonstrates their physical might but also serves as a celebration of their connection to nature and the bountiful resources provided by the land. With their unmatched strength and their exceptional ability to adapt to the Alaskan Klondike, they embody the spirit of the untamed wilderness.

Puffkin :

Ability Score Increase Your Constitution and Wisdom scores each increase by 1

Age: In any fairy ring, a single mushroom may evolve into a Puffkin. All Puffkin are linked before manifestation through a mycelial network. Their consciousness does not settle into a mushroom cap until that cap has the capacity to produce spores. There does not seem to be any factors besides chance that determine which sentience from the mycelial consciousness will enter a Puffkin. Maturity is determined by actions and personality rather than physical age, as the body itself may only have been produced a day, a month, a year or ten years ago and by seemingly random happenstance has become host to the mind of a Puffkin.

Puffkin tend to be on the welcoming side of lawful, though Puffkin of any and every alignment are common.

Size: Puffkin undergo dramatic growth after their awakening. They begin their lives as Tiny creatures, and grow to be Small at level 3 and Medium at level 5.

Speed: Your base walking speed is 15 feet. At level 3 this increases to 25 feet, and at level 5 it increases again to 30 feet.

Languages: You can speak and write Common and your choice of Rocky Mountain Arcanian, Appalachian or Costal Arcanian. 

Rapport Spores: As an action, a Puffkin can release spores around itself. Each creature within 20 feet of itself that the puffkin chooses gains a telepathic connection to all other creatures affected by this feature, including the puffkin who released the spores. This telepathy has a range of 30 feet and cannot be used through a solid barrier. This feature can be used 3 times a day, and lasts for 15 minutes. Puffkin can use this feature to communicate simple concepts with nearby fungus, though they cannot grant the other affected creatures the ability to do so.

Distress Spores

Whenever a Puffkin is hurt, it alerts nearby allies through spores. All other Puffkin within 120 feet, as well as those currently affected by the puffin's rapport spores can sense its pain and know a vague direction. The Puffkin can use its reaction when it sends this signal to give more precise information, choosing either the distance or a precise direction.

Soft Body: Because of your soft yet surprisingly adaptive body, you are resistant to fall damage. 

Distracting Spores: You can use your action to release a cloud of spores to a target location within 15 feet of you. For the next minute, the spores emit a nonverbal sound (footsteps, waves, etc.) or a smell. When you reach level 5, you can sneeze adorably once per day as a reaction, changing the memories of creatures in a 15-foot cone in front of you as if by the spell gift of gab.

Ability Score Increase: Your Constitution score increases by another 1 for a total of 2.

Darkvision: Used to living underground, you can see easily in dark conditions. You can see in dim light as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light for 60 feet in front of you.

Sudden Bloom: Once per day when you are hit by an attack, you may gain temporary hit points equal to 1d8 + your Constitution modifier as a reaction. When you reach level 5, you can cast the spell gaseous form on yourself, with a reduced duration of 15 minutes.

Ability Score Increase: Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1

Age: Bigfoot live long lives, reaching maturity at around 25 years old and living up to 300 years.

Size: Bigfoot stand between 7 and 8 feet tall and weigh between 600 and 900 pounds. Your size is Medium.

Natural Camouflage: Bigfoot are masters at blending into their natural surroundings. You have proficiency in the Stealth skill when you are in a natural environment (such as forests, mountains, and caves). Keen Senses: Bigfoot have incredible senses, allowing them to navigate even in the darkest and most rugged terrain. You have proficiency in the Perception skill.

Languages: You can speak, read, and write Common and Sylvan.

Nature's Guardian: Bigfoot are protectors of the natural world, and have a natural connection to the land. You have the ability to cast the druidcraft cantrip, and once you reach 3rd level, you can cast the entangle spell once per day. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

Resilient: Bigfoot are known for their ability to withstand the harsh conditions of the wilderness. You have advantage on saving throws against extreme weather conditions, such as blizzards, sandstorms, and extreme heat.

Halfwudgie Traits

Ability Score Increase: Your Wisdom score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1.

Age: Halfwudgies reach adulthood at the age of 12 and can live up to 75 years, their connection to the untamed wilderness granting them resilience.

Alignment: Halfwudgies are often drawn towards a sense of justice and protecting the natural world, leading them towards alignments that uphold harmony and goodness.

Size: Your size is Large, standing taller and more imposing than most humanoids, a testament to your otherworldly lineage.

Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet, allowing you to navigate the treacherous terrains of the wild with ease.

Cryptid Defense: As a bonus action, you can produce 1d4 sticky quills from your back once per short rest. These quills, reminiscent of the creatures that roam the untamed lands, can be thrown at your foes with uncanny accuracy. The range of these quills is 20/60, and on a successful hit, the target takes piercing damage equal to 1d4 + your Strength or Dexterity modifier, showcasing your innate connection to the wild.

Cryptid Resilience: Your exposure to the untamed forces of the Arcanian Wilds has granted you an inner strength. You have advantage on saving throws against being frightened, remaining steadfast even in the face of fear.

Cryptid Ancestry: Growing up amidst the enigmatic creatures of the Wilds has sharpened your senses and attunement to the natural world. You gain proficiency in the Insight skill, allowing you to perceive the hidden truths and intentions of those around you.

Quill Barrage: Embracing the primal instincts within, starting at 3rd level, you can unleash a devastating barrage of quills upon your foes. By channeling the raw power of the Arcanian Wilds, you can use your action to release a cone-shaped wave of quills, reaching up to 15 feet. Each creature caught in the cone must make a Dexterity saving throw against a DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier. Those who fail the save suffer piercing damage equal to 2d6 + your Constitution modifier, and they are plagued by an uncanny disadvantage on their next saving throw before the end of your next turn. Those who succeed take half damage and are unaffected by the disadvantage. After using this feature, you must regain your connection with the Wilds through a short or long rest before you can harness its power again.

Primal Intuition: Your time spent immersed in the natural wonders of the Arcanian Wilds has honed your senses to a razor-sharp edge. You gain proficiency in the Perception skill, allowing you to keenly observe and sense the subtleties of your surroundings, whether it's tracking elusive prey or detecting hidden dangers in the unforgiving wilderness.

In the old west Arcanian setting, the Halfwudgies are a remarkable fusion of untamed wilderness and humanoid nature. They embody the rugged spirit of the Wilds, harnessing their cryptid ancestry to protect the balance between civilization and nature. Embrace your primal instincts and let the wild rhythms of your heritage echo through the untamed lands of the old west. Whether you roam as a solitary guardian or rally others to your cause, your Halfwudgie nature will bring an air of mystery and untamed power to the tales of the Arcanian Wilds.
