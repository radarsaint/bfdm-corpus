Oath of the Drifter

Oath of the Drifter Paladins who swear the Oath of the Drifter are solitary warriors, wanderers of the world who embrace a life of solitude and self-reliance. They forge their path through the wilderness, surviving by their skills and resourcefulness.

Tenets of the Drifter

The tenets of the Drifter emphasize self-sufficiency and independence in the face of the ever-changing world.

Walk Alone: Embrace the solitude and find strength in self-reliance. Depend on no one but yourself.

Adaptability: Be flexible and adaptable, ready to face any challenge that comes your way. Survive and thrive in the harshest environments.

Wanderer's Code: Live by a personal code of honor, holding true to your principles and never compromising your beliefs.

Channel Divinity

When you take this oath at 3rd level, you gain the following two Channel Divinity options.

Solitary Challenge

:

You can use your Channel Divinity to challenge a creature to face you in single combat. As a bonus action, choose one creature you can see within 30 feet. The creature must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier) or be compelled to engage you in combat. For the next 1 minute, the creature has disadvantage on attack rolls against any creature other than you. Additionally, if the creature willingly moves away from you, it must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, its movement is restricted and it cannot move further away from you than 30 feet until the start of your next turn.

Unyielding Spirit

:

You can use your Channel Divinity to strengthen your resolve. As an action, you make a Charisma (Intimidation) check, and you gain a +5 bonus to that check and to all intimidation checks for the next 10 minutes. You can use this feature even if an intimidation check has already failed in this encounter.

Lone Hunter

At 3rd level, you embrace the ways of the lone hunter. You gain proficiency with survival skills and one of the following skills of your choice: Nature, Stealth, or Perception.

Additionally, you gain the ability to move stealthily at a normal pace while traveling alone. When you are not traveling with a group, you can move silently and blend into your surroundings, making it difficult for others to track or detect you.

Desert's Resilience

Starting at 7th level, your time spent in harsh environments has hardened your body and mind. You gain resistance to fire and poison damage. Additionally, you have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

Shadow Strike

At 15th level, you have honed your ability to strike from the shadows with deadly precision. When you hit a creature with an attack while you have advantage on the attack roll, you deal additional damage equal to your Charisma modifier.

Solitary Mastery

At 20th level, you have reached the pinnacle of solitary mastery. You have become a master of survival and adaptability, able to overcome any obstacle you encounter.

Using your action, you undergo a transformation. For 1 minute, you gain the following benefits:

You have advantage on all ability checks and saving throws.

When you take damage, reduce the damage taken by 2 for every damage die rolled.

Once on each of your turns, when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can choose to deal maximum damage instead of rolling.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
