Life Path, Rancher

Life Path: Rancher.

Life Path is in addition to your background. It does not replace your background. This choice has major economic impacts for your character. There is no starting cash or equipment outside of this choice.

As a Rancher in the vast and rugged wilderness, you have chosen a life of taming the land, raising cattle, and honing your skills as a caretaker of livestock. Your ranch is your pride and joy, and your days are filled with the demands of tending to your herd, driving cattle across the open range, and navigating the intricacies of the livestock market. Here is a breakdown of the mechanics associated with being a Rancher in 5e:

Cattle Acquisition:

As a Rancher, you start with no money but possess the ability to acquire cattle. You can begin with a base number of cattle, let's say 10, which you receive as part of your initial ranching endeavors. These cattle are valued at 250 gold dollars per head. The value of your cattle is unique to you and prices given to other players will not apply to you.

Extra Dimensional Barn:

Your experience as a Rancher has granted you the knowledge of creating and maintaining an extra dimensional barn, a space where you can safely house your cattle. Twice per day, you can use a ritual to open a temporary portal to this extradimensional space. The barn can hold a number of cattle equal to your proficiency bonus multiplied by 5. This unique feature ensures the safety and well-being of your valuable livestock, protecting them from harm and theft. Your cows will not be fed or watered within the extra dimensional space and must be released for at least 8 hours a day.

Driving Cattle:

As a skilled Rancher, you have mastered the art of driving cattle across vast distances. When embarking on a cattle drive, you can lead your herd through various terrains and obstacles, utilizing your Animal Handling proficiency to guide the cattle effectively. The DC for successfully driving the cattle depends on the difficulty of the terrain and any potential external factors. Failure to navigate the challenges may result in lost or scattered cattle, requiring additional time and effort to regather them.

Selling Cattle:

Part of being a successful Rancher involves knowing the livestock market and making shrewd business decisions. When you decide to sell your cattle, your knowledge of market trends and your ability to negotiate prices come into play when you get your herd appraised. Your proficiency in Animal Handling and Insight can help you assess the market demand and gauge fair prices for your livestock. Additionally, your reputation as a Rancher may grant you advantages in finding potential buyers and securing lucrative deals.

The cattle market is subject to fluctuation, influenced by various factors such as demand, supply, and external events. At the beginning of each week, you can roll a d20 to determine the market condition. On a roll of 1-10, the market is considered stable, with cattle prices remaining relatively unchanged. On a roll of 11-15, the market experiences moderate appreciation, increasing the value of your cattle by 10% for that month. On a roll of 16-20, the market undergoes significant appreciation, increasing the value of your cattle by 20% for that appraisal.

While tending to your cattle, there are events and risks that may impact your herd. when the day before arriving in a town (once per week) roll a d20 to determine any significant occurrences:

On a roll of 1-3, your herd suffers from a disease outbreak or other natural calamity, resulting in the loss of 10% of your cattle.

On a roll of 4-6, rustlers target your ranch, attempting to steal some of your cattle. Roll a d10 to determine the number of cattle stolen, which are removed from your inventory.

On a roll of 7-9, your cattle breed successfully, resulting in a 10% increase in your herd size (your cow's birthing process does weird things, because magic.). Roll a d10 to determine the number of new calves.

On a roll of 10-12, your ranch experiences a period of optimal conditions, resulting in a 10% increase in the value of your entire herd.

On a roll of 13-15, your herd faces an infestation or predator attack, resulting in the loss of 5% of your cattle.

On a roll of 16-18, a neighboring rancher offers you a trade deal, allowing you to exchange a portion of your herd for a different type of livestock or valuable equipment.

On a roll of 19-20, your ranch becomes famous for breeding exceptional cattle, increasing the value of your entire herd by 20%.

Ranch Expansion and Investment:

As you accumulate wealth from selling cattle, you have the option to expand your dimensional barn, invest in new equipment, or diversify your livestock. These investments may provide additional benefits or income opportunities, increased cattle capacity, or the ability to raise other valuable livestock or even have your barn spontaneously increase your herd population.

Figurine of Fowl's Bounty

(Requires Attunement):

Cost: $10,000

This magical figurine takes the form of a finely crafted, miniature chicken coop. When activated, it transforms into a life-sized coop within the dimensional barn, complete with nesting boxes, perches, and a small outdoor area for the chickens to roam.

Effect:

The coop houses a flock of magical chickens that lay extraordinary eggs with unique properties. Each morning, a total of 1d6 eggs are magically laid in the coop, replenishing daily.

Buff:

Whenever a party member consumes one of these magical eggs, they gain the following benefits for 1 hour:

Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks.

Advantage on Dexterity saving throws.

A glide speed of 5 feet. This involves frantically flapping your arms.

The ability to make attacks of opportunity even when unconscious.

Note:

Only one magical egg can be consumed per hour, and the effects do not stack.

While the figurine is not in use, it reverts to its original miniature form, allowing for easy storage and transportation. Activating the figurine again will transform it back into the full-sized chicken coop, ready to provide its magical bounty once more.

Grazing Amulet

(Requires Attunement, Consumable):

Cost: 500 Dollars.

This intricately crafted amulet is adorned with symbols of lush pastures and grazing cattle. When activated by a rancher by attaching it to a lassoed cow, it emanates a gentle magical aura that envelops the cattle within the dimensional barn. The amulet harnesses the essence of fresh grazing, providing nourishment and contentment to the cattle despite their confinement.

When activated, the Grazing Amulet the following effects occur:

Simulated Grazing:

The cattle within the dimensional barn gain the benefits of natural grazing, even in the absence of open pastures. They experience the sensation of fresh grass and vegetation, which satisfies their grazing instincts. This helps maintain their overall health, vitality, and contentment.

Enhanced Nutrition:

The magical field created by the amulet enhances the nutritional value of any feed or forage provided to the cattle. It ensures that the food they consume before entering the dimensional barn is rich in essential nutrients, promoting their growth, strength, and overall well-being. This allows them to stay within the barn for up to 3 days with out being let out for grazing. This effect ends if the cattle are released from the barn early.

Reduced Stress:

The simulated grazing effect helps alleviate the stress and restlessness often associated with confined spaces. The cattle remain calm and content, reducing the chances of aggressive behavior or attempts to escape from the dimensional barn.

Activating the Grazing Amulet requires spending a short rest within close proximity to the cattle while wearing or handling the amulet.

Spectral Herdcaller's Lariat

(Requires Attunement):

Cost 250 Dollars.

This enchanted lariat is crafted from shimmering ethereal threads and adorned with intricate carvings of grazing cattle. When attuned and used within the dimensional barn, it creates a spectral connection between the rancher and the cattle. By gently twirling the lariat in a circular motion and speaking a command word, the rancher can summon a spectral manifestation of a lead rope that attaches to each individual cow within the herd. The spectral rope enables the rancher to effortlessly guide and direct the cattle, even from a distance. The herd responds to the rancher's commands as if they were physically present, allowing for precise movement and control. The spectral connection also allows the rancher to communicate simple commands telepathically to the cattle, ensuring smooth and efficient management of the herd.

Milker's Strength Elixir Bucket

(Requires Attunement):

This enchanted bucket is specifically designed to assist ranchers in the milking process. When used with the dimensional barn, it automatically extracts milk from the cows, streamlining the milking procedure and ensuring a consistent supply of milk.

Effect: Once per day, the Milker's Strength Elixir Bucket automatically collects milk from the cows within the dimensional barn, producing 1d4 uses of a special potion known as "Milker's Strength Elixir."

Milker's Strength Elixir Potion:

This creamy potion is derived from the milk of magically enhanced cows. When consumed, it grants enhanced physical strength and provides temporary hit points to the drinker.

Effect:

When a party member consumes one dose of the Milker's Strength Elixir potion, they gain the following benefits for 1 hour:

Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.

Temporary hit points equal to 5 + their Constitution modifier.

Note:

Each day, the bucket will produce a random number of Milker's Strength Elixir potions, ranging from 1 to 4 doses. The potions do not stack, and consuming additional doses beyond the first has no further effect until the temporary hit points wear off. The bucket will reset and refill with potions each day.

Shepherd's Whistle (Requires Attunement):

Cost 300 Dollars.

This magical whistle enhances the abilities of the rancher in tending to their flock. When blown, the Shepherd's Whistle summons spectral sheep to the dimensional barn, creating a magical enclosure within its extradimensional space.

Effect:

Once per day, the Shepherd's Whistle can be blown to summon 1d4+1 spectral sheep. These spectral sheep provide a soothing aura that benefits the one creature per summoned sheep.

Buff:

While within the vicinity of the spectral sheep, the party gains the following benefits for 1 hour:

Advantage on Wisdom (Nature) checks.

Advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks made when dealing with animals.

Immunity to the spell "Sleep"

Note: The spectral sheep remain within the dimensional barn until the duration expires or they are dismissed by the rancher. They do not engage in combat and cannot leave the confines of the barn.

Saddle of Expedient Transport (Requires Attunement):

Cost:

40,000 Dollars

This finely crafted saddle is enchanted to facilitate quick and efficient transportation of both the rancher and their livestock. It enhances the speed and endurance of the mounted cattle, allowing for faster journeys and improved productivity. The saddle can be used both within and outside of the dimensional barn.

Effect:

When the rancher places the Saddle of Expedient Transport on a cow within the dimensional barn or any cow outside, the saddle's magic enhances the creature's speed and endurance. The mounted cattle gain the following benefits:

Movement speed of the cow is increased by 10 feet.

The cow has Advantage on Constitution saving throws to resist exhaustion caused by long journeys or strenuous activities.

The cow can be ridden by the rancher as a mount, allowing for easier and faster movement. While not mounted, the cow returns to its docile bovine state. It resumes being a herd beast that does not act reliably the way a dog or horse would.
