Life Path, Gambler

Life Path: Gambler

Gain the following gear and the proficiency to use them. 

When using these include +2 for the tool and +your proficiency bonus. 

A set of gaudy gambler  clothes.

1  Tier One  Marker.

1 mutt. A faithful companion and friend.

1 pony

2 Pistols.

750 dollars.
A deck of beveled card, or other simple cheating insturment.

As the sun rises over the lawless expanse of the Wild West, you find yourself standing on the precipice of an exhilarating new adventure. With a sly grin on your face and a loaded deck of playing cards hidden up your sleeve, you join a group of outlaws and ruffians, ready to carve your own path in this unforgiving frontier. The City of Legend, a haven for those who thrive on deceit and risk, beckons you from afar.


Whispers of ill-gotten gains and daring heists have spread like wildfire among the renegade community. Legends tell of a hidden location deep within the untamed wilderness, accessible only by navigating the treacherous river that cuts through the rugged landscape. Rumor has it that this secret hideaway holds a bounty of wealth and priceless treasures, just waiting for audacious hands to claim them. To reach this clandestine haven, you and your fellow rogues must embark on a perilous journey through the lawless land, using cunning and guile to outsmart both the unforgiving terrain and your rivals.


As you step onto your trusty steed, a horse as cunning and unpredictable as you are, you feel the power and freedom that comes with riding the open plains. The rhythmic sound of hooves against the dusty ground sets the pace for your journey, as the vast and untamed wilderness stretches out before you. The wind carries the scent of possibility and adventure, while the distant howls of coyotes echo through the rugged canyons.


Along your way, the untamed frontier unravels in all its rugged beauty. Wide open prairies, rocky cliffs, and hidden canyons pass by, each holding secrets and opportunities for those willing to take risks. You navigate through treacherous paths, your horse deftly avoiding obstacles and leading you toward the allure of uncertain riches. The haunting calls of wildlife fill the air, while the majestic sight of wild horses running freely reminds you of the untamed spirit that burns within you.


After days of traversing the harsh landscapes, you finally arrive at your destination: a hidden oasis nestled in the heart of the lawless land, where whispers suggest a high-stakes gambling den awaits, teeming with wealth and the thrill of chance. The sight of fellow gamblers, their own horses tethered nearby, fills you with a sense of camaraderie and competitive spirit.


With your horse securely hitched, you step onto solid ground, ready to embrace your role as a cunning gambler and embrace the risks and rewards that await you in this lawless land. The City of Legend lies just beyond, a den of iniquity where you can put your skills to the test, engage in high-stakes games of chance, and forge alliances with unscrupulous allies who understand the thrill of the gamble.


As you set foot on solid ground, your heart brimming with anticipation and audacity, you know that your path as a gambler has just begun. The risks are high, but the rewards are enticingly lucrative. With each hand you play and every calculated risk you take, you hope to amass not only wealth and power but also the reputation and fear that come with being a legendary gambler.


The journey ahead may be treacherous, but the allure of the gamble and the promise of untold riches drive you forward. The City of Legend awaits, and the path to becoming a notorious gambler is laid out before you. It's time to stake your claim, outwit your rivals, and leave your indelible mark on this wild and lawless frontier, one daring bet at a time.

The Gambler's Mark. An IOU.
The path of the Gambler is a journey defined by risk, chance, and the pursuit of fortune. It is a life filled with high-stakes games, strategic bets, and an inherent understanding of the ebb and flow of luck. The Gambler's path intertwines with the world of gambling, where wagers are made, debts are incurred, and favors are exchanged. At the heart of this path lies the unique mechanic of The Marker Chip. The Gambler navigates their way through a web of intricate relationships, using these chips as both currency and markers of obligations. The chips serve as a tangible representation of the debts owed and the favors to be called upon. As the Gambler progresses along their path, they amass a collection of these chips, each with its own value and significance. The chips become tools of negotiation and influence, allowing the Gambler to call in favors from others or offer them as payment for services rendered. The more valuable the chip, the greater the favor it represents or the larger the debt it signifies. However, the path of the Gambler is not without its risks and trade-offs. The allure of fortune and the thrill of gambling can lead to both great triumphs and devastating losses. Every wager carries consequences, and the Gambler must navigate these risks with careful calculation and a keen understanding of the odds. The Gambler's journey is one of skill and intuition. They master the art of reading opponents, spotting opportunities, and knowing when to take calculated risks. They become adept at manipulating the flow of luck, using their chips strategically to tilt the odds in their favor or to call upon the debts owed to them. Throughout their path, the Gambler faces both allies and adversaries. They engage in high-stakes games, form alliances, and navigate the complex web of gambling dens, casinos, and underground networks. They encounter individuals from all walks of life, from fellow gamblers seeking fortune to powerful figures with their own agendas. As the Gambler progresses, they acquire not only wealth but also a reputation. Their name becomes known within the gambling circles, and their collection of chips becomes a testament to their skill, influence, and the debts owed to them. They become both feared and respected, sought after for their insight and connections. But the path of the Gambler is not a solitary one. It is a life intertwined with others, bound by the debts and favors represented by the Marker Chips. The Gambler must navigate a delicate balance of maintaining their own obligations while strategically calling in favors when needed. In the end, the path of the Gambler is a thrilling, unpredictable, and often dangerous journey. It requires a combination of skill, luck, and shrewdness to navigate the intricate world of gambling, debts, and favors. The Poker Chips serve as the Gambler's compass, guiding them through this treacherous terrain, as they strive to leave their mark on the world of chance and fortune.

Marker Chips are more than just money. These poker chip icons are a social currency and effect game mechanics. Only a gambler may leave a gambling hall with poker chips to use as Marker Chips. Once a Marker has been spent, its magic is depleted and is worthless.


Overview:


Reputation points: These represent the standing and recognition earned by gamblers in the River's Edge Poker League. They serve as a measure of a gambler's skill, success, and notoriety within the gambling community. These points reflect the gambler's ability to navigate the high-stakes games, win against formidable opponents, and uphold their honor and integrity at the poker table. Accumulating reputation points signifies the gambler's progression through the ranks, unlocking access to higher-tier games, exclusive opportunities, and the respect and admiration of their peers. Reputation points serve as a tangible representation of a gambler's journey, marking their ascent from novice players to legendary high rollers in the world of gambling. Rep must be earned, not gifted. Gaining chips from IOUs, pickpocketing, gifts, drunken declarations of love etc do not count toward your progression to the next tier of gaming. 

Tiered System: The markers are organized into five tiers, each representing a different level of gambling prestige and risk. As players progress through the tiers, the value and rarity of the markers increase.

Betting Structure: Each tier has its own betting structure, determining the level of the game and the type of opponents and events players will encounter. Higher tiers feature grand tournaments and exclusive competitions.

Marker Generation: Markers are generated when you give poker chips in lieu  of other forms of payment. Shops don't accept these. But humanoids that are friendsly to you will.

Granting the Marker as an IOU or unlock the next tier:
The cost for each tier of marker is the same as the cost to advance to the next tier.
10 chips will either buy you a common marker or access to  uncommon games.
25 chips will either buy you a uncommon marker or access to  rare games.
60 chips will either buy you a rare marker or access to very rare games.
100 chips will either buy you a legendary marker, or access to the big game.  

Trade-offs and Benefits: Each tier has its own unique set of benefits and trade-offs associated with the IOU Markers. Higher-tier markers provide more powerful advantages but also impose more significant drawbacks or limitations on the gambler. While benefits cannot stack, the negative trade off effects do.

Customization: IOU Markers can be customized with exotic materials, engravings, runes, or jewels, adding a touch of personalization and style to each chip. These RP details belong to you.
Gambler: The Gambler is the player character who possesses and wields the Poker Chips. They are the one engaging in games of chance, placing bets, and using the Poker Chips to gain advantages and benefits. The Gambler relies on their skill, luck, and strategic choices to win games and accumulate wealth or valuable markers.
Bearer: The Bearer is the individual or entity to whom the Gambler owes a favor or debt. When the Gambler gives their Poker Chip to the Bearer, the Bearer becomes the recipient of the marker's abilities. The Bearer can call upon the powers or benefits associated with the specific Poker Chip they hold, utilizing them to their advantage.
Suits: Each gambler has a preference for one of the four suits. Rewards will be determined by which suit your character has an affinity for.

Vipers (♥): Representing the dangerous and seductive, the Vipers suit embodies deceit, charm, and manipulation. These cards depict the treacherous personalities and cunning schemes prevalent in the game.

Ghosts (♦): Reflecting the ethereal and elusive, the Ghosts suit represents the fleeting nature of fortune and the unpredictable twists of fate. These cards embody the specters, illusions, and unexpected events that can alter the course of the game.

Shadows (♠): Symbolizing the concealed and mysterious, the Shadows suit is associated with covert actions, cunning, and hidden agendas. These cards represent the enigmatic characters and dark secrets that lurk in the shadows.

Ravens (♣): Symbolic of intelligence and strategy, the Ravens suit embodies tactics, calculated moves, and calculated risks. These cards depict the cleverness and resourcefulness required to outsmart opponents.

Remember: Trade offs are cumulative. 

When a barer uses a marker, the gambler suffers the trade off from it's tier and each previous tier.

Welcome to the prestigious River's Edge Poker League, where the thrill of high-stakes gambling awaits! As a gambler, you will navigate through various tiers of games, each offering unique challenges and opportunities to amass reputation points. This guide will walk you through the mechanics of the Poker Chip system, progression between tiers, and the exciting rewards that await you.


The Tiers:

1. Common Tier:

   - Players start their journey here, participating in common-level games, and with the ability to turn their poker chips into an IOU that can be used by other players (called bearers in this document) in combat or other non-gambling related adventures.

   - Games in this tier feature Tier 1: Silver Dime Chips.

   - Each Marker Chip obtained represents 1 reputation point.

   - Participating in a Common game earns reputation points equivalent to the number of Marker Chips earned.

   - Winning a Common game grants 2 Marker Chips plus 1 additional Marker Chip for each player with the Gambler life path.

 

2. Uncommon Tier:

   - To enter the Uncommon Tier, players must accumulate 10 reputation points in the form of 10 Silver Dime Chips from the Common Tier. 

   - Games in this tier feature Tier 2: Double Dime Marker Chips.

   - Each Marker Chip obtained represents 2 reputation points.

   - Participating in an Uncommon game earns reputation points equivalent to twice the number of Marker Chips earned.

   - Winning an Uncommon game grants 3 Marker Chips plus 1 additional Marker Chip for each player with the Gambler life path.


3. Rare Tier:

   - Players can ascend to the Rare Tier by accumulating 25 reputation points from the Common and Uncommon Tiers.

   - Games in this tier feature Tier 3: High Roller Marker Chips.

   - Each Marker Chip obtained represents 5 reputation points.

   - Participating in a Rare game earns reputation points equivalent to five times the number of Marker Chips earned.

   - Winning a Rare game grants 4 Marker Chips plus 1 additional Marker Chip for each player with the Gambler life path.


4. Very Rare Tier:

   - Players can enter the Very Rare Tier by accumulating 60 reputation points in the Common, Uncommon, and Rare Tiers.

   - Games in this tier feature Tier 4: Prestige Marker Chips.

   - Each Marker Chip obtained represents 10 reputation points.

   - Participating in a Very Rare game earns reputation points equivalent to ten times the number of Marker Chips earned.

   - Winning a Very Rare game grants 5 Marker Chips plus 1 additional Marker Chip for each player with the Gambler life path.


5. Legendary Tier:

   - The pinnacle of gambling prowess, the Legendary Tier awaits those who amass 100 reputation points from all previous tiers.

   - Games in this tier feature Tier 5: Fortune Chips.

   - Each Fortune Chip obtained represents 20 reputation points.

   - Participating in a Legendary game earns reputation points equivalent to twenty times the number of Fortune Chips earned.

   - Winning a Legendary game grants 6 Marker Chips plus 1 additional Marker Chip for each player with the Gambler life path.


**Progression and Reputation Points:**

- Players accumulate reputation points by obtaining Marker Chips and winning games.

- Each tier has specific reputation point requirements to progress to the next tier.

- The number of reputation points earned is determined by the tier of the game and the value of the Marker Chips obtained.

- Marker Chips given away or transferred to other players do not count towards progression to the next tier.

- Once a tier has been unlocked, it remains unlocked unless a Dungeon Master decides otherwise.

Set up for "River's Edge Showdown" begins by gathering a group of players ready to test their wits and cunning in a high-stakes game of deception. The dealer for the first round is determined, through rolling for initiative. Each player starts with an equal amount  of money determined by the players. This is an intense game of skill and deception. As the players gather around the table, the atmosphere is thick with tension and anticipation. The deck consists of four suits, each representing a unique aspect of this dark fantasy old west-themed game.

To determine the cards each player holds, the game begins with a roll for initiative. In the order rolled, players attempt to gain two cards of their choice by manipulating the dealer through contested skill check combos. Players can assume an attacking stance or a defending stance, utilizing various skills such as Sleight of Hand, Perception, Deception, Insight, Stealth, Performance, Persuasion, and Investigation. Successful contested rolls allow players to select their desired cards, while failed attempts result in two random cards dealt face down.

Once the card acquisition phase is complete, the first player in initiative becomes the big blind and pays the ante. The game then proceeds through several phases:

Phase 1: Cheating

Players who successfully acquired their desired cards during the initiative round are aware of the cards they selected, while those who failed are unaware. The players can choose to attack or defend by indicating their target player.

Attackers make contested rolls against their targets using the chosen skill check combos. Successful attacks grant the attackers various options such as stealing a card from the defender, forcing the defender to replace a known card with a randomly generated one, casting doubt, intimidation, or confusion upon the defender, imposing a -2 penalty to their future cheating or defending checks.

Phase 2: Communal Cards

Three communal cards are revealed. Roll a 1d4 to determine the suit of the communal cards: Shadows (♠), Vipers (♥), Ghosts (♦), or Ravens (♣). Roll a 3d13 to determine the value of each card.

Phase 3: Betting

Players, in initiative order, can choose to bet, fold, or check based on their hand strength and strategic considerations.

Phase 4: Individual Card Reveal

Players reveal one card from their hand. If a player is unaware of their card, generate one using the same suit and value generation rules as the communal cards.

Phase 5: Betting Continues

Players, in initiative order, can bet, fold, or check based on their updated hand strength and the revealed communal and individual cards.

Phase 6: Final Showdown

In this final phase, attackers won't have to give up their hidden cards. Instead, they'll concentrate their attack on either the communal cards or their opponents' undisclosed cards.

As an aside: Remember, the communal cards are those that everyone can see, while undisclosed cards are those that players have not revealed yet.

For attackers, if you choose to attack the communal cards, you're tryin' to manipulate 'em to benefit your hand or disrupt your opponents. A successful attack on the communal cards might swap one of 'em with a new one from the deck or remove a specific communal card from the table.

If you opt to attack your opponents' undisclosed cards, you'll try to affect their hands in some way, maybe force 'em to swap one of their hidden cards with a new one or even steal a specific undisclosed card if your skill check succeeds.

As for defenders, you'll be guardin' against the attackers' attempts to change the communal cards or affect your hand. If you defend successfully, you'll prevent the attacker from making any changes to the communal cards or your hidden cards. 

The suits' advantages are as follows:

Shadows (♠): Attacking with Shadows provides a bonus to contested rolls, reducing the defender's DC by 2.

Vipers (♥): Attacking with Vipers grants a +2 bonus to contested rolls.

Ghosts (♦): Defending with Ghosts provides a +2 bonus to defense rolls, making it harder for attackers to succeed.

Ravens (♣): Attacking or defending with Ravens grants a +2 bonus to contested rolls.

Scoring

The Kid's Flush: A, K, Q, J, 10 of the same suit.

Sights Straight: Five cards of the same suit in sequential order.

High Noon: Four cards of the same rank.

Frontier Brothel: A combination of three of a kind and a pair.

Dusty Flush: Any five cards of the same suit.

Ragtime mess: Five consecutive cards of different suits.

Three Dames: Three cards of the same rank.

Double Trouble: Two sets of two cards of the same rank.

Bandit Brothers: Two cards of the same rank.

Lucky POS: The highest-ranking card in hand
