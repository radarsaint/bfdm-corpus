Calico, the Bobcat Patron of Arcania's Southwest

In the vast and untamed landscapes of Arcania's Southwest, the demigod Calico reigns as the enigmatic Bobcat Patron, embodying the spirit of freedom, fertility, and revelry. With a head adorned by the distinctive patterns of a calico bobcat, she represents the vibrant and diverse tapestry of this sacred land. Calico's captivating gaze, reflecting the colors of the desert sunset, holds a mischievous spark that hints at her fondness for celebration and liberation. Her presence radiates an aura of untamed energy, inviting those who seek her favor to embrace the uninhibited joys of life.

As the Bobcat Patron of Arcania's Southwest, Calico governs three interconnected domains that form the essence of her portfolio:

Freedom:

Calico champions personal liberty and the pursuit of one's truest self. She inspires individuals to break free from societal constraints, encouraging them to explore their passions, express their individuality, and live authentically. Under her guidance, the Southwest becomes a haven for those who seek refuge from oppressive forces, a place where the wild spirit thrives.

Fertility:

Calico's influence extends to the fertile aspects of life, both in the physical and metaphorical sense. She governs the cycles of growth and abundance that bless Arcania's Southwest, ensuring bountiful harvests, flourishing wildlife, and the prosperity of its inhabitants. Additionally, Calico's touch brings forth the blossoming of creativity, ideas, and new beginnings, infusing the land and its people with the power of life's renewal.

Revelry:

The roots of Calico's origin lie in the spirited festivals that celebrate the Southwest's vibrant culture. Her connection to joyful abandon and communal merriment is embodied in the grand festival known as the Calico Revelry. During this lively event, held under the starlit sky, laughter, music, and dance fill the air. The festival embraces the sacred balance between indulgence and self-expression, inviting revelers to partake in the joys of life without inhibition.

Worshipers of Calico honor her through exuberant rituals and celebrations that embrace the themes of her domains. The Calico Revelry serves as an annual occasion where communities gather to embrace their freedom, revel in the fertility of the land, and unleash their creative spirits. Participants indulge in lively dances, partake in spirited libations, and wear vibrant calico patterns, symbolizing their connection to the demigod's multifaceted essence.

Devotees of Calico seek her blessings for the liberation of their souls, the blossoming of their endeavors, and the preservation of Arcania's Southwest's untamed beauty. In return, she grants them the spirit of uninhibited celebration, empowering them to live life with an open heart, express their true selves, and find inspiration in the untamed energy of the Southwest.

The

Joybringer Domain

is a cleric subclass devoted to the spirit of celebration, joy, and communal merriment, inspired by the presence of Calico, the Bobcat Patron of Arcania's Southwest. These clerics embody the essence of the Old West, infusing their rituals and blessings with the energy of revelry while remaining true to the cowboy code.

Domain Spells

1st Level: Bless, Heroism

3rd Level: Enhance Ability, Prayer of Healing

5th Level: Beacon of Hope, Spirit Guardians

7th Level: Freedom of Movement, Guardian of Faith

9th Level: Mass Cure Wounds, Dream

Bonus Proficiency

When you choose this domain at 1st level, you gain proficiency with the Performance skill. Additionally, you gain proficiency with one musical instrument of your choice. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make that uses the Performance skill.

Channel Divinity: Joyful Inspiration

Starting at 2nd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to inspire your allies with the infectious joy of Calico's presence. As an action, you present your holy symbol and choose a number of creatures within 30 feet of you, up to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of one creature). Each chosen creature gains temporary hit points equal to your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier. These temporary hit points last for 1 hour or until depleted. Additionally, whenever a creature with temporary hit points from this feature deals damage with a weapon attack, they can add your Wisdom modifier to the attack roll (but not to the damage roll).

Euphoric Trance

At 17th level, you can enter a state of euphoric trance as an action, embracing the essence of Calico's revelry. While in this state, you emanate an aura of vibrant energy that extends 30 feet from you. Any friendly creatures in this aura gain the following benefits:

They have resistance to radiant damage.

Whenever they make an attack roll, they can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to the attack roll or saving throw.

They have advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks.

This state lasts for 1 minute, and you can end it early by using a bonus action. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

The Joybringer, influenced by the presence of Calico, embodies the spirit of celebration, joy, and the cowboy code in the Old West. These clerics use their divine powers to inspire camaraderie, spread merriment, and bolster the resolve of their allies, infusing the frontier with the uplifting energy of revelry.

Domain of Savage Fertility

The Domain of Frontier's Blessing is a cleric subclass that embraces the juxtaposition of fertility and the unforgiving nature of the Old West. These clerics are driven by a deep understanding of the preciousness of life and the courage it takes to bring forth new generations in a harsh and perilous world. They honor the resilience and tenacity required to survive in the face of constant danger, finding joy in the success and growth of life against all odds.

Domain Spells

1st Level:

Bless, Cure Wounds

3rd Level:

Enhance Ability, Aid

5th Level:

Beacon of Hope, Revivify

7th Level:

Death Ward, Guardian of Faith

9th Level:

Mass Cure Wounds, Raise Dead

Bonus Proficiency

When you choose this domain at 1st level, you gain proficiency with the Survival skill. Additionally, you gain proficiency with one type of artisan's tools or a weapon commonly used in the Old West.

Channel Divinity: Frontier's Resilience.

Starting at 2nd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to invoke the resilience of the frontier. As a bonus action, you grant yourself or a willing creature within 30 feet temporary hit points equal to your cleric level + your Wisdom modifier. Additionally, the target gains resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage until the start of your next turn.

Blessing of New Life

At 6th level, your connection to the life-giving forces of the frontier allows you to bless newborns and celebrate their potential. As an action, you can touch a willing creature, bestowing upon them the Blessing of New Life. For the next 8 hours, the target gains the following benefits:

They have advantage on saving throws against disease and poison.

The first time they are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, they automatically stabilize and regain 1 hit point.

Blessing of Renewed Life

Also, at 6th level, you gain the ability to harness the primal forces of the frontier to protect and nurture life. As an action, you can create an aura of revitalizing energy that extends 30 feet from you. The aura lasts for 1 minute or until you are incapacitated. While the aura is active, you and friendly creatures within it gain the following benefits.

Whenever a creature starts its turn in the aura or enters it for the first time on a turn, they regain hit points equal to your cleric level.

Whenever a creature within the aura makes a saving throw against an effect that would impose the charmed, frightened, or stunned condition, they have advantage on the saving throw.

Friendly creatures within the aura have advantage on ability checks made to resist exhaustion.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

Frontier's Fortune

Starting at 8th level, you have a knack for turning the odds in your favor. Whenever you or a creature within 30 feet of you makes a saving throw or an ability check, you can use your reaction to grant them a bonus equal to your Wisdom modifier. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

Visage of Triumph

At 17th level, you become a beacon of hope and resilience, inspiring those around you to overcome any obstacle. As an action, you emit a powerful aura of determination and survival within 30 feet of you. For the next minute, you and friendly creatures in the aura gain the following benefits:

Advantage on death saving throws.

Resistance to all damage.

Whenever a creature within the aura makes an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, they can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to the result.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

The Domain of Frontier's Blessing represents the profound connection between fertility and survival in the unforgiving world of the Old West. These clerics embody the bravery and resilience required to bring forth life in a brutal and perilous environment. They find solace and joy in the success of new life against all odds, celebrating the strength and potential of each individual. With their divine powers, they inspire hope, offer protection, and nurture the precious lives that venture into the wild and untamed frontier.

Domain of Mischievous Whims.

The Domain of Mischievous Whims is a cleric subclass that embodies the playful and cunning nature of Calico, the bobcat-headed demigoddess of the American Southwest. These clerics embody mercurial cat- like mischief and revel in the unpredictable and unconventional. These clerics combine their religious devotion with a wicked sense of humor, finding joy in the suffering and confusion they inflict upon others.

Domain Spells

1st level:

Disguise Self, Charm Person

3rd level:

Invisibility, Phantasmal Force

5th level:

Fear, Major Image

7th level:

Confusion, Hallucinatory Terrain

9th level:

Modify Memory, Mislead

Bonus Proficiency

When you choose this domain at 1st level, you gain proficiency with sleight of hand and deception skills. Additionally, you gain proficiency with a set of tools of your choice: thieves' tools or a gaming set.

Channel Divinity: Prankster's Delight.

Starting at 2nd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to unleash a malevolent prank upon your foes. As an action, choose a creature within 30 feet of you. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw against your cleric spell save DC. On a failed save, the creature becomes subjected to one of the following malicious effects of your choice for 1 minute.

Chaotic Mirror:

The creature's reflection in nearby reflective surfaces takes on a wicked life of its own. It becomes trapped within the mirror, unable to escape or interact with the real world. During this time, the creature is incapacitated and considered restrained.

Withering Hex:

The creature is hexed with an insidious curse that drains its vitality. It suffers disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

Pervasive Paranoia:

The creature's mind is plagued by twisted illusions and fears, causing it to have disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws and be unable to distinguish friend from foe.

You can only apply one prank effect to a creature at a time.

At the end of the creatures turn, it may attempt a new saving throw against your Spell Save DC, ending the effects on a success.

Prankster's Delight Improvements:

At 6th level, the malevolent pranks you unleash become even more potent. While a creature is affected by your Prank, it suffers additional effects depending on the chosen prank:

Chaotic Mirror:

The creature also takes 2d6 psychic damage at the start of each of its turns while trapped within the mirror.

Withering Hex:

The creature's speed is reduced to 0, and it takes 1d8 necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns.

Pervasive Paranoia:

The creature is now also unable to take reactions and takes 1d6 psychic damage at the start of each of its turns.

Misdirection

Starting at 8th level, you can twist the perceptions of others, making it difficult for them to determine your true location. As a bonus action, you can expend one use of your Channel Divinity to gain the benefits of the Blur spell. This effect lasts for 1 minute or until you are incapacitated.

Additionally, whenever you cast a spell of 1st level or higher that affects only you, you can choose to have it originate from a point within 30 feet of you that you can see.

Trickster's Gambit

At 17th level, you become the ultimate master of trickery, capable of bewildering your enemies with your unpredictable actions. As a bonus action, you gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

You have advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) and Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

You can use your Channel Divinity: Prankster's Delight feature without expending a use of Channel Divinity.

When you hit a creature with an attack, you can choose to impose one of the following effects on the target until the start of your next turn: the target has disadvantage on all attack rolls, or the target has disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
