Path of the Lumber Jacked

The Lumber Jacked are truly the largest and most sturdy of their tribes. Sometimes mistaken for small giants, these barbarians are known for being able to cleave a path of destruction through their enemies.

Path of the Lumber Jacked Features

3rd Level: Shattering Hew, Heavy Hitter

6th Level: Lumbering Charge

10th Level: Crosscut

14th Level: Devastating March

Shattering Hew

Starting at 3rd level, your rage empowers you with the ability to cause explosive destruction with every swing. While raging, you may target a structure, vehicle, object (medium-sized or larger), terrain feature, or a creature that has been deprived of the ability to move in any way with a melee attack. The AC to hit with this attack is 5 unless the target has a listed AC. If you hit the target, roll the damage for the attack and force all other creatures (including yourself) within 10 feet to make a Dexterity saving throw. The DC of this saving throw is 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier. Any creature that fails the saving throw takes half of the damage that was dealt to the original target as piercing damage. Any creature that passes the saving throw takes no damage.

Heavy Hitter

At 3rd level, when you strike a structure, vehicle, object, or terrain feature with a weapon that has the Heavy property, you deal double damage. This may be applied to Shattering Hew, increasing the damage from half of the original value to the full amount.

Lumbering Charge

At 6th level, you have learned how to use your momentum to knock down your foes. While raging, if you have moved at least 20 feet towards a creature, you may use a bonus action to make a melee attack against that creature. If the attack hits, roll the damage as normal, but also the creature must make a Strength saving throw, the DC of which is equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier. If the creature is greater than one size category above you and fails this save, it is knocked prone. Otherwise, if it fails the saving throw, you may push the creature up to 20 feet away from you and knock them prone. If this push results in the creature being pushed into a structure, vehicle, object, or terrain feature that is at least as big as them, they take an additional 2d6 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage from the impact.

Crosscut

At 10th level, your attacks cleave a wide swath through foes. Whenever you make a melee attack, you may target up to 3 creatures within 5 feet of you. Roll to attack once and compare the roll to the AC of all targeted creatures, then roll the damage once and apply that to each creature your attack hits. Only if you hit at least one creature with this feature does it consume a use of this feature. You may use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency modifier, and you regain all expended uses of it when you finish a long rest.

Devastating March (Level 14):

Starting at 14th level, your immense presence and powerful strides allow you to move through the space of any creature that is unable to move or has been deprived of the ability to move. When you move through the space of a creature in this way, that creature takes bludgeoning damage equal to your level in this class.

Additionally, when you use Devastating March, you can move up to your remaining movement speed for the current turn. This movement can be used before, during, or after striking a creature, providing you have enough movement remaining. Remember that movement expended to move through a creature's space still counts towards your total movement for the turn.
