Arcanian Warlock options.

The Rail Baron Pact

As a warlock, you have forged a pact with the powerful and enigmatic beings known as the Rail Barons. These ancient entities are the personification of the rapid industrialization and expansion of railroads in the realm of Arcania. They are shrewd, influential, and possess an insatiable hunger for wealth, power, and control.

Expanded Spell List

The Rail Baron lets you choose from an expanded list of spells when you learn a warlock spell. The following spells are added to the warlock spell list for you.

1st level:

Expeditious Retreat, Grease

2nd level:

Enhance Ability, Web

3rd level:

Blink, Haste

4th level:

Fabricate, Dimension Door

5th level:

Passwall, Teleportation Circle

Pact of Expansion

Starting at 1st level, when you choose this pact, you gain proficiency in Athletics and History skills. Additionally, you can use your bonus action to gain temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier + your warlock level (minimum of 1).

Profitable Deals

At 6th level, your connection with the Rail Barons grants you an uncanny knack for striking lucrative bargains and exploiting opportunities for personal gain. You have advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks related to negotiations, contracts, and business transactions. Additionally, you can use your expanded spell list spells without expending a spell slot once per long rest.

Iron Wheels of Dominion

At 10th level, the Rail Barons bestow upon you the ability to traverse great distances with incredible speed. You can cast the spell Phantom Steed without expending a spell slot. The steed summoned through this feature gains a +10-foot bonus to its speed. Additionally, you gain resistance to forced movement effects, such as pushes or pulls.

Dominion's Vengeance

At 14th level, your patrons grant you the power to unleash their wrath upon those who would dare oppose you. As an action, you can choose a creature within 60 feet of you and force it to make a Wisdom saving throw against your warlock spell save DC. On a failed save, the creature takes 8d8 force damage and is pushed 20 feet away from you. On a successful save, the creature takes half damage and isn't pushed. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

The Rail Baron pact is a unique option for warlocks seeking a connection to the influential beings behind the rise of railroads in the realm of Arcania. With their powers of expansion, profit, and vengeance, warlocks who form a pact with the Rail Barons gain the ability to navigate vast distances, strike profitable deals, and punish their enemies with unrelenting force. Choose this pact and become a force to be reckoned with, both on and off the rails.

Pact Boon:

Railbound Companion

At 3rd level, as part of your pact with the Rail Barons, you gain a unique companion closely tied to the realm of railroads and industrialization. This companion, known as a Railbound, manifests as a mechanical creature imbued with arcane energies and the essence of the Rail Barons' influence.

The Railbound companion is a loyal ally, offering assistance and protection as you traverse the rail-laden landscapes of Arcania. Choose one of the following Railbound companions: the Railhound, the Iron Drake, or the Steam Crawler. The Railbound companion's statistics are detailed at the end of this description.

While your Railbound companion is within 100 feet of you, you gain the following benefits:

-

Railbound Link:

You can communicate telepathically with your Railbound companion, allowing you to share information and coordinate strategies seamlessly.

Mechanized Assistance:

Your Railbound companion can aid you in various tasks. It can perform simple actions and interact with objects as if it were an independent creature. Additionally, it can use its reaction to provide you with advantage on a single ability check or saving throw.

Choose the Railbound companion that best suits your playstyle and needs:

Railhound:

The Railhound is a swift and nimble companion, resembling a mechanical hound with razor-sharp claws. It excels in tracking, scouting, and chasing down enemies. While your Railhound companion is active, you gain advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and have advantage on initiative rolls.

Iron Drake:

The Iron Drake is a powerful and imposing companion, resembling a massive mechanical dragon with wings of steel and fire. It excels at being an imposing figure. While your Iron Drake companion is active, you gain a bonus to spell attack rolls equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of +1) and have resistance to fire damage.

Steam Crawler:

The Steam Crawler is a versatile and resourceful companion, resembling a spider-like mechanical construct. It excels in stealth, manipulation, and intricate tasks. While your Steam Crawler companion is active, you gain advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks and have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

Your Railbound companion acts independently in combat, following your general instructions, but it always acts in the best interest of its pact partner. Should your Railbound companion be destroyed, you can perform a ritual over 1 hour to summon a new one, provided you have access to a railway or railroad track.

Rail Tracker:

The Railhound has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to track creatures and detect hidden or concealed objects within 30 feet of it. Additionally, it can use its reaction to make a Wisdom (Perception) check against a creature it can see that is attempting to Hide, and if successful, it reveals the creature's location.

Evasive Maneuvers:

When the Railhound takes the Dodge action, it gains a +2 bonus to its Armor Class until the start of its next turn.
