World Lore: Hermetika


Deep within the realms of Hermetika, a world of mystery and peril, lies a tale of ancient hermetic myth and clandestine societies. Divided into two distinct lands, Arcania and Emperyian, Hermetika weaves a tapestry of grand adventures, divine struggle, and the pursuit of hidden knowledge.


Arcania: The New World

Arcania, known as the New World, serves as a haven for those who sought refuge from the cataclysms of Emperyian. Its vast and diverse landscapes stretch from the majestic Sierra Nevada Mountains to the lush Sequoia Forests, offering both sanctuary and untamed frontiers. Here, a new civilization has emerged, shaped by the trials of survival and the pursuit of enlightenment.


Within Arcania, three distinct life paths beckon aspiring adventurers. The Rancher, tethered to the land and its bounties, harnesses the power of nature and stewardship. The Miner, delving deep into the earth's riches, uncovers hidden treasures and the secrets of the subterranean realms. The Gambler, navigating the San Gabriel River, embraces risk and chance, seeking fortune and fate along their path.


Yet, Arcania is not free from its own perils. The malevolent presence of Croatoan, also known as He Who Walks Behind (HWWB), casts a shadow over the land. Legends caution against speaking his name aloud, for the mere mention draws his attention, unleashing darkness and chaos. The inhabitants of Arcania have devised cryptic ways to reference him, employing a code that shields them from his omnipresent gaze.


At the heart of Arcania's governance are the Wardens, exceptional beings who embody humility and reject godhood. Denied divine entry into the New World, gods turn their gaze towards Emperyian, leaving the Wardens as protectors of the land. These remarkable individuals, gifted with incredible powers, navigate a delicate balance, shielding Arcania from the destructive forces of the Old World.


Emperyian: The Old World

Emperyian, the Old World, stands in the throes of cataclysmic turmoil. Croatoan's insatiable hunger for divine power has devoured the neutral-aligned gods, leaving the factions of good and evil locked in a relentless struggle. The once prosperous realm now bears the scars of a divine conflict, as forces clash and vie for dominance.


In Emperyian, secret societies devoted to hermetic mysticism and Kabbalistic teachings flourish. Echoes of the Golden Dawn, Rosicrucianism, and other esteemed orders resonate within its fractured borders. The pursuit of hidden knowledge and ancient wisdom drives these enigmatic organizations, each with its own agenda and hidden truths.


The Struggle for Balance

As the denizens of Hermetika grapple with the remnants of divine power and the influence of Croatoan, heroes arise to navigate this perilous landscape. They unravel ancient prophecies, delve into forgotten relics, and form alliances to protect the fragile equilibrium between good and evil.

It is within this world, where mortals and extraordinary beings alike converge, that destiny awaits those who dare to confront the encroaching darkness. Heroes will rise, forged by courage and virtue, to determine the fate of Hermetika.


Setting specific lore: "Welcome to Legend"


Within the vast expanse of Hermetika, a world shrouded in mysticism and secrets, lies a myriad of landscapes and realms waiting to be explored. Venture further into the depths of this intricate setting, where legends collide and destinies intertwine.


The City of Legend


At the heart of Arcania's westward trek lies the City of Legend, a beacon of civilization amidst the untamed wilderness. Known as Los Angeles in the world we inhabit, this sprawling metropolis represents the pinnacle of human achievement. The Hollywood hills rise against the backdrop of the Pacific Ocean, while the streets hum with vibrant energy and diverse cultures.

Within the City of Legend, a tapestry of intrigue unfolds. Secret societies, hidden enclaves, and clandestine organizations dwell in the shadows, each with their own agenda and hidden truths. Delve into the depths of these esoteric circles, where hermetic mysticism and ancient knowledge hold sway, and uncover the enigmatic forces that shape the destiny of Hermetika.


The Sierra Nevada Mountains: Treacherous Peaks


As you embark on your treacherous trek from Bodfish Boomtown, high in the Sierra Nevada Mountains, be prepared to confront nature's formidable challenges. Towering peaks, veiled in mist and adorned with ancient ruins, provide a picturesque yet perilous backdrop. Majestic waterfalls cascade down moss-covered cliffs, while hidden caves and winding trails beckon the adventurous.


Beneath the surface, the mountains hold secrets untold. Cryptic encounters with legendary creatures such as Bigfoot, The Mothman, and Wendigos will test your mettle and push the boundaries of your courage. Prepare to face these monstrous beings in epic battles that may determine the fate of your expedition.


The Sequoia Forest: Enchanted Depths


As you traverse through the Sequoia Forest, a sense of enchantment permeates the air. Towering ancient trees, whispering with the wisdom of ages, create a mystical ambiance that embraces wanderers. Sunlight filters through the dense foliage, casting ethereal rays upon the forest floor, where magical creatures dwell and mythical beasts roam.


But within this serene realm lies a delicate balance. Ancient guardians protect the forest's hidden sanctuaries, defending against those who would exploit its power for nefarious purposes. As you navigate these enchanted depths, forge alliances with the forest's protectors, unravel ancient prophecies, and discover the sacred groves that hold the key to restoring harmony to the land.


The Southwestern Deserts: Land of Trials

Venturing further into the southwest, a land of arid deserts and scorching sun awaits. Here, the influence of Croatoan looms large, casting a malevolent shadow over the desolate landscapes. Within these treacherous wastelands, survival is a daily struggle, and ancient ruins whisper tales of forgotten civilizations and lost relics.

The journey through the southwestern deserts will test your endurance and resourcefulness. Encounter rogue wardens loyal to Croatoan, as they seek to undermine the balance established by their humble counterparts. Engage in battles of wit and strength, unearthing hidden truths and unraveling the enigma that surrounds the demi-godlike ruler of the Old World.


The Great Barrier: Separating Realms.

A formidable barrier separates the Old World and the New, a metaphysical divide that prevents gods from crossing into Arcania. This barrier, a mystical force of immense power, shields the New World from the divine turmoil and manipulations of the gods. Within Arcania, the Wardens stand as protectors, wielding their extraordinary abilities to safeguard the land and its people.

Yet, the Great Barrier serves as a double-edged sword. While it shields Arcania from the destructive forces of gods, it also limits the direct influence of divine power on the New World. Instead, Arcania is governed by the Wardens, individuals who have chosen the path of humility and rejected the opportunity of godhood through Apotheosis. These Wardens, possessing incredible abilities honed through their connection to the land and the elements, ensure the delicate balance of Arcania. United under a common cause, they strive to maintain order, protect the realm from external threats, and thwart the nefarious ambitions of Croatoan, the embodiment of chaos and devourer of divine power. The struggle between good and evil intensifies in the Old World, where factions battle for supremacy in the absence of divine intervention. With the neutrally aligned gods consumed by Croatoan, the forces of good and evil vie for control, their conflicts echoing across the ancient landscapes. It is within this chaotic backdrop that heroes and villains rise, their choices shaping the destiny of both worlds. Columbia: Warden of The Empire

In the North East of Arcania, the resolute figure of Columbia stands as the embodiment of liberty and the guardian of individual freedoms. Her domain is marked by bustling cities, thriving communities, and the pursuit of progress. As the Warden of Liberty, Columbia's presence radiates with passion and energy, igniting the hearts of her wards with the fiery spirit of independence.

Personality Traits: Passionate, fierce, patriotic, idealistic.

Symbol: Columbia's symbol is that of an uplifted torch, a beacon of hope and freedom that shines brightly against a teal background. It represents the eternal flame of liberty and the relentless pursuit of individual rights and self-determination.

Domain: Columbia governs the vibrant North East, a region where great cities and picturesque towns coexist in a delicate balance. From the towering spires of Empire City (analogous to New York) to the coastal beauty of Seabreeze Harbor, her domain pulsates with the energy of progress and the diverse aspirations of its inhabitants.

Warden's Virtue: Liberty Above All. Columbia embodies the ideal that freedom is the cornerstone of society. She champions the rights of individuals and fiercely defends their autonomy, urging them to exercise their liberties responsibly and strive for a society where every person has the opportunity to pursue their dreams without hindrance.

Notable Locations: The North East is a tapestry of remarkable landmarks and iconic cities. From the academic splendor of the Ivory towers in Harvard, renowned for its prestigious magical institutions, to the grandeur of Empire City, seat of power and governance, the region exudes a sense of ambition and intellectual fervor.

Verdantreach: This ia a place where the boundaries between the mortal world and the Feywild blend and intertwine. The air is heavy with mist and the gentle caress of rain, casting an ethereal veil over the sparsely populated landscape. Cranberry bogs, shrouded in a soft, otherworldly glow, exude a haunting beauty, their silent waters reflecting the muted colors of the moor. Towering maple forests stand like sentinels, their gnarled branches reaching towards the somber sky, while wisps of fog weave through their ancient trunks. In this realm, time seems to slow, and a sense of tranquility pervades the mist-laden valleys. The inhabitants of Verdantreach, few in number, live in harmony with the mystical forces that permeate the land, finding solace in the quietude of the moor. It is a place where the beauty of nature is embraced in its raw and serene form, a testament to the enchanting allure of the untamed wilderness.

Warden's Influence: Columbia's influence extends through the hearts and minds of her wards. Her impassioned speeches and resolute actions inspire them to stand up for what they believe in and to fight against injustice and oppression. She encourages the pursuit of dreams and the exploration of new frontiers, always reminding her people that their individual liberties are precious and worth defending.

Columbia's Gift: Those touched by Columbia's influence possess an unwavering spirit of independence and a deep-rooted belief in the power of individuality. They are driven by a desire to make a difference in the world, fueled by the flame of liberty that burns within them. With their fiery determination and a relentless pursuit of justice, they strive to create a society that upholds the principles of freedom and equality.

Embrace the vibrant energy of Columbia's domain, where the pursuit of individual liberties intertwines with the collective aspiration for progress. Stand tall in the face of adversity, defend the rights of the oppressed, and let the flame of liberty guide your path. In the North East, under Columbia's watchful gaze, the spirit of freedom thrives, and the pursuit of a better tomorrow is a shared endeavor.

Cascadia: Voyager of the Emerald Tides

Cascadia, the enigmatic Warden of the North West, holds sway over a region characterized by sprawling forests, majestic mountains, and abundant natural beauty. Her presence is both subtle and pervasive, as she weaves herself into the fabric of everyday life, tending to the needs of the land and its inhabitants with a gentle touch. Cascadia's domain is a realm of untamed wilderness and individuals pursuing their passions, where personal freedom and the pursuit of happiness are cherished values.

Personality Traits: Approachable, affable, perceptive, adaptable.

Symbol: Cascadia's symbol is a representation of two hands—one reaching upward toward a blooming rose, symbolizing aspiration and fulfillment, and the other reaching downward, clutching a wilted rose, representing the inevitability of decay and impermanence. This duality embodies the balance she seeks to maintain within her domain.

Domain: Cascadia governs the vast territories of the North West, encompassing dense forests, cascading waterfalls, and hidden valleys. It is a land of lush greenery, where the whispers of the wind and the harmony of nature are ever-present. Within her domain, individuals are free to pursue their dreams and find solace in the embrace of the wild. Cascadia's ethos revolves around finding fulfillment in the pursuit of desires rather than the attainment of possessions. She encourages her wards to appreciate the beauty of the journey, to relish in their aspirations, and to let go of the attachment to material possessions that can hinder true happiness.

Notable locations: Emerald City (Seattle) thrives as the major city, where the natural world blends seamlessly with urban life. It is a vibrant and diverse metropolis known for its thriving arts scene, innovative technology companies, and a strong sense of community that embraces both progress and environmental sustainability.

Mount Rainier, rising majestically above the North West region, holds a special significance as the home of the Sasquatch race. The mountain serves as a sacred place where the Sasquatch find solace and wisdom. Its snow-capped peak and pristine wilderness offer breathtaking vistas and opportunities for adventurous exploration.

The Puget Sound, with its intricate waterways, islands, and coastal towns, becomes a haven for pirate adventures in Cascadia's domain. Smugglers, treasure hunters, and seafaring rogues navigate the waters, seeking hidden riches and secret coves. The Sound buzzes with excitement, tales of buried treasure, and the thrill of the high seas.

Cascadia's presence: Cascadia walks among her people, blending seamlessly into the tapestry of their lives. She takes on various forms—a wandering traveler, a friendly neighbor, or an amiable stranger at a pub. Her interactions are marked by genuine curiosity and an innate ability to make others feel at ease, allowing her to observe the pursuit of happiness without imposing her will upon them.

Those who find themselves drawn to the North West are often imbued with a sense of adaptability and an affinity for the natural world. They possess a keen perception of their surroundings and an intuitive understanding of the delicate balance between personal aspirations and communal harmony. Their connection to Cascadia's domain grants them the potential for skills in survival, wilderness lore, or the art of diplomacy, enabling them to navigate the challenges of the untamed wilderness and foster harmony among diverse communities.

Explore the untamed beauty of Cascadia's domain, where the pursuit of happiness intertwines with the rhythms of nature. Wander through the verdant forests, forge friendships with the diverse inhabitants, and learn to embrace the fleeting beauty of life in the North West. In Cascadia's realm, the journey is as important as the destination, and the pursuit of one's dreams is celebrated above all else.

Oro: The Gilded Sovereign

Oro, the formidable Warden known as the Gilded Sovereign, rules over the expansive desert lands of Ungoverned California and the Mojave Desert. With the visage of a lion-headed cat person and majestic wings, he exudes an aura of power and opulence. Oro's domain, aptly named the Golden Expanse, is a realm where the pursuit of personal gain and unyielding ambition reign supreme. Assertive, opportunistic, and indomitable, Oro embodies the essence of those who choose to defend their wealth and seize what is rightfully theirs.

Symbol: The symbol of Oro is a gilded lion's head, representing his regal stature, commanding presence, and indomitable spirit. It serves as a reminder of his unwavering determination to protect and preserve the riches of his domain.

Domain: The Golden Expanse encompasses the vast desert lands of Ungoverned California and the Mojave Desert. Within this domain, the Brown Bear Republic thrives, a collection of city-states populated by the anthropomorphic Brunelocks. These resourceful beings embody Oro's values, utilizing their opportunistic nature to forge their own destinies in pursuit of affluence.

Warden's Virtue: None May Take What Is Mine. Oro's essence reflects the tenets of the desert lands he governs. As the Gilded Sovereign, he champions the rights of his people to defend their wealth and possessions. Asserting his authority with unyielding force, Oro ensures that those who attempt to trespass or lay claim to what belongs to his wards do so at their peril.

Notable Locations: Oro's dominion starts across the vast and untamed lands of Ungoverned California. Here, the rugged terrain of the Sierra Nevada desert meets the vast expanse of the salt flats, creating a landscape that embodies the spirit of freedom and resilience. The desert vistas of Joshua Tree and the sprawling Mojave Desert, home to the esteemed Merchant Guild of the Death Valley Queen, stand as testaments to the unforgiving yet awe-inspiring nature of this land. Untamed and wild, this region beckons to those who seek to challenge the boundaries of the untamed West.

Amidst the rugged landscapes, the Brown Bear Republic emerges as a formidable city-state nation. Nestled within the majestic Sierra Nevada mountains, the mining boom towns embody the spirit of ambition and untold wealth. The San Gabriel River winds its way through the lush valleys, offering a lifeline to those in search of fortune. Towering Sequoia redwood forests stand as ancient sentinels, their majestic presence reminding all who venture here of the enduring strength of the land.

At the heart of the Brown Bear Republic lies the city of Legend, a gleaming jewel set against the backdrop of the shining ocean. Within its borders, the enigmatic Seraphs, resembling ethereal and otherworldly beings, reside. These beings, reminiscent of biblically accurate angels, epitomize the dreams and aspirations that permeated the golden era of the American West. Legend, with its blend of mystique and opportunity, stands as a testament to the indomitable spirit that drives Oro's domain, beckoning both the hopeful and the intrepid to seek their destinies within its vibrant streets.

Warden's Influence: Oro's influence permeates the Golden Expanse, inspiring his subjects to adopt an assertive and opportunistic mindset. The Brunelocks, guided by his example, seize opportunities to accumulate wealth and power through hard work and trade, and harshly punish anyone who takes what isn't theirs. Oro's indomitable nature instills in his people the unyielding determination to protect what they have acquired and defend their interests at all costs.

Oro's Gift: Fortune's Embrace

Those drawn to Oro's domain are imbued with a touch of the untamed West and the indomitable spirit that resides within it. They possess an unwavering resilience and an innate ability to seize opportunities amidst adversity. Guided by Oro's influence, they navigate the unforgiving landscapes with a sense of purpose, ever vigilant in their pursuit of wealth and fulfillment.

Fontaine: Unfading Queen of the Flowing Glades

Fontaine, the Unfading Queen of the Everflowing Glades, holds dominion over a realm of savage beauty and untamed wilderness. Her domain encompasses the enigmatic Fountain of Eternal Youth, its magical waters breathing life into the vibrant swamps that stretch as far as the eye can see. Within the mystical Bayouque, the heart of her kingdom, Anansi the spider demigod reigns as mayor, weaving tales and legends that intertwine with the mysterious allure of the land.

Personality Traits: Mysterious, regal, alluring, tenacious, cunning.

Symbol: Fontaine's symbol is a delicate lily, its petals shimmering with an otherworldly glow. Representing the eternal cycle of life and rejuvenation, it signifies her connection to the everlasting waters of the glades and the perpetual vitality she embodies.

Domain: Fontaine's realm is a vast expanse of marshes, wetlands, and moss-laden trees, where nature thrives in its raw and primal form. The Everflowing Glades, nourished by the enchanted Fountain of Eternal Youth, harbor both beauty and danger, where life and death dance in an eternal embrace. The Bayouque, with its mystical allure and rich cultural tapestry, stands as a testament to the enchantment that permeates the land.

Warden's Virtue: Endless Vigor, Unyielding Grace. Fontaine embodies the everlasting energy of the glades, drawing strength from the timeless waters and channeling it into a graceful and unstoppable force. She encourages her subjects to embrace resilience, adaptability, and the everlasting vitality that flows through their veins.

Notable Locations: The Everflowing Glades are teeming with wondrous sights and remarkable settlements. From the mysterious Whispering Marsh, where ethereal whispers and secrets linger, to the vibrant Bayouque, a hub of artistic expression and mystical rituals. The Fountain of Eternal Youth, a focal point of her domain, emanates a mesmerizing glow, promising eternal life to those who dare to seek its mythical waters.

The land of Fontaine also harbors Gneorgia, a region ruled by the Peach Punk Gnomes. These industrious gnomes, fueled by potent peach-derived alcohol, have built a steampunk-inspired society. Their ingenious machines and whimsical inventions blend seamlessly with the natural surroundings, creating a unique fusion of nature and technology.

Warden's Influence: Fontaine's presence is felt in every rustling leaf, in the shimmering reflections of the water, and in the haunting melodies that echo through the glades. She embodies the enigmatic allure of the Everflowing Glades, drawing creatures of both light and darkness, weaving her web of influence with captivating charm and calculated grace.

Fontaine's Gift: Those who dwell within the Everflowing Glades are touched by the eternal energies of the land. They possess an ageless vitality and an affinity for the enchantments of nature. Gifted with enduring grace and a keen perception of the ever-changing currents of life, they navigate the swamps with resilience and adaptability.

Embrace the allure and mystique of Fontaine's realm, where the waters flow eternally, and life dances with eternal youth. Explore the depths of the Everflowing Glades, immerse yourself in the cultural tapestry of Bayouque, and witness the ingenuity of the Peach Punk Gnomes in Gneorgia. In the land of perpetual vitality, where legends and reality intertwine, Fontaine reigns as the Unfading Queen of the Everflowing Glades.
