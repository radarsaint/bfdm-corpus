Enforcer

Enforcers are often called brutes, thugs, or knee breakers. They generally can be found wielding items such as metal pipes, chains, broken bottles, chairs, or whatever else they may have on hand. It's rare that these sorts of rogues rely on stealth; instead, they are treated as the muscle that enforces their organization's will.

Enforcer Features

3rd Level: Brawler, Menacing

9th Level: Distracting Presence

13th Level: Frightful

17th Level: Brutality

Brawler

When you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with improvised weapons and medium armor.

Use this to scrounge up a improvised weapon.

(Link)

You also gain an additional way to use your Sneak Attack: you don't need advantage on the attack roll to use your Sneak Attack against a creature if you are within 5 feet of it, using an improvised weapon, and don't have disadvantage on the attack roll.

Menacing

Starting at 3rd level, you can choose to use Strength for Intimidation skill checks instead of Charisma.

Distracting Presence

Starting at 9th level, you can use shouting, running at enemies, and other methods of distraction to allow your allies to better sneak past enemies. If you succeed on an Intimidation skill check vs. an enemy or group of enemies' insight(s), you grant your allies +5 to any Stealth check they make against the enemies that failed their insight check while you continue to distract the enemies (up to 1 minute).

Improvised Weapons

As an Enforcer, you have access to a variety of improvised weapons that you can wield with devastating effect. Consult the Enforcer Improvised Weapons Table to determine the statistics and properties of your chosen improvised weapon. The table provides a range of options, including damage type, special properties, and potential drawbacks. Feel free to work with your DM to customize and expand this list to suit your campaign.

Frightful

Beginning at 13th level, when an enemy fails an insight check vs. your intimidation, you can use your reaction to force them to make a Charisma saving throw, DC 8 + Proficiency Bonus + Strength Modifier, or be frightened of you for 1 minute. If they fail this saving throw by 5 or more, they are also immobilized. Any enemy immune to the frightened condition is immune to this effect.

At the end of each of their turns, they may re-attempt this saving throw to end this effect.

Once you have used this feature on an enemy, you cannot re-attempt it on the same enemy for 24 hours. You may use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and all uses return after a long rest.

Brutality

When you reach 17th level, you have become a truly force of brutality. When you roll sneak attack damage, every sneak attack damage die that rolls a 1 is treated as a 3 instead. In addition, when you sneak attack an enemy, you may choose to slow their movement by half until the end of their next turn.

This revised description of the Enforcer subclass reflects the inclusion of an Enforcer Improvised Weapons Table for determining the statistics and properties of improvised weapons. The table allows for greater customization and variety in the weapons used by Enforcers.
