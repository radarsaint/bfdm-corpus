Background:

Baron Vanderbilt's Guardians

You hail from a group known as Baron Vanderbilt's Guardians, a select few who have sworn their allegiance and service to the influential Baron Cornelius Vanderbilt. As a member of this background, you are entrusted with protecting the Baron's interests, maintaining order, and ensuring the success of his extensive enterprises.

Skill Proficiencies:

Athletics, Insight

Tool Proficiencies:

One type of gaming set, Water vehicles

Languages:

Transcontinental

Equipment:

A set of fine clothes, a badge or emblem representing Baron Vanderbilt's Guardians, a gaming set of your choice, a set of traveler's clothes, and a belt pouch containing 15 gold

dollars.

Feature: Authority of Baron Vanderbilt

As a member of Baron Vanderbilt's Guardians, you wield a measure of authority within the realms of the Baron's influence. When interacting with individuals associated with the Baron's enterprises or those who acknowledge his power, you can assert your authority and garner their cooperation, within reason. You can also utilize your position to acquire basic supplies and lodgings that fall within the realm of the Baron's influence. Gambling halls fall within this influence.

Empire City Slicker

You come from the sprawling metropolis known as Empire City, a bastion of advanced technology and potent magic. As an Empire City Slicker, you have ventured from the urban wonders of Empire City to the untamed frontiers of the Old West, bringing with you a unique set of skills and knowledge that both fascinate and bewilder the rugged folks of the Wild West.

Tool Proficiencies

Languages:

Costal Arcanian. Morse' code.

Equipment:

A set of fine clothes that reflect the fashion of Empire City, a stylish accessory from Empire City, a set of artisan's tools of your choice, a gaming set of your choice, and a belt pouch containing 15 dollars.

Feature: Urban Ingenuity

As an Empire City Slicker, you possess a flair for the sophisticated and a keen mind shaped by the wonders of Empire City. While others may view you as a "city slicker," your unique skills and knowledge can be valuable assets in navigating the challenges of the Wild West. When interacting with individuals in the Old West, you can use your urban ingenuity to persuade, charm, or negotiate, gaining advantages in social situations.

As a Raven Herald, your connection to Odin's Ravens grants you a heightened sense of perception and intuition. When attempting to gather information or discern hidden truths, you can call upon the raven's insight. Verdantreach Explorer

You come from the picturesque state of Verdantreach, nestled in the heart of the North East region of Arcania. Scholar of the Ivory Tower

You have dedicated your life to the pursuit of knowledge and intellectual mastery within the hallowed halls of the Ivory Tower, a prestigious institution built by the visionary scholar known as Harvard. As a Scholar of the Ivory Tower, you have honed your mind and academic skills, delving into the realms of history, magic, and philosophy. You are well-versed in the scholarly arts and possess a deep understanding of various academic disciplines.

Skill Proficiencies:

Arcana, History

Tool Proficiencies:

One type of artisan's tools or one musical instrument

Languages:

Two additional languages of your choice

Feature: Scholar's Expertise

Your extensive studies and intellectual pursuits have made you an expert in your chosen field of study. Whenever you make an ability check that uses your chosen area of expertise, your proficiency bonus is doubled if you are proficient in the skill.

Harvard's Guidance

At 1st level, you gain access to a wealth of knowledge bestowed upon you by Harvard's teachings. You can spend 1 hour consulting ancient tomes, scrolls, or written works to gain advantage on one Intelligence (Arcana, History, or Investigation) check of your choice. Once you use this feature, you must finish a long rest before you can use it again.

Background: Steamboat Captain

Born and raised in the bustling city of Emerald, you developed a deep fascination for the steamboats that traversed the picturesque waters of Puget Sound. From an early age, you honed your skills in navigation, ship handling, and understanding the ever-changing currents of the Sound. Now, as a seasoned Steamboat Captain, you command the helm of a majestic vessel, ferrying passengers, cargo, and stories across the sparkling waters. Your knowledge of the hidden coves, treacherous shoals, and bustling ports along Puget Sound makes you a respected figure among the maritime community. You navigate through dense fog and rough waves, always ensuring the safe and efficient transportation of goods and people. Your reputation as a skilled captain grants you opportunities for lucrative contracts and opens doors to new adventures on the vast expanse of Puget Sound.

Skill Proficiencies:

Athletics, Perception

Tool Proficiencies: Navigator's tools

Languages:

One of your choice

Equipment:

A captain's hat, a set of fine clothes, a compass, a set of navigator's tools, a pouch containing 5 gold pieces (in dollars), and a logbook detailing your past voyages on Puget Sound.

Feature

: Nautical Expertise

As an experienced Steamboat Captain, you possess exceptional knowledge of water navigation and piloting vessels. You have advantage on checks made to navigate and pilot steamboats or other water vessels. Whether it's maneuvering through treacherous waters, reading navigational charts, or understanding the currents and tides, your expertise makes you a sought-after captain or crew member on any waterborne vessel. You can also secure discounted or free passage on most vessels, as your reputation precedes you.

Background: Pioneer

Point Defiance, a remote outpost on the edge of the untamed frontier, stands as a bastion of civilization amidst the wilderness. As a Pioneer, you have embraced the spirit of exploration and resilience, venturing into the untamed landscapes that stretch beyond the settlement. Clad in your worn pioneer's clothes and armed with your trusty hunting knife, you are a testament to the indomitable human spirit. Your navigator's tools serve as your guide, helping you chart unexplored territories and navigate the treacherous terrain with precision. Countless days and nights have been spent traversing the rugged landscapes, forging new paths, and encountering both the wonders and dangers of the wild. Your weathered journal holds the stories of your adventures, chronicling the majestic beauty of the Pacific Northwest and the untold tales hidden within the depths of the forest.

Navigator's tools

Languages:

Trailblazer

As a Pioneer of Point Defiance, you are no stranger to the challenges of the untamed frontier. You have honed your survival skills to navigate the rugged landscapes and unpredictable wilderness surrounding this remote outpost. You have advantage on Survival checks made to forage for food, find shelter, and track creatures in the wild. Additionally, you can use your History proficiency to recall information about the local geography, notable landmarks, and legends of the region.
