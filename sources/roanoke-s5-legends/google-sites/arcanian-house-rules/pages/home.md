House Rules

House rules are separate from server rules found

here.

Rules New to S5:

You must have a tent in order to take a long rest while traveling. Not having a tent to sleep in will cause you to take a point of exhaustion.

If your character is water dependent, it will be assumed that while in vicinity of the river, no ill effects need to be declared or tracked

Instant kill exploits (like the bag of holding/portable hole bombs) will be banned. They aren't fun for anyone unless its their first time seeing it.

Likewise no matter how many

Vials of oil you have, it will only burn for 5 hp per turn. Noah.

Homebrew items are subject to DM revision if a problem occurs.

Anything that recharges at dawn now recharges at High noon.

Pets, mounts and wealth are subject to be stricken down even while out of combat.

Flying characters (with flight orginating from an inherient flight speed) are restricted to moving from point a to point b. In order to stay in the air between turns, an athletics or acrobatics role will be made with a dc at DM’s discretion (similar to a concentration check.)

Our events have implemented an arcane property to all component pouches and arcane focuses. If your character is casting a spell whose components cost money, all components 100 gold dollars or less might be available in towns (dependent on bot generation), trading posts and trapper posts. Anything more expensive can be ordered through the catalog and delivered to the next post or town. Anything less expensive can be found via the crafting rules.

Control of creatures, familiars, hirelings, occurs after your turn ends. They will always be next in line from you in initiative. Instructing them costs your bonus action.

Pistols are considered simple and ranged. Long guns (riffles, shot guns etc.)are considered Martial and ranged.

Money:

We will be adopting more modern names for currency this year to help improve the immersion

Currency for this game is as follows:

Copper is referred to as pennies.

Silver is referred to as dimes.

Gold is referred to a dollars.

Platinum is interchangeable 10 dollar bank notes.

Ore has a 4:1 trade ratio. (IE: 4 gold nuggets can be smelted into 1 gold dollar coin)

Nuggets cannot be spent until minted. Minting can only occur at banks.

House rules

Our game is played in real time.

A short rest takes an actual hour. A long rest is typically taken when you leave the server for the day and is meant to represent an actual long rest. In other words, we have a built in mechanic to help you take time off of the server. This game will cause many to invest deeply in their character. Don’t allow fear of missing out on the game to cause you to miss out on taking care of your life and your mental health. This both a server rule, and a house rule.

When crafting equipment, learning tools languages or proficiencies, touch bases with a mod or admin to give us a heads up. The same goes for purchasing wondrous items from the catalog.

No Retconning.

If you say it, you’ve done it. The size of our event means that one person changing “what they would have done” will prompt a cascade of others wanting to also change their tactics. It makes a bottleneck that slows or even stops the game.

If absolutely necessary

for the integrity of the game, a DM may choose to retcon sparingly.

Respect the pause emoji.

As stated in the FAQ, The DM pause emoji is sacred. When it shows up in chat, do not interrupt, because your DM is about to give you critical information.

Any creature belonging to a player character (or any other classification of pet, servant, mount, familiar, demon or whatever) shares your initiative.

It goes after your turn. If it shares your action economy, simply use your turn to pass your action on to your pet.

Potions cost a bonus action to use. Goodberies and Sandiberries are the size of a grapefruit and take a bonus action to scarf them down.

Calm emotions will not be treated as dispel for charm effects.

It will prevent your friends from acting against their will for the duration of the spell, but they will remain under the calm emotions effect of ambivalence. Instead dispel will be able to undo charms of 3rd level or lower. In order to dispel higher level charms, simply upcast dispel to the same level as the charm, or cast it as an 8 hour ritual.

Out of combat: Don’t roll in chat without first declaring why/ what you are trying to accomplish to your DM.

Minor instances like insight checks between characters are fine with consent.

During combat: Don’t roll without being prompted by a dm or requesting a roll.

Beginning week one, the crafting system will go live.

This is a largely barter based economy. Learn to depend on others and be dependable.

Casting with material components.

Our events have implemented an arcane property to all component pouches and arcane focuses. If your character is casting a spell whose components cost gold, all components 100 gold or less will be available in towns, trading posts and trapper posts. Anything more expensive can be ordered through the catalog and delivered to the next post or town.

Talking limits during combat.

While speaking during combat is a free action, limit yourself to two sentences. This speeds up combat and reflects the 6 second period in which your action occurs. Players are welcome to use the out of character chat during game play.

Control of creatures, familiars, hirelings, occurs after your turn

ends. They will always be next in line from you in initiative. Instructing them costs your bonus action.
