Training

Learning skills, tools, instruments, vehicles or languages.

(Key terms in bold so you can skim when checking the doc later.)

You will be granted 4 hours of "Down time" per day. While practicing your new skills tools, instruments, vehicles or languages you will have a m

aximum of

4 attempts

to learn per day (1 per hour during down time.) Learning a skill takes a cumulative total of

20 passed checks

. The dc for these checks is

dc 12

.

These checks are

a flat 20

and do not gain the benefit of any bonuses, buffs, spells or help.

Learning a weapon proficiency or an armor proficiency.

Learning a

weapon proficiency

requires the aforementioned 20 checks. In addition the weapon must also be used successfully to make

10 attacks in combat.

For the combat checks, you may add the appropriate strength or dex modifier. Only a melee attack made against an appropriate target that hit will count toward your progression. You cannot practice on object, willing people or any other work around.

Similarly, learning an

armor proficiency

requires that the armor be worn in combat and melee attacks be made against your armor class. Only a melee attack made against your armor class (by an appropriate opponent) that fails to hit you will count toward your progression. In both cases, the checks cannot be aided in any other way. No help, inspiration or feature may be used. You must prove yourself proficient on your own. If you are having trouble finding a villain to take a swing at you, let us know. (Don't worry about it. This will be fine...)

Training:

Finding an appropriate trainer can help to speed the process.

PC trainers.

Players can be trainers.

This however must be a part of your backstory and comes at the cost of being able to be trained by other players. To put it more clearly:

Player character trainers cannot learn from other players.

Training guarantees 1 pass per day on your checks, It costs 50 dollars per day in either CR or AR to train. This must be farmed by the trainee. NPC trainers also exist, but only in one of the three towns we will visit. The cost for an NPC trainer is 100 dollars per check to give adv on each check.

Expertise with tools, instruments, and vehicles:

The training cost for raising your non skill (so nothing from the skill list that contains skills like Arcana, stealth, perception ect) proficiencies is an additional 40 checks raising your total to 60 cumulative passed checks. Weapons, armor languages and skills cannot gain expertise in this way.
