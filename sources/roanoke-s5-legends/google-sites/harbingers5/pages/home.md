Their journey into the arcane arts begins with a touch of the extraordinary, as the future itself comes knocking on their door. Whether through celestial alignments, prophetic dreams, or unexplained encounters with supernatural forces, the Harbingers are chosen vessels for the weave's potent magic.

From an early age, the signs of their destiny become apparent, as if the very stars in the sky whisper secrets exclusively meant for their ears. This unique limitation grants them unparalleled control over their magic, but it also demands a deep understanding of oneself. Failure to master the pooled weave within their being can result in dire consequences, as magical ailments and curses may afflict the unprepared.

While Harbingers possess the ability to glimpse into the future and unveil hidden truths, they often choose a path far removed from the pursuit of fame and recognition. Their motivations lie in the exploration and understanding of the weave that courses through them, rather than in the applause of crowds or the pursuit of power. Their presence on the battlefield is a testament to their resilience and indomitable spirit, as they navigate the ever-shifting currents of destiny with unwavering determination.

Interacting with a Harbinger is an experience that both intrigues and perplexes. Other spellcasters and magic wielders are captivated by their unique divination skills, seeking their insights and guidance. Regardless, the Harbingers' presence leaves an indelible mark on those they encounter, forever altering their perception of the weave and the possibilities it holds.

Embrace the path of the Harbinger, where the convergence of fate and magic ignites an extraordinary journey. Creating a Harbinger

When delving into the creation of a Harbinger, it is crucial to consider the unique circumstances that have shaped their path and the deep connection they possess with the weave of magic. While other spellcasters meticulously study ancient tomes or forge pacts with supernatural entities, a Harbinger's journey begins with an innate affinity for divination and an unbreakable bond with the threads of fate.

To embark on the creation of a Harbinger character, ponder the circumstances that led to their discovery of this latent power. Were they born with the gift, finding themselves thrust into a world where the future whispered its secrets directly to them? Or did they stumble upon an extraordinary event or encounter, where the touch of destiny transformed their very being? Perhaps it was a family curse, a blessing from a celestial entity, or exposure to the unfathomable forces that shape reality.

Consider how your Harbinger feels about the magic that courses through their veins. Do they embrace it wholeheartedly, eager to explore and master its potential? Or do they view it as a burden, a weight that they grapple with as they navigate the complexities of their existence? How do they cope with the intensity of their experiences, the profound connection to divination, and the revelations that unfold before them?

The choice of background for a Harbinger can further enhance their narrative and provide insights into their past and motivations. The Hermit background, for instance, reflects a solitary and introspective nature, suggesting that the Harbinger sought solace and understanding in seclusion. Perhaps they withdrew from society to immerse themselves in the study of ancient texts and obscure rituals, deepening their connection to the weave. Or maybe they retreated from the world due to a traumatic event, using their newfound abilities to protect themselves from unseen dangers.

Harbinger's Wrist Pocket (4th level):

At 4th level, Harbinger gains the ability to use a permanent extra-dimensional space known as the Wrist Pocket.

By flicking their wrist, Harbinger can make an object in their hand vanish.

The object must weigh no more than 15 pounds and only Harbinger can be holding it.

The object is transported to the extradimensional space, where it remains until Harbinger chooses to manifest it.

Harbinger can use their action to summon the object back to their free hand or use their action to return it to the extradimensional space.

This ability is a spell-like ability and is not subject to any form of disenchantment.

2nd Level: Mortality

The Harbinger gains an awareness of their own mortality and the death that fate has designed for them.

As an action, the Harbinger can invoke the weave within to limit the abilities of others.

The Harbinger can grant momentary resistance to any creature within 5 feet for a single round, choosing from different damage types.

6th Level: Dwindling Hope.

As an action, the Harbinger can magically force a Large or smaller creature they can see within 60 feet to make a Constitution saving throw against the Harbinger's spell save DC.

On a failed saving throw, the creature becomes lethargic and is limited to a single action, bonus action, or movement.

The creature can retry their saving throw at the end of their turn.

The Harbinger can use this feature a number of times per day equal to their Constitution modifier, regaining all expended uses after a long rest.

9th Level: Arresting Energy

When another friendly or neutral creature that the Harbinger can see within 60 feet fails a save that would result in damage, the Harbinger can grant resistance to that damage type for up to a minute.

This effect can be granted to a number of creatures of the Harbinger's choice.

This resistance effect does not stack with other forms of resistance.

14th Level: Harbinger of Endings Feature

As a reaction, the Harbinger can force the weave to become a physicals barrier, seeping forth from their soul to their skin for 6 seconds.

While the effect is active, any creature that targets the Harbinger with an attack or a harmful spell must make the attack at disadvantage.

If the attack forces the Harbinger to make a saving throw, they have advantage on the saving throw.

On a failed attack, the creature must choose a new target or attack itself if no other valid targets are available.

The Harbinger can use this feature a number of times equal to their proficiency modifier.

Harbinger of Fated Bonds

The Harbinger of Fated Bonds is blends the mystical art of divination with the precise mastery of reach weapons. As a Harbinger of Fated Bonds, you possess an innate ability to perceive the intricate threads of destiny and utilize the extended reach of your weapons to forge powerful connections between yourself and the touch-range spells you wield.

2nd Level: Weapon weaver

At 2nd level, you gain proficiency with reach weapons, such as glaives, halberds, or whips. These weapons become conduits for your divinatory powers, allowing you to channel touch-range spells through them. When you successfully hit a creature with a reach weapon attack, you can unleash the spell's effect upon the target, linking their fate to the spell's magic. Additionally, if the spell requires a saving throw, the target has disadvantage on the saving throw.

6th Level: Threads of Destiny

Omen's Doom:

Once per short rest, you can unleash a wave of impending doom upon your enemies. As an action, you conjure a swirling vortex of dark energy within 30 feet of you. Each creature of your choice within the vortex must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC or suffer the effects of a bestow curse spell for 1 minute. The cursed creature has disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws, and at the start of each of its turns, it takes necrotic or radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier.

9th Level: Omen of Dread

At 9th level, you gain the ability to unleash a chilling omen that taps into the primal fears of your target, causing them to react in different ways based on a d4 roll. As an action, you can make a touch attack against a creature within your reach, channeling your divinatory powers to inflict an Omen of Dread upon them. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, the target is overwhelmed by a surge of fear and is charmed by you until the end of your next turn. The target must roll a d4 to determine their reaction:

Fight: The target is consumed by aggression and must use its action on each of its turns to make a melee attack against a creature of its choice within its reach.

Flight: The target is gripped by panic and must use its movement on each of its turns to move as far away from you as possible, using the most direct route.

Freeze: The target is paralyzed by fear and becomes incapacitated, unable to take actions or reactions. The effect ends if the target takes any damage or if another creature uses an action to shake the target out of its stupor.

Fawn: The target becomes overwhelmed with a desperate need for your approval and seeks to appease you. It will try to help you or follow your commands to the best of its abilities, as long as they do not pose a direct threat to itself or its allies.

14th Level: Threads Unleashed

At 14th level, your command over the threads of destiny becomes absolute. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a reach weapon attack while a touch-range spell is channeled through it using your Omenweaver feature, you can extend the spell's effect to another creature within 10 feet of the target. The secondary creature must be within your reach and is affected by the same spell effect as the primary target, forging a fated bond between them.

Harbinger of Veils

The Harbinger of Veils subclass embodies the secrets and mysteries that lie beyond the perceivable world. Through their unique connection to the ethereal plane and their manipulation of veils, they gain unparalleled sight, stealth, and divinatory prowess. Their abilities allow them to navigate through the shadows, uncover hidden truths, and traverse the boundaries between realms.

2nd Level: Veiled Presence

The Harbinger gains proficiency in the Deception and Stealth skills.

Additionally, they can expend a spell slot to cast the Disguise Self spell without using a spell slot. They regain the ability to cast Disguise Self in this way after a short or long rest.

6th Level: Veilweaver's Manipulation

The Harbinger gains the ability to manipulate the fabric of reality within a limited area.

As an action, they can create a 20-foot-radius sphere of magical distortion centered on themselves.

This distortion provides half-cover to the Harbinger and their allies within the area.

Additionally, enemies within the area have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to perceive anything outside the sphere.

This effect lasts for 1 minute or until the Harbinger dismisses it as a bonus action.

9th Level: Enigmatic Shroud

The Harbinger gains the ability to wrap themselves in an enigmatic shroud of illusion, enhancing their defensive capabilities.

As a bonus action, they can shroud themselves in illusions, gaining resistance to all damage until the end of their next turn.

Once they use this feature, they must finish a short or long rest before they can use it again.

14th Level: Master of Illusions

The Harbinger becomes a master of illusions, gaining the ability to cast major illusion once per long rest without using a spell slot.

Additionally, when they cast an illusion spell of 1st level or higher, they can choose to make it more potent.

Creatures that interact with the illusion or try to discern its nature must make an Intelligence saving throw against the Harbinger's spell save DC or be affected as if by the Phantasmal Force spell.

This effect lasts for the duration of the illusion spell.
