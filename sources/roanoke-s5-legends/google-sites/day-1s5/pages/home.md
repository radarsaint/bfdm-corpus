Bodfish notes

Sheriff Silas

Sheriff Silas McCallister is a weathered lawman with a stern demeanor. He is a tall, broad-shouldered man in his late fifties, with a salt-and-pepper beard that frames a weathered face. He wears a worn, but meticulously maintained, lawman's badge pinned to his leather vest. As players approach him, they notice a sense of authority and experience emanating from his presence.

Player:

"Sheriff, what can you tell us about these disappearances?"

Sheriff McCallister:

"Folks 'round here have been vanishing without a trace. Miners heading into the silver mines and never coming out. It's a mystery that's been plaguing us for months. I need your help to get to the bottom of it."

Player: "Why do you suspect foul play?"

Sheriff McCallister:

"These ain't your regular run-of-the-mill accidents or wanderings. It's as if the mines are swallowing people whole. No signs of struggle, no trace left behind. That ain't natural, I tell ya. We need someone with a keen eye and a willingness to dig deep to solve this."

Player: "Any leads or suspects?"

Sheriff McCallister:

"There's a handful of folks who've been acting mighty suspicious lately. Some outsiders who arrived in town not too long ago. They've been poking their noses where they don't belong. Could be they know more than they let on. Find 'em, question 'em, and see what you can uncover."

Player: "What's at stake if we don't solve this?"

Sheriff McCallister:

"If we don't put a stop to this, Bodfish Boom-town will wither away. Folks will lose faith, miners will leave, and the town will crumble. We can't let that happen. We owe it to the hardworking folks who built this place to keep it safe and prosperous."

Propmpts to use with Sheriff McCallister:

The Whistling Willow:

As players explore the outskirts of Bodfish, they come across an enormous willow tree. It is said that when the wind blows through its branches, it produces an otherworldly, melodic whistle. The players can spend some peaceful moments listening to the enchanting tune, feeling a sense of calm and tranquility wash over them.

The Luminescent Moths:

While wandering through the silver mines, players stumble upon a hidden chamber filled with luminescent moths. These ethereal creatures emit a soft glow, casting an enchanting light throughout the area. The moths flutter around the players, creating a magical ambiance. Interacting with them may reveal a clue or hidden message, guiding the players further in their investigation.

The Whispering Stones:

In a secluded part of Bodfish, players discover a small circle of stones with strange carvings. When they approach, they hear faint whispers emanating from the stones. The whispers seem to be a mixture of voices, ancient tales, and cryptic messages. Deciphering the meaning behind these whispers can lead to valuable insights or a hidden cache of supplies.

The Trickster Raccoon:

Players encounter a mischievous raccoon known as "Old Bandit" who has a penchant for stealing shiny objects. This raccoon has managed to swipe several valuable items from the local miners and has hidden them in a nearby hollow tree. Players can engage in a non-combative encounter as they attempt to outwit the clever creature and retrieve the stolen goods, showcasing their wit and resourcefulness.

The Healing Hot Springs:

Deep within the wilderness surrounding Bodfish, players stumble upon a serene hot spring. The water possesses mystical properties that can heal wounds and ailments. Taking a dip in these rejuvenating waters grants the players temporary hit point restoration and a sense of revitalization. This encounter offers a moment of respite and rejuvenation amidst the challenges they face.

ACE:

Introduction:

Ace:

Well, howdy there, partner! Looking to test your luck in these parts? I reckon you've come to the right place. Name's Ace, they call me "The Gambler." What brings you to Bodfish, the heart of the silver rush?

Player: Nice to meet you, Ace. I've heard there's some high-stakes gambling happening in these parts.

Ace:

You heard right, my friend. But if you're after the highest stakes of all time, then Legend City is the place for you. It's the gambling capital of the West, where fortunes are made and lost with the turn of a card or the roll of the dice.

Information about Legend City:

Player: Legend City, huh? Tell me more about it.

Ace:

Legend City is a sight to behold. It's the kind of place where dreams are born and shattered in the blink of an eye. You'll find the grandest saloons, the flashiest casinos, and the most skilled card sharps you'll ever come across. But beware, partner, the stakes are high, and the competition is fierce. Only the sharpest of minds and the luckiest of hands can come out on top.

The Highest Stakes:

Player: You mentioned something about the highest stakes of all time. What's that all about?

Ace:

Ah, you're talking about "The Showdown," partner. It's the most legendary gambling event in the entire West. It takes place once a year in Legend City, drawing the best gamblers from far and wide. It's a no-holds-barred tournament where the stakes are as high as they come. Legends are made and fortunes are won or lost in a single night.

The Prizes:

Player: What do the winners of "The Showdown" get?

Ace:

The winner of "The Showdown" walks away with more than just a hefty pile of dollars, my friend. The grand prize is a deed to the most valuable piece of land in all of Cascadia—a ranch in the heart of Legend City itself. It's a prize that can set you up for life if you're lucky enough to claim it. But remember, the road to victory is treacherous, and the competition is fierce.

Advice for Players:

Player: Any advice for someone thinking about entering "The Showdown"?

Ace:

The key to surviving "The Showdown" is to know your opponents, know the game, and trust your instincts. But be warned, partner, the highest stakes also mean the highest risks. Don't let your desire for glory blind you. Sometimes, folding your hand can be the smartest move you'll ever make. And remember, even if luck doesn't favor you, there's always another game to play.

Villain: Ezekiel.

Overheard Conversation:

While exploring a saloon in Bodfish, the players catch snippets of a conversation between two suspicious individuals. One says, "Our coins hold more than just value. They are eyes that see all. Keep them close, for they reveal our secrets." This cryptic dialogue hints at the hidden nature of the coins and their connection to the Illuminati.

Cryptic Symbol:

During their travels, the players come across a worn symbol etched into a tree or a wall. The symbol resembles an eye surrounded by intricate designs, evoking a sense of mystery and secrecy. This symbol, though seemingly insignificant at first, is a subtle nod to the Illuminati's presence and their surveillance methods.

Mysterious Encounters:

As the players interact with various NPCs in different towns, they encounter individuals who seem unusually well-informed about their activities and plans. These NPCs drop subtle hints or vague warnings, such as, "Beware the all-seeing eye that follows your every step. Trust not what glimmers in your pockets."

An Unusual Coin:

The players stumble upon a peculiar coin in the possession of a traveler or a merchant. Upon closer examination, they notice intricate markings on the coin that they haven't seen before. It bears resemblance to the cryptic symbol they encountered earlier. This serves as a clue that the coins themselves may hold secrets and could be used for tracking purposes.

Suspicious Banker:

In Legend City, the players interact with a seemingly friendly banker who offers them assistance in storing their wealth and valuables. However, keen observers notice that the banker pays particular attention to their coins and their transactions, exhibiting a level of scrutiny that goes beyond normal banking procedures. This hints at the connection between the bank and the tracking of the coins.

Main Quest

As the players enjoy their evening in the saloon, a seemingly ordinary patron takes notice of their group. The individual approaches their table, a sly smile playing upon their lips.

Mysterious Patron:

"Well, well, seems we have a group of individuals eager to make their mark on this wild frontier. I must say, I admire your spirit."

Player:

"What do you mean? Do we know each other?"

Mysterious Patron:

"Oh, not personally, my friends. But tales of opportunity seekers like yourselves have a way of spreading like wildfire in these parts. You see, I have a keen eye for potential.

Player:

"So, what brings you here? What do you want from us?"

Mysterious Patron:

"Straight to the point, I like that. I'll be honest with you—this treasure, this hidden promise—it's something I've pursued for quite some time. I've come close, so close that I can taste the riches. But fate has a funny way of putting obstacles in one's path."

Player: "If you're so close, why don't you find it yourself?"

Mysterious Patron:

"Ah, an astute question, my friend. The truth is, while I possess knowledge, experience, and resources, I lack something that you possess in abundance—freedom. You see, I'm bound by obligations, responsibilities, and powerful forces that limit my movements."

Player:

"Powerful forces? What are you talking about?"

Mysterious Patron:

"Let's just say that I'm part of a larger game, a grand design in which even the most cunning players must exercise caution. By enlisting your aid, I can operate behind the scenes, pulling the strings and ensuring that the path remains clear for you."

Player: "So, you want us to do the dirty work while you reap the rewards?"

Mysterious Patron:

"Not quite, my friend. Think of me as a guide, a mentor on this treacherous journey. I'll provide you with information, resources, and guidance. But make no mistake, the challenges you'll face will require your bravery, wit, and ingenuity. The reward, when it comes, will be shared, as it should be."

Player: "And what if we refuse? Why should we trust you?"

Mysterious Patron:

"Refusal is always an option, my friends. But remember, this treasure remains hidden, and its secrets guarded. If you choose not to seize this opportunity, the treasure will forever elude you. As for trust, I understand your skepticism. But know this—I have much to lose if I deceive you. Our fates are intertwined, and success lies in cooperation. The choice, ultimately, is yours."
