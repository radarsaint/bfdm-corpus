Cringe

Cantrip (Bard, Sorcerer)

Casting Time:

1 action

Range: 60 feet

Components:

M (Something Embarrassing)

Duration:

Instantaneous

Choose a target within range that you can see and hear. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become overwhelmed with an intense feeling of embarrassment and awkwardness. On a failed save, the target takes 1d4 psychic damage and has disadvantage on its next ability check, attack roll, or saving throw it makes before the end of its next turn.

At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d4 for each slot level above 5th.

Dread

1st-level Enchantment (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)

Casting Time:

1 action

Range:

60 feet

Components:

V, S

Duration:

Concentration, up to 1 minute

You weave sinister whispers into the air, invoking a sense of impending doom and unease. Choose a creature within range that you can see. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it becomes plagued by haunting whispers that fill its mind.

While affected by this spell, the target has disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and Charisma (Persuasion) checks as the whispers distract and unsettle its thoughts. Additionally, any creature that attacks the target gains advantage on the attack roll, as the whispers undermine the target's ability to defend itself effectively.

The target can attempt to end the effect at the end of each of its turns, making a successful Wisdom saving throw. On a success, the whispers dissipate, and the spell ends.

At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the duration increases by 1 minute for each slot level above 1st.

Frustration

1st-level Enchantment (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)

Casting Time:

1 action

Range:

Touch

Components:

V, S

Duration:

Instantaneous

You channel a surge of frustration into a touch, transferring it to a humanoid creature you touch. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target becomes overwhelmed by frustration, causing them to act in a volatile and unpredictable manner.

For the duration of one round, the target must make an immediate improvised weapon attack against a creature of your choice within its reach. If there are no eligible targets within reach, the target attacks itself in its frustration, using an improvised weapon if available.

After making the attack, the spell ends, and the target's frustration dissipates. They regain control over their actions and return to their normal state.

At Higher Levels:

When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st. The creatures must be within your touch range and meet the other requirements.

Bliss

Spell Level:

1st level

School:

Enchantment

Casting Time:

1 action

Range:

60 feet

Components:

V, S

Duration:

Concentration, up to 1 minute

Classes:

Bard, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard

Description:

You fill a creature you can see within range with overwhelming feelings of bliss and joy. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it becomes enveloped in a state of euphoria, experiencing the following effects:

The target gains temporary hit points equal to 1d6 + your spellcasting ability modifier.

The target has advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks for the duration, as its joyful disposition makes it more charming.

The target gains resistance to psychic damage for the duration, as its heightened sense of happiness shields it from mental harm.

At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the temporary hit points gained increase by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.

Boldness

2nd-level Enchantment (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)

Casting Time:

1 action

Range:

Self

Components:

V, S

Duration:

Concentration, up to 1 minute

You tap into your inner reservoir of confidence, casting aside doubt and hesitation. A surge of bold energy radiates from you, enveloping your being. While under the effects of this spell, you gain the following benefits:

You have advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks.

You have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

You can use your reaction to make a melee weapon attack against a creature that hits you with a melee attack.

Additionally, any creatures hostile to you that start their turn within 10 feet of you must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, they have disadvantage on their attack rolls against targets other than you until the start of their next turn.

At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the duration increases by 1 minute for each slot level above 2nd.

Amnesty

3rd-level Enchantment Spell

(

paladin, cleric, ranger

)

Casting Time:

1 action

Range:

30 feet

Components:

V, S, M (a small vial of potent amnesiac elixir worth at least 300 gold pieces, which is consumed upon casting)

Duration:

Concentration, up to 1 minute

You invoke the power of forgiveness, compelling the target to seek redemption for their past actions. Choose a creature within range that you can see. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target is overwhelmed with remorse and a deep desire to make amends.

While under the effects of this spell, the target seeks forgiveness by offering acts of assistance to those they have harmed during the current combat encounter. At the beginning of each of the target's turns, you can use your bonus action to command the target to move up to its movement speed in a direction you choose.

The caster also has the ability to choose one of the following actions for the target to take on each of its turns:

Help: The target assists an ally of the caster's choice within reach, providing advantage on the next ability check or attack roll the ally makes before the end of their turn.

Healing Touch: If the target has the ability to heal, it uses its action to administer healing to a creature of the caster's choice within reach, restoring hit points equal to a standard healing ability it possesses.

Stabilize: The target makes a Medicine check to stabilize a dying creature of the caster's choice within reach, preventing them from making death saving throws.

The target will prioritize these actions over any hostile or self-serving actions but will defend themselves if directly attacked. The target can reroll the Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the spell on a successful save.

Defiance

3rd-level Abjuration

Casting Time:

1 action

Range:

Self

Components:

V, S, M (a small stone or brick)

Duration:

Concentration, up to 1 minute

You tap into the essence of defiance, channeling it to create a powerful aura that bolsters your resolve. When you cast this spell, an invisible field of unwavering determination envelops you, radiating an aura of defiance in a 10-foot radius around you. The aura lasts for the duration.

While the Defiance aura is active, you and friendly creatures within its radius gain the following benefits:

Emotional Resilience: You have advantage on saving throws against being frightened. The aura instills you with unwavering courage and fortitude in the face of fear.

Inspiring Presence: When a friendly creature within the aura makes a weapon attack, it deals additional psychic damage equal to your spellcasting ability modifier. The sheer force of your defiance inspires your allies to strike with greater determination.

Defiant Retaliation: Whenever you or a friendly creature within the aura takes damage, the attacker suffers psychic damage equal to half the damage taken (rounded down). The sheer force of your unwavering defiance inflicts a mental backlash upon those who dare to harm you.

At Higher Levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the radius of the aura increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 3rd.

Dumb Ways to Die

4th-level Necromancy (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)

Casting Time:

1 action

Range:

60 feet

Components:

V, S, M (a small vial of black powder)

Duration:

Instantaneous

You unleash a chaotic burst of energy, manifesting as a dark cloud of swirling black powder. Choose a point within range as the epicenter of the spell. The cloud expands in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point.

Each creature within the sphere must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 6d6 necrotic damage and experiences a vision of various absurd and perilous scenarios that represent "dumb ways to die." These visions momentarily distract and confuse the affected creatures.

Additionally, creatures that fail the saving throw suffer disadvantage on their next attack roll or ability check they make before the end of their next turn as they are momentarily disoriented by the visions. However, creatures with an Intelligence score of 4 or lower are immune to these effects, as they are not able to comprehend the nature of the visions.

At Higher Levels:

When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 4th.

Exaggerated Examples of "Dumb Ways to Die" in the Old West:

1.

The Leeching Lunacy:

A poor soul, suffering from a minor ailment, seeks the help of a "doctor" who fervently believes in the ancient practice of bleeding. However, the doctor goes overboard, draining the patient of all their blood and ensuring an untimely demise.

2. S

nake Oil Swindle:

An unsuspecting individual falls victim to a charismatic snake oil salesman peddling a miraculous elixir. Believing the false promises of health and vitality, they consume the concoction, only to discover that it was laced with a deadly poison.

3.

Grizzly's Grudge:

A daring adventurer decides to prove their bravery by taunting a grizzly bear in the wilderness. The bear retaliates and bites off their head in a single swift motion, leaving behind a cautionary tale for those who underestimate nature's fury.

4.

Tainted Tins:

A hungry traveler, desperate for sustenance, purchases a can of dubious potted meat from a shady vendor. Unbeknownst to them, the meat is contaminated and leads to severe food poisoning, resulting in a grim end in the middle of nowhere.

5.

Insurance Fiasco:

In an attempt to secure a hefty insurance payout, a desperate individual resorts to drastic measures. They take a knife and attempt to cut off their own arm, but miscalculate the procedure and accidentally bleed out, all for naught.

6.

Dynamite Disarray:

An adventurous prospector stumbles upon a hidden cache of dynamite in an abandoned mine. Ignoring the warnings and lack of knowledge, they mishandle the explosive, leading to a catastrophic detonation that obliterates them and everything nearby.

Name: Shame

Enchantment

1 action

Range:

60 feet

Components:

V, S

Duration:

Instantaneous

Classes:

Bard, Warlock, Wizard

Description:

This spell inflicts a deep sense of shame and guilt upon a target creature, causing them to momentarily lose their will to fight or resist. Terrifying Pursuit

4th-level illusion (Warlock, Wizard, Druid)

Casting Time:

1 action

Range:

Touch

Components:

V, S, M (a small figurine representing the target creature)

Duration:

Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch a willing animal (that fits the following criteria: Beast, cr1 or lower that is size category small or tiny), transforming it into a horrifying caricature of itself. The creature gains a twisted and menacing appearance, and its eyes radiate an aura of dread. Choose a target creature within the animal's movement speed that it can see. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, it becomes frightened of the transformed creature for the duration of the spell.

While frightened, the target must use its movement to move away from the transformed creature, if able, on each of its turns. If the target is unable to move away due to obstacles or other creatures, it can attempt a saving throw at the end of its turn to try to end the frightened condition.

The transformed creature gains the following benefits:

Its movement speed increases by 10 feet.

It has advantage on Dexterity saving throws and ability checks.

It can use the Dash action as a bonus action on each of its turns to pursue the target.

If the target succeeds on the saving throw, the transformed creature becomes frightened of the target for the duration of the spell. The transformed creature must use its movement to move away from the target, if able, on each of its turns, and it cannot willingly move closer to the target. The effect ends when combat ends.

The spell's duration is concentration, and you must maintain concentration to keep the transformed creature pursuing the target. If your concentration ends, the spell ends.

At Higher Levels:

When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the duration increases by 1 minute for each slot level above 4th.

Spell:

Desolation

Level:

6th level Necromancy (Bard, Sorcery, Artificer, Ranger

Casting Time:

1 action

Range:

120 feet

Components:

V, S

Duration:

Concentration, up to 1 minute

Classes:

Wizard

Description:

You tap into the essence of desolation, unleashing a wave of bleak and sorrowful energy that envelops the area in an aura of despair. A sense of hopelessness and melancholy fills the hearts of those within a 30-foot radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range.

While the spell is active, the following effects occur within the aura:

Withering Gloom: The aura inflicts 6d8 necrotic damage on each creature that starts its turn within the area or enters it for the first time on a turn. A creature can make a Constitution saving throw, taking half damage on a success.

Lingering Sorrow: Any creature that fails a saving throw against the aura's effect becomes afflicted with lingering sorrow. The affected creature has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks, and it cannot regain hit points. Additionally, the creature's speed is halved.

Despairing Influence: Hostile creatures within the area have disadvantage on saving throws against being frightened. Additionally, any creature that fails a saving throw against the aura's effect must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature becomes overwhelmed by despair and falls prone, incapacitated by its profound hopelessness until the start of its next turn.

At Higher Levels:

When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the necrotic damage from the Withering Gloom increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 6th.

Spell: Contagious Despair

Level:

7th level

School:

Enchantment

Casting Time:

1 action

Range:

60 feet

Components:

V, S, M (a tear-shaped crystal)

Duration:

Concentration, up to 1 minute

Classes:

Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard

Description:

With the casting of this spell, you unleash a wave of overwhelming despair and hopelessness upon your enemies. Choose a point within range as the epicenter of the spell. A sphere with a 30-foot radius centered on that point becomes shrouded in an aura of deep despair.

Each creature in the area must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is overcome with overwhelming sorrow, becoming incapacitated and unable to take actions or reactions. The creature's movement speed is reduced to 0, and it can't benefit from any bonuses to its speed.

Additionally, while the spell is active, any creature that starts its turn within the area of despair takes 4d8 psychic damage as the weight of hopelessness weighs upon them.

At the start of each of the affected creature's turns, it can make another Wisdom saving throw to end the spell's effects. On a successful save, the creature breaks free from the despair and regains control of its actions.

At Higher Levels:

When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 8th level or higher, the psychic damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 7th.
