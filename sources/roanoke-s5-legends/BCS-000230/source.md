The year is 1849, Gold has been found “out west” in the Brownbear republic.

Badfish Boomtown.  
This is a silver mine boom town.

Georgie Bodfish was once a sea captain of dubious nature. His ranch was the only thing in these hills for 30 years. When the railroad paid him (handsomely) to install tracks through his land, he built his own telegram office. As the tracks were being laid down it took the railway 6 months before another place was available for the train to stop. Prospectors would need to start here in order to make their way overland and down into the valley.  Now, where there are prospectors, there is prospecting. And it wasn't long before silver was discovered in them thar hills. This is how Bodfish Ranh became known as Silver Town.

Boom towns are notoriously lawless.   
The sudden influx of money into what was recently a tiny trading post has brought in hundreds of people scrambling to make a profit, all the while the town is ill equipped to deal with all of the residents, all of their problems, and all of their crimes.There is a single overwhelmed sheriff and a deputy.

Puffkin:  
“I was born to the sun and the soil just as you were born to bronze and cobbled stone.” The strange figure remarked. Orrap hesitated. She had been warned not to trust strangers, but this one seemed harmless enough. She nodded hesitantly as the creature continued. “And yet, we are not so different. We both think, both breathe, both hope and wish.” “But you aren’t like me.” Orrap replied. “You’re just a talking mushroom.” At this, a loud wheezing laugh burst from the small fungal form. “My apologies, dear.” The figure remarked. “Allow me to show you a different perspective.” The fungal form shook for a moment, before extending to its full height, revealing its limbs, face, and kind smile. “There we go.” “I-wow…” Orrap replied. She looked up at the adult puffkin in front of her, a lanky figure whose head appeared like a mushroom cap. Their skin was a bright yet gentle shade of orange, their limbs long and spindly, and their body sported various fungal growths. Yet despite all of that, they were unmistakably a humanoid. “Run along now, child.” The figure said from what Orrap could now recognize as a mouth. “Don’t want to worry your family.” Orrap nodded and smiled, before pausing. “What’s your name?” She remarked, wanting to know what to call her new friend. “Daralye” The figure smiled one last time before settling down into its previous stance. Orrap grinned and ran off, her thoughts filled with peoples not her own and new friends.  
   
   
Ability Score Increase Your Constitution and Wisdom scores each increase by 1  
   
Age Puffkin are born with generational memories through their parents, so are technically mentally mature after their few-month gestation period. However, the humanoid influence on their culture means that Puffkin are not considered adults until they are 10\. They live anywhere from 50-300 years, depending on climate, culture, and type of fungus they spring from.  
   
Alignment Puffkin tend to be on the welcoming side of lawful, though puffkin of any and every alignment are common. Size Puffkin undergo dramatic growth after their maturity. They begin their adult lives as Tiny creatures, and grow to be Small at level 3 and Medium at level 5\.  
   
Speed Your base walking speed is 15 feet. At level 3 this increases to 25 feet, and at level 5 it increases again to 30 feet.  
   
Languages You can speak and write Common. Puffkin communicate with each other telepathically most often, but use common when speaking out loud. Rapport Spores As an action, a puffkin can release spores around itself. Each creature within 20 feet of itself that the puffkin chooses gains a telepathic connection to all other creatures affected by this feature, including the puffkin who released the spores. This telepathy has a range of 30 feet and cannot be used through a solid barrier. This feature can be used 3 times a day, and lasts for 15 minutes. Puffkin can use this feature to communicate simple concepts with nearby fungus, though they cannot grant the other affected creatures the ability to do so.  
   
Distress Spores Whenever a puffkin is hurt, it alerts nearby allies through spores. All other puffkin within 120 feet, as well as those currently affected by the puffkin’s rapport spores can sense its pain and know a vague direction. The puffkin can use its reaction when it sends this signal to give more precise information, choosing either the distance or a precise direction.  
   
Natural Birth You have proficiency in the Nature skill.  
   
Subrace There are two main subraces of Puffkin: Sunrise Puffkin and Sunset Puffkin  
   
Sunrise Puffkin  
Sunrise puffkin often inhabit places like faerie circles and more often interact with other humanoid races, often forming long-lasting relationships with nearby villages. Their smaller appearances tend to make them more endearing than their sunset cousins, though they are just as fierce when provoked.  
   
Ability Score Increase Your Charisma score increases by 1  
   
Natural Cuteness As an action, you can look pleadingly at those around you. Until the end of your next turn, your allies gain advantage on persuasion checks against humanoids within 10 feet of you that can see you. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.  
   
Soft Body Because of your soft yet surprisingly adaptive body, you are resistant to fall damage.  
   
Distracting Spores You can use your action to release a cloud of spores to a target location within 15 feet of you. For the next minute, the spores emit a nonverbal sound (footsteps, waves, etc.) or a smell. When you reach level 5, you can sneeze adorably once per day as a reaction, changing the memories of creatures in a 15-foot cone in front of you as if by the spell gift of gab.  
   
Sunset Puffkin  
Sunset puffkin are tougher and hardier than their sunrise cousins, though they are no less kind or benevolent. While not as familiar with other humanoid races, they will form friendships with them all the same, especially with those who keep especially late or especially early hours. They tend to make their homes underground, though they steer clear of any malevolent forces.  
   
Ability Score Increase Your Constitution score increases by another 1 for a total of 2\.  
   
Darkvision Used to living underground, you can see easily in dark conditions. You can see in dim light as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light for 60 feet in front of you.  
   
Bioluminescense As a bonus action, you can emit dim light in a radius of 15 feet centered on you. You can end this effect as a bonus action. Once per day, you can cause the dim light to become bright light, no action required. This bright light lasts for one minute or until you end it as a bonus action.  
   
Sudden Bloom Once per day when you are hit by an attack, you may gain temporary hit points equal to 1d8 \+ your Constitution modifier as a reaction. When you reach level 5, you can cast the spell gaseous form on yourself, with a reduced duration of 15 minutes.

Welcome to Arcania.  
The year is 1849, Gold has been found “out west” in the Brownbear republic.

Arcania is in The New World.

The entirety of the earth is founded on something called "The world's cornerstone."

It is in fact a tesseract with the new world folded away from the old world. 

The old world contains all of the previous Gods and Goddesses. The current state of the old world is that all of the Neutral aligned gods have been consumed by a creature (my bbeg) called Croatoan (a sibriex)  
Without the neutral Gods, Good and Evil Gods went to war over the domains of the missing neutral gods.

And so, Arcania was folded away and hidden behind a barrier that prevented full gods from entering. 

Within the barrier The highest class of creature are called The Wardens.   
They are Columbia, Cascadia, Fontaine, Oro, Sandigil and Crockett. Each embodies a set of ideals, and had the opportunity to become gods of the old world, but refused to undergo the Apotheosis (god making process). It is required of a warden that they were given the opportunity and refused.

The wardens, demigods, Exarch seraphs and cryptids walk amongst us. They are not some intangible inaccessible thought creatures.

Wardens are reasonable choices for Clerics (if you don't want to use wotc).   
demigods, and seraphs are reasonable additions to your choices for warlocks (again this is preference, not requirement.)

To the North East is the domain of Columbia the Warden of Liberty and the countries of These United States of Arcania, refugee colonies from the old world given sanctuary from the wars. And the Jefferson Empire. (not available for this game.)  
Her words are “Liberty above all.” Her emotions can be volatile. When Columbia loves a town, an idea or a hero, she has no reservations about heavily influencing situations. Columbia is the least omniscient of the wardens. She does not accept the worship or gifts of gratitude offered by those she guards, choosing to avoid even the appearance of domination. Her symbol is that of the uplifted torch, gold on a teal background. It is the most popular decoration in her region.  
Notable locations include:  
Empire City (the most populous and advanced city on the continent. This is the New York city analog for our game. Think waterdeep from Faerune.

Roanoke island. A floating Island off the coast of Virginia that is the pinnacle of magic in the game. (Think Netheril from Faerune.)

Gneorgia. Gnomish georgia. Known for pioneering fuel from peach moonshine. This is a primarily Kimmilian settlement. (one of the homebrew races).

To the South East is the Domain of Fontaine, the Warden of Life.  
Fontaine, the Warden of Life governs the South East.   
Her words are ”We shall go on.” Life is sacred in the south east in the extreme. Fontaine does not suffer the death of her wards by any means of violence. Even animals cannot kill one another in her region. To eat meat in her region is to let the rest of the body of your prey or victim live on. There are those who revel in their near immortality. And there are those who languish, unable to perish. The Floridian jungles and swamps are bizarre in the extreme. None who take up residence there for more than a year may leave. When Fontaine counts you as hers, allowing you to leave is depriving you of your right to continue to be alive. Her symbol is an all seeing eye set in an orange blossom.  
Notable locations include:   
Mayanmi, location of the Fountain of Youth. This is Fontaine's personal city. Outsiders are forbidden.

Bayouque: (New Orleans) This fantastical city with it's french influence has several unique features. First is that it has a patreon demi god: Anansi the spider, keeper of stories. Next, Arcanian Creole is a language that does not contain tenses. For instance "My father is dead. He plays golf." is a reasonable thing to say.

To the North West, you have Columbia.  
Cascadia, the Warden of Desire.  
Her words are “Wanting is Joy. Having is death.” Recognizing Cascadia is remarkably hard. She lives a normal life. And she tends to the mundane needs of those around her. She does  not interfere in the pursuit of happiness of others so long as that pursuit does not interfere with anyone else’s happiness. She walks with strangers, and is quick to make friends with a person in a pub. In the Wilds of her region, one is likely to find all manner of odd and reclusive persons pursuing things they could not elsewhere. Cascadia’s symbol is two hands. One reaching upward toward a blooming rose. The other reached downward clutching a wilted rose.

Oro, Warden of the South West.  
Oro is the first non-humanoid warden in Arcania. His words are "None shall   
attack us with impunity." The South Western regions of Arcania are the richest of the continent. The wealth of gold mines is money that can be picked up out of the earth by anyone determined or lucky enough to find it.  
A wealth of fantastic creatures both sentient and monstrous. Some in great abundance. Others are rarer than treasure. Some are singular manifestations of myriad ley lines that traverse it from Death Valley to the Sentinels of Sequoia. Oro has no symbol, no home, and walks amongst the Cryptids Critters and Varmints of his domain with far fewer interactions with the humanoids who live there.

Oro is the only warden who is a non humanoid monster (an Androsphinx).

Legend.

Campaigns set in the western nation of the Brown Bear republic can be a scramble to claim your piece of the wealth. It will undoubtedly bump up against the Brown Bear republic whose political meritocracy is held to through both savage violence and strict customs. Legend is a colony city settled by the denizens of the Gold Rush. And the towns, homesteads and claims in the region are marked by a lack of adequate policing. Before the rush, the number of easterners who found their way west was tiny in comparison. With gold available in the region open to any who could just pick it up and take it the masses began to move. Many failed to arrive, lost to the wild frontier, the roaming rockies (a pod of tarasque) or the badlands. Those who arrived were brought in covered wagons by trail men. Covered to keep them from the horrors and temptations that abound along the North West Passage. (edited)

Politics:  
The natives of The Brown Bear republic are the bruneborn.  
These grizzlybear humanoids have a "double blind meritocracy."

In order to become a leader, you must be nominated by a professional. They must work beneath you, be a customer or be a merchant from another guild. The nominations are voted upon and the top 10 are sequestered for a period of one month. During this time, they set to the task of writing a document containing all of their political views, intentions, their personal history including both accomplishments and failures.

They do not sign these documents. They are then copied and voted upon by the public. This is how their leaders are chosen.   
Hidden messages or any other intentional indicators of identity are punishable by death.  
Their towns and cities are few and far between however, and the gold rush has brought forth new settlements, cities and regions.
