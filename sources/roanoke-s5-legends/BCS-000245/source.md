**Learning skills, tools, instruments, vehicles or languages**.

Maximum of 4 attempts to learn per day (1 per hour during down time.)  
Learning a skill takes a cumulative total of 20 passed checks.The dc for these checks is dc 12\. **These checks are a flat 20 and do not gain the benefit of any bonuses, buffs, spells or help.**

**Learning a weapon proficiency or an armor proficiency.**

Learning a weapon proficiency requires the aforementioned 20 checks. In addition the weapon must also be used successfully to make 10 attacks in combat. For the combat checks, you may add the appropriate strength or dex modifier. Only a melee attack made against an appropriate target that hit will count toward your progression. 

Similarly, learning an armor proficiency requires that the armor be worn in combat and melee attacks be made against your armor class. Only a melee attack made against your armor class (by an appropriate opponent) that fails to hit you will count toward your progression. 

In both cases, the checks cannot be aided in any other way. No help, inspiration or feature may be used. You must prove yourself proficient on your own.

**Animal (AR) and Animaln’t (ARn’T) resources.**  
Maximum of 2 attempts to gather per day (2 per hour during down time.)  
In monetary terms 1 penny \= 1 Gathered Resource or GR. AR available from traders. GR available from trappers.  
Traders and trappers are not available in cities or towns, and can only be found at trading posts or wandering in the wild.

These checks are for materials to craft equipment only (non-wondrous).   
Your DC for these checks will be DC12.   
You will need either a Survival or Investigation check for AR. Pass: 1d20 worth of AR. Fail: 1d10  
Likewise, you will need either a Nature or Animal Handling check for GR. A pass will gain you 1d20 worth of AR. A fail will gain you 1d10.

**Practice items**: Passing checks to create practice items will produce items that work only for you, and you alone (not even hirelings or familiars or anything else under your control). They cannot be sold and hold no value. Failed checks produce unusable junk. Resources cannot be reclaimed from practice attempts. If an item does not have a practice cost listed and is not specifically labeled as restricted or as having a N/A for a practice cost then the formula is x 0.5 the crafting costs in AR and/or GR as appropriate.

**Prerequisites:** In the case that an item **could** be crafted by more than one proficiency, choose **only one** tool proficiency to advance. In the case that an item **must** be crafted by more than one proficiency, advance your practice total for **each of** the required proficiencies.

**Crafted items:** These can be sold Typically at 1/3rd to 1/4th the shop price. Most stores will have a cap on how much they are willing to buy from the group as a whole (not just the individual). Items with a shop cost of (N/A) cannot be purchased and must be crafted. Items with the tag of (restricted) will be harder to buy or sell, may be considered weapons and therefore susceptible to certain hidden mechanics.

Tools cannot be crafted unless you are already proficient in that tool.
