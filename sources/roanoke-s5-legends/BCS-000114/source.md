Way of GunFu
Some Monks become specialists in hand to hand combat,
while others become experts in specific weapons, and yet
others learn to channel their inner power to create
supernatural effects. Monks that master the Way of Gunfu
blend melee and ranged tecniques to fight unharmed.
Way of Gunfu Features
Class Level Feature
3rd Path of the Firefight
6th Ki Dodge, Ki infused Bullets
11th Interrupting Strike
17th Focused Beatdown
Path of the Firefight
When you choose this tradition at 3rd level, your special
martial arts training leads you to master the use of certain
weapons. You gain the following benefits.
Firearm proficiency. You gain proficiency with firearms,
which includes using them as improvised weapons. These
are considered monk weapons for you.
One with the gun. You may use a firearm to replace one
unarmed strike you make with a flurry of blows. At level 11
you may replace both unarmed attacks from your flurry of
blows this way.
Ki Dodge
At 6th level, you have gained the ability to focus your senses
to dodge any attack. As a reaction, when an attack hits you
but before damage is assigned, you may use an amount of ki
equal to the differance between your AC and the triggering
attack. When you do, your AC increases to be just enough to
cause the attack to miss. Your AC will stay that high until the
beginning of your next turn.
Ki infused Bullets
At 6th level, you extend your ki into your Firearms, granting
you the following benefit.
Magic Firearm. Your attacks with firearms (including when
you use them as improvised weapons) count as magical for
the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to
nonmagical attacks and damage
Interrupting Strike
At 11th level, when you use your Ki Dodge class feature, you
may also make an attack against the creature that triggered
your Ki Dodge if they are within range. When you make this
attack roll, you gain a bonus to the attack and damage rolls
equal to the ki you spent on the Ki Dodge.
Focused Beatdown
At 17th level, on your turn, you can take one additional action.
From the moment you use this additional action, until the
end of your turn, all attacks you make are made with
advantage.
Once you use this feature, you must finish a short or long
rest before you can use it again
